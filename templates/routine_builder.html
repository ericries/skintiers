Routine builder

Assemble a routine from products on the site and see, at a glance, how strong the lineup is and what it actually contains. Nothing is saved to a server: your whole routine is encoded in this page's link, so you can bookmark it or share it.

How it works
  1. Search for a product and add it to your morning (AM), evening (PM), or Weekly routine.
  2. Set how often you use each one, or reorder the steps with the arrows.
  3. Read the dashboard: overall strength, your actives grouped by how strong the evidence for each one is, and heads-up flags for redundancy (two retinoids, a doubled active) or a heavy load of potentially-irritating actives.
  4. Use Copy link to save or share the routine. The link is the routine.
