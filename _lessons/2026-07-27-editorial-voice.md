# Lessons: editorial voice — why our first profiles came out bloated

**Date:** 2026-07-27

## Feedback this round (from the user, on the CeraVe product page and generally)
- Product pages had throat-clearing and research-process narration ("tiers are never blended").
- General category evidence ("does moisturizing work") was ON the product page instead of linked.
- "No independently sourced X found" padding — and sometimes misleading (implying no derm
  recommends CeraVe, which is implausible; we just lacked a *sourceable* one).
- Unnecessary disclaimers ("links may change / not endorsements"; "cited for composition only").
- A product page should answer ONE thing: how good is THIS product vs. peers — buy it or not.

## Five whys — root cause
1. Bloated? Templates prescribed a fixed set of sections regardless of whether each had substance.
2. Why fixed regardless of substance? We built the anatomy as a completeness checklist ("every
   tier present; say 'none found' rather than pad").
3. Why optimize for completeness? We inherited "rigor = comprehensive + show all tiers + announce
   gaps" from the anti-hallucination discipline and conflated *honest* with *exhaustive*.
4. Why the conflation? Anti-hallucination rules push toward verbosity; we never added a
   countervailing concision / reader-value principle.
5. Why no concision principle? We defined the content standard around SOURCING correctness and
   never defined a page's PURPOSE from the reader's side.

**ROOT CAUSE:** we specified how to *source* a page but never what a page is *for*. Absent a
purpose-driven "earns its place" rule, agents filled every section, and thoroughness read as
throat-clearing.

## Rules going forward
- Purpose-first authoring. A section/claim earns its place only if it serves the page's job.
- Product page purpose = a buying verdict vs. peers. Omit everything not decision-relevant.
- General category/mechanism evidence lives on a concept/ingredient/goal page; product pages link.
- Omit-empty: never announce an absence; delete the section. Never invent (silence, not fabrication).
- Comparisons: substance or a "broadly similar" link.
- No citation editorializing; no needless disclaimers/hedges.
- Codified in `docs/writing-guide.md` — the canonical voice reference for all authoring/review agents.
- Rigor = every INCLUDED claim bulletproof AND nothing unearned included. Honesty ≠ exhaustiveness.
