Treatment of melasma using kojic acid in a gel containing hydroquinone and glycolic acid (Dermatologic Surgery, 1999). Split-face RCT in 40 Chinese women isolating kojic acid's incremental contribution: the side receiving kojic acid cleared in 60% of patients vs. 47.5% without it over 12 weeks. A frequently cited, mechanistically clean trial for kojic acid's added benefit that would ground a kojic-acid ingredient page or the PIH section of hyperpigmentation.md. Relevant to [[hyperpigmentation]], [[hydroquinone]], [[glycolic-acid]]. PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10417583/ (a detailed lay summary is planned).
