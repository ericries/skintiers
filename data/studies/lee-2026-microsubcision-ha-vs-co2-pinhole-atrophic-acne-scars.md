A split-face comparison of two in-clinic procedures for atrophic [[acne-scars]], read one month after a single session, which found no statistically significant difference between them.[^study]

**Design:** "A 4-week prospective, single-center, evaluator-blinded, randomized, split-face study".[^study] Split-face is the design strength: each patient is their own control, which removes between-person variation from the comparison.

**Population / n:** Not reported. The abstract gives no participant count, and, checked by string search, contains no occurrence of patient, subject, participant, n =, female, male, mean or Fitzpatrick. A split-face trial with an unstated sample size cannot be weighed, and that single absence does more damage to this result than any other limitation below.

**Intervention / comparator:** needleless microsubcision with hyaluronic acid on one half-face against the carbon dioxide pinhole method on the other, one session each.[^study] Assessment used "the VISIA imaging tool, reflectance confocal microscopy (RCM), and histopathologic analysis" at one month.[^study]

**Primary result (verbatim):** "Microsubcision with HA improved scarring depth and postacne erythema more than CO 2 pinhole method, although the differences were not statistically significant."[^study] The histology and RCM findings are described rather than compared: microsubcision "induced diffuse soft tissue remodeling and evenly distributed fine collagen fibers", and RCM showed "evenly distributed hyper-reflective collagen fibers in the dermis and regular honeycomb patterns in the epidermis".[^study] No comparative statistic accompanies either. "No serious adverse effects were observed."[^study]

**Effect size / interpretation:** Unreportable, and the direction should not be read as a finding. The abstract publishes no means, no point differences, no p-values and no confidence intervals, checked by string search. What it reports is a non-significant difference, which is an absence of demonstrated difference rather than evidence that the two procedures are equivalent; with no sample size, it is also impossible to tell whether the study was large enough to detect a difference that exists. The authors' own conclusion, that microsubcision "shows a nonsignificant trend toward superiority", is more careful than most, and a trend that is not significant is still not a result.

**Applicability + key limitation:** Generalises, at most, to adults having a single session of either procedure for atrophic acne scars at one Korean centre, assessed four weeks later. The key limitation for anyone acting on it is the timeframe compounded by the missing denominator: collagen remodelling after a resurfacing procedure continues for months, so one month after one session is early to judge either arm, and without an n there is no way to size the comparison at all. Procedure context is on [[fractional-co2-laser-resurfacing]] and [[microneedling]]; the filler ingredient's own evidence is on [[hyaluronic-acid]], and post-acne redness on [[post-inflammatory-erythema]].
