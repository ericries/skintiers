Profilometric evaluation of photodamage after topical retinaldehyde and retinoic acid treatment (Journal of the American Academy of Dermatology, 1998). Head-to-head RCT showing retinaldehyde produces comparable wrinkle/roughness reduction to retinoic acid with better tolerability — the primary efficacy evidence for retinaldehyde specifically.
