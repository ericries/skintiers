Molecular basis of sun-induced premature skin ageing and retinoid antagonism (Nature, 1996). Defines the core mechanism of photoaging (UV -> AP-1 -> MMP collagenase/gelatinase/stromelysin -> collagen breakdown) and shows retinoids counteract it — the mechanistic backbone underlying every retinoid-for-photoaging claim on the site.
