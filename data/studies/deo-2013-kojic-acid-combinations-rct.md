Kojic Acid vis-a-vis its Combinations with Hydroquinone and Betamethasone Valerate in Melasma: A Randomized, Single Blind, Comparative Study of Efficacy and Safety (Indian Journal of Dermatology, 2013). Four-arm, single-blind RCT (80 patients) factorially testing kojic acid alone against its combinations with hydroquinone and betamethasone valerate, clarifying which components drive efficacy versus side effects. Gives a well-powered, dedicated kojic-acid trial beyond the two older split-face studies, suitable for a kojic acid ingredient page's core evidence section. Relevant to [[hyperpigmentation]], [[hydroquinone]]. PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23918998/ (a detailed lay summary is planned).
