Oral isotretinoin for acne (Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, 2018). Cochrane systematic review (31 RCTs, 3,836 participants) assessing oral isotretinoin efficacy and adverse events across dosing regimens — the modern efficacy counterpart to the site's existing 1982 mechanistic isotretinoin studies, which only address sebum suppression, not clinical outcomes. Relevant to [[acne]], [[isotretinoin-sebum-suppression]]. PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30484286/ (a detailed lay summary is planned).
