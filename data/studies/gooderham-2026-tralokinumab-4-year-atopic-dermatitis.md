A post hoc interim analysis of an open-label extension trial reported that the injectable IL-13 biologic tralokinumab, plus optional topical anti-inflammatories, kept most adults with moderate-to-severe [[atopic-dermatitis]] at clear-to-mild disease over roughly four years. The catch: it measured only the subgroup who had already completed one year of treatment, and there was no comparator arm.[^1]

**Design:** Post hoc interim analysis of the open-label extension trial ECZTEND (NCT03587805), single-arm (tralokinumab plus optional topical corticosteroids/calcineurin inhibitors), with no placebo comparator. Included patients who had completed 52 weeks of tralokinumab in the phase III ECZTRA 1 or ECZTRA 2 trials (NCT03131648/NCT03160885) and then enrolled in ECZTEND ≥152 weeks before the 30 April 2022 data cut-off.[^1]

**Population:** n=347 adults with moderate-to-severe atopic dermatitis, a responder-enriched subgroup, since enrolment required completing one year of tralokinumab in the parent trials first.[^1]

**Intervention vs comparator:** Tralokinumab (anti-IL-13 monoclonal antibody) plus optional TCS/TCI. No comparator; this measures maintenance, not efficacy versus placebo.[^1]

**Primary result:** At ECZTEND Week 152, 52.6% (95% CI 45.2; 59.8) achieved IGA 0/1 (clear/almost clear) and 84.5% (95% CI 78.4; 89.1) achieved EASI-75 versus parent-trial baseline; 84.5% reached EASI ≤7 (no-to-mild), 68.0% (95% CI 60.8; 74.5) reached Worst Weekly Pruritus NRS ≤4 (no-to-mild itch), and 79.0% (95% CI 72.1; 84.6) reached DLQI ≤5. Responses were stable: 71.4% (95% CI 66.3; 76.0) maintained EASI ≤7 on ≥80% of days between Weeks 16 and 152.[^1]

**Effect size:** Not a between-group difference. There is no comparator, so these are single-arm maintenance rates in patients who had already responded. Over ECZTEND, 28.5% discontinued (7.5% for lack of efficacy, 5.8% for adverse events). Most frequent adverse events by incidence rate: nasopharyngitis (13.6), upper respiratory tract infection (4.4), conjunctivitis (2.3).[^1]

**Generalizes to:** Adults with moderate-to-severe atopic dermatitis who have already tolerated and responded to about a year of tralokinumab. Tralokinumab is a prescription injectable biologic, not a topical or over-the-counter skincare product; this study says nothing about topical care.[^1]

**Key limitation:** This is a post hoc interim analysis of an uncontrolled, open-label extension in a responder-enriched population (only patients who completed 52 weeks enrolled), so the high maintenance rates reflect survivors of the parent trials, not efficacy against placebo, and cannot be read as a response rate for a treatment-naive patient. The parent trials and extension were funded by the manufacturer (LEO Pharma), and several authors are employees or shareholders.[^1]
