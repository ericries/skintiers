**What it tested.** Whether Encorelane, described by its authors as "a novel ingredient blend of saccharide isomerate, ribose, and fructooligosaccharides", improves barrier function and visible ageing signs in sensitive skin.[^study]

**Design, population, n.** Two halves. In vitro, a 3D epidermal model (EpiKutis) irritated with sodium lauryl sulfate, plus a UVA/UVB exposure arm for matrix components. In vivo, "A 6-week double-blind, half-face study in 23 sensitive-skin subjects".[^study] The clinical half is split-face and placebo-controlled, which is a sound design at a small size.

**Intervention vs comparator.** Encorelane against placebo on the opposite side of the same face, over six weeks.[^study] The in vitro work compares treated cultures against an untreated control.

**Primary result, with the numbers that exist.** In vivo, "Compared to the placebo, Encorelane significantly improved TEWL, R2, F4, and crow's feet wrinkles (p < 0.05)".[^study] In vitro it "significantly reduced the levels of IL-1α, TNF-α, IL-6, IL-8, and PGE2 (p < 0.05)" and raised AQP3, filaggrin, loricrin, TGM1, several collagens and laminin 5.[^study]

**Effect size: none reported.** The abstract gives direction and p-values and no magnitudes, no confidence intervals, and no baseline-to-endpoint values for any of TEWL, R2, F4 or wrinkle score. A reader cannot tell whether the improvement is cosmetically meaningful or merely statistically detectable in 23 people. This is the single most important limitation for routine analysis and it is why nothing on this site should cite a number from this trial.

**Who it generalizes to.** Adults with self-identified sensitive skin, for six weeks, using one proprietary blend in an unstated vehicle. The in vitro arm generalizes to cell culture and should not be read as a human finding; the SLS-irritation and UV-exposure models are provocations, not everyday skin.

**The one key limitation.** **Every author is employed by the company that owns the ingredient, and the paper declares no conflict.** All seven are listed at the "Research & Innovation Center, Proya Cosmetics Co. Ltd, Hangzhou, China", Encorelane is that company's proprietary blend, and the declaration reads "The authors declare no conflicts of interest".[^study] Sponsor authorship is common and does not make a result wrong. Declaring none while every author works for the owner is a reporting failure, and it is the reason this page treats the findings as a manufacturer's internal evaluation rather than independent evidence.

**How to use this on the site.** As background only. Do not grade any ingredient from it, do not quote a magnitude from it, and prefer independent evidence where it exists. Related hubs are [[sensitive-skin]], [[skin-barrier-repair]] and [[anti-aging]]. None of the three blend components has a page here, and none should get one on the strength of this paper alone.
