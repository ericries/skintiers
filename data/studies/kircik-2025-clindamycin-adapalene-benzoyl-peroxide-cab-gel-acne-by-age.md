A post hoc pooled analysis of three randomized trials found that fixed-dose clindamycin phosphate 1.2% / adapalene 0.15% / benzoyl peroxide 3.1% (CAB) gel produced similar acne clearance in younger (9 to 24 years) and adult (25 years and older) patients, with about half of CAB-treated patients reaching clear or almost clear skin at 12 weeks versus under a quarter on vehicle.[^1]

**Design:** Post hoc pooled analysis of one phase 2 (NCT03170388) and two phase 3 (NCT04214652, NCT04214639) randomized trials of once-daily CAB gel versus vehicle gel in moderate-to-severe acne, with participants grouped by age.[^1]

**Population / n:** Participants aged 9 years and older with moderate-to-severe acne, pooled and split into "younger" (9 to 24 years, n=515) and "adult" (25 years and older, n=142).[^1]

**Intervention / comparator:** Fixed-dose [[clindamycin-topical|clindamycin phosphate]] 1.2% / [[adapalene]] 0.15% / [[benzoyl-peroxide]] 3.1% (CAB) gel once daily, versus vehicle gel.[^1]

**Primary result (verbatim figures):** At week 12, treatment success (a "greater than or equal to 2-grade reduction from baseline in Evaluator's Global Severity Score and clear/almost clear skin") was reached by "9-24: 50.6%; greater than or equal to 25: 49.0%" of CAB-treated participants versus "15.7%; 20.6%" on vehicle ("P<0.01, both"). CAB "yielded >70% reductions in inflammatory/noninflammatory lesions vs 45% to 62% with vehicle (P<=0.001, all)." The analysis states "CAB efficacy was similar across age groups."[^1]

**Effect size / interpretation:** Roughly half of CAB-treated patients in each age band achieved clear or almost clear skin, about 2.4 to 3.2 times the vehicle rate, with lesion-count reductions above 70 percent; the effect did not differ meaningfully between adolescents/young adults and older adults, supporting CAB gel for moderate-to-severe [[acne]] across ages.[^1]

**Applicability + key limitation:** Applies to moderate-to-severe acne patients aged 9 and older. This is a post hoc age-subgroup pooling rather than a pre-specified age-stratified trial, the comparator is vehicle (not an active acne regimen), follow-up is 12 weeks, and only the PubMed abstract was accessed, so per-trial breakdowns, the adult subgroup's smaller size (n=142), and adverse-event details beyond "mild-to-moderate severity" with "no age-related trends" could not be independently verified.[^1]
