Application of moisturizer to neonates prevents development of atopic dermatitis (Journal of Allergy and Clinical Immunology, 2014). Published back-to-back with Simpson 2014 in the same JACI issue, this independent RCT in a different population (Japan vs. US/UK) corroborates the same primary-prevention finding, giving two independent primary sources for the 'moisturizing from birth prevents AD' claim (satisfying the site's triple-source-independence discipline). Not currently cited anywhere in the corpus; would pair with Simpson 2014 on atopic-dermatitis.md and the barrier goal pages.
