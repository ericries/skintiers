Isotretinoin treatment for acne and risk of depression: A systematic review and meta-analysis (Journal of the American Academy of Dermatology, 2017). Landmark meta-analysis (31 studies) on the long-debated isotretinoin/depression link, widely cited in dermatology guidelines. The site currently only has the 1982 mechanistic sebum-suppression studies for isotretinoin and no modern safety synthesis. Relevant to [[acne]], [[isotretinoin-sebum-suppression]].
