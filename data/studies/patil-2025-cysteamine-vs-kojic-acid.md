Comparison of the Effectiveness and Safety of 5% Cysteamine and 2% Kojic Acid Creams in the Treatment of Melasma in Indian Adult Females (Cureus, 2025). The most recent (2025) direct randomized head-to-head between the two ingredients named in the task brief (72 women, 16 weeks): both improved mMASI and melanin content with no statistically significant difference between them. Uniquely bridges cysteamine and kojic acid coverage and would be the natural cross-reference from either ingredient's page or from hyperpigmentation.md's comparative-efficacy section. Relevant to [[hyperpigmentation]], [[hydroquinone]]. PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40296942/ (a detailed lay summary is planned).
