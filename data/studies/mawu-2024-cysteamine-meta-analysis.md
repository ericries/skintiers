Efficacy and safety of cysteamine 5% cream for the management of melasma: a systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials (Archives of Dermatological Research, 2024). Recent pooled meta-analysis of 7 RCTs concluding cysteamine 5% cream has efficacy comparable to hydroquinone 4% and superior to placebo, with a similar adverse-effect profile (erythema, irritation). Provides the aggregate-evidence anchor for a future cysteamine ingredient page in the same style as the Liu 2021 network meta-analysis anchors hyperpigmentation.md for the other agents. Relevant to [[hyperpigmentation]], [[hydroquinone]]. PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39673630/ (a detailed lay summary is planned).
