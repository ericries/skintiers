The combination of glycolic acid and hydroquinone or kojic acid for the treatment of melasma and related conditions (Dermatologic Surgery, 1996). The original comparative split-face trial (39 patients) establishing kojic acid as a tyrosinase-inhibiting alternative to hydroquinone within a glycolic-acid vehicle; kojic acid is named in the task brief and in the site's own Davis & Callender 2010 citation as a tyrosinase inhibitor, but has no dedicated ingredient page or primary-trial citation yet. Relevant to [[hyperpigmentation]], [[hydroquinone]], [[glycolic-acid]]. PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8634807/ (a detailed lay summary is planned).
