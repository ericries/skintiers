High sun protection factor sunscreens in the suppression of actinic neoplasia (Archives of Dermatology, 1995). A controlled trial in 37 high-risk patients (existing actinic keratoses or nonmelanoma skin cancer history) in Lubbock, Texas, finding regular high-SPF sunscreen use significantly reduced new precancerous lesion formation. A third independent population (distinct from both Nambour and the Thompson/Marks Australian trial) for the sunscreen-uv-filters.md photoprotection-evidence discussion, and specifically relevant to a high-risk/secondary-prevention population not otherwise represented.
