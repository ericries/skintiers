The effect of sunscreen on vitamin D: a review (British Journal of Dermatology, 2019). Systematic review of 4 experimental studies, 3 field trials, and 69 observational studies concluding there is little evidence that real-world sunscreen use meaningfully lowers vitamin D status, while noting no trial has tested today's high-SPF products. This directly informs (and could correct/nuance) the 'Common Marketing Claims' quarantine section of sunscreen-uv-filters.md, which does not currently address the vitamin-D objection to sunscreen use at all — a very common consumer question the page is silent on. PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30945275/ (a detailed lay summary is planned).
