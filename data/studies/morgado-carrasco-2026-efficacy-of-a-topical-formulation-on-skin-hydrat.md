A 28-day randomized, split-leg, untreated-controlled trial in adults with xerosis found that a topical formulation of panthenol, niacinamide, and dipotassium glycyrrhizate significantly increased corneometric skin hydration and reduced transepidermal water loss (TEWL) versus the untreated contralateral leg.[^1]

**Design:** Randomized controlled trial with an intra-individual, split-leg design (treated pretibial area vs. untreated contralateral leg as control), 28 days.[^1]

**Population / n:** n=35 participants (31 women; mean age 47.3 ± 2.0 years) with xerosis.[^1]

**Intervention / comparator:** A topical formulation containing [[panthenol]], [[niacinamide]], and dipotassium glycyrrhizate, applied twice daily for 28 days to one pretibial area, versus the untreated contralateral leg (no vehicle-only control arm).[^1]

**Primary result (verbatim figures):** Corneometric hydration: baseline 21.3 ± 1.0 c.u.; day 7, 33.9 ± 1.4 c.u., an increase of 66.2% (p < 0.001); day 28, 35.2 ± 1.2 c.u., an increase of 74.1% (p < 0.001). TEWL: baseline 9.2 ± 0.3 g/h/m²; day 7, 8.1 ± 0.2 g/h/m², a decrease of 12.0% (p < 0.001); day 28, 7.9 ± 0.2 g/h/m², a decrease of 14.1% (p < 0.001).[^1]

**Effect size / interpretation:** Hydration rose by roughly two-thirds to three-quarters over baseline by day 7 through day 28, and TEWL fell by roughly 12% to 14%, both effects present at the earliest (day 7) timepoint and sustained/strengthened through day 28, with all changes statistically significant (p < 0.001 for hydration, p < 0.001 for TEWL).[^1]

**Applicability + key limitation:** Applies to adults with xerosis (mostly women, mean age ~47) using this three-ingredient formulation on pretibial skin over 4 weeks; the authors state that "the absence of a vehicle control precludes attribution of effects specifically to the active ingredients," and that "patient-reported outcomes should be interpreted with caution due to the unblinded design." Only the PubMed abstract was accessed, not the full text, so no additional figures beyond those above can be verified.[^1]
