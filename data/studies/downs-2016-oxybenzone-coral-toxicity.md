Toxicopathological Effects of the Sunscreen UV Filter, Oxybenzone (Benzophenone-3), on Coral Planulae and Cultured Primary Cells and Its Environmental Contamination in Hawaii and the U.S. Virgin Islands (Archives of Environmental Contamination and Toxicology, 2016). The primary study behind Hawaii's and other jurisdictions' oxybenzone sunscreen bans, showing coral bleaching, deformity, and genotoxicity at environmentally realistic oxybenzone concentrations. sunscreen-uv-filters.md currently dismisses 'reef-safe' as 'an unregulated environmental term... not an efficacy claim' without citing any primary environmental-toxicity evidence; this is the actual study underlying that consumer claim and belongs either there or on a future oxybenzone ingredient page (oxybenzone has no ingredient page yet despite being a common US organic filter). PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26487337/ (a detailed lay summary is planned).
