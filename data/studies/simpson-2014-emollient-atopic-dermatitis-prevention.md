Emollient enhancement of the skin barrier from birth offers effective atopic dermatitis prevention (Journal of Allergy and Clinical Immunology, 2014). A landmark primary-prevention RCT not currently cited anywhere in the corpus. It would materially strengthen data/conditions/atopic-dermatitis.md and the barrier goal pages by adding a genuine prevention angle (moisturizing from birth to lower AD incidence) that is currently absent — the existing evidence base is all about treating/moisturizing established dry or eczematous skin, not primary prevention. Relevant to [[atopic-dermatitis]], [[moisturizing]], [[skin-barrier-strengthening]]. PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25282563/ (a detailed lay summary is planned).
