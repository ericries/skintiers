Reduction of solar keratoses by regular sunscreen use (New England Journal of Medicine, 1993). A separate Australian RCT (588 adults, one summer, SPF 17 vs base cream) showing daily sunscreen use reduced solar keratosis counts and increased lesion remission with a dose-response relationship. This is the first true independent replication of the sunscreen/actinic-keratosis-prevention claim on sunscreen-uv-filters.md — a different trial, different population, and an earlier date than the Nambour (Hughes/Green) cohort already cited there, so it directly strengthens (and adds nuance to) the page's honest caveat that its RCT evidence otherwise rests on a single cohort. PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8377777/ (a detailed lay summary is planned).
