The safety of nanosized particles in titanium dioxide- and zinc oxide-based sunscreens (Journal of the American Academy of Dermatology, 2009). A widely cited dermatology review concluding no evidence of nanoparticle penetration beyond the stratum corneum for either titanium dioxide or zinc oxide. There is no titanium-dioxide.md ingredient page yet despite titanium dioxide being one of only two mineral filters (alongside zinc oxide, which does have a page); this study would seed that future page and cross-reference zinc-oxide.md's existing Gulson/Mohammed penetration studies with a titanium-dioxide-inclusive companion source. PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19646780/ (a detailed lay summary is planned).
