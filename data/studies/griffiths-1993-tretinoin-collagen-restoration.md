Restoration of collagen formation in photodamaged human skin by tretinoin (retinoic acid) (New England Journal of Medicine, 1993). First mechanistic RCT showing tretinoin increases procollagen I synthesis (~80% increase vs ~14% decrease with vehicle) in photodamaged skin, giving a biological explanation for clinical wrinkle improvement. Already cited inline in goals/anti-aging.md; deserves its own studies page. Relevant to [[anti-aging]], [[tretinoin]], [[retinoids]].
