Effectiveness of spironolactone for women with acne vulgaris (SAFA) in England and Wales: pragmatic, multicentre, phase 3, double blind, randomised controlled trial (BMJ, 2023). The largest RCT to date of oral spironolactone for persistent acne in adult women (410 participants); found significantly higher treatment success vs placebo, more pronounced at 24 weeks than 12 weeks, with a good safety profile. Directly fills the 'hormonal therapy' gap that acne.md currently only names as out-of-scope without citing evidence. Relevant to [[acne]]. PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37192767/ (a detailed lay summary is planned).
