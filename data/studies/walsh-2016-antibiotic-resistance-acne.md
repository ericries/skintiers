Systematic review of antibiotic resistance in acne: an increasing topical and oral threat (Lancet Infectious Diseases, 2016). Widely-cited systematic review documenting rising Cutibacterium (Propionibacterium) acnes resistance to topical and oral antibiotics; directly extends the antibiotic-resistance point already made (but not deeply sourced) in benzoyl-peroxide.md, and supports why BPO/antibiotic combinations are recommended.
