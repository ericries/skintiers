The U.K. Working Party's Diagnostic Criteria for Atopic Dermatitis. I. Derivation of a minimum set of discriminators for atopic dermatitis (British Journal of Dermatology, 1994). The primary derivation study (224 patients, blinded dual observers, multiple logistic regression) behind the diagnostic criteria used verbatim in the 'How to know you have this' section of data/conditions/atopic-dermatitis.md (footnote). It has no dedicated study page yet despite being the evidentiary backbone for how the condition page teaches recognition of AD. Relevant to [[atopic-dermatitis]]. PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7918015/ (a detailed lay summary is planned).
