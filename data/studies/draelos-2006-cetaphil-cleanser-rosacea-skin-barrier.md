A single-arm before/after study in 30 patients with mild-to-moderate rosacea found that two weeks of twice-daily washing with Cetaphil Gentle Skin Cleanser produced no significant rise in transepidermal water loss and maintained skin hydration, with a post hoc analysis also showing reduced investigator-rated rosacea severity.[^1]

**Design:** Single-arm, sequential (non-randomized, non-blinded per the abstract) clinical trial: a 2-week washout period cleansing with Dove Sensitive Skin Bar, followed by a 2-week study period cleansing twice daily with [[cetaphil-gentle-skin-cleanser|Cetaphil Gentle Skin Cleanser]], with no rosacea therapy during either period. Skin barrier function (TEWL, corneometry) and rosacea severity were monitored throughout.[^1]

**Population / n:** 30 patients with mild to moderate [[rosacea]].[^1]

**Intervention / comparator:** Intervention was twice-daily use of Cetaphil Gentle Skin Cleanser (a nonalkaline, nonirritating cleanser) for 2 weeks. The comparator was each patient's own washout-period baseline, established during 2 weeks of twice-daily cleansing with Dove Sensitive Skin Bar, not a concurrent control arm.[^1]

**Primary result (verbatim figures):** "No significant increase in TEWL was demonstrated at any point during the study, indicating that the gentle skin cleanser did not damage the skin barrier. Additionally, the cleanser was shown to maintain skin hydration." A post hoc analysis found "a significant reduction (P<.05) in investigator-assessed rosacea severity on the cheeks, forehead, and nose at the end of week 1 and on the cheeks, forehead, and chin at the end of week 2 compared with the end of the washout period." "There were no increases in erythema, scaling, dryness, stinging, burning, or lack of smoothness in the skin during the 2-week study period."[^1] The abstract gives no numeric TEWL or corneometry values, and no exact severity scores: only the significance threshold (P<.05) for the severity reduction.

**Effect size / interpretation:** No effect size (e.g., mean TEWL change, percentage, or score) is stated for the barrier-tolerability finding; it is reported only as "no significant increase." The rosacea-severity reduction is reported only as significant at P<.05, from a post hoc (not pre-specified) analysis, with no magnitude given.[^1]

**Applicability + key limitation:** Applies narrowly to this exact cleanser used twice daily for 2 weeks in mild-to-moderate rosacea patients off other rosacea therapy; it supports cleanser tolerability, not a treatment claim. Key limitations: the design is a single-arm sequential comparison against the patients' own washout baseline, not a randomized or blinded controlled trial; the headline severity finding was a post hoc analysis rather than a pre-specified primary endpoint; and only the PubMed abstract was accessed, not the Cutis full text, so no additional numeric detail (exact TEWL/corneometry values, precise sample flow) could be verified.
