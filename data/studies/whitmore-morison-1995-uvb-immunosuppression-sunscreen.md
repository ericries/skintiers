Prevention of UVB-induced immunosuppression in humans by a high sun protection factor sunscreen (Archives of Dermatology, 1995). Demonstrates a mechanistic endpoint (UVB-induced suppression of contact hypersensitivity to DNCB) not covered anywhere in the current sunscreen pages, which otherwise document only aging/cancer clinical endpoints. Adds evidence for a plausible mechanism connecting sunscreen use to reduced skin-cancer risk (UV-induced local immune suppression is thought to contribute to carcinogenesis) and would support a future 'How It Works' expansion on sunscreen-uv-filters.md. PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7574828/ (a detailed lay summary is planned).
