A systematic review and meta-analysis of 5 RCTs (332 participants) found that Lactobacillus-based probiotic/postbiotic therapy did not significantly reduce inflammatory, non-inflammatory, or total acne lesion counts versus comparators.[^1]

**Design:** Systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials evaluating oral or topical Lactobacillus-based probiotic or postbiotic interventions in patients with acne vulgaris; the abstract's title labels it a "GRADE" review, but the abstract text itself states no explicit GRADE certainty rating.[^1]

**Population / n:** 5 RCTs, 332 participants total, with acne vulgaris.[^1]

**Intervention / comparator:** Oral or topical Lactobacillus-based probiotic/postbiotic therapy versus placebo or benzoyl peroxide (comparators pooled across the 5 trials).[^1]

**Primary result (verbatim figures):** Non-inflammatory lesions: mean difference "-1.39 (95% CI -5.10 to 2.32, p = 0.46)." Inflammatory lesions: mean difference "-0.08 (95% CI -1.28 to 1.11, p = 0.89)." Total lesion counts: mean difference "-9.07 (95% CI -20.71 to 2.57, p = 0.13)." Heterogeneity across studies was reported as "moderate to low." The conclusion states Lactobacillus-based probiotics "do not provide significant clinical benefits in reducing inflammatory lesions, non-inflammatory lesions, and total acne lesion counts" versus controls.[^1]

**Effect size / interpretation:** All three pooled mean differences favor the Lactobacillus arm numerically (fewer lesions) but every 95% CI crosses zero and every p-value is well above 0.05 (p = 0.46, 0.89, 0.13), so none reaches statistical significance; the review concludes no significant clinical benefit for [[probiotics]]-based Lactobacillus therapy in [[acne]].[^1]

**Applicability + key limitation:** Applies to adults/patients with acne vulgaris treated with oral or topical Lactobacillus probiotic/postbiotic products across 5 pooled RCTs; only the PubMed abstract was accessed (not the full text), so per-trial sample sizes, specific strains/doses, adverse-event data, and any formal GRADE certainty grading could not be verified; the abstract itself reports none of these beyond the pooled effect estimates above.[^1]
