Prospective, randomized, double-blind assessment of topical bakuchiol and retinol for facial photoageing (British Journal of Dermatology, 2019). The pivotal RCT behind the 'bakuchiol is a retinol alternative' claim — both reduced wrinkles/pigmentation equally, but bakuchiol was better tolerated. Directly load-bearing for any bakuchiol-vs-retinoid comparison. Already cited inline in ingredients/bakuchiol.md and goals/anti-aging.md. Relevant to [[bakuchiol]], [[retinol]], [[anti-aging]].
