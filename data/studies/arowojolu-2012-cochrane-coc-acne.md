Combined oral contraceptive pills for treatment of acne (Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, 2012). Cochrane review (31 trials, 12,579 participants) establishing COCs reduce acne lesion counts and severity vs placebo; the standard evidence base for combined oral contraceptives in acne, pairing with the SAFA spironolactone trial to cover both major hormonal options acne.md names but doesn't yet cite. Relevant to [[acne]]. PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22786490/ (a detailed lay summary is planned).
