Evaluation of the efficacy of cysteamine 5% cream in the treatment of epidermal melasma: a randomized double-blind placebo-controlled trial (British Journal of Dermatology, 2015). The foundational RCT establishing cysteamine 5% cream as an efficacious depigmenting agent (50 patients, 4 months, Mexameter and MASI outcomes; 92% showed moderate-to-significant improvement). Cysteamine is named explicitly in the task brief but currently has zero coverage anywhere on the site (no ingredient page, not cited in hyperpigmentation.md or elsewhere) despite being a mainstream non-hydroquinone melasma option alongside azelaic acid and tranexamic acid. Relevant to [[hyperpigmentation]], [[hydroquinone]]. PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25251767/ (a detailed lay summary is planned).
