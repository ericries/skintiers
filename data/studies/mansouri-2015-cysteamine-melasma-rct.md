A double-blind randomized placebo-controlled trial found cysteamine 5% cream produced significantly greater reduction in melasma pigmentation than placebo over 4 months.[^1]

**Design:** Double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled trial; cysteamine 5% cream vs. placebo cream, applied once daily at bedtime for 4 months, with assessments at baseline, 2 months, and 4 months.[^1]

**Population / n:** 50 patients with epidermal melasma, randomized to cysteamine (n=25) or placebo (n=25).[^1]

**Intervention / comparator:** Cysteamine 5% cream once nightly vs. placebo cream once nightly, both for 4 months.[^1]

**Primary result (verbatim figures):** "After 2 and 4 months of application of cysteamine and placebo cream, the mean differences were 39.7 ± 16.6 and 26.2 ± 16 in the cysteamine group, and 63.8 ± 28.6 and 60.7 ± 27.3 in the placebo group, respectively. Statistically significant differences were found between the group outcomes at both points (P = 0.001 and P < 0.001). At the end of the treatment, the MASI scores were significantly lower in the cysteamine group vs. placebo (7.2 ± 5.5 vs. 11.6 ± 7.9, P = 0.02)." The abstract adds: "The IGA and patients' viewpoints indicated significant efficacy of cysteamine cream vs. placebo."[^1]

**Effect size / interpretation:** Cysteamine roughly halved the Mexameter pigmentation-difference score by 4 months (26.2 vs. 60.7 in placebo) and produced a lower MASI score (7.2 vs. 11.6), both statistically significant; the abstract does not report a percent-responder figure (e.g. no "% of patients improved" breakdown by IGA grade) or an adverse-event/safety summary.[^1]

**Applicability + key limitation:** Applies to patients with epidermal melasma treated for 4 months with a specific 5% cysteamine formulation; only the PubMed abstract was accessed, not the full text, so exact safety/tolerability data, statistical methods, and any subgroup detail could not be verified. Relevant to [[hyperpigmentation]] and positions cysteamine as a non-hydroquinone alternative alongside [[hydroquinone]].
