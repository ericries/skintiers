A multicenter, randomized, self-controlled trial in xerosis found that 4 weeks of daily use of a [[ceramides|ceramide]] and natural-oil body lotion improved hydration, TEWL, and scaling versus the untreated contralateral leg, alongside lipidomic and microbiome shifts.[^1]

**Design:** Multicenter, randomized, self-controlled trial: the lotion was applied daily to one leg for 4 weeks, with the other leg of the same subject serving as an untreated control. Skin radiance, scaliness, smoothness, stratum corneum hydration, transepidermal water loss (TEWL), and pH were measured at intervals, alongside lipidomics and microbiomics analyses of lipid metabolism and microbial structure.[^1]

**Population / n:** n = 68 (self-controlled, paired-leg design); the abstract does not describe the population beyond this (no age, sex, or baseline-severity breakdown stated).[^1]

**Intervention / comparator:** Ceramide- and natural oil-based body lotion applied daily to one leg for 4 weeks, versus the same subject's contralateral leg as an untreated control.[^1]

**Primary result (verbatim figures):** "The body lotion enhanced skin hydration, radiance, and smoothness, while decreasing TEWL and scaling. Lipidomics showed higher levels of essential lipids in the treatment group. Microbiome analysis revealed increased diversity, with more Firmicutes and Cutibacterium and less Proteobacteria, indicating improved skin barrier and microbial balance." A figure caption accompanying the article states results are "expressed as mean ± SD (n = 68), #p < 0.05 compared between the treatment and control groups," but the abstract gives no specific percentages, fold-changes, or per-outcome p-values.[^1]

**Effect size / interpretation:** The abstract reports directional, qualitative improvements (hydration up, TEWL and scaling down, essential-lipid levels up, microbiome diversity shifted toward more Firmicutes/Cutibacterium and less Proteobacteria) with only a general p < 0.05 threshold noted in a figure caption; no quantitative effect size (percent change, fold-change) is stated in the text.[^1]

**Applicability + key limitation:** Applies to xerosis (dry, scaling skin) managed with a ceramide/natural-oil body lotion over 4 weeks in a paired-leg design; only the PubMed abstract (and its associated figure caption) was accessed, not the full text, so exact magnitudes of the hydration/TEWL/lipidomic/microbiome changes and any per-outcome statistics could not be verified.[^1]
