A phase 3 randomized trial found that once-daily oral icotrokinra, an interleukin-23 receptor antagonist peptide, produced high and durable clearance of plaque psoriasis at hard-to-treat "high-impact" sites (scalp, genitals, hands and feet) through 1 year, supporting it as an oral option for [[psoriasis]].[^1]

**Design:** Phase 3 randomized controlled trial (ICONIC-TOTAL), randomized 2:1, placebo-controlled and double-blind through week 16, after which placebo participants transitioned to icotrokinra; 1-year (week 52) durability reported using nonresponder imputation, across 90 sites in 11 countries. Published in J Eur Acad Dermatol Venereol.[^1]

**Population:** 311 participants aged 12 or older with plaque psoriasis (body surface area at least 1%, Investigator's Global Assessment at least 2) and at least moderate scalp, genital, or hand/foot involvement: n=208 icotrokinra, n=103 placebo (92 of whom transitioned to icotrokinra at week 16).[^1]

**Intervention vs comparator:** Once-daily oral icotrokinra 200 mg versus placebo, with the placebo comparison running only through week 16.[^1]

**Primary result:** Among icotrokinra-randomized participants, response rose through week 24 and was durable through week 52 for overall clear or almost clear skin (IGA 0/1: 67% to 70%) and across high-impact sites (scalp 72% to 78%; genital 85% to 90%; hand/foot 54% to 62%). Complete clearance (a score of 0) during weeks 24 to 52 reached IGA 0 in 44% to 51%, scalp in 57% to 66%, genital in 73% to 84%, and hand/foot in 44% to 58%. Mean nail improvement (mNAPSI) rose from 33% at week 16 to 62% at week 52, and 88% (275/311) completed treatment through week 52.[^1]

**Effect size:** High absolute clearance rates sustained to 1 year across every high-impact site, strongest at genital sites; adverse-event rates were similar to placebo through week 16 with no new safety signal through week 52.[^1]

**Generalizes to:** Adolescents and adults (12 and older) with high-impact-site plaque psoriasis who want an oral rather than injectable therapy.

**Key limitation:** The placebo comparison ran only through week 16 before placebo participants crossed over, so the 1-year figures are single-arm durability with no concurrent control and no active comparator; nonresponder imputation was used; and the trial was funded by Johnson & Johnson, the drug's developer, with many authors being employees or company-funded investigators. Icotrokinra is a systemic oral drug, not a topical; the condition itself is covered on [[psoriasis]].
