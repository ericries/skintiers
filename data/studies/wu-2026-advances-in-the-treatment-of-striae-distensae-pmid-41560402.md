A PRISMA-guided systematic literature review found several energy-device and combination therapies to be efficacious for [[striae-distensae]], with fractional microneedling + PRP, fractional CO2 laser + PRP, and 1565 nm non-ablative fractional laser (NAFL) singled out as notable advances.[^1]

**Design:** Systematic literature review (not a primary trial) searching PubMed, Web of Science, and Scopus, following PRISMA guidelines, "prioritiz[ing] randomized controlled trials, meta-analyses, and substantial clinical studies published between 2010 and 2025."[^1]

**Population / n:** Not a patient population. The unit of analysis is the published literature on striae distensae treatment from 2010-2025. The abstract does not state the number of studies included in the review.[^1]

**Intervention / comparator:** Narrative synthesis across multiple treatment modalities (alpha hydroxy acids, injectable fillers, 1540 nm Er:Glass laser, 532 nm Nd:YAG laser, picosecond lasers, intense pulsed light, fractional microneedling, fractional CO2 laser, platelet-rich plasma (PRP), and 1565 nm NAFL, including combinations); the abstract does not describe a single head-to-head intervention-vs-comparator design.[^1]

**Primary result (verbatim figures):** "Striae distensae (SD) is a common cutaneous condition with an estimated incidence of 56%." "Efficacious treatments include alpha hydroxy acids, injectable fillers, 1540 nm Er:Glass laser, 532 nm Nd:YAG laser, picosecond lasers, intense pulsed light, and combination therapies." The 1565 nm NAFL shows "favorable efficacy, safety, and tolerance, with enhanced outcomes in combinations." "Fractional microneedling plus PRP and fractional CO2 laser resurfacing with PRP achieve good results in SD rubra and alba." The abstract states no efficacy percentages, response rates, or p-values for any of these treatments.[^1]

**Effect size / interpretation:** None stated. This is a qualitative/narrative synthesis of which modalities the reviewed literature judged efficacious, not a pooled or quantitative meta-analysis; no effect sizes, response rates, or statistical comparisons are given in the abstract.[^1]

**Applicability + key limitation:** Broadly applicable as an overview of the current striae distensae treatment landscape (SD rubra vs. alba, combination strategies), but it is a narrative systematic review, not primary trial data. It reports which treatments the underlying studies found efficacious without giving pooled effect sizes, study count, or per-treatment numbers, and only the PubMed abstract was accessed here, not the full text, so the underlying primary studies it cites have not been individually verified.[^1]
