A comparative study of topical 5% cysteamine versus 4% hydroquinone in the treatment of facial melasma in women (International Journal of Dermatology, 2020). Quasi-randomized, evaluator-blinded, multicenter head-to-head trial (40 women, 120 days) showing hydroquinone 4% outperformed cysteamine at both 60 and 120 days (mMASI reduction 53% vs 38% at 120 days). Gives the site an honest, sourced comparator for cysteamine's real-world efficacy relative to the existing hydroquinone.md benchmark, avoiding overstating cysteamine as an equal alternative. Relevant to [[hyperpigmentation]], [[hydroquinone]]. PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32864760/ (a detailed lay summary is planned).
