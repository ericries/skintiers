Common loss-of-function variants of the epidermal barrier protein filaggrin are a major predisposing factor for atopic dermatitis (Nature Genetics, 2006). The landmark genetic study establishing filaggrin loss-of-function variants (carried by ~9% of Europeans) as a major predisposing factor for atopic dermatitis, cited as 'establishes a key role for impaired skin barrier function in the development of atopic disease.' Already quoted inline (footnote) on data/conditions/atopic-dermatitis.md but has no dedicated study page of its own; it is arguably the single most-cited foundational paper in the skin-barrier/AD literature and would let atopic-dermatitis.md, ceramides.md, skin-barrier-repair.md, and skin-barrier-strengthening.md all link to one canonical page instead of repeating the citation.
