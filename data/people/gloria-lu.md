Gloria Lu is a cosmetic chemist and co-founder, with [[victoria-fu|Victoria Fu]], of Chemist Confessions, a skincare-education Instagram account, podcast, and product line.[^bi]

## Background

Lu studied chemical engineering; Beauty Independent describes both Chemist Confessions founders as former L'Oréal formulators, and the two launched the Chemist Confessions Instagram account in August 2017 to explain formulation science and push back on skincare marketing claims.[^bi] Her relevance here is that role: a formulation-trained chemist who evaluates ingredient and product claims against the science, the same evidence-first lens this site uses. See her co-founder [[victoria-fu]] and the fellow cosmetic chemists [[perry-romanowski]] and [[michelle-wong]].
