Ranella Hirsch, MD, FAAD, is a board-certified dermatologist practicing in Cambridge, Massachusetts, known for cosmetic and laser dermatology and for combating skincare misinformation on social media.[^npi][^doximity][^scd]

## Credentials and practice

The federal NPI registry lists Hirsch (NPI 1528113883) as an active, sole-proprietor dermatologist with a primary taxonomy of Dermatology, Massachusetts license 216043, and a practice address at 777 Concord Ave, Suite 206, Cambridge, MA.[^npi] Doximity independently corroborates that address and specialty, and adds that she earned her medical degree from the State University of New York Downstate Medical Center College of Medicine (class of 1998), completed an internal medicine internship at Yale-New Haven Medical Center and a dermatology residency at SUNY Downstate Health Sciences University, is board certified in dermatology by the American Board of Dermatology, and holds active Massachusetts and New York medical licenses.[^doximity] Her practice's site adds that her medical degree was summa cum laude with Distinction in Research, and that she completed advanced laser and cosmetic dermatology training at Massachusetts General Hospital, Harvard Medical School.[^scd]

She is a Fellow of the American Academy of Dermatology, the American Society for Dermatologic Surgery, the American Society for Laser Medicine and Surgery, and the International Society for Cosmetic Dermatology, and serves on the editorial boards of Dermatology Times, Cosmetic Dermatology, The Dermatologist, and Cutis, per her practice's site.[^scd] The same source states she is a past president of the American Society of Cosmetic Dermatology and Aesthetic Surgery, describing her as the youngest person to hold elected leadership of a major dermatology society, and that she completed a three-year term chairing that society's Scope of Practice Committee and received its President's Award for contributions to patient safety.[^scd]

## Public skincare education

Hirsch has been described as "Expert Derm" by Allure, a characterization corroborated independently of her own practice site by an industry profile of the skincare brand she co-founded.[^scd][^beautymatter] A roundup of dermatologists to follow on Instagram reports that Hirsch, posting as @ranellamd, "devotes a sizeable number of Instagram posts to beating down misinformation as well as to explaining the ins and outs of basic skincare to her followers," citing as an example a post explaining that a skincare product is often judged to have failed before it has been given enough time to work.[^glam] The same piece quotes her voicing concern about how skincare misinformation spreads on platforms like TikTok: "If you see one bad video that tells you sunscreen is terrible, you're going to be shown 20 more videos like that."[^glam]

## Atolla

Hirsch is a co-founder of Atolla, an MIT-born skincare brand launched in August 2019 that used data science and machine learning to formulate customized serums delivered by subscription.[^beautymatter] An industry profile of the company describes her as a board member who guided ingredient selection and dermatologic best practices alongside co-founders Meg Maupin and Sid Salvi.[^beautymatter] Function of Beauty announced its acquisition of Atolla in August 2021, naming Hirsch a co-founder and "a leading dermatologist" alongside CEO Meghan Maupin.[^prnewswire] This is a commercial affiliation to weigh when reading her product commentary and endorsements.
