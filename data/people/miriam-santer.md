Miriam Santer is professor of primary care research at the University of Southampton and the corresponding, first-listed author of the [[santer-2023-safa-spironolactone-acne|SAFA trial]], a randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial of oral spironolactone for women with facial acne.[^pmc]

## Background

Her affiliation, per the published trial, is the Primary Care Research Centre in the University of Southampton's Faculty of Medicine.[^pmc] She is a primary care researcher, not a dermatologist; her relevance here is as the trial's lead investigator. The study's findings and limitations are covered on its own page, [[santer-2023-safa-spironolactone-acne]].
