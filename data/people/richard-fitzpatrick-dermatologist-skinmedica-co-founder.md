Richard E. Fitzpatrick is a San Diego dermatologist identified by [[skinmedica|SkinMedica]]'s own site as the brand's co-founder; a Carlsbad Chamber of Commerce account of the company's 1999 founding independently describes him the same way, as the "well-known San Diego dermatologist" whose small Carlsbad cosmeceutical firm the brand grew out of.[^1][^2] No board-certification record was verified in this review.

SkinMedica's product line built on his company includes three products already reviewed on this site: [[skinmedica-tns-advanced-serum|TNS Advanced+ Serum]], the brand's flagship growth-factor serum; [[skinmedica-retinol-complex-1-0|Retinol Complex 1.0]]; and [[skinmedica-ha5-rejuvenating-hydrator|HA5 Rejuvenating Hydrator]].
