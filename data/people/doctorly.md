**Disclosure that matters when reading their device videos:** their comparison of laser, LED and microcurrent devices is sponsored. Its transcript thanks the maker Tria three times and adds a discount link, so it is not relayed here.[^tria] Shah also has a skincare line: [[remedy-science]], whose own store titles itself "Remedy Science by Dr. Muneeb Shah", so his picks in that range are own-brand rather than independent.
