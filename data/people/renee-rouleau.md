Renée Rouleau is a licensed esthetician and founder of the eponymous Renée Rouleau Skin Care line.[^1] Interviewed by Forbes, she says she "went to school for cosmetology and hair was not for me, and so I became an aesthetician," and describes starting her company in Dallas around 1996 on a nine-skin-type classification system she developed, rather than the traditional three or four categories.[^1] No independent state licensing-board record for the credential was located; this profile relies on that interview.
