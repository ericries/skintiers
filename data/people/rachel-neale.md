Rachel E. Neale is a researcher in the Population Health Department at QIMR Berghofer Medical Research Institute and the first and corresponding author of a 2019 systematic review on sunscreen and vitamin D, [[neale-2019-sunscreen-vitamin-d|covered on its own page]].[^pubmed]

## Background

Her affiliation, per PubMed's record for the review, is the Population Health Department at QIMR Berghofer Medical Research Institute in Herston, Queensland, Australia.[^pubmed] She is a population-health researcher, not a dermatologist; her relevance here is as the review's lead author. Its finding, that routine sunscreen use does not meaningfully lower blood vitamin D, is cited on [[vitamin-d-topical]].
