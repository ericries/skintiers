Dr. Chris Tomassian is a board-certified dermatologist and the founder of The Dermatology Collective in Glendora, California. He earned his medical degree from the University of Missouri-Kansas City and completed his dermatology residency at the University of Kansas, and describes his clinical focus as cosmetic and medical dermatology, with a specialty in lasers and acne scarring.[^bio] On TikTok (@dr.tomassian) he posts short, science-based skincare explainers, and his videos are cited across this site on pigmentation and acne actives including [[hydroquinone]], [[azelaic-acid]], [[tretinoin]], [[adapalene]], [[retinol]], [[benzoyl-peroxide]], and [[ketoconazole]], plus [[melasma]] and the combination product [[epiduo-adapalene-benzoyl-peroxide-gel]].

One caveat when weighing his specific product picks: his public bio lists a business booking contact that suggests managed brand partnerships, so treat named product recommendations as potentially commercial. His mechanism and how-to explainers stand on their own.
