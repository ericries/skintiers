Samantha Rose Ellis is a dermatologist licensed in California and the founder of the skincare brand [[prequel|Prequel]].[^1][^2]

## Credentials and affiliations

The federal National Plan and Provider Enumeration System (the NPI registry), maintained by the US Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services, lists Samantha Rose Ellis as a physician holding a Doctor of Medicine degree, with a primary specialty taxonomy of Dermatology and California medical license A144841.[^1]

Dermatology articles indexed in PubMed under the author name Samantha R. Ellis give two institutional affiliations: the Department of Dermatology at the University of California, Davis, in Sacramento, and PotozkinMD Skincare Center in Danville, California.[^3][^4]

## Selected publications

Ellis is a co-author of a 2020 review in the Journal of the American Academy of Dermatology on the histopathologic features of dermatologic toxicities from immune checkpoint inhibitor therapy.[^3] Ellis is also a co-author of a 2019 review in the journal Microorganisms on the skin and gut microbiome in common dermatologic conditions.[^4]

## Prequel

Ellis is the founder of Prequel, a skincare company. The statements in this section are Prequel's own. They are reproduced with attribution as the company's positioning, and are not endorsements or findings independently established here.

Prequel's About page carries a first-person statement attributed to Ellis: "I'm Dr. Sam Ellis, a board-certified medical and cosmetic dermatologist and the founder of Prequel."[^2] The evidence for any individual Prequel product is assessed on that product's own page, for example [[prequel-lucent-c-vitamin-c-serum|Prequel Lucent-C Brightening Vitamin C Serum]].
