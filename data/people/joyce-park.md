Joyce Park, MD, is "a board-certified dermatologist based in Washington, founder of Skin Refinery, a virtual dermatology clinic, and a skincare and beauty content creator at Tea with MD."[^bio] She "attended college and medical school at Stanford University, and completed her dermatology residency at NYU," and publishes science-based skincare education on YouTube (@TeawithMD), the channel her video cards across this site are drawn from.[^bio]

One disclosure to weigh when reading her product recommendations: Dr. Park monetizes picks through a ShopMy affiliate storefront and has her own hair-care line, so her specific product endorsements are treated on this site as promotional rather than independent. Her educational explainers (how a condition works, how to use an ingredient) are cited on their own merits.
