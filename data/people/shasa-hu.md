Shasa Hu is a board-certified dermatologist and Director of the Cosmetic Division at the University of Miami's Dr. Phillip Frost Department of Dermatology and Cutaneous Surgery, where she also holds the rank of Associate Professor.[^1]

NBC News/Today.com quoted Hu describing L-ascorbic acid's general profile as well-evidenced but prone to oxidation and requiring a low, potentially irritating pH, in coverage of the [[trader-joes-nourish-vitamins-c-e-facial-serum|Trader Joe's Nourish Vitamins C & E Facial Serum]]; she was not reported to have tested or endorsed that specific product.[^2]
