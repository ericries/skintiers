Jen Novakovich founded The Eco Well, a cosmetics science-communication platform running a podcast, blog and e-summit series. Her own site describes her as "a science communicator and cosmetic scientist with a niche expertise in ecodesign" who "has spent roughly a decade as a technical consultant helping beauty brands with formula and claims development".[^about]

Her transcript-verified videos are embedded on this site's [[sunscreen-uv-filters]], [[preservatives]], [[bakuchiol]] and [[microplastics-in-cosmetics]] pages, mostly on the question of how cosmetic claims and scares get made rather than on product recommendations.

Note the relationship she has with the industry she covers: her consulting work has included claims development for beauty brands, and The Eco Well is Patreon-supported.[^about] That does not make her commentary unreliable, but it is a paid industry relationship a reader should know about.
