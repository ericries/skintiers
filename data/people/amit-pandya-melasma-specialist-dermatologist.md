Amit Pandya, MD, is a board-certified dermatologist with the Sutter Health system whose clinical focus includes pigmentary disorders such as melasma and vitiligo.[^1] He is a co-author of the placebo-controlled, double-blind randomized trial of oral tranexamic acid for moderate-to-severe melasma, cited on [[tranexamic-acid]].[^2]
