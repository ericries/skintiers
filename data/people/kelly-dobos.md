Kelly Dobos (B.A. Chemistry, Oberlin College; M.S. Pharmaceutical Sciences (cosmetic science emphasis), James L. Winkle College of Pharmacy, University of Cincinnati). 20-year cosmetic chemist (skin care and color cosmetics; brands including Bonne Bell, Purell, Jergens); served as President of the Society of Cosmetic Chemists (2019) and created the Florence E. Wall Award for Women in Cosmetic Science; frequent on-record subject-matter expert for outlets including Allure, New Beauty, NPR, and The Washington Post.
