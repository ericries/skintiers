Kelly Dobos is a cosmetic chemist with two decades of experience formulating skin care and color cosmetics for brands including Bonne Bell, Purell, and Jergens, and served as President of the Society of Cosmetic Chemists in 2019.[^bio]

As SCC president, Dobos is quoted commenting on [[zoe-diana-draelos|Zoe Diana Draelos]] winning the Society's inaugural Florence Wall Women in Cosmetic Chemistry Award, saying "Dr. Draelos has made significant technical contributions in the cosmetic science field."[^cosmeticsbusiness]
