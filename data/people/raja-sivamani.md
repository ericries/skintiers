Raja Sivamani is a researcher affiliated with the Department of Dermatology at the University of California, Davis, and with Pacific Skin Institute and Integrative Skin Science and Research in Sacramento, California; he discloses paid consulting and scientific-advisory relationships with several skincare and pharmaceutical companies, including Burt's Bees.[^pmc]

Sivamani is a co-author on a head-to-head trial of [[bakuchiol]] against [[retinol]] for photoaging (see [[dhaliwal-2019-bakuchiol-retinol-photoaging]]), on an industry-funded open-label serum study cited on the [[tetrahexyldecyl-ascorbate]] ingredient page, and on a Burt's Bees-funded, open-label acne trial cited on the [[inulin]] ingredient page, where he discloses a paid consulting relationship with that funder.[^pmc]
