Randy Schueller is a cosmetic chemist who co-founded The Beauty Brains, a consumer-facing science blog and podcast, with fellow cosmetic chemist [[perry-romanowski]].[^1] The two recorded the show's first 165 episodes together before Schueller retired from it in 2018.[^1]
