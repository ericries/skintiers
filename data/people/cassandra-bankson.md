Cassandra Bankson is a skincare content creator who publishes acne-focused education online.[^1] Murad's editorial site describes her as "a licensed medical esthetician" and a "model and skinfluencer," featured in its Skincare Seven video series.[^1] No independent state licensing-board record for the credential was located; this profile relies on that media description.[^1]
