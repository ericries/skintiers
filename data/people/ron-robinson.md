Ron Robinson is a cosmetic chemist, founder, and CEO of BeautyStat Cosmetics.[^1]

## Background

CEW's professional profile describes Robinson as a "veteran cosmetic chemist with over 20 years experience in creating innovative, big-selling beauty products" for Clinique, Estee Lauder, La Mer, Revlon, Avon, and Lancome before he started BeautyStat Cosmetics in 2019.[^1] The same profile says his brand's Universal C Skin Refiner serum "has been named one of the best by Allure, Vogue and Harper's Bazaar," a designation from those outlets, not a claim this site verifies independently.[^1]

No product, ingredient, or video page on this site currently cites Robinson or BeautyStat.
