Ron Robinson (Cosmetic chemist; B.S., Adelphi University (Chemistry/Biology)). Veteran cosmetic chemist with 20+ years developing products for Clinique, Estee Lauder, La Mer, Revlon, Avon, and Lancome; founded the BeautyStat education blog in 2009 and later the BeautyStat Cosmetics brand, explaining ingredient chemistry (e.g., stabilized L-ascorbic acid) to consumers.
