Heather D. Rogers, MD, is a dermatologist, described on her brand's own site as a "nationally-acclaimed, procedural dermatologist" and the founder of Doctor Rogers Skin Care.[^bio]

She founded Doctor Rogers RESTORE, an eponymous skincare line best known for a healing balm; that positioning is the brand's own marketing, not an independently verified claim.[^bio] Doctor Rogers RESTORE has no product page on this site yet.
