Whitney Bowe (MD). Board-certified dermatologist and Assistant Clinical Professor in the Waldman Department of Dermatology at the Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai. Physician-researcher on the gut-skin axis and acne (h-index 21, 45 research publications per her Mount Sinai scholar profile), and creator of the widely-adopted 'Skin Cycling' routine concept; large public/educational following built on translating her own published research.
