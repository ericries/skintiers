Sheldon Pinnell, MD, was Chief Emeritus of the Division of Dermatology and J. Lamar Callaway Professor Emeritus of Dermatology at Duke University, where he served as division chief for 15 years before retiring in 2010; he died in Durham, North Carolina, on July 4, 2013, at age 76.[^1][^2] SkinCeuticals' own corporate site credits him as the brand's founding scientist.[^3] His Duke laboratory ran the percutaneous-absorption studies that set the pH and concentration rules for topical L-ascorbic acid, and the pig-skin and human studies showing ferulic acid stabilizes and boosts the photoprotection of a combined vitamin C and vitamin E serum; both later studies disclosed his consulting relationship with SkinCeuticals and a related patent.[^4][^5] That research is covered on [[ascorbic-acid-vitamin-c]] and [[ferulic-acid]], and the product it produced is [[skinceuticals-c-e-ferulic]].
