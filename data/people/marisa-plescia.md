Marisa Plescia is a cosmetic chemist and the founder of FemChem Beauty, a formulation research-and-development consultancy.[^1] She holds four graduate degrees in cosmetic and fragrance science, business, and STEM education from the University of Cincinnati Cosmetic Science program and ISIPCA in France.[^1] She is a longtime volunteer with the Society of Cosmetic Chemists, having chaired its Twin Cities chapter from 2020 to 2022, and is set to serve as president of the SCC's board of directors in 2026.[^1] She also teaches cosmetic-science courses and seminars and has been featured as a subject-matter expert in outlets including The Washington Post, NPR, and The New York Times.[^1]
