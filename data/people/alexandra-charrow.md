Alexandra Charrow, MD, is a dermatologist practising in Boston. The federal NPI registry returns exactly one record for the name, with Dermatology as the primary taxonomy and an active Massachusetts licence, plus a second taxonomy in Internal Medicine.[^npi] That dual registration is relevant to the condition she appears here discussing, since hidradenitis suppurativa is treated as a systemic inflammatory disease rather than a surface one.

She appears on this site in a single transcript-verified interview, embedded on [[hidradenitis-suppurativa]], in which dermatologist [[abigail-waldman|Dr. Abigail Waldman]] introduces her as clinical director of the hidradenitis suppurativa clinic at Brigham and Women's Hospital.[^video] Charrow describes an average delay of roughly seven to ten years between first symptoms and diagnosis, and splits treatment by whether the disease behaves more like Crohn's or more like acne.

She is not a creator on this site's roster; the channel is Waldman's.
