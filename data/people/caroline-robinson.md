Caroline Robinson, MD, FAAD, is, in her practice's words, "a board-certified dermatologist and the founder and CEO of Tone, a luxury aesthetic medicine practice specializing in advanced, science-driven skincare."[^bio] Based in Chicago, she publishes science-based skincare education on YouTube (@crobinsonmd), the channel her video cards across this site are drawn from, covering topics including [[sunscreen-uv-filters|sunscreen]], [[niacinamide]], [[peptides]], [[azelaic-acid]], [[anti-aging]], and [[dark-circles]].

One disclosure to weigh when reading her product recommendations: Dr. Robinson links a ShopMy affiliate storefront in nearly every video and sells products through her practice's Tone Shop, so her specific product picks are treated on this site as promotional rather than independent. Her educational explainers (how an ingredient works, how to use it) are cited on their own merits.
