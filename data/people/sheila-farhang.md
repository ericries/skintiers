Sheila Farhang, MD, is a double-board-certified dermatologist and Mohs micrographic skin cancer surgeon who founded and leads Avant Dermatology & Aesthetics, with offices in Tucson, AZ and Beverly Hills, CA.[^1][^2] She also performs Mohs surgery at A to Z Dermatology in Chandler and Casa Grande, AZ.[^2] Her NPI registration lists a primary Arizona dermatology license (taxonomy 207N00000X), consistent with her board certification.[^3] She attended the University of Texas for undergraduate and medical school, then completed a dermatology residency at the University of South Florida and a dual fellowship in cutaneous oncology and Mohs surgery at the Skin Institute of South Florida.[^2] She posts skincare content on TikTok and Instagram as [dr.sheila_derm](https://www.instagram.com/dr.sheila_derm/).[^1]

**Commercial interest to weigh.** In 2026 she launched her own cosmetics line, Dose (of) Derm, announcing it on her own TikTok account: "So I made it myself."[^4] She describes a foundation with mineral SPF 45, a dual-ended contour and blush stick and a lip stain, all built on what the same post calls a "proprietary TriBiome Longevity Complex" of peptides, exosomes and microbiome support.[^4] Her educational clips are carded here on [[azelaic-acid]], [[benzoyl-peroxide]], [[salicylic-acid]], [[peptides]], [[copper-peptides]], [[sunscreen-uv-filters]], [[acne]], [[body-acne]], [[hyperpigmentation]] and [[skin-barrier-strengthening]]. Her product picks are not, because an own-brand line overlapping those same categories makes them promotion rather than recommendation.
