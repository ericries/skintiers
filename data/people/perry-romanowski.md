Perry Romanowski is a cosmetic chemist, Vice President of Element 44 Inc, and a past President of the Society of Cosmetic Chemists (SCC) who co-founded The Beauty Brains, a podcast and website that answers reader questions about cosmetic-industry marketing claims.[^lcf][^scc2026][^tbb]

## Background and career

Romanowski holds a B.S. in Chemistry and Biochemistry from DePaul University, according to his own professional bio; a separate conference bio credits him with a bachelor's in chemistry and a master's degree in biochemistry, also from DePaul.[^lcf][^teamworks] He has been formulating cosmetic products since the early 1990s, most recently as a senior project leader in the hair care innovation group at the Alberto Culver Company, where he worked on brands including VO5, TRESemme, Nexxus, and St. Ives before Alberto Culver was acquired by Unilever.[^cc][^lcf] He is currently Vice President of Element 44 Inc, a Chicago-based company he describes as producing Chemists Corner and other cosmetic-science education sites and products.[^cc][^scc2026]

## Society of Cosmetic Chemists

Romanowski was previously President of the Society of Cosmetic Chemists (USA & Canada), and has held other national and regional SCC offices.[^teamworks] He teaches the SCC's continuing-education courses, including its Beginning Cosmetic Chemistry class.[^lcf][^pcf] Effective January 1, 2026, the SCC's own board announcement lists him as the organization's national Vice President, representing Element 44 and the SCC Midwest Chapter.[^scc2026] He has also served as Chair of Education on the IFSCC Praesidium, the leadership body of the International Federation of Societies of Cosmetic Chemists, since 2019.[^teamworks]

## The Beauty Brains and Chemists Corner

Romanowski and fellow cosmetic chemist Randy Schueller founded The Beauty Brains as a blog in 2006 to answer consumer questions about cosmetic science; the two recorded its first 165 podcast episodes before Schueller retired from the show in 2018, after which cosmetic chemist Valerie George became Romanowski's co-host.[^tbb] The site describes its purpose as helping readers "cut through the confusing, misleading and sometimes false information" from beauty companies, answering questions in what it says is an unbiased, science-based way rather than a sales pitch.[^tbb] Romanowski also founded Chemists Corner, an online cosmetic-science education company, and its Practical Cosmetic Formulating and Complete Cosmetic Chemist training courses for people learning to formulate cosmetic products.[^cc][^pcf]

## Books

Romanowski co-authored, with Schueller, *Beginning Cosmetic Chemistry*, a textbook now in a third edition published by Allured that is used in several college cosmetic-science programs.[^cc][^tbb] The two also co-authored *It's OK to Have Lead in Your Lipstick*, a 2013 book that opens by addressing what the American Council on Science and Health called the top unfounded health scare story of 2007, and that says it separates real risks in cosmetic ingredients from media scaremongering and marketing claims around "natural" and "organic" labeling.[^goodreads]
