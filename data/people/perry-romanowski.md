Perry Romanowski (B.S. in Chemistry and Biochemistry, DePaul University; Vice President, Element 44 Inc.; past President, Society of Cosmetic Chemists (SCC)). Co-creator of The Beauty Brains podcast/site and founder of Chemists Corner and Practical Cosmetic Formulating training; 25+ years developing cosmetic products (including work at Alberto Culver on VO5, TRESemme, Nexxus, St. Ives) and a long-running public educator on cosmetic-science myths.
