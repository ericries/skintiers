Zoe Diana Draelos (MD, FAAD). Board-certified dermatologist and Consulting Professor of Dermatology at Duke University; served ~10 years as editor-in-chief of the Journal of Cosmetic Dermatology and sits on ~38 journal editorial boards. One of the most prolific cosmetic-dermatology researchers in the field, credited as principal investigator on 600+ studies and author of 500+ published papers plus textbook chapters, making her a leading evidence source (rather than media personality) for cosmetic ingredient/formulation claims.
