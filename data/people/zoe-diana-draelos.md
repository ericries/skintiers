Zoe Diana Draelos, MD, FAAD, is a board-certified dermatologist in High Point, North Carolina, and Consulting Professor of Dermatology at Duke University School of Medicine, known in the field chiefly as a clinical trialist, textbook author, and journal editor in cosmetic dermatology rather than as a public-facing personality.[^npi][^doximity][^wiley]

## Credentials and practice

Draelos holds a Rhodes Scholarship and an undergraduate degree in mechanical engineering, and earned her medical degree at the University of Arizona College of Medicine, where she completed an internal medicine internship and a dermatology residency.[^history][^avidas][^doximity] She moved to North Carolina in 1987 and became board certified in dermatology that year.[^history] The federal NPI registry lists her (NPI 1518940170) as an active dermatologist with additional taxonomies in dermatopathology, procedural dermatology, and pediatric dermatology, and records her North Carolina medical license and practice address at 2444 N Main St, High Point.[^npi]

In 1988 she founded Dermatology Consulting Services, PLLC, a High Point clinical and research practice where she still serves as principal investigator; her practice's site describes a research facility that runs FDA Phase I through IV trials.[^history][^avidas][^investigator] Both a Wiley textbook's publisher biography and her Doximity listing independently describe her, beyond her own site, as Consulting Professor in the Department of Dermatology at Duke University School of Medicine.[^wiley][^doximity]

## Research output and clinical trials

A PubMed author search for "Draelos ZD" returns more than 330 indexed publications dated from 1996 to 2026, an independently countable measure of how much of her work has passed peer review into the indexed literature.[^pubmed] Her practice's site puts her total output higher, at roughly 650 published papers and principal-investigator credit on about 862 studies, alongside 14 authored books, more than 40 textbook chapters, and membership on 38 journal editorial boards; a 2019 trade-press profile tied to an award she received put the same tallies, at that earlier date, at 541 papers, 621 studies as principal investigator, and the same 38 editorial boards, so the scale predates and is independent of her own current figures even where the exact counts differ.[^investigator][^cosmeticsbusiness]

Several of her trials and papers already anchor other pages on this site. A double-blind study she ran with Matsubara and Smiles found a 2% niacinamide moisturizer significantly lowered sebum excretion in one population, evidence discussed on [[niacinamide]] and [[oily-skin]].[^niacinamide-study] A separate trial of hers found Cetaphil Gentle Skin Cleanser did not raise transepidermal water loss in rosacea patients, covered on [[cetaphil-gentle-skin-cleanser]].[^cetaphil-study] She is also a co-author on the two vehicle-controlled ivermectin 1% cream trials for papulopustular rosacea, discussed on [[rosacea-papulopustular]], and on the National Rosacea Society Expert Committee's 2019 update on standard rosacea management, cited on [[rosacea]].[^ivermectin-study][^rosacea-guideline]

## Textbooks and journal editorship

Draelos is the author of "Cosmetics in Dermatology," first published by Churchill Livingstone in 1990, and of "Cosmetic Dermatology: Products and Procedures," now in a third edition from Wiley (2022).[^googlebooks-cosmetics][^wiley] She also authored the fourth edition of "Cosmeceuticals" in Elsevier's Procedures in Cosmetic Dermatology series, published in 2024.[^elsevier-cosmeceuticals]

Her practice's site and an independent conference biography both describe roughly a decade as editor-in-chief of the Journal of Cosmetic Dermatology, published by Wiley.[^investigator][^dermupdate] She has served on the Board of Directors of the American Academy of Dermatology and of the American Society for Dermatologic Surgery, and in 2019 was the inaugural recipient of the Society of Cosmetic Chemists' Florence Wall Women in Cosmetic Chemistry Award; then-SCC president [[kelly-dobos|Kelly Dobos]] said at the time that "Dr. Draelos has made significant technical contributions in the cosmetic science field."[^cosmeticsbusiness]
