Shereene Idriss is a dermatologist licensed in New York and the dermatologist known on social media as PillowtalkDerm.[^1][^2]

## Credentials and affiliations

The federal National Plan and Provider Enumeration System (the NPI registry), maintained by the US Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services, lists Shereene Idriss as a physician holding a Doctor of Medicine degree, with a primary specialty taxonomy of Dermatology and New York medical license 269568. The record gives her practice location as Idriss Dermatology at 80 West 40th Street in New York, identifies her as a sole proprietor, and was first enumerated in 2009.[^1]

Idriss Dermatology describes her as "a fellow of the American Academy of Dermatology" and a member of the American Society for Dermatologic Surgery and the Dermatologic Society of Greater New York.[^2] The practice states that "Every single treatment at our practice is performed by a board-certified dermatologist."[^3] According to Idriss Dermatology, she has practiced dermatology in New York since 2013.[^2]

## Training

Idriss Dermatology states that Idriss received her medical degree from George Washington University Medical Center, "followed by a four-year residency at Tufts Medical Center in Boston."[^2]

## Selected publications

Dermatology articles indexed in PubMed under the author name Shereene Z. Idriss carry institutional affiliations consistent with that training, including George Washington University Medical Center and centers in Boston.[^4][^5] Idriss is the first author of a 2009 survey study in Archives of Dermatology on online support communities for patients with psoriasis; it reported that greater participation in those communities was associated with improved quality of life (P = .002), while it found no such association with psoriasis severity.[^4] Idriss is also the first author of a 2009 review in the Journal of Drugs in Dermatology on malathion for head lice and scabies.[^5]

## PillowtalkDerm and Dr. Idriss Skincare

According to Idriss Dermatology, Idriss began in 2018 sharing educational videos "straight from her bed on Instagram, quickly becoming known as #PillowtalkDerm."[^2] The practice says her audience grew "to over 3 million followers across social platforms."[^2]

Idriss launched a skincare brand, Dr. Idriss, in 2022, sold at Sephora.[^2][^6] The brand describes itself as "a collection of fact-based, science-backed skincare solutions by Dr. Shereene Idriss" and describes her as "a leading board certified dermatologist."[^6]
