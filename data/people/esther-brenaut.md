Esther Brenaut works in the Dermatology Department at the University Hospital of Brest and in the LIEN laboratory at Univ Brest, the two affiliations recorded on her papers as recently as 2026.[^recent]

She is here as a co-author of the International Forum for the Study of Itch position paper that produced the Delphi-consensus definition of sensitive skin, alongside [[laurent-misery]], who is its first author.[^ifsi] That definition is quoted in full and worked through on [[sensitive-skin]].

Her recent work continues on the same question of how sensory symptoms map onto skin disease, including a 2026 response letter arguing about whether the sensory symptoms of scalp psoriasis amount to a sensitive scalp syndrome.[^recent]

No title, rank, degree or clinical credential is claimed here, because the publication record is the only source consulted.
