Valerie Callender is a board-certified dermatologist based in Glenn Dale, Maryland, quoted in NewBeauty's round-up of dermatologist-recommended CeraVe products.[^1]

Callender is quoted recommending [[cerave-hydrating-facial-cleanser|CeraVe Hydrating Facial Cleanser]] "for my patients with normal or dry sensitive skin on their face," and says she uses [[cerave-pm-facial-moisturizing-lotion|CeraVe PM Facial Moisturizing Lotion]] herself because "the ceramides help to nourish my skin and protect it from the cold weather."[^1]
