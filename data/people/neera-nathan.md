Neera Nathan, MD, MSHS, is a board-certified dermatologist and fellowship-trained Mohs surgeon. Harvard Health, where she is a contributor, describes her as "a dermatologist and researcher at Massachusetts General Hospital and Lahey Hospital and Medical Center," with interests in "dermatologic surgery, cosmetic dermatology."[^bio] She publishes science-based skincare education on YouTube (@dermatologysurgeon), the channel her video cards across this site are drawn from, covering [[acne]], [[keratosis-pilaris]], [[enlarged-pores]], and actives including [[tretinoin]], [[adapalene]], [[azelaic-acid]], [[salicylic-acid]], [[glycolic-acid]], [[zinc-oxide]], and [[sulfur]].

One disclosure to weigh when reading her product recommendations: Dr. Nathan links a "Shop my Favorites" affiliate storefront, so her specific product picks are treated on this site as promotional rather than independent. Her educational explainers (how a condition works, how to use an ingredient) are cited on their own merits.
