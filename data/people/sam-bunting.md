Sam Bunting is a UK medical doctor (MA Cambridge, MBBS UCL, MRCP) who completed seven years of specialist training in medical dermatology and now practices cosmetic dermatology at her Harley Street clinic, Dr Sam Bunting + Associates, in London.[^bio] Her site lists [[acne]] and [[rosacea]] among the concerns treated.[^site]

She also runs Dr Sam's, an eponymous skincare line built around the brand's own four-step "cleanse, activate, hydrate, protect" routine framework; that framing is the brand's own marketing, not an independently verified claim.[^bio] Dr Sam's Skincare has no product page on this site yet.
