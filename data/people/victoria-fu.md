Victoria Fu is a cosmetic chemist and co-founder, with [[gloria-lu|Gloria Lu]], of Chemist Confessions, a skincare-education Instagram account, podcast, and product line.[^aiche][^biblio]

## Credentials and background

Fu majored in chemical engineering at the University of California, San Diego.[^aiche] She holds a master's degree in nanoengineering, with graduate thesis work on nanomedicine and drug-carrier development that she has said was applied to acne research.[^argos][^beautyindependent]

Fu worked as a formulator at L'Oreal before leaving for what she has described as a job with more lighthearted, faster-turnaround projects; an account of Chemist Confessions' origin published by the American Institute of Chemical Engineers says she and Lu met while working together in a lab at L'Oreal.[^beautyindependent][^aiche] She has also worked as a skincare chemist at Kiehl's, specializing in anti-aging products.[^aiche]

## Chemist Confessions

Fu and Lu started Chemist Confessions as an Instagram account in August 2017, while neither was working a full-time job, posting explainers on skincare ingredients and formulation.[^beautyindependent] It has grown into a blog, a podcast, and a product line that Fu and Lu co-own, including Aquafix, The Better Oil, Balm Voyage, and Mr. Reliable.[^beautyindependent][^biblio]

## Skincare Decoded

Fu and Lu are co-authors of "Skincare Decoded: The Practical Guide to Beautiful Skin," published by Weldon Owen in March 2021, and of a "Revised and Expanded" edition Weldon Owen published in December 2024.[^biblio][^target] The publisher's description frames the book as two professional chemists and beauty-industry insiders answering questions such as whether a ten-step routine is necessary and whether a $100 eye cream is worth it.[^biblio]
