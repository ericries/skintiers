Linda Stein Gold is a board-certified dermatologist and Director of Dermatology Clinical Research and Division Head of Dermatology at Henry Ford Health in Michigan.[^1]

She is a co-author on several trials cited on this site: the vehicle-controlled trials behind [[ivermectin-topical|topical ivermectin]]'s FDA approval and used on [[soolantra-ivermectin-1-cream|Soolantra]], a long-term trial of ivermectin against azelaic acid in [[rosacea-erythematotelangiectatic|rosacea]], the approval trial for oxymetazoline cited on [[oxymetazoline-topical]], a phase 3 trial of topical minocycline foam for acne (see [[stein-gold-2019-topical-minocycline-foam]]), and the American Academy of Dermatology's acne guidelines cited on [[acne]].
