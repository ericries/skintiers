Aleksandra Brown, D.O., is a dermatologist in Blacksburg, Virginia, with a primary Dermatology taxonomy under a Virginia license.[^npi] She is the most-cited creator here, carded on 32 pages.

Her clips are carded mainly on peptides and on drugstore moisturiser lines: [[peptides]], [[copper-peptides]], [[argireline-acetyl-hexapeptide-8]], [[palmitoyl-pentapeptide-4-matrixyl]], [[glycolic-acid]], [[anti-aging]] and [[seborrhoeic-keratosis]], plus the CeraVe range ([[cerave-foaming-facial-cleanser]], [[cerave-hydrating-facial-cleanser]], [[cerave-daily-moisturizing-lotion]]) and the [[cerave]], [[cetaphil]], [[aveeno]] and [[eucerin]] brand pages.

**How to read her product picks.** Her ranking and favourites videos route every product link through a ShopMy affiliate storefront, so this site treats those picks as effectively paid; her purely educational uploads carry no links. She does disclose non-sponsorship where it applies, writing "This video is not sponsored by CeraVe." on the ranking video whose links are nonetheless affiliate links.[^money]

**Where the credential comes from.** Her practice lists her individually as "Aleksandra Brown, D.O., F.A.O.C.D., F.A.A.D." and calls her "a board-certified dermatologist at River Ridge Dermatology."[^team] That is her employer's own bio, not a board registry; the AOCD and AAD directories are search applications no plain fetch could query.[^team]
