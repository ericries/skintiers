Aleksandra Brown, D.O., is a dermatologist practising in Blacksburg, Virginia. Her NPI record lists a primary Dermatology taxonomy under a Virginia license.[^npi] She is the most-cited creator on this site, with video cards on 32 pages.

Her clips are carded mainly on peptides and on drugstore moisturiser lines: [[peptides]], [[copper-peptides]], [[argireline-acetyl-hexapeptide-8]], [[palmitoyl-pentapeptide-4-matrixyl]], [[glycolic-acid]], [[anti-aging]] and [[seborrhoeic-keratosis]], plus the CeraVe range ([[cerave-foaming-facial-cleanser]], [[cerave-hydrating-facial-cleanser]], [[cerave-daily-moisturizing-lotion]]) and the [[cerave]], [[cetaphil]], [[aveeno]] and [[eucerin]] brand pages.

**One thing this page does not claim.** This site's creator roster describes her as board-certified (FAOCD, FAAD), and her video cards carry that wording. Neither was verified from a primary source for this page: the NPI taxonomy records a specialty, not a board certification, and her practice's site makes the claim of its dermatologists collectively without naming her.[^npi][^practice]
