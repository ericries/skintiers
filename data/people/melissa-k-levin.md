L'Oréal Paris's Derm Intensives marketing identifies Melissa K. Levin as "Dr. Melissa K. Levin, MD, Board-Certified Dermatologist" and quotes her recommending its retinol serum.[^loreal]

## What she's quoted on

In that brand marketing, Levin says: "I recommend Pure Retinol to help reduce wrinkles and exfoliate, improving skin's texture and tone. Start with 0.3% and let your skin acclimate."[^loreal] That quote appears on the [[loreal-paris-revitalift-0-3-pure-retinol-serum|L'Oréal Paris RevitaLift 0.3% Pure Retinol serum]] page, where the manufacturer's own headline efficacy claim for the product is evaluated.
