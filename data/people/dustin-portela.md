Dustin Portela, DO, is a board-certified dermatologist and dermatologic surgeon and the founder of Treasure Valley Dermatology & Skin Cancer Center in Boise, Idaho.[^1][^2] He posts dermatology and skincare content on TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube and co-hosts a podcast called Skinfluence.[^2]
