Eric L. Simpson, MD, MCR, is Professor of Dermatology at Oregon Health & Science University and a board-certified dermatologist, per OHSU's faculty listing.[^ohsu] His atopic dermatitis research is cited on this site: a neonatal-emollient prevention trial he led underlies [[skin-barrier-strengthening]], and moisturizer-comparison and probiotic-therapy studies he co-authored are cited on [[cerave-moisturizing-cream]], [[cetaphil-moisturizing-cream]], and [[probiotics]].[^simpson2014][^leshem2020][^jacobson2024]
