Differin Gel is a once-daily, over-the-counter [[adapalene]] 0.1% gel for acne, sold in a 45 g (1.6 oz) tube for $23.99 as listed on Target.[^target]

> Adapalene is a synthetic retinoid the FDA classifies as an acne treatment in its own right, with a solid evidence base at the 0.1% strength used here ([[adapalene]]). Differin markets itself as an "Rx-strength" retinoid available over the counter at that same 0.1% strength, so this tube is a standard-strength, standard-formula delivery of an already well-evidenced OTC active rather than a novel or diluted one.[^target][^differin]

## Summary

This is a leave-on gel treatment, not a cleanser or spot treatment, applied as a thin layer over the whole affected area rather than dabbed on individual spots. The Target listing's Drug Facts label gives the directions as: "Use once daily. Clean the skin gently and pat dry before applying the product. Cover the entire affected area with a thin layer."[^target]

The formula's single active is [[adapalene]] at 0.1%, an FDA-approved topical retinoid for acne with a solid evidence base built on a Cochrane review and a pooled meta-analysis of comparative trials against benzoyl peroxide and tretinoin (see [[adapalene]] for that evidence). The Drug Facts label confirms the active ingredient as "adapalene (retinoid)(0.1%)" for the purpose "Acne Treatment," matching the FDA over-the-counter monograph strength.[^target] Because this is the same molecule and concentration marketed as an "Rx-strength" retinoid now sold over the counter, the ingredient-level evidence applies directly rather than by extrapolation.[^target][^differin] The rest of the formula is a short, functional base with no fragrance and no other treatment actives.[^target]

Differin's own site describes the brand as "THE CREATORS OF ADAPALENE RETINOID" and markets itself as a "DERMATOLOGIST RECOMMENDED ACNE TREATMENTS BRAND," language addressed as [marketing, not independent verification, below](#common-marketing-claims).[^differin]

This page found no fetchable Differin-branded clinical study specific to this SKU beyond the Drug Facts label and marketing copy reviewed here (see [What's In It](#whats-in-it) and [Sources](#sources) for what was verifiable); the grade above rests on the general adapalene evidence, not on a trial of this exact product.

## What's In It

Active ingredient, per the Target Drug Facts label:

- Adapalene 0.1% ([[adapalene]]), a synthetic retinoid, the sole treatment active[^target]

Inactive ingredients, per the same label: "carbomer 940, edetate disodium, methylparaben, poloxamer 182, propylene glycol, purified water and sodium hydroxide, may contain hydrochloric acid to adjust ph."[^target] The listing describes the product as water-based, oil-free, alcohol-free, fragrance-free, and non-comedogenic.[^target]

## Common Marketing Claims

Label and marketing claims not established by product-specific evidence above:

- **"Up to 87 percent reduction in acne breakouts after 12 weeks."** This figure appears on the Target product listing as brand-sourced copy rather than a cited clinical trial with a named study, sample size, or comparator; no independent source for the underlying trial was located.[^target]
- **"Rx-strength acne-fighting retinoid."** Adapalene 0.1% gel was originally a prescription drug and is now also sold over the counter at the same strength, so the phrase is consistent with the ingredient's regulatory history ([[adapalene]]), but no separate clinical claim about this specific tube was located.[^target]
- **"DERMATOLOGIST RECOMMENDED ACNE TREATMENTS BRAND" / "CLINICALLY PROVEN INGREDIENTS" / "BACKED BY SCIENCE."** Brand-stated tagline copy on Differin's own site; no named survey, methodology, or third-party source for these characterizations was located.[^differin]
