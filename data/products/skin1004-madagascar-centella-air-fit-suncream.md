SKIN1004 sells this formula as the Centella Air-Fit Suncream Plus, a beige-tinted, non-nano mineral (physical) sunscreen rated SPF50+ PA++++, built on zinc oxide and titanium dioxide rather than chemical UV filters.[^1]

> A mineral sunscreen, not a chemical or hybrid one: [the full ingredient list](#whats-in-it) confirms zinc oxide and titanium dioxide as the only UV filters, and the brand states it is formulated without oxybenzone, octinoxate, avobenzone, or octocrylene.[^1] Its SPF50+ PA++++ rating is a Korean (MFDS) label claim, not FDA-tested, so [it is not independently verified against the US test method](#the-evidence); mineral sunscreen's general broad-spectrum case is set out on [[sunscreen-uv-filters]]. Centella asiatica extract, 12th-listed at an undisclosed concentration, carries [evidence that is much stronger for wound healing than for everyday soothing](#the-evidence); niacinamide, 9th-listed and also undisclosed, has modest general brightening evidence, covered on [[niacinamide]]. As of 2026-08-07 the 50 ml size is listed sold out on the brand's own international storefront, and no listing was found at Target.[^1][^2]

## Summary

Centella Air-Fit Suncream Plus is part of SKIN1004's Madagascar Centella line, the same "cica" line as the [[skin1004-madagascar-centella-asiatica-100-ampoule]]. It is a facial sunscreen aimed at oily and combination skin, sold with a slight beige tint and described by the brand as leaving "a matte and slightly powdery finish" that creates "an airy UV protective layer on the skin."[^1]

Its full ingredient list, confirmed on the brand's own product page, names only two UV filters: zinc oxide and titanium dioxide, both mineral (physical, non-nano) filters.[^1] The brand states the formula is "Reef Safe," made without benzophenone-3, benzophenone-4, benzophenone-8, ethylhexyl methoxycinnamate (octinoxate), butyl methoxydibenzoylmethane (avobenzone), or octocrylene, the common chemical filters it explicitly excludes.[^1] Neither filter's concentration is disclosed. The label rates the product SPF50+ PA++++, the top of the Korean SPF and PA (UVA) label scales, but that rating comes from Korea's MFDS testing system rather than the FDA's, so it carries no independent US verification.[^1]

Beyond the filters, the formula lists niacinamide 9th and centella asiatica extract 12th, both at undisclosed concentrations, plus madecassoside (one of centella's active triterpenes) far down the list alongside fragrance-associated compounds (citronellol, geraniol, linalool).[^1] The brand markets niacinamide for "skin brightening," a cosmetic claim; niacinamide's general topical evidence is modest and mostly small-trial, covered on [[niacinamide]]. Centella asiatica's best-documented human evidence is for wound healing rather than everyday soothing, covered on [[centella-asiatica]]; no product-specific trial of this sunscreen was found.

As of 2026-08-07 the brand's own international storefront lists the 50 ml size at $18.00 (sale $16.20) but shows it sold out, and a Target search for the product returned no listing.[^1][^2] It does not currently appear to be a US-market SKU.

## The Evidence

No product-specific trial was found for this sunscreen; the product-specific facts are its confirmed filter composition and its self-reported Korean label rating.

Zinc oxide and titanium dioxide are both established broad-spectrum mineral filters; zinc oxide's own absorption and photostability profile is set out on [[zinc-oxide]]. Neither filter's concentration is disclosed on the brand's page, so the strength of protection this specific formula delivers cannot be independently estimated from the label alone.[^1] The SPF50+ PA++++ rating is the brand's own claim, tested under Korea's MFDS system rather than the FDA's OTC monograph process used for US Drug Facts labels, so it has not been independently verified for the US market.[^1]

> **Bottom line.** The confirmed filter set, zinc oxide and titanium dioxide only, supports a genuine mineral broad-spectrum sunscreen, and mineral filters' general UVB/UVA coverage is documented on [[sunscreen-uv-filters]] and [[zinc-oxide]]. The specific SPF50+ PA++++ number is a self-reported Korean label claim rather than an FDA-tested or independently lab-verified figure.
