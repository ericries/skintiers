Neutrogena Rapid Wrinkle Repair Retinol Serum (full name on the current listing: Rapid Wrinkle Repair Retinol Renewal Serum) is a 1 fl oz leave-on facial serum built around 0.1% retinol, sold by Neutrogena for $27.99.[^1]

> A drugstore retinol serum that discloses its retinol concentration, 0.1%, which lines up with a concentration shown effective in a published vehicle-controlled trial ([[retinol]]). No published trial has tested this exact serum, and its "smoother and more radiant" one-week claim comes only from the manufacturer's own marketing copy as repeated on a retailer listing.[^1]

## Summary

Neutrogena Rapid Wrinkle Repair Retinol Renewal Serum is a 1 fl oz bottle priced at $27.99 ($27.99 per fluid ounce) on Target's listing.[^1] Its labeled active is [[retinol]], the alcohol form of vitamin A and the most-studied over-the-counter retinoid, declared on the ingredient list at 0.1%.[^1] That disclosed figure matters because it is possible to check it against the published literature: a 52-week, vehicle-controlled trial of a 0.1% stabilized retinol found real improvement in crow's feet fine lines and mottled pigmentation, [covered in full on [[retinol]]](#the-evidence). Many mass-market retinol products, including [[roc-retinol-correxion-deep-wrinkle-night-cream|RoC's]] and [[cerave-resurfacing-retinol-serum|CeraVe's]] comparable serums, do not disclose a percentage at all, so this listing is more checkable than those.

The formula is built on a water, glycerin, and light ester emollient base (C12-15 alkyl benzoate, ethylhexyl palmitate) with retinol following as the fifth ingredient, then a set of amino-acid and plant-derived ingredients (carnosine, trehalose, portulaca oleracea extract, yeast extract) that are not independently evidenced actives on their own trials.[^1] Retinol's own fine-wrinkle and pigment evidence, and its milder, slower profile relative to prescription [[tretinoin]], is set out on [[retinol]] and [[retinoids]]; no published trial has tested this specific serum.

Target's listing repeats the manufacturer's marketing language that the serum "improves the look of fine lines, wrinkles, pores, texture, and firmness" and that "skin is instantly smoother and more radiant with a reduced look of fine lines after 1 week," but this is manufacturer copy carried on a single retailer page rather than a published or independently verified trial, [quarantined below](#common-marketing-claims).[^1] At $27.99 for 1 fl oz, it sits above [[trader-joes-retinol-night-serum|Trader Joe's]] 0.3% retinol serum at about $10 and near [[roc-retinol-correxion-deep-wrinkle-night-cream|RoC's]] and [[cerave-resurfacing-retinol-serum|CeraVe's]] undisclosed-percentage retinol products at about $22 each, well under [[skinmedica-retinol-complex-1-0|SkinMedica's]] $96 undisclosed-percentage retinol complex.

## The Evidence

Topical retinol has independently replicated, vehicle-controlled trial evidence for improving fine wrinkles and pigment in both intrinsically aged and photodamaged skin, milder and slower than prescription [[tretinoin]]; the full case, including the exact trial statistics, is set out on [[retinol]] and [[retinoids]]. One of those two cited trials tested a 0.1% stabilized retinol over 52 weeks and found improved crow's feet fine lines and mottled pigmentation against vehicle, the same concentration this product's ingredient list discloses.[^1]

No published trial tests this exact Neutrogena serum, its "Retinol Renewal" formulation, or its non-retinol ingredients (carnosine, trehalose, portulaca oleracea extract, yeast extract, sarcosine) in isolation. The 0.1% disclosure is a composition fact that lets the concentration be checked against the category trials; it is not itself evidence that this finished formula performs the same as the tested one.

> **Bottom line.** No study has isolated this serum's own effect. Its case rests on the general vehicle-controlled evidence for retinol at 0.1%-0.4% on [[retinol]], and the product's disclosed 0.1% concentration matches one of the two cited trials, a checkable data point that several same-priced competitors do not offer.
