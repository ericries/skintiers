## Summary

EltaMD UV Clear is a broad-spectrum SPF 46 facial sunscreen aimed at acne-prone and sensitive skin, and one of the most dermatologist-recommended daily facial sunscreens. Its health job is UV protection: it pairs octinoxate 7.5% with zinc oxide 9.0%, so despite its sensitive-skin, mineral-leaning reputation it is a chemical-and-mineral hybrid, not a pure mineral sunscreen.[^eltamd] It also carries niacinamide and hyaluronic acid as secondary skincare actives. The evidence for the UV filters themselves lives on [[sunscreen-uv-filters]]; the niacinamide evidence on [[niacinamide]].

## The Evidence

The core health benefit is broad-spectrum photoprotection, which lowers photoaging and skin-cancer risk. As an FDA-regulated over-the-counter sunscreen it is tested to Broad Spectrum SPF 46, high UVB protection from octinoxate 7.5% plus zinc oxide 9.0%.[^eltamd] UVA coverage comes from the zinc oxide, a genuine broad-spectrum mineral filter but a weaker UVA performer than modern filters, and there is no additional UVA filter, since octinoxate protects only against UVB. No published trial tests this exact product; the case rests on its disclosed, FDA-permitted filter concentrations and the general filter evidence on [[sunscreen-uv-filters]]. The brand also markets niacinamide to help reduce blemishes and the look of discoloration; niacinamide's own evidence is modest and lives on [[niacinamide]], and this product does not disclose its niacinamide percentage.[^eltamd]

## What's In It

Active sunscreen filters: octinoxate 7.5% (UVB) and zinc oxide 9.0% (broad-spectrum mineral).[^eltamd] Secondary skincare actives the brand highlights are niacinamide (vitamin B3) and hyaluronic acid.[^eltamd] It is sold oil-free, non-comedogenic, and fragrance-free, positioned for sensitive and acne-prone skin, in a 1.7 oz size.[^eltamd]

## Common Marketing Claims

EltaMD markets UV Clear for acne-prone and sensitive skin and highlights niacinamide to help reduce blemishes and the appearance of discoloration.[^eltamd] The broad-spectrum SPF 46 protection is FDA-tested and substantiated; the niacinamide and hyaluronic-acid benefits are secondary cosmetic claims whose general evidence lives on [[niacinamide]] and [[hyaluronic-acid]], not product-specific trials. Although it is often grouped with mineral, sensitive-skin sunscreens, it contains octinoxate, a chemical UVB filter that some regions (for example Hawaii) restrict over coral-reef concerns.
