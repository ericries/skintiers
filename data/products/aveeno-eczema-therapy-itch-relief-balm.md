Aveeno Eczema Therapy Nighttime Itch Relief Balm is an over-the-counter eczema balm built around colloidal oatmeal 1%, an FDA skin-protectant active, in a rich oat-oil-and-glycerin base.[^1]

> An OTC skin-protectant drug: the Drug Facts label lists colloidal oatmeal 1% as the active, which "temporarily protects and helps relieve minor skin irritation and itching due to rashes, eczema."[^1] It is the same colloidal oatmeal active as Aveeno's other eczema products, in a heavier balm format aimed at overnight use; the evidence for the active is on [[colloidal-oatmeal]] and [[atopic-dermatitis]].

## Summary

This is the overnight-balm member of the Aveeno Eczema Therapy line. It is regulated as an OTC drug: the label states the active as "colloidal oatmeal 1%," the purpose as skin protectant, and the use as temporarily protecting and relieving minor skin irritation and itching due to rashes and eczema.[^1]

Colloidal oatmeal's evidence is on [[colloidal-oatmeal]]: an FDA-monograph skin protectant with modest but real support for the itch and irritation of eczema-prone skin. The balm format is thicker and more occlusive than a lotion, which suits dry, cracked, eczema-prone skin overnight, but that is a vehicle choice; the active and its evidence are the same. No trial of this exact balm was located.

## The Evidence

The defensible facts are the active (colloidal oatmeal 1%) and the rich emollient base with oat oil and oat extract.[^1] For eczema-prone skin, a heavy colloidal-oatmeal balm protects and hydrates the barrier and eases itch, useful maintenance care, especially overnight, but a supportive moisturizer rather than a treatment for flares, which is the domain of anti-inflammatory therapy on [[atopic-dermatitis]].

> **Bottom line.** A colloidal oatmeal 1% overnight eczema balm, a reasonable, rich maintenance moisturizer for dry, itchy, eczema-prone skin.[^1] It is barrier care and symptom relief, not flare treatment; see [[atopic-dermatitis]].

## What's In It

Active ingredient: "Colloidal Oatmeal 1%."[^1] Inactive ingredients, from the label: glycerin, water, cetearyl alcohol, hexyldecanol, dimethicone, cetyl alcohol, oat (Avena sativa) kernel oil, oat kernel extract, benzyl alcohol, ethylhexylglycerin, sodium cetearyl sulfate, benzoic acid, stearic acid, and dipotassium phosphate, among others.[^1] The base is a rich emollient system with added oat oil and oat extract.

## Common Marketing Claims

- **"Eczema therapy" / "itch relief."** Label-supported at the skin-protectant level: colloidal oatmeal 1% temporarily protects and relieves minor irritation and itching due to eczema.[^1] It is maintenance care, not flare treatment.
- **"Nighttime."** A usage suggestion for the heavier balm format, not a distinct clinical claim.[^1]
