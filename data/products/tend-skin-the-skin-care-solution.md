Tend Skin The Skin Care Solution is a leave-on liquid applied after shaving, waxing, or hair removal, marketed to reduce razor bumps and ingrown hairs; its active is acetylsalicylic acid (aspirin), a salicylate, in a mostly isopropyl-alcohol base, per [INCIDecoder's ingredient listing for this product](https://incidecoder.com/products/tend-skin-solution). The Tend Skin Company lists the 4 oz bottle at $19.99.[^tendskin]

> The active is a salicylate keratolytic, the same class of ingredient that treatment reviews for razor bumps rate as a mid-tier topical mainstay, real but modest, and not a substitute for changing how the hair is shaved; see [[pseudofolliculitis-barbae]]. No published trial isolates this specific product, and the formula is mostly isopropyl alcohol, which can sting and dry skin that has just been shaved or waxed.

## Summary

Tend Skin markets the product as reducing "the appearance of unsightly ingrown hairs, razor bumps and burns from shaving, waxing, electrolysis and laser hair removal."[^tendskin] It is sold as a 4 oz bottle ($19.99), a 2.5 oz roll-on ($18.99), an 8 oz bottle ($29.99), and a 16 oz bottle ($39.99).[^tendskin]

The listed ingredients, per [INCIDecoder](https://incidecoder.com/products/tend-skin-solution), are isopropyl alcohol, butylene glycol, acetylsalicylic acid, cyclomethicone, glycerin, diglycerin, and polysorbate 80. Acetylsalicylic acid (aspirin) is the salicylate active; isopropyl alcohol is the base and leads the ingredient list, which is what makes the product astringent and drying.

## The Evidence

Topical salicylates are a keratolytic mainstay treatment reviews cite for razor bumps (pseudofolliculitis barbae), grading them a mid-tier add-on rather than a cure, because the underlying cause is the shaved hair itself; that evidence and its limits are set out on [[pseudofolliculitis-barbae]] and [[salicylic-acid]]. No independently published trial of this exact product is cited here; its case rests on the ingredient class.

Isopropyl alcohol leads the ingredient list. Isopropyl-alcohol-based products are astringent and drying, and can sting on freshly shaved, waxed, or otherwise broken skin, a property of the formula rather than a study finding. Because the active is a salicylate, it is also relevant to anyone with a salicylate or aspirin sensitivity.

> **Bottom line.** A salicylate keratolytic (the same mid-tier mainstay category graded on [[pseudofolliculitis-barbae]]) delivered in a mostly isopropyl-alcohol liquid; no product-specific trial is cited here, and the alcohol base is a real tradeoff on freshly shaved or broken skin.

## What's In It

The full ingredient list (INCI), per [INCIDecoder's ingredient listing for this product](https://incidecoder.com/products/tend-skin-solution): Isopropyl Alcohol, Butylene Glycol, Acetylsalicylic Acid, Cyclomethicone, Glycerin, Diglycerin, Polysorbate 80.

Notable: [[salicylic-acid|acetylsalicylic acid]] (aspirin) is the salicylate keratolytic active; isopropyl alcohol is the lead ingredient and the base solvent; glycerin and diglycerin are humectants; cyclomethicone is a silicone emollient; polysorbate 80 is an emulsifier/solubilizer.
