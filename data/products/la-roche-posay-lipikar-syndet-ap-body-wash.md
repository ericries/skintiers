La Roche-Posay sells this fragrance-free, soap-free cleansing wash in the US as "Lipikar AP+ Moisturizing Body & Face Wash," also labeled "Lipikar Wash AP+" on-package, for extra-dry, sensitive skin; the "Lipikar Syndet AP+" name used to queue this page is the line's soap-free ("syndet") naming used on the brand's other-market sites and by third-party ingredient databases for the same formula.[^brand]

> Niacinamide's barrier-lipid and water-loss benefits are established at the category level, see [[niacinamide]], and this wash's declared formula lists it eighth of twenty-three ingredients, ahead of shea butter and a Vitreoscilla filiformis extract the brand brands "Aqua Posae Filiformis," see [[vitreoscilla-filiformis-lysate]]. Both actives sit in a wash rinsed off within seconds rather than a leave-on product, which caps how much either can plausibly do here.[^brand]

## Summary

La Roche-Posay markets this as a gentle, foaming, moisturizing wash for extra-dry, sensitive skin that "provides long-lasting, 24 hour hydration," is Accepted by the National Eczema Association, is dermatologist-tested, and is labeled suitable for babies as young as two weeks and for patients undergoing chemotherapy (with the caveat not to use on broken skin and to consult a medical professional first).[^brand] It is sold in 100 mL, 200 mL, and 400 mL sizes; the 100 mL size lists at $9.99.[^brand]

The declared full ingredient list leads water, glycerin, sodium laureth sulfate, and coco-betaine, with niacinamide eighth of twenty-three ingredients, shea butter (Butyrospermum parkii butter) thirteenth, and Vitreoscilla ferment twelfth.[^brand] By comparison, the brand's own leave-on [[la-roche-posay-lipikar-balm-ap-m|Lipikar Balm AP+M]] carries the same niacinamide-plus-Vitreoscilla-filiformis story but is designed to stay on skin, rather than being rinsed off within seconds like this wash.

Niacinamide's barrier-lipid and hydration effects are supported at the category level, covered on [[niacinamide]]. The evidence for Vitreoscilla filiformis extract, the ingredient La Roche-Posay brands "Aqua Posae Filiformis" and promotes as microbiome science, rests on a small, manufacturer-run trial and cell-culture mechanism work, covered on [[vitreoscilla-filiformis-lysate]]. The brand's National Eczema Association acceptance and "clinically shown" language are the brand's own marketing statements, not an independently reviewed trial result.

## The Evidence

No independent study of this specific wash was located. Topical niacinamide raising ceramide and free-fatty-acid synthesis and lowering water loss in dry skin is established at the category level, see [[niacinamide]]; this wash's declared panel places niacinamide eighth of twenty-three ingredients.[^brand] Vitreoscilla filiformis extract's clinical case rests on a small trial in atopic-dermatitis patients plus cell-culture and tissue-explant mechanism work, all from work associated with L'Oreal's own research group, see [[vitreoscilla-filiformis-lysate]]; it is listed twelfth of twenty-three ingredients here.[^brand]

Both actives face the same structural limit in this format: a wash is rinsed off within seconds, unlike a leave-on moisturizer, so whatever benefit niacinamide and Vitreoscilla filiformis extract show in leave-on studies does not automatically transfer to a rinse-off cleanser.

> **Bottom line.** The wash's case rests on the general category-level evidence for niacinamide and for Vitreoscilla filiformis extract, both of which come from leave-on or in-vitro research rather than from a study of this rinse-off product, and both actives sit mid-list in a formula meant to be washed away within seconds.

## What's In It

Full declared ingredient list from the brand: water, glycerin, sodium laureth sulfate, coco-betaine, polysorbate 20, PEG-200 glyceryl stearate, PEG-7 glyceryl cocoate, niacinamide, mannose, sodium hydroxide, trisodium ethylenediamine disuccinate, Vitreoscilla ferment, Butyrospermum parkii (shea) butter, glycol distearate, acrylates copolymer, citric acid, cocamide MEA, PEG-55 propylene glycol oleate, pentylene glycol, polyquaternium-11, propylene glycol, sodium chloride, sodium benzoate.[^brand] The functional base is a sulfate/betaine surfactant system (sodium laureth sulfate plus coco-betaine) typical of a gentle body wash, with niacinamide and shea butter added for moisturizing and Vitreoscilla ferment added for the brand's microbiome-soothing claim.[^brand]
