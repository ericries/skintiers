Prequel Urea Repair 10% Moisturizing Milk, sold by [[prequel|Prequel]] (a skincare brand founded by dermatologist [[samantha-ellis|Dr. Samantha Ellis]]) under the name "Urea Advanced Relief Moisturizing Milk," is a fragrance-free face-and-body lotion built around 10% urea.[^pdp][^target]

> A lightweight, fragrance-free lotion whose headline ingredient is 10% urea, a concentration confirmed on both Prequel's own product page and Target's listing, alongside niacinamide, glycerin, and shea butter.[^pdp][^target] Moisturizing dry or barrier-impaired skin this way has a well-evidenced general case, covered on [[moisturizing]]; no independently reported trial isolates this exact lotion, and the manufacturer's "clinically tested 24 hour moisture" claim is [reported here as marketing, not independently verified](#common-marketing-claims).[^pdp][^target]

## Summary

Prequel Urea Repair 10% Moisturizing Milk is a fragrance-free lotion that Prequel markets for both face and body use on dry, rough, itchy, or flaky skin.[^pdp] Its own product page names the formula "Urea Advanced Relief Moisturizing Milk" and describes it as "a restoring face and body urea lotion that reestablishes optimal levels of hydration."[^pdp] The formula's [full ingredient list](#whats-in-it) leads with water and then urea, second on the label, ahead of emollients such as shea butter and jojoba esters, the humectant glycerin, and niacinamide further down the list.[^pdp][^target]

The distinguishing ingredient is urea itself, stated at 10% on Prequel's own product page and separately confirmed at "10% pure urea" in Target's product description.[^pdp][^target] Urea functions in skincare as a humectant at lower concentrations, of the kind used here, and as a keratolytic, softening and loosening thickened or scaling skin, at higher concentrations; this formula does not disclose which mechanism it is primarily leaning on beyond the stated 10% figure.[^pdp] No dedicated concept page for urea is cited here, so its general evidence base is not detailed on this page; the well-evidenced case for moisturizing dry and barrier-impaired skin generally, which this formula's overall composition (humectants plus emollients) fits into, is set out on [[moisturizing]].

Prequel also markets the lotion as carrying the National Eczema Association and National Psoriasis Foundation seals, and describes it as dermatologist-developed and dermatologist-tested; Target's listing separately lists "Dermatologist-Developed," "Dermatologist-Tested," "Hypoallergenic," "Allergy-Tested," "Non-Comedogenic," and "Safe for Eczema Prone Skin."[^pdp][^target] Those are brand and retailer claims rather than an independently reported trial, and are [catalogued as marketing below](#common-marketing-claims).

It sits in the mid-price range for the category. Prequel lists the 13.5 fl oz / 400 mL bottle at $22.00, and Target lists the same 13.5 fl oz size at $21.99 (see [Where to Buy](#where-to-buy), accessed 2026-08-07).[^pdp][^target]

## The Evidence

No published, independently reported trial of this exact lotion is cited on the two sources reviewed here. Prequel's own product page claims "clinically tested 24 hour moisture," but neither Prequel's page nor Target's listing discloses a study design, sample size, or methodology behind that figure, so it is treated as an unverified manufacturer claim rather than reported evidence.[^pdp][^target]

> **Bottom line.** No independently reported study isolates this exact lotion's own effect. The formula's case rests on its confirmed 10% urea concentration[^pdp][^target] plus a humectant-and-emollient base (glycerin, shea butter, niacinamide, jojoba esters), which fits the general, well-evidenced pattern for moisturizing dry and barrier-impaired skin covered on [[moisturizing]]; the manufacturer's own 24-hour-moisture claim carries no disclosed data.
