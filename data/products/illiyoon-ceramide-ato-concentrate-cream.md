Illiyoon Ceramide Ato Concentrate Cream, sold by [[illiyoon|Illiyoon]], is a 5.07 fl oz face and body cream that Target's listing titles "The comfort cream for a confident skin barrier," built around what the listing calls "Melting Ceramide Capsule Technology."[^target]

Ceramides are a genuine barrier lipid that skin can be measurably depleted in, and ceramide moisturizers reliably move barrier measurements such as water loss, though that has not translated into a proven edge over a plain base on eczema severity itself in the controlled trial covered on [[ceramides]]. ## Summary

Illiyoon Ceramide Ato Concentrate Cream is a face and body moisturizer from the South Korean brand [[illiyoon|Illiyoon]], sold at Target in a 5.07 fl oz size titled "Illiyoon Ceramide Ato Concentrate Cream 5.07 oz - The comfort cream for a confident skin barrier."[^target] Target's listing describes a proprietary "Ceramide Skin Complex" of "micro-encapsulated ceramide capsules" that "melt upon contact with your body heat," claiming "up to 100 hours of proven hydration," and states the formula is fragrance-free, free of seven common irritants, and free of parabens, mineral oil, artificial color, and synthetic fragrance.[^target]

Ceramides are structural lipids of the skin's outer barrier, and topical ceramide moisturizers have been shown in a controlled trial to lower transepidermal water loss and raise hydration measurements compared with a matched base, without a measurable edge on eczema severity itself; that general case, with its numbers and limits, is set out on [[ceramides]]. [INCIDecoder's ingredient listing for this product](https://incidecoder.com/products/illiyoon-ceramide-ato-concentrate-cream) confirms the formula's full declared order and shows a single ceramide-analog ingredient, Hydroxypropyl Bispalmitamide MEA, labeled "Ceramide Pc-104" at 6310 ppm (about 0.63%), 14th of 30 ingredients, well after water, butylene glycol, and glycerin.[^inci] That is a narrower ceramide profile than a cream naming several distinct ceramide subtypes (such as Ceramide NP, AS, or EOP); [the full list is enumerated below](#whats-in-it). No independently published trial of this exact cream is cited here.

Target's listing also carries cosmetic and marketing language, including "Intense, Long-Lasting Barrier Repair," "high concentrations of ceramide capsules," and "Soothes and Calms Sensitive Skin"; those are [reported below as retailer marketing copy, not independently verified findings](#common-marketing-claims).[^target]

It sits in the mid-to-premium range for a 5.07 fl oz cream. Target lists it at $39.99 with no discount shown (see [Where to Buy](#where-to-buy), accessed 2026-08-07).[^target]

## The Evidence

No independently published trial of Illiyoon Ceramide Ato Concentrate Cream itself is cited here. Its case rests on the general, controlled-trial evidence that ceramide moisturizers lower measured water loss and raise hydration readings versus a matched inactive base, without a demonstrated edge on eczema severity, set out with its study design and numbers on [[ceramides]]. INCIDecoder's ingredient listing for this product confirms the formula's single ceramide-analog ingredient, Hydroxypropyl Bispalmitamide MEA ("Ceramide Pc-104"), at a declared 0.63%, and shows cholesterol and the fatty acids stearic acid and palmitic acid elsewhere in the same 30-ingredient list; those are the same three lipid classes ([[ceramides]], [[cholesterol]], and fatty acids) that the physiologic-lipid research on [[ceramides]] describes as working together in the skin barrier, though the concentrations and ratio of those three ingredients in this specific formula are not disclosed beyond the ceramide-analog's 0.63% figure.[^inci]

> **Bottom line.** No study isolates this exact cream's own effect. Its case rests on the general, controlled-trial evidence for ceramide moisturizers on barrier measurements, covered on [[ceramides]], applied to a formula whose single named ceramide-analog ingredient is confirmed at about 0.63% by the ingredient database cited here,[^inci] alongside cholesterol and two fatty acids at undisclosed concentrations.
