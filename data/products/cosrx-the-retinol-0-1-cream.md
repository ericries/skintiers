COSRX The Retinol 0.1 Cream is a 20ml leave-on facial cream from the South Korean brand [[cosrx|COSRX]], listing retinol at a disclosed 0.1% and priced at $27.00 on the manufacturer's own site.[^cosrx][^yesstyle]

> A South Korean retinol cream that discloses its concentration on the ingredient list, an entry-level 0.1% the brand markets as "irritation-reduced" for first-time retinol users.[^cosrx] Topical retinol's fine-wrinkle and pigment benefits at the category level are covered on [[retinol]] and [[retinoids]]; this cream's 0.1% dose matches the lower end of the concentrations shown effective in those controlled trials, [detailed under The Evidence](#the-evidence).[^cosrx]

## Summary

COSRX The Retinol 0.1 Cream is a 20ml (0.67 fl oz) night cream sold for $27.00 on COSRX's own site; an independent retailer listing for the same 20ml SKU confirms that $27.00 list price (selling it at a 20% discount, $21.60, at the time checked).[^cosrx][^yesstyle] The product name and ingredient list both state the retinol concentration directly: the ingredient declaration lists "Retinol (0.1%)."[^cosrx]

The labeled active is [[retinol|retinol]] itself, a well-studied over-the-counter retinoid with vehicle-controlled trial evidence for improving fine wrinkles and pigment, [set out in full on [[retinol]] and [[retinoids]]](#the-evidence). Unlike many mass-market retinol products that name "retinol" without a percentage, this cream discloses its dose, and 0.1% sits at the low end of the concentration range those category trials tested, closely matching one 52-week trial's own tested dose, [detailed under The Evidence](#the-evidence).

COSRX markets the cream as "an irritation-reduced solution created with the optimal ratio of key ingredients for first-time retinol users," and its own directions describe a gradual introduction: applying a small amount at night, every other day on limited areas for the first one to two weeks, then moving to daily use across the face from week three onward.[^cosrx] Around the retinol, the formula carries a base of shea butter, soybean oil, panthenol, tocopherol (vitamin E), and several forms of [[hyaluronic-acid|hyaluronic acid]], plus adenosine, an ingredient the brand does not make a specific efficacy claim for in this product's own marketing copy.[^cosrx]

## The Evidence

Whether topical retinol improves photoaged and naturally aged skin is covered on [[retinol]] and [[retinoids]]: in short, vehicle-controlled trials support gradual improvement in fine wrinkles and pigment. One of those vehicle-controlled trials used a 0.1% stabilized retinol, the same concentration this cream discloses on its own ingredient list,[^cosrx] though in a different formulation; the trial design and its numbers are on [[retinol]]. No published trial tests this exact cream or its specific combination of retinol with shea butter, panthenol, and the hyaluronic acid complex.

> **Bottom line.** The retinol case for this cream rests on category-level evidence for the ingredient at a matching, disclosed 0.1% dose, not on a trial of the product itself.[^cosrx]

COSRX's own marketing describes retinol as "the most proven anti-aging ingredient available OTC," a claim the brand attributes to itself; the category-level evidence for retinol is real and is graded independently on [[retinol]].[^cosrx]
