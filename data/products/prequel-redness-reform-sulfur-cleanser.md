Prequel Redness Reform Sulfur Cleanser (Prequel) is a foaming, sulfur- and azelaic-acid-based facial cleanser Prequel positions for redness and reactive, rosacea-prone skin, made by Prequel, a skincare brand whose product page identifies its founder, Dr. Samantha Ellis, as a dermatologist.[^pdp]

> A rinse-off cleanser built around 2.5% colloidal sulfur and azelaic acid at an undisclosed concentration, both ingredients with a long history of topical acne and rosacea use ([[sulfur]], [[azelaic-acid]]). Azelaic acid's best evidence for rosacea comes from a 15% leave-on gel; whether a lower, undisclosed concentration in a cleanser that is rinsed off within minutes does anything comparable is not tested here. Prequel lists it among its products carrying the National Rosacea Society Seal of Acceptance, a claim on Prequel's own page and [reported here as marketing, not independently confirmed for this SKU](#common-marketing-claims).[^pdp]

## Summary

Redness Reform Sulfur Cleanser is a foaming wash Prequel markets for sensitive, reactive, redness-prone skin, sized at 6 fl oz (177 mL) and priced at $18.00 on the manufacturer's site.[^pdp] Prequel's own marketing description states the formula is built with "2.5% colloidal sulfur, azelaic acid, cica, bisabolol, panthenol and aloe to support a calmer, clearer, more even-looking complexion while maintaining a healthy skin barrier."[^pdp] The azelaic acid concentration is not disclosed anywhere on the product page.[^pdp]

What distinguishes this cleanser from a plain foaming wash is its two active ingredients: colloidal sulfur at a stated 2.5%, and azelaic acid at an unstated concentration.[^pdp] Both are ingredients with a history of topical use in acne and rosacea, covered in general on [[sulfur]] and, in more depth, on [[azelaic-acid]], where the well-replicated evidence is for a 15% leave-on gel, not a rinsed-off cleanser. That evidence base is a leave-on product held on skin for hours; this is a wash-off cleanser, and Prequel's own directions are to lather it onto wet skin and rinse, with an optional 2-3 minute "flash mask" hold as an alternative use.[^pdp] Whether either active reaches an effective concentration and dwell time in this format has not been tested for this product.

The cleanser lists water, glycerin, and several mild surfactants ahead of the two actives on the ingredient list, with a supporting cast of calming ingredients (centella asiatica extract, bisabolol, panthenol, chamomile flower water, and aloe) further down; see [What's In It](#whats-in-it) for the full list.[^pdp] Prequel's page also states the product is "clinically proven to perform well on rosacea-prone skin, with zero tolerability issues," a claim with no cited methodology or sample size, [reported as marketing rather than folded into the grades above](#common-marketing-claims).[^pdp]

Prequel lists it among the products in its line carrying the National Rosacea Society Seal of Acceptance; the Seal reflects an independent dermatologist panel's screen for ingredients tied to barrier damage, flushing, and stinging, a designation explained in more detail on [[prequel-gleanser-glycerin-cleanser]].[^pdp] It is priced in line with the rest of the Prequel cleanser line, at $18.00 for 6 fl oz on the manufacturer's site (see [Where to Buy](#where-to-buy), accessed 2026-08-07).[^pdp]

## The Evidence

No published, independent trial of this exact cleanser was located. Sulfur and azelaic acid are both established topical actives with their own evidence bases, covered on [[sulfur]] and [[azelaic-acid]]. For azelaic acid specifically, the general evidence base is for a 15% leave-on gel, detailed on [[azelaic-acid]]. That evidence does not transfer directly to this product, which discloses no azelaic acid concentration and is formulated as a cleanser rinsed off within minutes rather than left on skin.[^pdp]

> **Bottom line.** The cleanser's active ingredients, sulfur and azelaic acid, have their own evidence bases for acne and rosacea at disclosed leave-on concentrations, but this product's azelaic acid concentration is undisclosed and the format is a brief-contact, rinse-off wash rather than the leave-on gel that evidence applies to.[^pdp]
