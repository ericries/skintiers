Cetaphil Redness Relieving Daily Facial Moisturizer is a tinted, 100% mineral broad-spectrum SPF 40 moisturizer aimed at redness-prone and rosacea-prone skin, combining sun protection, a green-toned tint, and mild soothing ingredients.[^dailymed]

> Two different things are going on here, and only one is a treatment. The sunscreen is the real value: it is a fully mineral SPF 40 (titanium dioxide and zinc oxide, no chemical filters), and daily sun protection is genuinely first-line for rosacea because sun is a top trigger (see [[rosacea]]).[^dailymed] The "redness relieving" part is mostly cosmetic and immediate: the product is tinted with green and iron-oxide pigments that optically cancel red, plus caffeine and mild soothers.[^dailymed] Judge it as a good, gentle mineral sunscreen that also visually mutes redness, not as a rosacea treatment.

## Summary

This is a drug-regulated sunscreen: the label lists the active ingredients as titanium dioxide and zinc oxide with the purpose "Sunscreen," and the standard OTC sunscreen use, helping prevent sunburn and, used as directed, decreasing the risk of skin cancer and early skin aging from the sun.[^dailymed] It is a 100% mineral (physical) SPF 40, with no chemical UV filters, at roughly 10.1% titanium dioxide and 7.8% zinc oxide.[^dailymed]

The "redness relieving" positioning is delivered three ways, all visible in the inactive list: a tint (chromium oxide greens plus iron oxides) that neutralizes the look of redness by colour correction, caffeine as a temporary vasoconstrictor, and the soothing agents allantoin and dipotassium glycyrrhizate (a licorice derivative), in a glycerin and silicone base.[^dailymed] The actual medical treatments for rosacea redness, azelaic acid, brimonidine, ivermectin, and metronidazole, are ranked on [[rosacea]] and are not what this product contains.

## The Evidence

Two uses, graded above.

**Sun protection (the labeled use, and the strongest part).** A 100% mineral broad-spectrum SPF 40 is genuine, well-evidenced photoprotection, and because ultraviolet exposure is one of the most common rosacea and facial-redness triggers, a gentle daily sunscreen is a first-line, guideline-backed step for this skin type; the condition-level evidence is on [[rosacea]] and the filter evidence on [[sunscreen-uv-filters]].[^dailymed] Being fully mineral rather than chemical is a reasonable fit for reactive skin.

**Reducing the look of redness (cosmetic, immediate).** This is real but largely optical. The green and iron-oxide tint cancels red visually the moment it is applied, which is legitimate colour correction rather than treatment.[^dailymed] Caffeine can transiently constrict surface vessels, and allantoin and dipotassium glycyrrhizate are mild soothers, so the product can calm and mask redness in the moment, but none of this changes the underlying rosacea.

> **Bottom line.** A well-designed, gentle mineral SPF 40 that also visually mutes redness through a green tint, useful for redness-prone and rosacea-prone skin as the sunscreen step.[^dailymed] Value it for the photoprotection and the immediate colour correction, not as a treatment for the redness itself; for that, see [[rosacea]].

## What's In It

As a drug the label separates the active from the rest. Active ingredients: titanium dioxide and zinc oxide (purpose: sunscreen), roughly 10.1% and 7.8%.[^dailymed] Notable inactive ingredients, from the label: allantoin, caffeine, dipotassium glycyrrhizate, glycerin, tocopherol, and the tinting pigments chromium oxide greens and iron oxides, in a dimethicone and silicone base.[^dailymed]

## Common Marketing Claims

- **"Redness relieving."** Delivered mainly by an optical green tint plus caffeine and mild soothers, not by treating rosacea; the durable benefit for redness-prone skin is the sun protection itself.[^dailymed]
- **"Prevents sunburn / reduces the risk of skin cancer and early aging."** Standard, accurate OTC sunscreen claims for a broad-spectrum SPF 40 used as directed.[^dailymed]
