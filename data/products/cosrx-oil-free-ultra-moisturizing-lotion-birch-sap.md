COSRX Oil-Free Ultra-Moisturizing Lotion with Birch Sap is a lightweight Korean lotion built on birch sap (Betula Platyphylla Japonica Juice) in place of most of the water, with glycerin, betaine, [[dimethicone]] and sodium hyaluronate and no added facial oils, sold in a 100 ml pump bottle.[^1]

> A cheap, lightweight moisturizer aimed at oily and combination skin. The health job it does is hydration at a modest level: humectants like glycerin and sodium hyaluronate bind water in the outer skin, the best-evidenced part of moisturizing, covered on [[moisturizing]]. What is distinctive is the format rather than a special active, a watery, oil-free lotion that hydrates without a greasy after-feel. "Oil-free" here means the formula adds no oils; it does not reduce how much oil the skin produces.[^1]

## Summary

The Oil-Free Ultra-Moisturizing Lotion is a thin, fast-absorbing lotion COSRX positions for "oily and combination skin," used after cleansing and toning and before a cream or sunscreen.[^1] Its health role is a moisturizing one: humectants such as [[glycerin]], betaine and sodium hyaluronate ([[hyaluronic-acid]]) draw water into the outer skin and hold it there. Binding water this way, and slowing its loss, is one of the better-evidenced routines in dermatology, and that general case is set out on [[moisturizing]]. The strongest moisturizing evidence sits with dry, barrier-impaired and eczema-prone skin; for oily and combination skin a light lotion like this delivers hydration and comfort, a more modest, cosmetic-leaning benefit.

What sets it apart from a plainer moisturizer is the format and the base, not a unique active. Most of the water is replaced by birch sap (Betula Platyphylla Japonica Juice), listed first and stated at 70%, and the formula's slip comes from a silicone ([[dimethicone]]) rather than plant oils or waxes.[^1] That is what "oil-free" describes: no added oils, so the lotion sits light and non-greasy on skin that already feels oily. This is a cosmetic property of the feel. An oil-free moisturizer of this kind hydrates without adding to the greasy feel, but it does not act on sebum, the oil the skin itself makes, and the formula carries no oil-controlling active such as [[niacinamide]], a retinoid, or a BHA.

On reception, the lotion is widely sold and rated 4.4 out of 5 across 124 reviews at Ulta ([see Where to Buy](#where-to-buy), accessed 2026-07-28). It is inexpensive: the manufacturer and Ulta list the 100 ml bottle at about $25 ([see Where to Buy](#where-to-buy), accessed 2026-07-28).

The product-specific evidence is in [The Evidence](#the-evidence); COSRX's "vitamins and antioxidants" and skin-calming language is separated out under [Common Marketing Claims](#common-marketing-claims).

## The Evidence

Holding water in the outer skin with humectants, and slowing its loss, is one of dermatology's better-evidenced routines; the humectants that do it, [[glycerin]], sodium hyaluronate ([[hyaluronic-acid]]) and betaine, and how well that is evidenced, are set out on [[moisturizing]]. What is specific to this product is its oil-free format and its birch-sap base.

No clinical trial of this exact lotion is published; its grades rest on the category evidence for humectant moisturizers linked above and on its composition.

The lotion is oil-free by composition: the ingredient list contains no plant oils or waxes, and its slip and light emollience come from a silicone ([[dimethicone]]) and fatty-alcohol emulsifiers rather than an oil.[^1] That keeps the finish light on oily and combination skin, a cosmetic benefit of the feel. It hydrates by binding water in the outer skin; it does not change the skin's own oil (sebum) output, for which the formula carries no active such as [[niacinamide]], a retinoid, or a BHA.[^1]

## What's In It

The full ingredient list (INCI) as declared by the manufacturer.[^1] The ingredients with a functional role are linked to their own pages; the rest are the base, texture, and preservative system.

Water-binding and soothing ingredients:

- birch sap (Betula Platyphylla Japonica Juice), listed first and stated at 70%, the water-substitute base[^1]
- [[glycerin]], betaine and sodium hyaluronate ([[hyaluronic-acid]]), humectants that bind water
- [[dimethicone]], a silicone that adds slip and light emollience without an oil
- panthenol and allantoin, common soothing and skin-conditioning ingredients
- [[tocopherol]] (vitamin E), an antioxidant

Base, texture, and preservative system: butylene glycol and 1,2-hexanediol (solvent-humectants), cetearyl alcohol (a fatty-alcohol emulsifier and thickener), cetearyl olivate, sorbitan olivate, sorbitan isostearate and polysorbate 60 (olive-derived and other emulsifiers), hydroxyethyl acrylate/sodium acryloyldimethyl taurate copolymer and xanthan gum (thickeners), sodium lactate (a humectant and pH buffer), ethyl hexanediol and ethylhexylglycerin (preservative-boosters), and 4-terpineol, a component of tea tree oil.[^1]

The ingredient list declares no plant oils, no added parfum/fragrance, and no drying alcohol.[^1]
