Paula's Choice Skin Perfecting 2% BHA Liquid Exfoliant is a simple leave-on exfoliant built around 2% [[salicylic-acid|salicylic acid]], a beta-hydroxy acid that works inside oily pores to loosen blackheads and dead skin. Dermstore, an authorized retailer, lists the 118 mL bottle at $37.00.[^dermstore]

> This is a genuinely well-formulated product because its one active is genuinely well-evidenced: 2% salicylic acid is the beta-hydroxy acid dermatologists reach for to clear blackheads, congestion, and comedonal acne, and it works because it is oil-soluble enough to get inside the pore (the general evidence is on [[salicylic-acid]]). The formula around it is short and sensible: water, a couple of humectant solvents, a solubilizer, a little green tea extract, and sodium hydroxide to hold the low pH the acid needs. The claim to read skeptically is "shrinks enlarged pores": salicylic acid clears pores so they look smaller and less obvious, but it does not permanently change pore size.

## Summary

Paula's Choice markets this as its "#1 product worldwide" and an "effective formula" that "unclogs and shrinks enlarged pores."[^pc] It is a watery leave-on liquid, not a rinse-off, applied after cleansing and before moisturizer.

The ingredient list is short: water, methylpropanediol and butylene glycol (humectant solvents), 2% salicylic acid (the active, fourth on the list), polysorbate 20 (a solubilizer that keeps the formula clear), Camellia oleifera (green tea) leaf extract, sodium hydroxide (a pH adjuster), and tetrasodium EDTA. The evidence-backed work here is done by the [[salicylic-acid|salicylic acid]]; the green tea is a minor antioxidant and everything else is the delivery base. It is listed at $37.00 for the 118 mL size at Dermstore ([see Where to Buy](#where-to-buy), accessed 2026-09-24).[^dermstore]

## The Evidence

The product-specific evidence is essentially the ingredient evidence: salicylic acid at its labeled 2%[^dermstore] is a long-established over-the-counter acne active that exfoliates the lining of the pore and helps clear blackheads and comedones, set out with its primary sources on [[salicylic-acid]]. The formula presents it at a concentration and a low pH consistent with that use, in a leave-on liquid rather than a rinse-off, which keeps the acid in contact with skin. No independently published trial of this exact product is cited here; its case rests on the well-studied active.

> **Bottom line.** A minimalist, sensibly formulated 2% salicylic acid[^dermstore] leave-on whose active is a genuinely well-studied beta-hydroxy acid for blackheads and congestion, though treatment guidelines rank benzoyl peroxide and topical retinoids above it for acne overall (see [[salicylic-acid]]). It exfoliates and clears pores rather than shrinking them, and like any BHA it is an exfoliant to build up slowly, not a moisturizer or a treatment for inflammatory or hormonal acne on its own.
