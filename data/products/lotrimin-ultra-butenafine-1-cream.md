## Summary

An over-the-counter antifungal cream from Bayer whose active is **butenafine hydrochloride 1%**, not the clotrimazole most people associate with the Lotrimin name.[^dailymed] **Bottom line:** for athlete's foot between the toes it carries a cure claim and an unusually short one-week option, and [[butenafine]] is graded good on its own evidence. For athlete's foot anywhere else on the foot, the label itself says effectiveness is unknown.

> **Two things on the box are easy to get wrong.** [[lotrimin-af-antifungal-cream|Lotrimin AF]] is clotrimazole; Lotrimin Ultra is butenafine. Different molecules, same family name. And four separate current labels use the Lotrimin Ultra name with identical active ingredient and four different Uses lists, so the variant named for your condition may legally cover less than a differently named box of the same cream.[^dailymed]

## The Rubric

- **Effect size: notable, between the toes.** The Uses section reads "cures most athlete's foot between the toes".[^dailymed] A cure claim is a higher bar than the relief wording common on this shelf, and the directions offer two regimens: "apply to affected skin between and around the toes twice a day for 1 week (morning and night), or once a day for 4 weeks".[^dailymed] A one-week course is short for a topical antifungal; the evidence for the molecule is on [[butenafine]] and is not restated here.
- **Effect size: unknown, on the sole or sides.** Immediately after the cure claim the same sentence continues: "Effectiveness on the bottom or sides of foot is unknown."[^dailymed] This is the manufacturer declining to claim the moccasin-type distribution, and it appears on three of the four Lotrimin Ultra labels read. A product that tells you where it has not been shown to work is giving you more than most.
- **Record finding: one drug, four labels, four different Uses.** All four current Lotrimin Ultra labels declare "Butenafine hydrochloride 1%".[^dailymed] The one simply called Lotrimin Ultra lists only athlete's foot between the toes. Lotrimin Ultra Jock Itch lists only jock itch. Lotrimin Ultra Ringworm lists ringworm, jock itch and athlete's foot between the toes. Lotrimin Ultra Athletes Foot lists athlete's foot between the toes, jock itch and ringworm.[^dailymed] The two narrow labels are the ones named after a single condition.
- **Dosing changes with the indication, not with the tube.** For ringworm and jock itch the Ringworm label directs "apply once a day to affected skin for 2 weeks"; for athlete's foot between the toes the regimen is the one-week twice-daily or four-week once-daily course above.[^dailymed] Same cream, different schedules.
- **Composition.** The inactive ingredients are benzyl alcohol, cetyl alcohol, glycerin, glyceryl monostearate SE, polyoxyethylene (23) cetyl ether, propylene glycol dicaprylate, purified water, sodium benzoate, stearic acid, trolamine and white petrolatum.[^dailymed] No fragrance is declared. Benzyl alcohol is the preservative here and is a recognised contact allergen in a minority of people; see [[contact-dermatitis]].

## The Evidence

None specific to this product. Butenafine's trial evidence is set out on [[butenafine]], which grades it good, and the comparison against the other over-the-counter antifungals belongs to [[otc-antifungal-creams-by-labelled-scope]] rather than here.

What this page adds is the label itself, and the label is more informative than most. It names the one anatomical site where the drug is proven, names the site where it is not, and sets a schedule per indication.

> **Bottom line.** A well-evidenced molecule in a product that is honest about its limits. Check the active ingredient rather than the brand, and check the Uses on the specific box, because the Lotrimin Ultra name covers four different sets of claims.

## Common Marketing Claims

> Quoted from the FDA-filed label and **not** endorsed beyond what it says.

- "cures most athlete's foot between the toes"[^dailymed] A cure claim, scoped to one site on the foot, with "most" doing real work.
- "relieves itching, burning, cracking, and scaling which accompany this condition"[^dailymed] Symptom relief, which is a separate claim from cure and is the one most antifungal boxes lead with.
- The brand name itself. Lotrimin Ultra and Lotrimin AF are different drugs, and nothing on the shelf makes that obvious.
