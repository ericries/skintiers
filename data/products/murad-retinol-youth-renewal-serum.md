Murad Retinol Youth Renewal Serum is a cosmetic retinol serum from [[murad]], marketed for the visible signs of early skin aging and built around a proprietary retinoid blend the brand calls its "4x Active Retinoid Technology."[^brand]

> A retinol anti-aging serum whose active class genuinely works: retinol is a real anti-aging retinoid, with its evidence set out on [[retinol]] and [[retinoids]]. Two honest caveats: the serum does not disclose how much retinol it contains, and its eye-catching "clinically proven" figures come from Murad's own consumer and clinical-panel testing, not independent trials. Judge it as a reasonable retinol serum, priced at the premium end, not as a uniquely proven one.

## Summary

This is Murad's flagship retinol serum. The brand describes it as "powered by 4x Active Retinoid Technology... an encapsulated retinol for quick, deep, stable and targeted delivery, a gentle yet fast-acting retinol, a stabilized retinoid with barrier-nourishing omega-6," plus a soothing component.[^brand] In plain terms, it is a blend of retinol and a stabilized retinoid with a delivery system and buffering ingredients, which is a sensible way to formulate retinol; the concentration is not stated.

What the serum does rests on retinol, whose anti-aging evidence lives on [[retinol]] and [[retinoids]], not on anything unique to this product. The brand's clinical claims are its own panel results, flagged below.

## The Evidence

No independent trial of this serum was located. The product-specific facts are its formulation (a retinol-plus-stabilized-retinoid blend with a delivery system) and its marketing claims.[^brand] Because retinol is an evidenced anti-aging active, a well-made retinol serum can plausibly improve fine lines and texture over weeks to months; that general expectation, and its limits, are covered on [[retinol]].

Murad's headline results, "In 14 days: 100% showed visible improvements in elasticity, firmness and radiance, 93% showed visible improvements in fine lines, wrinkles, and dark spots, and 96% agree skin looks healthier," and "In 7 days: Clinically proven to improve 10 visible signs of early skin aging," carry a dagger footnote marking them as the brand's own consumer and clinical-panel studies.[^brand] Those are marketing evidence, not independent RCTs, and the undisclosed retinol concentration makes the product hard to compare against graded options.

> **Bottom line.** A competently formulated, premium-priced retinol serum. The active (retinol) is genuinely anti-aging, but the concentration is undisclosed and the "clinically proven" numbers are brand panels, so buy it as a retinol serum you like, not because it is better proven than cheaper retinols; see [[retinol]] for what to expect.[^brand]

## What's In It

The functional core is the brand's retinoid blend: an encapsulated retinol for delivery, a fast-acting retinol, and a stabilized retinoid with omega-6, plus soothing and barrier-supporting ingredients.[^brand] The full INCI list and the retinol percentage are not printed on the product page; the brand markets the blend rather than a stated concentration.
