Biodance Bio-Collagen Real Deep Mask is a Korean hydrogel sheet mask sold as a four-piece single-use set (forehead, two cheeks, chin) totaling 34 g, marketed around a "bio-collagen" claim for hydration and plumping.[^brand]

> A hydrogel mask whose namesake ingredient looks thin once the label is read closely. The brand's own ingredient declaration lists "Collagen Extract" at 55,000 ppb, about 0.0055%, and 25th of 34 ingredients, well behind niacinamide, allantoin, and hydrolyzed hyaluronic acid.[^brand] Collagen is also a large protein, roughly 300 kDa, that a 2023 literature review says has "poor permeability due to high molecular weight" when applied topically, regardless of dose.[^collagen] The more defensible short-term effect is the ordinary hydrogel-mask one: occlusion plus humectants giving temporary surface hydration and plumping, not collagen delivery.[^brand]

## Summary

Biodance Bio-Collagen Real Deep Mask is a four-piece hydrogel sheet mask (34 g per application) built on a water base with glycerin, niacinamide, and several ferment filtrates, sold in Korea as boxes of 16 sets and also listed by name under the same brand on Amazon.com (see [Where to Buy](#where-to-buy)).[^brand] Its marketing centers on "bio-collagen," but the brand's own full ingredient list places "Collagen Extract" at 55,000 ppb (about 0.0055%) and 25th of 34 ingredients, behind niacinamide, allantoin, and hydrolyzed hyaluronic acid.[^brand] Topical collagen is also a large molecule, and a 2023 literature review found that "the topical administration of collagen is limited by poor permeability due to high molecular weight (±300 kDa)," a mechanical limit independent of how much is in the jar.[^collagen]

What the formula more plausibly delivers is the ordinary hydrogel-sheet-mask effect: a short-lived plumped, hydrated look from occlusion combined with humectants glycerin and hydrolyzed hyaluronic acid, the general evidence for which is set out on [[hyaluronic-acid]]. Niacinamide sits higher in the list (5th of 34) but at no declared percentage; the general case for niacinamide on pigmentation and barrier support is set out on [[niacinamide]]. The product's Korean listing also carries the country's official "whitening" and "wrinkle-improvement" functional-cosmetic designations; the brand page states the combined designation but does not specify which ingredient qualifies each function.[^brand]

Its grades below rest on the ingredient list itself, general ingredient-level evidence linked out to [[niacinamide]], [[adenosine]], and [[hyaluronic-acid]], and one literature review on topical collagen's permeability limits (see [The Evidence](#the-evidence)). It's listed at 60,500 KRW discounted from 80,000 KRW for a 16-application box on the brand's Korean site[^brand]; no verifiable US retail price was found (see [Where to Buy](#where-to-buy)).

## The Evidence

No published trial isolating this specific product, or its declared 55,000 ppb collagen extract, was found. The grades above rest on three separate strands: the product's own declared ingredient list and order, general ingredient-level evidence linked out to [[niacinamide]], [[adenosine]], and [[hyaluronic-acid]], and independent literature on topical collagen's physical limits.

On the "bio-collagen" claim specifically: the brand's own product page states the collagen extract is present at 55,000 ppb, which is about 0.0055%, and it appears 25th among 34 declared ingredients, after the base, film-formers, and several named actives (niacinamide, allantoin, hydrolyzed hyaluronic acid).[^brand] Separately from concentration, a 2023 literature review of collagen delivery strategies states plainly that "the topical administration of collagen is limited by poor permeability due to high molecular weight (±300 kDa)," and that hydrolysis or a specialized delivery system is needed to improve penetration; the brand's ingredient list does not declare the collagen extract as hydrolyzed.[^collagen] Taken together, neither the declared amount nor collagen's basic physical chemistry supports the "bio-collagen" name as the mask's active hydrating or firming mechanism.

The more defensible mechanism is the general one for any hydrogel sheet mask: occlusion from the hydrogel plus humectants (glycerin, hydrolyzed hyaluronic acid) drawing and holding water at the skin surface, producing a real but short-lived plumped, hydrated look; the broader evidence for hyaluronic acid's role in that effect is on [[hyaluronic-acid]]. Niacinamide and adenosine are both present, at undeclared concentrations, and both carry Korea's official functional-cosmetic designations on this product's own listing (whitening for niacinamide, wrinkle-improvement for adenosine); the general, modest, evidence bases behind each are set out on [[niacinamide]] and [[adenosine]].[^brand]

> **Bottom line.** No trial isolates this product. Its headline "bio-collagen" ingredient is declared at a trace 55,000 ppb (about 0.0055%) and sits near the bottom of a 34-item ingredient list, and independent literature says topical collagen's own size limits skin penetration regardless of dose. The more plausible near-term effect is ordinary hydrogel-mask hydration from humectants and occlusion, plus modest, ingredient-general evidence behind the niacinamide and adenosine also present.[^brand][^collagen]
