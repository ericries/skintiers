A radiofrequency console cleared for wrinkles whose file does something unusual and something very usual at once. **Bottom line:** it publishes exact power, frequency and fluence figures, which most of this category does not, and it contains no clinical data whatsoever. What Pollogen is and what else it holds is on [[pollogen]].

> **The numbers are there, the evidence is not.** The specification table gives the device 6.0 W maximal RF output power, 1 MHz frequency and 6.0 W/cm2 total power density, against 5.7 W and 5.7 W/cm2 for the predicate.[^k233766] Then, checked by string search of the whole extracted file, it contains no occurrence of clinical study, randomised, placebo, sham or subjects.[^k233766] Equivalence here is an engineering argument, which is what the 510(k) route asks for and is not evidence about skin.

## Summary

Two clearances, both Summaries, both under product code PAY: K233766 decided 2024-05-07 and K242227 decided 2025-10-12, applicant Pollogen, Ltd., General and Plastic Surgery panel.[^openfda] PAY is FDA's "Over-The-Counter Radiofrequency Coagulation Device For Wrinkle Reduction" code at 21 CFR 878.4420, class II, defined as "An electrosurgical device for over-the-counter aesthetic use is a device using radiofrequency energy to produce localized heating within tissues for non-invasive aesthetic use".[^pay]

**Only one of the two files could be read.** K233766's Summary is in FDA's document archive and was read in full. K242227's is not there yet, so for the 2025 clearance this page has the index record and nothing else.[^openfda]

**Two applicators, two very different claims.** The radiofrequency applicator "is intended for use in the non-invasive treatment of mild to moderate facial wrinkles for adult users who have Fitzpatrick Skin Types I-IV".[^k233766] The second applicator "is intended to provide massage by a mechanical vibration of an electrically powered applicator", and that is the whole of its cleared claim.[^k233766]

**It is a Special 510(k) on the firm's own device.** The single predicate is "Pollogen Ltd. STOP U Model UXV K220322", covered on [[pollogen-stop-u-tripollar]].[^k233766]

**No price.** A tabletop professional console with no consumer listing fetched.

## The Rubric

**Reducing mild to moderate facial wrinkles (cosmetic)**
Graded unknown on anecdotal evidence. There is no clinical data in the file to grade. The clearance establishes that this device is technologically similar to a device already on the market, which is a different question from whether either of them softens a wrinkle, as [[fda-approved-vs-cleared-skincare]] sets out.

**Whatever the OxyGeneo applicator is thought to do (cosmetic)**
Graded unknown on anecdotal evidence, and the cleared wording is the point. A vibrating massage applicator is cleared as a vibrating massage applicator. Any claim beyond that is outside what this filing supports.

**Anything for Fitzpatrick skin types V or VI (cosmetic)**
Graded unknown on anecdotal evidence. The indication names types I to IV and the file goes no further.

## The Evidence

**What the file does publish is worth crediting.** The comparison table runs the electrical parameters side by side: energy levels, maximal RF output power, frequency, total power density, pulse duration listed as continuous, waveform biphasic, wave shape sinusoid.[^k233766] Against the LED devices ranked on [[home-led-devices-by-what-the-file-publishes]], most of which publish no dose at all, this is a comparatively open filing.

**What it does not publish is the thing that would matter.** No study, no participants, no outcome measure. The route taken is a Special 510(k), which exists for modifications to a manufacturer's own cleared device and is not designed to generate new clinical evidence.[^k233766]

**Where the evidence actually rests.** On [[pollogen-stop-u-tripollar]], and on whatever that filing in turn rested on. [[home-rf-devices-by-whose-evidence-the-file-rests-on]] follows those chains for this category, which is the useful way to read a device whose own file contains no data.

## Manufacturer Claims

This section is quarantined and discounted; treat everything here as unproven unless it also appears in The Evidence above. No brand page was fetched, so these are the claims the filing itself makes.

- **Mild to moderate facial wrinkle treatment.** This is the cleared indication, not a demonstrated result. Nothing in the file tests it.[^k233766]
- **The OxyGeneo applicator.** Cleared for mechanical vibration massage only.[^k233766] Treat any oxygenation or exfoliation claim attached to the name as unsupported by this filing.
- **A higher power figure than the predicate.** 6.0 W against 5.7 W is a specification difference, not a demonstrated benefit, and the file offers no evidence that the higher figure does more.[^k233766]
