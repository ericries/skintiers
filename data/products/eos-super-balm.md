## Summary

A waxy, buttery lip balm sold in twenty flavours at $5.99 a tube, marketed on "24-hour moisture".[^brand] **Bottom line:** as a balm it is unremarkable in a good way, a real emollient and occlusive stack with [[petrolatum]] fifth on the list. The problem is the seventh ingredient. Flavour is the thing lip balms are most often blamed for when lips get worse instead of better, and this one is built entirely around it.

> **The free-of list omits the one thing worth being free of.** The brand page advertises the formula as "Free of:​ Parabens, Phthalates, Gluten, Silicones, Drying Alcohols, Synthetic Dyes".[^brand] Flavourant is not on that list, and it is seventh of 23 declared ingredients.[^brand] Meanwhile eos's own site copy for its lip balm range calls it "dermatologist recommended, hypoallergenic and made for sensitive skin".[^brand] Those two statements sit on the same page.

## The Rubric

- **Effect size: modest, as a balm.** The declared list opens with octyldodecanol, synthetic beeswax, bis-diglyceryl polyacyladipate-2 and castor oil, with [[petrolatum]] fifth, beeswax sixth, [[shea-butter]] ninth, then avocado oil and cocoa butter.[^brand] That is a conventional occlusive and emollient balm, and it will behave like one.
- **Evidence quality: anecdotal.** "A deeply conditioning lip treatment that delivers 24-hour moisture and soothing care" is the brand's headline claim.[^brand] No measurement, duration protocol or study is published alongside it on the page read for this profile, and nothing independent was found.
- **Effect size: unknown, for sensitive skin.** Hypoallergenic has no agreed regulatory definition, and the brand gives no protocol, sample or result for it, nor for "dermatologist recommended".[^brand] A formula can be free of parabens and silicones and still contain a common contact allergen, which this one does.
- **Composition note: there is no unflavoured version.** The brand's own product feed, pulled on 2026-11-01, returns 20 Super Balm entries. All 20 are named flavours: Cotton Candy Snow, Candy Cane Swirl, Strawberry Sorbet, Strawberry Limoncello, Raspberry Rosé, Coconut Milk, Birthday Cake, Raspberry Kiwi Splash, Watermelon Frosé and Honey Apple as single tubes, with the rest multipacks and minis of the same.[^feed] If flavour is the ingredient you react to, this line has nothing to offer you.

## The Evidence

There is no trial of this product. What there is instead is a well-known mechanism, and a dermatologist on record explaining why people miss it.

Board-certified dermatologist Andrea Suarez, reviewing this exact five-tube Costco pack, makes the point that "Flavorant is um lip balm speak for fragrance", quoted here with the machine transcript's disfluency intact, that allergy to it is easy to develop, and that the reaction is a delayed type hypersensitivity which intensifies with repeated exposure.[^drdray] That delay is the whole trap: the lips get worse a day or two after application, so the balm looks like the cure rather than the cause, and people reapply more. She is also fair to the brand about it, saying of the contact cheilitis issue that arose for eos years ago that "It's not a problem with them per se, it's a problem with all non-straight-up petroleum jelly lip balms".[^drdray] That framing is right: the flavour question belongs to the category, not to one brand.

None of that means this balm will give you cheilitis. It means that if your lips are getting worse on a flavoured balm, the balm is a candidate, and that the fix is a plain petrolatum product rather than another flavour. General lip evidence lives on [[lip-care]]; what [[contact-dermatitis]] is and how it is diagnosed lives there.

## Common Marketing Claims

> The claims in this section are quoted from eos's own site and are **not** endorsed here.

- "A deeply conditioning lip treatment that delivers 24-hour moisture and soothing care."[^brand] No measurement is published for the 24 hours.
- "Free of:​ Parabens, Phthalates, Gluten, Silicones, Drying Alcohols, Synthetic Dyes".[^brand] All verifiable from the list, and all true. Flavourant, seventh on that same list, is absent from the free-of set.
- "dermatologist recommended, hypoallergenic and made for sensitive skin".[^brand] Site copy for the lip balm range. No protocol, no named dermatologist, no result.
- "Shea Butter Melts into dry lips to smooth, comfort, and restore moisture" and "Avocado Oil Packed with vitamins to condition and soften while helping prevent flaking".[^brand] Ingredient-level storytelling; shea butter is ninth and avocado oil tenth.
