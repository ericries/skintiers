Paula's Choice Skin Perfecting 2% BHA Liquid Exfoliant is a leave-on liquid exfoliant built on 2% salicylic acid (a beta hydroxy acid), in a short, fragrance-free formula the manufacturer lists at pH 3.5.[^pc]

> A watery, leave-on salicylic acid exfoliant for clogged pores and mild breakouts. Salicylic acid is an over-the-counter acne active recognized by US drug regulation, and this is a plain, well-made 2% version at the low pH that lets it work, though the American Academy of Dermatology recommends salicylic acid only conditionally and ranks benzoyl peroxide and topical retinoids above it (see [[salicylic-acid]]). It is among the most-awarded exfoliants in beauty press, named best exfoliant in Allure's 2025 Readers' Choice Awards, and the manufacturer lists it at $37 for 4 fl oz.[^pc][^allure]

## Summary

Skin Perfecting 2% BHA Liquid Exfoliant is a watery, leave-on toner built around one active: 2% [[salicylic-acid|salicylic acid]], the oil-soluble beta hydroxy acid used in nonprescription acne products.[^pc] Its health-oriented job is to shed built-up dead skin and clear the sebum-filled pore, which is why salicylic acid appears in so many acne and blackhead products. Salicylic acid is a real, drug-monograph acne active, and a second-tier one: the 2024 American Academy of Dermatology acne guideline recommends it only conditionally and places benzoyl peroxide and topical retinoids ahead of it, and the controlled acne trials behind it are small and low-certainty. That category evidence, graded skeptically, is set out on [[salicylic-acid]].

The product itself is a faithful, minimal way to wear that active. The list is short (eight ingredients), fragrance-free and alcohol-free, and set by the manufacturer to pH 3.5, the acidic range in which salicylic acid penetrates skin ([[salicylic-acid]] covers why low pH matters).[^pc] What it is good at is what salicylic acid is good at, unclogging pores and smoothing rough texture; the brighter tone and softer-looking pores that reviewers describe are cosmetic effects. The manufacturer's directions say to follow it with sunscreen in the daytime, and note that anyone with a salicylate allergy should check with a physician before using salicylic acid products.[^pc]

It is one of the most decorated exfoliants in beauty press. Allure named it the winner in the exfoliant category of its 2025 Readers' Choice Awards.[^allure] Marie Claire gave it a 2025 Skin & Hair Award for best exfoliator.[^mc] It is mid-priced for its category: the manufacturer lists the 4 fl oz bottle at $37 ([see Where to Buy](#where-to-buy), accessed 2026-07-28).[^pc]

## The Evidence

Salicylic acid is a well-established nonprescription acne active. It sits on the US over-the-counter acne monograph at 0.5% to 2%, and in the one head-to-head trial a Cochrane review could pool it landed level with the prescription retinoid tretinoin, while dermatology guidelines place benzoyl peroxide and topical retinoids ahead of it; that evidence, with its limits, is on [[salicylic-acid]].[^pc] What is specific to this product is the delivery: it carries salicylic acid at the top monograph strength (2%) in a low-pH (3.5) leave-on vehicle, the concentration and acidity the ingredient page identifies as the conditions under which salicylic acid penetrates and exfoliates.[^pc]

Paula's Choice cites two of its own studies on the product page, on pore appearance and oil and on the skin barrier. Both are small, company-commissioned studies described in marketing terms rather than published trials, and are set out under [Common Marketing Claims](#common-marketing-claims).[^pc]

## What's In It

The full ingredient list as declared by the manufacturer.[^pc] The active is linked to its own page; the rest are the solvent, humectant, solubilizer and pH system.

Active:

- [[salicylic-acid|salicylic acid]] 2%, the oil-soluble beta hydroxy acid, at the top over-the-counter acne strength[^pc]

Other functional ingredient:

- green tea (Camellia oleifera) leaf extract, a plant antioxidant that contains epigallocatechin gallate (EGCG)[^pc]

Base, solvent and preservative system: water, methylpropanediol and butylene glycol (humectant solvents), polysorbate 20 (a solubilizer), sodium hydroxide (pH adjuster) and tetrasodium EDTA (a chelator).[^pc]

Two composition facts matter for this product. The list is unusually short, eight ingredients, with no fragrance, no drying alcohol, and no other actives such as [[niacinamide]] competing with the salicylic acid.[^pc] And it is acidic by design: the manufacturer states pH 3.5, and salicylic acid penetrates skin better at low pH, as covered on [[salicylic-acid]].[^pc]

## Common Marketing Claims

Label and marketing terms not established by the graded evidence above:

- **"Clinically proven to visibly reduce pores & control oil."** The manufacturer attributes this to "an independent clinical study with 32 subjects after 4 weeks."[^pc] It is a small, company-commissioned study, and pore appearance and oil are cosmetic endpoints.
- **"Clinically proven to improve skin barrier."** Attributed by the manufacturer to "an independent clinical study of 29 subjects."[^pc] A small company-commissioned study.
- **"Clinically proven to boost cell turnover for smooth, clear, glowing skin in as little as 1 week."** A manufacturer performance claim on cosmetic endpoints.[^pc]
- **"Gentle formula with soothing green tea supports skin's barrier."** A positioning claim for the green tea extract.[^pc]
