A silicone-based scar stick, $18 for 5.7 g.[^brand] **Bottom line:** the silicone is real and leads the ingredient list, but the evidence this product borrows from was built on gels and sheets, not sticks, and that gap is the whole question. The case for silicone is at [[silicone-gel-and-sheeting-for-scars]] and is not re-argued.

> **The format is not the format that was trialled.** Silicone earns its `mid` grade from gel and sheeting studies, where the material forms and then *stays* as a continuous occlusive layer, which is the proposed mechanism. This is a wax stick you rub on. Prequel says it "forms a protective barrier".[^brand] Whether a rubbed-on wax stick holds an unbroken film the way a sheet does is the question that decides whether the borrowed evidence applies, and nothing found here answers it.

## Summary

### What is in it

Dimethicone leads, then sunflower seed wax, caprylic/capric triglyceride, dimethicone crosspolymer, C10-18 triglycerides, shea butter, C13-15 alkane and beeswax.[^inci] Twenty-two ingredients in all. The silicone claim is consistent with the ordering: dimethicone is first, and two further silicones appear later.

Prequel states the formula is "60% silicone".[^brand] That figure is the brand's own and was not independently verified; no page here has measured it.

The actives it names after the silicone sit lower: the onion ferment at nine, then the three centella triterpenoids (asiaticoside, madecassic acid, asiatic acid) and bisabolol.[^inci]

## The Rubric

**The silicone is genuine, and genuinely first.** Unlike products that name an active and bury it, dimethicone opens this list. For an occlusive scar product that is the right shape for the claim.

**But the delivery format breaks the inheritance.** This is the one thing worth carrying away. The trials behind the `mid` grade, including [[wiseman-2020-silicone-gel-vs-pressure-garment-burn-scars-children-pmid-31565952]], used gels and sheets. A stick is more convenient, and convenience plausibly improves how often people actually use it, which matters for a treatment measured in months. It is still a different physical product from the one tested.

**The supporting cast is the weak part.** Onion bulb ferment and centella are each graded `weak` here on their own evidence ([[allium-cepa-onion-extract]], [[centella-asiatica]]). Three weak ingredients do not combine into a strong one, and no study of this particular combination was found.

**The pricing claim checks out, which is worth saying.** Prequel offers an 18 g Jumbo at $48 and says you "Get over 3x" the amount and "save 15%".[^brand] Both are accurate: 18 g is 3.16 times 5.7 g, and $2.67 per gram against $3.16 is a 15.6% saving. A small thing, but brands get this arithmetic wrong often enough that checking it is worthwhile.

## The Evidence

None specific to this product. No trial of the Scar Solutions Stick was sought or found, and Prequel cites none in the copy read for this page.[^brand] The grade above is inherited from the silicone class and is discounted for the format gap described above.

What would settle it is not another silicone trial but a simple one: does this stick leave a continuous occlusive film after normal use? That is a measurable property and nobody appears to have published it.

## Common Marketing Claims

Quarantined. Prequel describes it as "A targeted formula that reduces the look of red and healing scars, stretch marks and post blemish marks" and says the triterpenoids and bisabolol "improve the appearance of discoloration".[^brand] Note that these are appearance claims throughout, which is the correct register for a cosmetic and is not the same as changing the scar itself. The brand also positions the stick applicator as allowing "convenient, frequent massage", and massage is doing some unmeasured share of any result here.[^brand]

## Who it is for

Someone treating a small, reachable scar who will actually keep using it, and who understands they are buying the silicone principle in an untested format. For a scar worth treating properly, the gel and sheet formats are what the evidence was built on.
