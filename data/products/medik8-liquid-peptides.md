Medik8 Liquid Peptides is a 30ml leave-on facial serum from the British brand Medik8, formulated around a blend the brand describes as "10 powerful peptides in a 30% blend" and priced at £59.00 on the manufacturer's own site.[^medik8]

> A multi-peptide serum whose full ingredient list confirms at least ten distinct peptide or peptide-like ingredients, but whose brand claim to be "clinically proven" rests, by the brand's own footnote, on an independent consumer-perception study rather than an instrumental or vehicle-controlled clinical trial of this product.[^medik8] The evidence for topical peptides as an anti-aging category is thin and largely preliminary, [covered on [[peptides]]](#the-evidence); no published trial of this exact 30% multi-peptide blend was found.

## Summary

Medik8 Liquid Peptides is sold as a 30ml dropper-bottle serum for £59.00 on Medik8's own site, marketed as "a targeted daily peptide serum for the first signs of ageing."[^medik8] The brand's product description states it is "expertly formulated with 10 powerful peptides in a 30% blend, to help slow & prevent fine line formation" and references a "drone-targeted technology" meant to deliver the peptide blend "where they're needed most in the skin."[^medik8]

This is a cosmetic product, not a drug: none of its ingredients are FDA-regulated actives, and no cosmetic serum can legally claim to change skin structure, only to improve its visible appearance. The ingredient list, pulled from [incidecoder](https://incidecoder.com/products/medik8-liquid-peptides), lists water, glycerin, and butylene glycol first, followed immediately by a run of named peptides: Palmitoyl Tetrapeptide-7, Palmitoyl Tripeptide-1, Copper Heptapeptide-14 Pantothenate, Heptapeptide-15 Palmitate, Arginine/Lysine Polypeptide, Palmitoyl Tripeptide-38, Carnosine, Acetyl Hexapeptide-8, Pentapeptide-18, Palmitoyl Tripeptide-5, and Tetrapeptide-21, before moving into humectants (sodium hyaluronate, inulin, panthenol) and a standard preservative and texture system. That is at least ten distinct peptide-class ingredients placed at the front of the list, consistent with the brand's "10 powerful peptides" claim, though the ingredient list alone cannot confirm the brand's stated 30% total concentration for that blend.[^medik8]

Peptide anti-aging evidence at the category level, and for the specific peptide classes present here such as [[palmitoyl-tetrapeptide-7]] (a Matrixyl 3000 component) and [[argireline-acetyl-hexapeptide-8]] (an Acetyl Hexapeptide-8), is set out on [[peptides]] and those individual pages; in short, most trials are small, short, or funded by the ingredient's own manufacturer, and effects reported are modest at best. No trial specific to this serum's exact blend or its "drone-targeted technology" delivery claim was found.

## The Evidence

Whether topical peptides measurably improve fine lines is covered at the ingredient-class level on [[peptides]], with specific-ingredient detail on [[palmitoyl-tetrapeptide-7]] and [[argireline-acetyl-hexapeptide-8]]: in short, the trial base is preliminary, with small sample sizes and frequent industry funding. No published, peer-reviewed trial of this exact serum, or of Medik8's specific 10-peptide, 30% blend, was found.

> **Bottom line.** The case for this serum rests entirely on category-level peptide evidence and the brand's own unpublished consumer-perception study, not on a peer-reviewed trial of the product itself.[^medik8]

Medik8's own marketing describes the serum as "clinically proven to visibly smooth fine lines in just 7 days," but the asterisk on that claim in the brand's marketing imagery is attributed by the brand to an independent consumer study, meaning self-reported perception of results rather than an instrumental measurement against a vehicle control.[^medik8] The brand also displays a separate claim of "98% agree formula is instantly hydrating," which reads as a consumer-survey statistic rather than a clinical outcome.[^medik8]

## Common Marketing Claims

Label and marketing terms not established by the graded evidence above:
