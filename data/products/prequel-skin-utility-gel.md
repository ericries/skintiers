Prequel Skin Utility Gel is a fragrance-free, water-based skin-protectant gel built around 20 percent glycerin, made by Prequel, a skincare brand whose product page identifies its founder, Dr. Samantha Ellis, as a dermatologist.[^pdp]

> A humectant-led gel that labels glycerin as its Active Ingredient at 20 percent, the format and concentration used on over-the-counter skin-protectant labels, paired with dimethicone, panthenol, and niacinamide.[^pdp] The National Rosacea Society's own brand listing independently confirms it carries the Society's Seal of Acceptance, alongside six other Prequel products (see [Where to Buy](#where-to-buy), accessed 2026-08-07).[^pdp][^nrs]

## Summary

Skin Utility Gel is a lightweight, fragrance-free gel Prequel positions as a multi-purpose "skin protectant" for skin that is dry, cracked, chapped, chafed, or "recovering," to be applied "as needed."[^pdp] Its label follows an Active/Inactive Ingredient format, naming Glycerin 20.0% as the sole Active Ingredient, the same humectant-led approach behind ordinary glycerin-based moisturizers; that general barrier-support case is set out on [[glycerin]].

What sits alongside the glycerin is a short list of supporting ingredients rather than a distinct treatment active: [[dimethicone]] for a light occlusive finish, and a "Vitamin B Complex" of [[panthenol]], panthenyl triacetate, and [[niacinamide]] that Prequel's page says works "to hydrate, reduce skin irritation, and increase barrier recovery."[^pdp] The formula also lists naringenin, a citrus flavonoid Prequel calls a "bioactive soothing complex" and says is "clinically tested to reduce redness and dehydration," a manufacturer claim not corroborated by an independent source located for this profile and reported as marketing in [Common Marketing Claims](#common-marketing-claims).[^pdp]

Prequel lists the product among those carrying the National Rosacea Society's Seal of Acceptance, and the Society's own brand listing independently confirms Skin Utility Gel by name, alongside six other Prequel products.[^pdp][^nrs] The Seal reflects a screen against ingredients "known to be generally unsuitable for use by people with sensitive rosacea skin," not a test that the product treats rosacea or reduces its symptoms.[^nrs]

The gel is a companion to Prequel's Skin Utility Ointment: the brand's own FAQ describes the ointment as 45 percent petrolatum in an anhydrous format suited to treating "minor burns, scraps, and cuts," while the gel is water-based and "not intended for this."[^pdp] Prequel lists the 1.7 fl oz (50 mL) gel at $18.00 (see [Where to Buy](#where-to-buy), accessed 2026-08-07); no independently fetchable retailer listing for this exact SKU was located to corroborate that price.[^pdp]

## The Evidence

No published, independent trial of this exact gel was located. Its core mechanism rests on the general, well-evidenced case for glycerin as a humectant that supports barrier function and reduces transepidermal water loss, set out on [[glycerin]].

The product-specific fact behind its rosacea-related marketing is a seal, not a study: the National Rosacea Society's own brand listing for Prequel names "Skin Utility GEL Multi-Purpose Skin Protectant" among the products carrying its Seal of Acceptance, alongside Prequel's Redness Reform Sulfur Cleanser, AM/PM Modern Moisturizer, Redness Reform Soothing Serum, Gleanser, Sun Barrier Mineral Sunscreen, and Universal Skin Solution.[^nrs] The Society describes the Seal as confirming a product "does not contain ingredients that are known to be generally unsuitable for use by people with sensitive rosacea skin," a formulation screen, not a clinical test of the product's effect on rosacea.[^nrs]

Beyond glycerin, dimethicone, and the Vitamin B Complex ingredients, the formula's remaining distinguishing ingredient, naringenin, carries only Prequel's own unsourced claim of being "clinically tested to reduce redness and dehydration"; no independent trial or named testing body was located.[^pdp]

> **Bottom line.** The gel's National Rosacea Society Seal is independently confirmed and reflects that its ingredients cleared a screen for rosacea-safe formulation, not a demonstrated treatment effect. Its measurable, well-evidenced job is glycerin-led humectant barrier support.[^pdp][^nrs]

## What's In It

The full ingredient list as declared on the manufacturer's label, which follows an Active/Inactive Ingredient format.[^pdp]

Active Ingredient: [[glycerin|Glycerin]] 20.0%, labeled as the product's sole active.[^pdp]

Inactive, notable and functional ingredients:

- Water (Aqua), the base
- [[dimethicone]], a silicone that adds a light occlusive, non-greasy finish
- [[panthenol]] and panthenyl triacetate, part of what Prequel calls a "Vitamin B Complex"
- PEG/PPG-18/18 dimethicone, a silicone-based emollient
- Hydroxyethyl acrylate/sodium acryloyldimethyl taurate copolymer, a texture-building polymer
- [[niacinamide]], the other member of the "Vitamin B Complex"
- Naringenin, a citrus-derived flavonoid Prequel markets as a "bioactive soothing complex"

Base, texture, and preservative system: C15-23 alkane, 1,2-hexanediol, C13-14 alkane, phenoxyethanol, decyl glucoside, tetrasodium glutamate diacetate, citric acid, and sodium hydroxide.[^pdp]

The label states a pH of 4.25 (+/-) and declares no added fragrance.[^pdp]
