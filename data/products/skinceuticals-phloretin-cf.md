SkinCeuticals Phloretin CF is a morning antioxidant serum that combines vitamin C, ferulic acid, and phloretin, sold in a 1 oz bottle.[^1]

> A sibling to [[skinceuticals-c-e-ferulic|SkinCeuticals C E Ferulic]] that swaps in phloretin, a plant-derived antioxidant, alongside vitamin C and ferulic acid. Vitamin C is a well-evidenced antioxidant, and its general case is set out on [[ascorbic-acid-vitamin-c]]; ferulic acid's role as a stabilizer and photoprotection booster is documented for the brand's other, separately published 15% C / 1% E / 0.5% ferulic acid formula on [[ferulic-acid]], not for this exact combination. The retail listing available does not state this product's active concentrations, so how it compares to that confirmed formula is unresolved. It is listed at $159.56 (marked down from $216.55) for 1 oz on Target.[^1]

## Summary

Phloretin CF is positioned by its retail listing as "an advanced antioxidant serum formulated to help protect skin from environmental aggressors while improving the look of tone and clarity," built around a combination of vitamin C, ferulic acid, and phloretin, and marketed for "normal, combination, and oily skin types."[^1] The listing names ascorbic acid as the product's primary active ingredient.[^1]

What sets it apart from the brand's flagship [[skinceuticals-c-e-ferulic|C E Ferulic]] is the addition of phloretin in place of some of the vitamin E, and a different intended skin type (C E Ferulic is not skin-type-restricted on its own product page). The general antioxidant case for vitamin C, the active both formulas share, is well established and covered on [[ascorbic-acid-vitamin-c]]. Ferulic acid's documented stabilizing and photoprotection-boosting role, established in Lin 2005 and Murray 2008, was measured in the brand's other 15% L-ascorbic acid / 1% vitamin E / 0.5% ferulic acid formula, not in this one, so that specific benefit is not confirmed for Phloretin CF; see [[ferulic-acid]] for what those studies show. No product-specific study of this three-antioxidant combination was found ([see The Evidence](#the-evidence)). The listing's brightening, firmness, and discoloration claims are retailer marketing language, not clinical findings ([see Common Marketing Claims](#common-marketing-claims)). It is priced comparably to the brand's other flagship serums: $159.56 for 1 oz as listed on Target, marked down from $216.55 ([see Where to Buy](#where-to-buy), accessed 2026-08-07).[^1]

## The Evidence

Topical vitamin C is a well-studied antioxidant with characterized roles in defending skin against oxidative UV damage and supporting collagen; that general case is mapped on [[ascorbic-acid-vitamin-c]] and [[vitamin-c]]. Ferulic acid's best-documented skincare role, stabilizing and boosting the measured photoprotection of a vitamin C and vitamin E serum, is established for the brand's separate C, E, and ferulic acid formula and summarized on [[ferulic-acid]]. No study of Phloretin CF itself, or of this specific vitamin C, ferulic acid, and phloretin combination, was found among the sources available for this page.

## What's In It

The retail listing describes the formula as combining vitamin C, ferulic acid, and phloretin, and names ascorbic acid as the primary active ingredient; it does not state concentrations or a full ingredient declaration. The listing states the product is unscented.[^1]

- [[ascorbic-acid-vitamin-c|Vitamin C]] (ascorbic acid), the reference antioxidant form of vitamin C
- [[ferulic-acid|Ferulic acid]], a plant antioxidant used elsewhere in the brand's lineup to stabilize and boost vitamin C formulas
- [[phloretin|Phloretin]], a plant-derived (apple-peel) antioxidant compound
