Medicube's PDRN Pink Collagen Exosome Shot Serum is a Korean "pink collagen" serum named for three trendy actives, PDRN, exosomes, and collagen, that its own ingredient list places at trace concentrations, over a humectant-and-niacinamide base.[^inci]

> The name sells PDRN, exosomes, and collagen, but the declared ingredient list tells a different story: the salmon-DNA "PDRN" (Sodium DNA) and the exosomes are each listed at just 10 ppm (0.001%), near the bottom, with collagen extract also low.[^inci] Topical PDRN's evidence is already thin (see [[polydeoxyribonucleotide-pdrn]]), and at 0.001% there is no reason to expect the regeneration the marketing implies. The one active present at a plausibly useful level is niacinamide, fifth on the list. Judge it as a decent niacinamide-and-humectant hydrating serum wearing a much fancier name.[^inci]

## Summary

This is one of Medicube's PDRN "Pink Collagen" line, a category of K-beauty serums built on ingredients associated with in-clinic "salmon DNA" skin-booster injections. The declared ingredient order is the whole story: water, butylene glycol, dipropylene glycol, glycerin, then [[niacinamide]] in fifth position, followed by texture and preservative ingredients.[^inci] The actives the product is named and marketed for appear far down and at disclosed trace levels: Sodium DNA (the PDRN ingredient) at 10 ppm, Exosomes at 10 ppm, and Collagen Extract just after, with a small amount of [[retinol]] and a long tail of hyaluronic-acid forms and peptides below that.[^inci]

Because it is a rinse-on leave-on serum, the humectants and niacinamide near the top are the ingredients doing measurable cosmetic work; the marquee actives are present in the formula but at concentrations far below anything the "collagen regeneration" positioning would require.

## The Evidence

No independent, controlled trial of this serum was located; its case rests on its ingredient list and the general evidence for its actives, which lives on their own pages.

**Hydration and niacinamide conditioning.** The base is a standard, effective humectant system, and niacinamide's fifth-place position means it is likely present at a cosmetically meaningful level (the brand does not disclose the percentage). The general niacinamide evidence for barrier support and tone is set out on [[niacinamide]] and is not repeated here.[^inci]

**PDRN, exosomes, and collagen.** These are the named selling points and the weakest part of the case. The INCI declares the PDRN ingredient as "Sodium DNA (10 ppm)" and "Exosomes (10 ppm)", i.e. 0.001% each, with a low collagen extract.[^inci] Topical PDRN is graded weak on [[polydeoxyribonucleotide-pdrn]] even at fuller inclusion, so a 0.001% dose provides no basis for a regeneration or firming claim; topical "exosomes" and "collagen extract" at similar traces are likewise cosmetic-conditioning inclusions, not actives at a treatment dose.

> **Bottom line.** A pleasant niacinamide-and-humectant hydrating serum sold on PDRN, exosome, and collagen names that its own label puts at 0.001%.[^inci] If you want those marquee actives to actually do something, this is not the vehicle; if you want a niacinamide hydrator, a plainer, cheaper [[niacinamide]] serum delivers the same working ingredient without the fairy-dusted headliners.

## What's In It

Declared ingredient list, in order, from the product's INCI: Water, Butylene Glycol, Dipropylene Glycol, Glycerin, Niacinamide, Sodium Polyacrylate, Calcium Aluminum Borosilicate, Hydrolyzed Sponge, Caprylyl Glycol, Cetearyl Olivate, Phenyl Trimethicone, Sorbitan Olivate, Glyceryl Acrylate/Acrylic Acid Copolymer, Ethylhexylglycerin, Adenosine, Disodium EDTA, PVM/MA Copolymer, Panthenol, Lactobacillus Extracellular Vesicles (50 ppm), Cyanocobalamin, Sodium DNA (10 ppm), Milk, Exosomes (10 ppm), Collagen Extract, Macadamia Ternifolia Seed Oil, Caprylic/Capric Triglyceride, Alcohol, Hydrogenated Lecithin, Polysorbate 20, Retinol, and a long tail of hyaluronic-acid forms and peptides.[^inci] The ppm figures in parentheses are the manufacturer's own declared inclusion levels for those actives.[^inci]

## Common Marketing Claims

- **"PDRN," "collagen," and "exosome" regeneration and firming.** The declared levels are 10 ppm (0.001%) Sodium DNA, 10 ppm Exosomes, and a low collagen extract, far below any treatment dose; topical PDRN's own evidence is weak (see [[polydeoxyribonucleotide-pdrn]]).
