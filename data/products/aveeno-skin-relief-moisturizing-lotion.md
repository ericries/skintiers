Aveeno Skin Relief Moisturizing Lotion (Fragrance-Free) is an over-the-counter skin-protectant moisturizer whose Drug Facts active is dimethicone 1.2%, in an oat-containing emollient base.[^1]

> An OTC skin-protectant drug, but a general one: the Drug Facts active is "Dimethicone 1.2%," and the labeled use is to "help prevent and temporarily protect chafed, chapped, or cracked skin" and guard against wind and cold, not to treat eczema.[^1] Despite the oat-containing base Aveeno is known for, dimethicone is the regulated active here, so judge it as a solid barrier-protecting daily moisturizer.

## Summary

This is Aveeno's fragrance-free daily body lotion. It is regulated as an OTC drug, and the regulated active is a skin protectant, not an antifungal or anti-itch drug: the label states the active as "dimethicone 1.2%," with the use of helping prevent and temporarily protect chafed, chapped, or cracked skin and protecting from the drying effects of wind and cold weather.[^1]

That places it as a barrier-protecting daily moisturizer rather than a treatment product. The oat (Avena sativa) flour, oil, and extract in the base are what give it the Aveeno feel, but colloidal oatmeal is not listed as the Drug Facts active; dimethicone is. For eczema-specific colloidal-oatmeal products, see [[eucerin-eczema-relief-cream]] and [[aveeno-eczema-therapy-itch-relief-balm]].

## The Evidence

The defensible facts are the active (dimethicone 1.2%, an FDA skin-protectant monograph active) and the oat-containing emollient base.[^1] Dimethicone is an occlusive silicone that reduces water loss and protects chapped skin; that is a genuine, modest moisturizer-and-barrier benefit. The label makes no eczema-treatment claim, and none is supported for this product; its value is that of a good fragrance-free daily lotion.

> **Bottom line.** A fragrance-free dimethicone skin-protectant lotion, a reliable daily moisturizer for dry, chapped skin.[^1] It is barrier protection, not eczema therapy; for colloidal-oatmeal eczema products see [[atopic-dermatitis]] and the siblings above.

## What's In It

Active ingredient: "Dimethicone 1.2%."[^1] Inactive ingredients, from the label: water, glycerin, distearyldimonium chloride, petrolatum, isopropyl palmitate, cetyl alcohol, oat (Avena sativa) kernel flour, benzyl alcohol, oat kernel oil, steareth-20, sodium chloride, shea butter extract, and more oat fractions, among others.[^1] The base pairs humectants and occlusives with Aveeno's characteristic oat ingredients, and it is fragrance-free.

## Common Marketing Claims

- **"Skin relief" / soothing for dry skin.** Consistent with the dimethicone skin-protectant active and emollient base; a moisturizer benefit.[^1]
- **Implied eczema/oatmeal positioning.** The Drug Facts active is dimethicone, not colloidal oatmeal, and the label makes no eczema-treatment claim; for that, see the colloidal-oatmeal products and [[atopic-dermatitis]].[^1]
