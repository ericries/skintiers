TNS Advanced+ Serum is [[skinmedica|SkinMedica]]'s flagship anti-aging serum, built around Human Fibroblast Conditioned Media, a growth-factor-bearing extract from cultured human fibroblasts, marketed under the retailer-quoted description "powered by TNS-MR and Renessensce (RSC) Advanced."[^1]

> Topical growth factors are a category with real evidence in wound healing but a thin, mostly uncontrolled or industry-linked evidence base for cosmetic anti-aging on intact skin, and the growth-factor proteins involved are large enough that how much actually gets through the skin barrier is an open question; see [[epidermal-growth-factor]].

## Summary

TNS Advanced+ Serum is sold as a physician-dispensed product through [[skinmedica|SkinMedica]], the brand whose whole "TNS" (Tissue Nutrient Solution) line is built around growth-factor extracts. This serum's ingredient list is led, after water, by Human Fibroblast Conditioned Media, with a smaller peptide component (dimer tripeptide-43) and plant-extract additions described in a retailer listing as its "Renessensce (RSC) Advanced" blend.[^1] It is a premium product: a 1 fl oz (30 mL) bottle lists for $295 at Dermstore.[^1]

Whether topical growth factors do anything meaningful on intact facial skin has a real but limited evidence base, covered in full on [[epidermal-growth-factor]]: controlled-trial support exists for a single growth factor (EGF) applied to open wounds, but the cosmetic-serum literature on intact skin is thin, mostly uncontrolled, and often industry-funded, and growth-factor proteins are far larger than the size range compounds generally need to be to cross intact skin on their own ([the evidence](#the-evidence)). No independent, product-specific clinical trial of TNS Advanced+ Serum itself was found in this review; the product is graded on that category evidence, conservatively, since its central active ingredient class has not been independently validated for this exact use ([the evidence](#the-evidence)).

Composition-wise, the formula's actives are Human Fibroblast Conditioned Media and a small blend of peptides and plant extracts; it contains no retinoid, no vitamin C, and no exfoliating acid ([what's in it](#whats-in-it)).

## The Evidence

Whether growth factors applied to skin do anything, and how strong that evidence is, is covered on [[epidermal-growth-factor]]: real controlled-trial evidence exists for topical EGF gel healing open wounds, while the evidence for growth-factor cosmetic serums on intact skin is thin, largely uncontrolled or industry-linked, and complicated by the size of growth-factor proteins relative to what is generally thought to cross intact skin.

No independently published, product-specific trial of TNS Advanced+ Serum, its exact Human Fibroblast Conditioned Media formulation, or its RSC Advanced peptide blend was found in this review. The serum is graded on the category evidence for topical growth factors above, rather than on any product-specific data, which is why the frontmatter grades the effect minimal and the evidence anecdotal.

## What's In It

The full ingredient list (INCI) as it appears on a retail listing of this exact SKU.[^1]

Growth-factor and peptide actives:

- Human Fibroblast Conditioned Media, the growth-factor-bearing extract the "TNS" line is built around; see [[epidermal-growth-factor]]
- Dimer Tripeptide-43, a signal peptide; see [[peptides]]
- Whey Protein, Chlorella Vulgaris Extract, Vitis Vinifera (Grape) Flower Cell Extract, and Pyrus Malus (Apple) Fruit Extract, additional protein and plant-extract ingredients in the blend

Humectants and texture agents:

- [[glycerin]], Glycereth-7 Triacetate, Propanediol, Butylene Glycol
- Saccharide Isomerate, Pullulan, Sclerotium Gum, Xanthan Gum, Polyacrylate Crosspolymer-6, Polyacrylate-13 (thickeners and film-formers)
- Shea Butter Ethyl Esters, PEG-12 Dimethicone, Lecithin, Polyisobutene (emollients)

Base and preservative system: Aqua/Water/Eau, Pentylene Glycol, Ethoxydiglycol, Lactococcus Ferment Lysate, Hydroxyacetophenone, Tocopheryl Acetate, Sodium Citrate, Polysorbate 20, Aminomethyl Propanol, Disodium EDTA, Phenoxyethanol, Ethylhexylglycerin.[^1]

The formula contains no retinoid, no vitamin C, and no exfoliating acid.
