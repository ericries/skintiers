Youth To The People Superfood Antioxidant Cleanser is an 8 oz gel face wash from [[youth-to-the-people]] built around kale, spinach, and green tea plant extracts, sold by Target at $33.20 as of 2026-08-07 (15% off a $39.00 list price).[^target]

> Target's listing describes the product as "a gentle, gel-based face wash powered by plant extracts like kale, spinach, and green tea to remove daily buildup without stripping skin."[^target] It is a rinse-off cleanser: whatever antioxidant benefit those plant extracts might otherwise offer depends on skin contact time, and a gel that is washed off shortly after application does not get the sustained leave-on exposure that antioxidant formulations are typically built for. The better-evidenced way to support skin health day to day is leave-on moisturizing, covered on [[moisturizing]], not this cleanser.
