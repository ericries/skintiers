An acne toner from the witch hazel brand, in which witch hazel is not the drug. **Bottom line:** the FDA label names salicylic acid 2% as the active and acne treatment as its purpose; witch hazel is listed among the inactive ingredients. That is not a trick, and the bottle says "WITH 2% SALICYLIC ACID" on the front, but it does mean the evidence for this product is the evidence for [[salicylic-acid]] rather than for [[witch-hazel]].

> **Not one of Thayers' seven drug products uses witch hazel as an active.** The FDA drug listing directory returns seven Thayers records. Six declare salicylic acid, at 2% or 1.5%, and the seventh declares sulfur at 10%.[^ndc] Witch hazel is a recognised over-the-counter astringent active in the United States, and this brand, whose name is synonymous with it, does not file it as one anywhere.

## Summary

The label lists the active as "Salicylic acid 2%" and the purpose as "Acne treatment".[^label] Its stated uses are "for the treatment of acne", "clears acne blemishes and allows skin to heal" and "helps prevent the development of new acne blemishes".[^label]

Witch hazel appears second on the inactive list, which reads in full: "water, hamamelis virginiana (witch hazel), bark/leaf/twig extract, aloe barbadensis leaf extract, sodium borate, melalecua alternifolia (tea tree) leaf oil, cymbopogon flexuosus oil, glycerin, decyl glucoside, cocamidopropyl betaine, citric acid, caprylyl glycol, phenoxyethanol, ethylhexylglycerin", reproducing the label's own misspelling of Melaleuca.[^label]

The bottle photographed on this page is the Lemon variant at 12 fl oz (355 mL), read off that image. **No price is given on this page:** the brand's own product URLs returned 404 on 2026-11-16 and no primary source for a current price was found.

## The Rubric

- **Effect size: modest, and it belongs to the salicylic acid.** 2% is the standard monograph strength for an acne treatment. The evidence is at [[salicylic-acid]] and is not restated here.
- **Record finding: the named ingredient is the inactive one.** Witch hazel is on the inactive list.[^label] The site grades [[witch-hazel]] at tier weak, and that page records that its own recognised over-the-counter indication is not acne. So the ingredient in the product's name is neither the drug here nor evidenced for the condition this product treats.
- **The front of the bottle is honest about it.** "BLEMISH CLEARING TONER" and "WITCH HAZEL" are the largest type, with "WITH 2% SALICYLIC ACID" printed below in smaller letters, as the product photograph on this page shows. The hierarchy favours the brand story over the active, which is ordinary marketing rather than concealment.
- **Two more botanicals, also inactive.** Tea tree oil and lemongrass oil are on the same inactive list.[^label] [[tea-tree-oil]] is graded weak here on its own evidence, and neither is doing regulated work in this bottle.
- **This is not the famous one.** The toner most people mean when they say Thayers witch hazel is the rose petal cosmetic at [[thayers-rose-petal-facial-toner]], which makes no drug claim and is not in the drug directory at all. They share a brand and a hero ingredient and are regulated completely differently.

## The Evidence

None specific to this product. [[salicylic-acid]] carries the acne evidence, [[witch-hazel]] and [[tea-tree-oil]] their own, and no trial of this formulation is cited on the label or found here.

> **Bottom line.** A competent salicylic acid toner, at the 2% the label declares, wearing a witch hazel name.[^label] Judge it as the former.

## Common Marketing Claims

> Quoted from the FDA label and the bottle, and **not** endorsed here.

- "clears acne blemishes and allows skin to heal"[^label] Monograph wording for a 2% salicylic acid product, not a claim specific to this formula.
- "helps prevent the development of new acne blemishes"[^label] The same; the evidence belongs to the active, at [[salicylic-acid]].
- "WITCH HAZEL" and "ALOE VERA FORMULA" on the front of the bottle, read from the product photograph on this page. Both are inactive ingredients on the label beneath.[^label]
