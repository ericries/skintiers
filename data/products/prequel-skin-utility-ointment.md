Prequel Skin Utility Ointment is a fragrance-free skin-protectant ointment that lists Petrolatum 45% as its sole Active Ingredient, made by Prequel, a skincare brand whose product page identifies its founder, Dr. Samantha Ellis, as a dermatologist.[^pdp]

> A petrolatum-led ointment labeling Petrolatum 45% as its Active Ingredient, the same drug-facts format used on plain over-the-counter skin-protectant products, built on an oil-and-wax base with no water in the declared ingredient list.[^pdp] Petrolatum's occlusive case is well established and covered on [[petrolatum]]; this profile found no independent trial of this exact formula, and no independent retailer page corroborating the manufacturer's declared inactive-ingredient list (see [What's In It](#whats-in-it)).[^pdp][^target]

## Summary

Skin Utility Ointment is a fragrance-free ointment Prequel markets as a "multi-purpose skin protectant" that "moisturizes and helps restore the skin barrier while protecting dry, chapped, cracked, and irritated skin," to be applied "as needed."[^pdp] Its label follows the same Active/Inactive Ingredient format as the companion [[prequel-skin-utility-gel|Skin Utility Gel]], but names a different active: Petrolatum 45%, rather than the Gel's Glycerin 20.0%. Petrolatum is the reference occlusive against which other moisturizing agents are measured; that general barrier-support case is set out on [[petrolatum]].

The ingredient list built around that petrolatum base is entirely oil-, wax-, and emollient-based, with no water (Aqua) declared, unlike the water-based Gel.[^pdp] Alongside the petrolatum sit [[dimethicone]] for a silicone finish, [[tocopherol]] (vitamin E) as an antioxidant, and several plant-derived extracts and oils (rice bran, sunflower, rosemary leaf, rapeseed-derived sterols and oil copolymer) that Prequel does not attach a specific efficacy claim to on its product page.[^pdp]

Prequel's product page displays seals for the National Rosacea Society, National Eczema Association, and National Psoriasis Foundation, a manufacturer claim reported in [Common Marketing Claims](#common-marketing-claims); this profile did not independently verify those seals against the issuing organizations' own listings.[^pdp] Prequel lists the 2 oz (56.6 g) ointment at $18.00; Target's listing for the same product separately shows a regular price of $17.99, marked down to $15.29 as a clearance item, corroborating the size and approximate price without matching it exactly (see [Where to Buy](#where-to-buy), accessed 2026-08-07).[^pdp][^target]

## The Evidence

No published, independent trial of this exact ointment was located. Its core mechanism rests on the general, well-evidenced case for petrolatum as the reference occlusive that slows transepidermal water loss, set out on [[petrolatum]], and on the category-level evidence that occlusive moisturizers help dry, chapped, or cracked skin, set out on [[moisturizing]].

Beyond the 45% petrolatum, the remaining distinguishing ingredients (bisabolol, tocopherol, and the rice bran, sunflower, and rosemary extracts) carry no product-specific efficacy claim or study on Prequel's page; the page attaches no percentage or clinical claim to any of them.[^pdp]

> **Bottom line.** No study has isolated this exact ointment's own effect. Its occlusive case rests on petrolatum's well-established mechanism at a labeled 45% concentration, covered on [[petrolatum]] and [[moisturizing]].[^pdp]

## What's In It

The full ingredient list as declared on the manufacturer's label, which follows an Active/Inactive Ingredient format.[^pdp] No independent retailer ingredient listing for this exact SKU was located to corroborate it; Target's page for the product did not display an ingredient list.[^target]

Active Ingredient: [[petrolatum|Petrolatum]] 45%, labeled as the product's sole active.[^pdp]

Inactive, notable and functional ingredients:

- Paraffinum Liquidum (mineral oil), an occlusive oil
- Ozokerite, a mineral wax that firms the ointment's texture
- [[dimethicone]], a silicone that adds a lighter, less tacky finish
- Brassica Campestris/Aleurites Fordi Oil Copolymer, a plant-oil-derived emollient
- Bisabolol, a chamomile-derived compound
- Cetylhydroxyproline Palmitamide, a lipoamino acid additive
- Oryza Sativa (Rice) Bran Extract
- Helianthus Annuus (Sunflower) Extract
- [[tocopherol]] (vitamin E), an antioxidant
- Rosmarinus Officinalis (Rosemary) Leaf Extract
- Brassica Campestris (Rapeseed) Sterols

Base, texture, and preservative system: Stearic Acid, 1,2-Hexanediol, and Hexyldecanol.[^pdp]

The label declares no added fragrance.[^pdp]
