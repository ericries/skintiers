COSRX Advanced Snail 96 Mucin Power Essence is a South Korean leave-on essence whose ingredient list opens with snail secretion filtrate, which COSRX states makes up 96.3 percent of the formula, sold in 30 mL, 50 mL and 100 mL sizes.[^1]

> A snail-mucin essence built around a labeled 96.3% snail secretion filtrate, from the same brand as the [[cosrx-6-peptide-skin-booster-serum|6 Peptide Skin Booster Serum]]. The marketed hydration and "glass skin" glow benefits rest on thin, largely anecdotal evidence: the controlled human research on snail secretion filtrate tests a different, manufacturer-supplied extract, not a high-percentage essence like this one.[^1] Sodium hyaluronate and panthenol, listed further down the ingredient list, add smaller but better-evidenced hydration.[^1]

## Summary

COSRX Advanced Snail 96 Mucin Power Essence is a thin, leave-on essence that COSRX markets for "deep hydration, improved texture without irritation and 'Glass Skin' like glow," describing it as designed "for all skin types, especially sensitive or post-acne skin."[^1] What distinguishes it from a plainer hydrating toner is concentration: snail secretion filtrate is first on the ingredient list, and COSRX states the essence is "96.3 percent snail secretion filtrate."[^1] It is sold on the brand's own site in 30 mL, 50 mL and 100 mL sizes; at Target, the 30 mL (1.01 fl oz) size lists for $8.50 ([see Where to Buy](#where-to-buy), accessed 2026-08-07).[^1][^2]

Snail secretion filtrate is a well-known K-beauty ingredient, but [most of the controlled human research on it tests a different material](#the-evidence): a patented extract from a different preparation than the high-percentage essence format sold here.[^1] Because of that mismatch, the hydration and glow benefits marketed for this essence sit on thin, largely anecdotal evidence; the general evidence for the ingredient, including where it is better supported, is on [[snail-secretion-filtrate|snail secretion filtrate]]. The essence's base also includes sodium hyaluronate and panthenol, humectants with solid, well-established category-level hydration evidence even though both sit well down the ingredient list behind the snail filtrate and the preservative system; more on that evidence is on [[moisturizing]].[^1]

The brand's own testing and marketing claims are quarantined under [Common Marketing Claims](#common-marketing-claims).

## The Evidence

No published trial of a high-percentage snail-mucin essence, of the kind sold here, is cited on [[snail-secretion-filtrate|snail secretion filtrate]]; the controlled human research collected there instead tests a different, patented Cryptomphalus aspersa extract supplied by its manufacturer, in a small split-face wrinkle trial and a small radiation-dermatitis pilot, both cosmetic-or-narrow findings rather than evidence for a 96%-mucin essence like this one. Sodium hyaluronate and panthenol, both declared on this essence's label, are established humectants with solid category-level hydration evidence; see [[hyaluronic-acid|hyaluronic acid]] and [[moisturizing]].[^1]

## What's In It

The full ingredient list (INCI) as declared by the manufacturer, in order.[^1] The notable/functional ingredients are linked to their own pages; the rest is the base, texture and preservative system.

- Snail Secretion Filtrate ([[snail-secretion-filtrate|snail mucin]]), first on the list, at a labeled 96.3%
- Sodium Hyaluronate ([[hyaluronic-acid|hyaluronic acid]]), a humectant
- Panthenol, a humectant and skin-conditioning agent ([[panthenol]])
- Allantoin and Arginine, additional soothing and moisturizing-factor components

Base, texture and preservative system: Betaine, Butylene Glycol and 1,2-Hexanediol (solvents/humectant-solvents), Sodium Polyacrylate and Carbomer (thickeners), Phenoxyethanol and Ethyl Hexanediol (preservatives), and Aqua/Water.[^1]

The ingredient list declares no added fragrance.[^1]

## Common Marketing Claims

Label and marketing claims not established by the product-specific evidence above:

- **"Deep hydration, improved texture without irritation and 'Glass Skin' like glow,"** marketed as a **"K-Beauty Favorite Essence for Glass Skin."** Positioning language; the controlled human evidence for snail secretion filtrate tests a different extract than this essence, see [[snail-secretion-filtrate|snail secretion filtrate]] and [The Evidence](#the-evidence).[^1]
- **"+35% boost in natural glow and radiance, -80% reduction in visible redness,"** attributed to testing by "Dermacosmetic Skin Science Laboratory in Korea from May 26 to June 10, 2025, on 20 adult participants."[^1] A single small brand-run test on this product, with no independently published protocol or results.
- **"Clinically tested 96% snail mucin essence proven ... to enhance glow, reduce redness, and deeply hydrate all skin types."** Refers to the same brand-run 20-participant test above, not an independently reviewed trial.[^1]
