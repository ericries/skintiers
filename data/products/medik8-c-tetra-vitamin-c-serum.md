Medik8 C-Tetra is a 30 ml leave-on serum, listed by the manufacturer at £45, built around a disclosed 7% tetrahexyldecyl ascorbate (THDA), a lipid-soluble ester of vitamin C, rather than the reference form, L-ascorbic acid, used in serums such as SkinCeuticals C E Ferulic.[^1]

> C-Tetra is a single-active vitamin C serum built on tetrahexyldecyl ascorbate, a lipid-soluble vitamin C ester, dosed at a disclosed 7%.[^1] The antioxidant and collagen-cofactor case for topical vitamin C rests almost entirely on L-ascorbic acid, covered on [[ascorbic-acid-vitamin-c]], and the derivative literature mapped on [[vitamin-c]] reports that conversion of lipid-soluble esters like this one to that active form is in doubt. A disclosed 7% of THDA is therefore not evidence of the same strength as a disclosed 7% of L-ascorbic acid, and no independent or published trial of this product exists.

## Summary

C-Tetra is a morning-use antioxidant serum built around [[tetrahexyldecyl-ascorbate|tetrahexyldecyl ascorbate (THDA)]], a lipid-soluble derivative of vitamin C rather than the reference form, [[ascorbic-acid-vitamin-c|L-ascorbic acid]], that most of the controlled human vitamin C research is built on. Medik8 discloses the concentration plainly on its own product page, "7% Vitamin C," and names the form as tetrahexyldecyl ascorbate on the full ingredient list.[^1] That kind of percentage disclosure is not universal among vitamin C serums, but the form matters as much as the number: the wider family of vitamin C derivatives and how each is evidenced is mapped on [[vitamin-c]], which reports that lipid-soluble esters like THDA show increased uptake in animal studies but carry doubt over how efficiently they convert to active ascorbic acid in skin.

The formula itself is oil-based rather than the aqueous, low-pH base L-ascorbic acid needs to penetrate skin: the full ingredient list leads with jojoba seed oil and two silicones (cyclopentasiloxane, cyclohexasiloxane) ahead of the THDA, followed by grapefruit peel oil, tocopheryl acetate ([[tocopherol|vitamin E]]), a stabilizing polymer, and four recognized fragrance-allergen constituents of the citrus oil (limonene, citral, linalool, geraniol).[^1] Medik8's own directions describe morning application: "After cleansing in the morning, massage 4 drops across the face, neck and décolletage. Leave to absorb before applying sunscreen."[^1]

No published clinical trial of this product exists. The manufacturer attributes its "7 days to visibly brighter smoother skin" and "4 weeks to visibly reduced wrinkles & dark spots" claims to its own consumer-testing panel, not to a cited or peer-reviewed study; [those claims are quarantined below](#common-marketing-claims). The general antioxidant case for topical vitamin C, and how it differs by form, [is set out on the ingredient pages linked above](#the-evidence).

## The Evidence

Topical L-ascorbic acid is a well-studied antioxidant with a modest, adjunct role in UV protection alongside sunscreen and a modest role supporting collagen synthesis; the full case, by use, is set out on [[ascorbic-acid-vitamin-c]]. That evidence base is built on the reference, unmodified form of vitamin C. THDA, the ester C-Tetra uses instead, sits among the derivatives [[vitamin-c]] describes as built to solve L-ascorbic acid's instability at the cost of needing to convert back to it in skin; for the lipid-soluble esters, that conversion is reported as being in doubt rather than established.

No published study has tested C-Tetra itself, and no published human trial isolates THDA's effect the way the trials behind L-ascorbic acid do.

> **Bottom line.** C-Tetra discloses its vitamin C concentration, 7% THDA, more plainly than many derivative-based serums, but THDA is not the form the controlled human vitamin C evidence was built on, and whether it converts efficiently to active ascorbic acid in skin remains unresolved. No trial of this product has been published.[^1]

## What's In It

The full ingredient list as disclosed by the manufacturer, in INCI order.[^1]

Notable/functional ingredients:

- [[tetrahexyldecyl-ascorbate|Tetrahexyldecyl ascorbate]] (THDA) 7%, a lipid-soluble vitamin C ester, the product's sole vitamin C form[^1]
- [[tocopherol|Tocopheryl acetate]] (vitamin E), a supporting antioxidant[^1]
- Citrus grandis (grapefruit) peel oil, an essential oil[^1]

Base, texture, and preservative-system ingredients, per the same listing: simmondsia chinensis (jojoba) seed oil, cyclopentasiloxane, cyclohexasiloxane, PPG-12/SMDI copolymer (a stabilizing polymer), and four fragrance-allergen constituents of the citrus oil, limonene, citral, linalool, and geraniol.[^1]

The formula contains no separate added fragrance ingredient beyond the citrus peel oil, and no ferulic acid.
