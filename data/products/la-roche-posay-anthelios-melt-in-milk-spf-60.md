La Roche-Posay Anthelios Melt-in Milk is a fragrance-free chemical sunscreen lotion for face and body, rated Broad Spectrum SPF 60 and water resistant for 80 minutes, with the UV filters avobenzone 3%, homosalate 10%, octisalate 5%, and octocrylene 7%.[^1]

> Regular use of a broad-spectrum sunscreen has randomized-trial support for slowing photoaging and reducing some skin cancers (see [[sunscreen-uv-filters]]). This product carries an FDA Broad Spectrum SPF 60, water-resistant-80-minute rating, a high SPF among broad-spectrum sunscreens.[^1]

## The Evidence

Whether regular sunscreen use works, and how, is covered on [[sunscreen-uv-filters]]. The randomized evidence there is for the practice of applying a broad-spectrum sunscreen. What is specific to this product is its FDA-tested label rating.

The Drug Facts panel lists the product as "Broad Spectrum SPF 60" and "water resistant (80 minutes)," with the active filters avobenzone 3% (a UVA filter) and homosalate 10%, octisalate 5%, and octocrylene 7% (UVB filters).[^1]

> **Bottom line.** In the standardized testing behind its label, the product rates Broad Spectrum SPF 60 and water resistant for 80 minutes.[^1] How much additional UVB a higher SPF blocks, and how real-world under-application lowers the SPF actually delivered, are covered on [[sunscreen-uv-filters]].

## What's In It

The active filters and inactive ingredients as declared on the manufacturer's FDA Drug Facts label.[^1] Ingredients with a functional role are linked to their own pages; the rest are the base, texture, and preservative system.

UV filters (Drug Facts actives):

- [[avobenzone]] 3%, a UVA filter
- [[homosalate]] 10%, [[octisalate]] 5%, and [[octocrylene]] 7%, UVB filters (octocrylene also helps photostabilize the avobenzone)

Other functional ingredients:

- [[glycerin]], a humectant
- [[dimethicone]], caprylyl methicone, trisiloxane, and polymethylsilsesquioxane, silicones that give the fast-absorbing feel
- [[tocopherol]] (vitamin E), diethylhexyl syringylidenemalonate, and senna alata (cassia alata) leaf extract, the antioxidants marketed as "Cell-Ox Shield"

Base, texture, and preservative system: water, styrene/acrylates copolymer, butyloctyl salicylate, alcohol denat., poly C10-30 alkyl acrylate, acrylates/dimethicone copolymer, PEG-100 stearate and glyceryl stearate (emulsifiers), potassium cetyl phosphate and inulin lauryl carbamate (emulsifiers), propylene glycol, PEG-8 laurate, acrylates/C10-30 alkyl acrylate crosspolymer and xanthan gum (thickeners), triethanolamine (pH adjuster), caprylic/capric triglyceride (emollient), phenoxyethanol, caprylyl glycol and p-anisic acid (preservatives), disodium EDTA (chelator), and maltodextrin.[^1]

Not in the formula: no oxybenzone and no octinoxate (two organic filters the label markets itself as free of), and no added fragrance.[^1]

## Common Marketing Claims

Label and marketing terms not established by the graded evidence above:

- **"Cell-Ox Shield" / "with antioxidants."** A formulation and marketing name for the tocopherol, diethylhexyl syringylidenemalonate, and senna alata extract in the base.[^1] The FDA-rated benefit on the label is the Broad Spectrum SPF 60 UV protection.[^1]
- **"Melt-in Milk," "velvety finish," "fast absorbing."** Texture and positioning claims. The texture assessment cited here is the Forbes skin-expert endorsement listed under Recommended In.
- **"Tested on sensitive skin," "oil-free," "non-comedogenic."** Manufacturer label claims, not independently tested outcomes.
