COSRX AHA/BHA Clarifying Treatment Toner is a leave-on toner from [[cosrx|COSRX]] built on a short, 11-ingredient formula that pairs glycolic acid with betaine salicylate, a salicylic-acid derivative.[^1][^2]

> An 11-ingredient toner combining an AHA (glycolic acid) and a BHA-type salicylate ester (betaine salicylate), with neither acid's concentration disclosed by the brand or Target.[^1][^2] Both actives sit in the lower half of the ingredient list, after six other listed ingredients, a lower position than the 2% salicylic acid disclosed on a dedicated leave-on exfoliant like [Paula's Choice 2% BHA](#see-also).[^1][^2] The general evidence for [each acid](#whats-in-it) is [modest and use-specific](#the-evidence); no trial isolates this product.

## Summary

COSRX AHA/BHA Clarifying Treatment Toner is a liquid toner with no fragrance ingredient in its INCI list, marketed for use after cleansing, applied to a cotton pad and wiped onto the face.[^1] Its [full ingredient list](#whats-in-it) runs to 11 items: water, willow bark water, apple fruit water, butylene glycol, 1,2-hexanediol, sodium lactate, glycolic acid, betaine salicylate, allantoin, panthenol, and ethylhexanediol, matched on the brand's own listing and, for its key actives, on a Target listing of the same product.[^1][^2]

Its two actives are glycolic acid, the smallest and most-used alpha-hydroxy acid (AHA), and betaine salicylate, an ester of salicylic acid used as a beta-hydroxy acid (BHA) ingredient. Neither the brand nor Target discloses a percentage for either, and both are listed after six other ingredients (water, two botanical waters, butylene glycol, 1,2-hexanediol, and sodium lactate), a lower position in the list than the 2% salicylic acid a dedicated leave-on exfoliant like [[paulas-choice-skin-perfecting-2-bha]] discloses.[^1][^2] Glycolic acid's general evidence is modest and use-specific: covered controlled trials found 8% glycolic and lactic creams "modestly useful" for photodamage and glycolic peels roughly level with other superficial peels for acne, on [[glycolic-acid]]. Salicylic acid's general evidence is strongest for wart removal and modest but guideline-recognized for acne at OTC strengths of 0.5% to 2%, covered on [[salicylic-acid]]; betaine salicylate is a salicylate ester rather than free salicylic acid, and no source fetched here states how its performance compares.

It is inexpensive for the category: the brand's own site lists the 150ml size at $10.00, marked down from a $20.00 regular price, and Target separately lists the same 150ml size at $20.00 through its Ulta Beauty storefront ([see Where to Buy](#where-to-buy), accessed 2026-08-07).[^1][^2] It carries no product-specific clinical trial; its case rests on a short, named-active ingredient list rather than a measured outcome.

## The Evidence

No trial isolates this product. Glycolic acid's clinical evidence, covering photoaging, acne peels, and melasma, is graded in full on [[glycolic-acid]]; its clearest health finding there is that 10% glycolic acid increases skin's sensitivity to UV light, an effect that reverses within a week of stopping. Salicylic acid's clinical evidence, strongest for wart removal and modest but guideline-recognized for acne, is graded in full on [[salicylic-acid]]. Neither ingredient page evidence isolates betaine salicylate, the specific salicylate ester used here, or the concentrations in this formula, which are not disclosed by the brand or by Target.[^1][^2]

## What's In It

The full ingredient list (INCI), in order, as declared by the brand.[^1]

- water
- salix alba (willow) bark water (a botanical extract; the COSRX product page does not disclose a percentage, but Target's listing alone claims "10% white willow bark," a single-source marketing figure[^2])
- pyrus malus (apple) fruit water (a botanical extract; the COSRX product page does not disclose a percentage, but Target's listing alone claims "10% of Apple Water," a single-source marketing figure[^2])
- butylene glycol (solvent/humectant)
- 1,2-hexanediol (preservative booster)
- sodium lactate (humectant/pH adjuster)
- [[glycolic-acid]] (AHA), seventh-listed; concentration not disclosed
- betaine salicylate, a [[salicylic-acid|salicylic acid]] (BHA) ester, eighth-listed; concentration not disclosed
- allantoin (soothing agent)
- panthenol (humectant)
- ethylhexanediol (co-solvent)
