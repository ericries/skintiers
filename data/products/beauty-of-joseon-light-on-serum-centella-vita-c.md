Beauty of Joseon Light On Serum: Centella + Vita C is a lightweight leave-on serum, 30 ml (1.01 fl oz), whose main vitamin C is the water-stable derivative 3-O-ethyl ascorbic acid, paired with centella asiatica extract.[^1]

> The clinical case for topical vitamin C (see [[ascorbic-acid-vitamin-c]]) is built on L-ascorbic acid at roughly 10 to 20% and a pH below 3.5 (Pinnell 2001). This serum's vitamin C is instead a derivative, 3-O-ethyl ascorbic acid, at a strength the manufacturer does not state, plus a trace of ascorbic acid polypeptide, so it is not the same form or concentration studied in that literature. Its centella content speaks to soothing, which is covered on [[centella-asiatica]].[^1][^2]

## The Evidence

Whether topical vitamin C brightens skin or supports collagen, and how, is covered on [[ascorbic-acid-vitamin-c]]. Whether centella asiatica soothes is covered on [[centella-asiatica]]. What is specific to this product is the form and strength of its vitamin C.

Pinnell 2001 set the penetration benchmark for topical L-ascorbic acid. In percutaneous absorption studies, "L-ascorbic acid must be formulated at pH levels less than 3.5 to enter the skin," the maximal concentration for optimal absorption was 20%, tissue levels were saturated after three daily applications, and the tissue half-life was about 4 days.[^2]

> **Bottom line.** The vitamin C form with the most penetration and efficacy data is L-ascorbic acid at up to about 20% and a pH below 3.5. This serum uses a derivative instead.[^2]

The manufacturer's declared ingredient list gives 3-O-ethyl ascorbic acid, a water-stable vitamin C derivative, as the named vitamin C, with a small amount of ascorbic acid polypeptide near the end of the list. The percentage of neither is stated.[^1]

> **Bottom line.** The vitamin C here is a derivative at an undisclosed concentration, not the L-ascorbic acid at 10 to 20% and low pH used in the penetration and photoprotection studies.[^1]

The ingredients as declared by the manufacturer.[^1] Those with a functional role are linked to their own pages; the rest are the base, texture, and preservative system.

Actives and functional ingredients:

The formula lists no added fragrance and no niacinamide (Beauty of Joseon's Glow Serum: Propolis + Niacinamide uses niacinamide).[^1]
