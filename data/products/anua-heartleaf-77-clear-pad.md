Anua Heartleaf 77% Clear Pad is a Korean two-sided toner pad built on a 77% Houttuynia cordata (heartleaf) extract base, paired with gluconolactone, a polyhydroxy acid (PHA), and a small, separately declared amount of Centella Asiatica extract, sold as 70 pads in a 160 ml package for $22.00 on the brand's own US site.[^anua]

> The brand's directions call for swiping the pad's "embossed side across face avoiding eyes and lips," then using the smooth side "as a hydrating mini face mask for an instant moisture boost," and state the pads are "Safe for daily use AM & PM."[^anua] No cited clinical trial isolates Houttuynia cordata extract or gluconolactone, the two ingredients specific to this formula; the pore, sebum, and hydration percentages on the product page are the brand's own unpublished figures (see [Common Marketing Claims](#common-marketing-claims)).[^anua]

## Summary

Anua Heartleaf 77% Clear Pad is a two-sided cotton toner pad: the brand describes one side as embossed for exfoliation before cleansing residue is removed, and the other as smooth for pressing onto the skin afterward.[^anua] Houttuynia cordata (heartleaf) extract is listed first in the declared ingredient list at 77%, ahead of water, making it the formula's aqueous base rather than a minor addition.[^anua] Third on the list is gluconolactone, a polyhydroxy acid (PHA) used for chemical exfoliation, with no concentration disclosed.[^anua] No salicylic acid (BHA) or other beta hydroxy acid appears in the declared ingredient list.[^anua]

Houttuynia cordata is the ingredient the pad is named for and its majority component, but the brand's own product page discloses no clinical trial of the extract in this or any formula; it is used across Korean skincare as a botanical base marketed for calming sensitized or acne-prone skin, covered on [[houttuynia-cordata]]. Toner pads combining a mild acid exfoliant with a soothing botanical base are commonly positioned in Korean skincare toward oily or acne-prone skin; the strongest-evidenced topical acne actives are graded on [[acne]].

Centella Asiatica extract, along with its four named triterpenes (madecassoside, asiatic acid, asiaticoside, and madecassic acid), is declared separately near the end of the ingredient list, after most of the base and botanical-extract blend, indicating a far smaller share of the formula than the 77% heartleaf base.[^anua] Centella's own evidence, mechanistic in the lab plus a handful of small human trials mainly for wound healing, is covered on [[centella-asiatica]]; this formula does not disclose the extract's concentration.

It is priced at $22.00, listed as a sale price for the 160 ml / 70-pad size, on the brand's own US site as of 2026-08-07.[^anua]

## The Evidence

No published trial tests this product, its 77% Houttuynia cordata base, or the gluconolactone (PHA) it pairs with it.[^anua] Centella Asiatica extract, declared separately and low in the ingredient list, has evidence set out in full on [[centella-asiatica]]: well-mapped triterpene mechanisms for wound healing and collagen support in cell and animal studies, with the human clinical record limited to a handful of small trials for wounds and to confounded or null trials for anti-aging use. That evidence was not generated with this pad or at a stated concentration of the extract.
