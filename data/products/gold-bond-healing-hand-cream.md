Gold Bond® Healing Hand Cream is a fragrance-containing, 3 oz cosmetic hand cream sold by [[gold-bond|Gold Bond]] that combines occlusive and humectant moisturizers with niacinamide and vitamins C and E, priced at $3.98 for 3 oz on Walmart.[^gb][^walmart]

> A fragrance-containing hand cream built on petrolatum, dimethicone, and glycerin, with niacinamide, vitamin C (magnesium ascorbyl phosphate), vitamin E (tocopheryl acetate), panthenol, and ceramide NG further down the ingredient list, none of the actives listed at a stated percentage.[^gb][^walmart] Niacinamide's general evidence lives on [[niacinamide]]; Gold Bond's own "24 hours" and "even through handwashing" hydration claims for this specific cream are marketing copy, not an independently published trial (see [Common Marketing Claims](#common-marketing-claims)).[^gb]

## Summary

Gold Bond markets this 3 oz tube for dry hands, describing it as "clinically shown to hydrate for up to 24 hours, even through handwashing" and formulated with "7 essential moisturizers and 3 nourishing vitamins," naming niacinamide ("to improve skin appearance"), vitamin C ("to protect against environmental stressors"), and vitamin E ("to help prevent moisture loss") as its three vitamin callouts.[^gb] Walmart's listing for the same 3 oz item separately calls it "hypoallergenic" and "dermatologist-tested."[^walmart]

Niacinamide's general evidence as a barrier-supporting, tone-evening ingredient is set out on [[niacinamide]] rather than restated here. What matters for this specific product is that none of its ingredients, including niacinamide, is disclosed at a stated concentration, and no independent trial of this exact formula was located; Gold Bond's own "up to 24 hours" hydration figure is not accompanied by a published methodology on the page checked here (see [The Evidence](#the-evidence)).[^gb]

The base of the formula is a standard occlusive-and-emollient hand cream: water, glycerin, stearyl alcohol, dimethicone, and petrolatum, along with aloe, ceramide NG, and hydroxyethyl urea (linked at [[hydroxyethyl-urea]]).[^gb] Unlike Gold Bond's Rough & Bumpy body cream, this hand cream's ingredient list includes "fragrance," so it is not fragrance-free.[^gb] It is sold at $3.98 for 3 oz on Walmart, with a matching gtin/UPC and product identity confirmed against Gold Bond's own product page (see [Where to Buy](#where-to-buy), accessed 2026-08-23).[^gb][^walmart]

## The Evidence

Niacinamide has broader, independently studied evidence as a barrier-supporting and skin-tone ingredient; that case is made on [[niacinamide]] and not repeated here. No independently published trial of this exact hand cream, or of this specific multi-vitamin, multi-moisturizer combination, was located. The product-specific facts available are its declared composition (niacinamide, magnesium ascorbyl phosphate, tocopheryl acetate, panthenol, and ceramide NG among a longer ingredient list, none at a disclosed percentage) and Gold Bond's own marketing claim, "Clinically shown to hydrate for up to 24 hours, even through handwashing," which appears on the brand's own product page without a cited study design, sample size, or methodology.[^gb]

> **Bottom line.** The base moisturizers (petrolatum, dimethicone, glycerin) are well-established occlusives and humectants, and niacinamide has real general evidence covered on [[niacinamide]], but no independent trial isolates this exact hand cream, none of its named actives is disclosed at a stated concentration, and Gold Bond's own 24-hour hydration claim is unaccompanied by a published methodology on the page checked here.[^gb]
