COSRX Advanced Snail 92 All-in-One Cream is a South Korean leave-on gel-cream whose ingredient list opens with snail secretion filtrate, which COSRX states makes up 92 percent of the formula, sold in 50g and 100g sizes (a 200g size is discontinued).[^1]

> A snail-mucin moisturizer built around a labeled 92% snail secretion filtrate, from the same brand as the [[cosrx-advanced-snail-96-mucin-power-essence|Advanced Snail 96 Mucin Power Essence]]. The marketed hydration, elasticity and "glass skin" glow benefits rest on thin, largely anecdotal evidence: the controlled human research on snail secretion filtrate tests a different, manufacturer-supplied extract, not a high-percentage cream like this one.[^1] Sodium hyaluronate, dimethicone and panthenol, listed further down the ingredient list, add smaller but better-evidenced moisturizing.[^1]

## Summary

COSRX Advanced Snail 92 All-in-One Cream is a lightweight, non-greasy gel-cream that COSRX markets as helping "repair stressed skin barriers and boost elasticity," describing it as designed "for all skin types, especially sensitive or dry skin."[^1] What distinguishes it from a plainer moisturizer is concentration: snail secretion filtrate is first on the ingredient list, and COSRX states the cream is "92%" snail secretion filtrate.[^1] It is sold on the brand's own site as a 100g jar or in 50g and 100g tubes; a 200g tube is listed as discontinued.[^1] As of this writing the 100g jar and 100g tube each list for $26 and the 50g tube for $15 on cosrx.com ([see Where to Buy](#where-to-buy), accessed 2026-08-23).[^1]

Snail secretion filtrate is a well-known K-beauty ingredient, but [most of the controlled human research on it tests a different material](#the-evidence): a patented extract from a different preparation than the high-percentage cream format sold here.[^1] Because of that mismatch, the hydration, elasticity and glow benefits marketed for this cream sit on thin, largely anecdotal evidence; the general evidence for the ingredient, including where it is better supported, is on [[snail-secretion-filtrate|snail secretion filtrate]]. The cream's base also includes sodium hyaluronate, dimethicone and panthenol, ingredients with solid, well-established category-level moisturizing evidence even though all sit well down the ingredient list behind the snail filtrate; more on that evidence is on [[moisturizing]].[^1]

The brand's own testing and marketing claims are quarantined under [Common Marketing Claims](#common-marketing-claims).

## The Evidence

No published trial of a high-percentage snail-mucin cream, of the kind sold here, is cited on [[snail-secretion-filtrate|snail secretion filtrate]]; the controlled human research collected there instead tests a different, patented Cryptomphalus aspersa extract supplied by its manufacturer, in a small split-face wrinkle trial and a small radiation-dermatitis pilot, both cosmetic-or-narrow findings rather than evidence for a 92%-mucin cream like this one. Sodium hyaluronate, dimethicone and panthenol, all declared on this cream's label, are established moisturizing ingredients with solid category-level evidence; see [[hyaluronic-acid|hyaluronic acid]], [[dimethicone]] and [[moisturizing]].[^1]

## What's In It

The full ingredient list (INCI) as declared by the manufacturer, in order.[^1] The notable/functional ingredients are linked to their own pages; the rest is the base, texture and preservative system.

- Snail Secretion Filtrate ([[snail-secretion-filtrate|snail mucin]]), first on the list, at a labeled 92%
- Sodium Hyaluronate ([[hyaluronic-acid|hyaluronic acid]]), a humectant
- Dimethicone, a silicone occlusive ([[dimethicone]])
- Panthenol, a humectant and skin-conditioning agent ([[panthenol]])
- Allantoin and Arginine, additional soothing and moisturizing-factor components
- Adenosine, an anti-wrinkle active ([[adenosine]])

Base, texture and preservative system: Betaine, Caprylic/Capric Triglyceride and Butylene Glycol (emollients/humectant-solvents), Cetearyl Olivate, Sorbitan Olivate and Cetearyl Alcohol (emulsifiers/thickeners), Carbomer, Sodium Polyacrylate and Xanthan Gum (thickeners), Ethyl Hexanediol and Phenoxyethanol (preservatives), Palmitic Acid, Stearic Acid and Myristic Acid (fatty acids/texturizers), and Water.[^1]

The ingredient list declares no added fragrance.[^1]

Label and marketing claims not established by the product-specific evidence above:

- **"K-Beauty Favorite Cream for Boosting Elasticity"** that "helps repair stressed skin barriers and boost elasticity ... leaving the skin smooth, resilient, and glowing with a 'Glass Skin' finish." Positioning language; the controlled human evidence for snail secretion filtrate tests a different extract than this cream, see [[snail-secretion-filtrate|snail secretion filtrate]] and [The Evidence](#the-evidence).[^1]
- **"+233.05% Deep Moisture, +82.73% Skin Relief"** in a "Why COSRX?" comparison table, with a note that the figures were "clinically tested by the Dermacosmetic Skin Science Laboratory (Korea) to deliver over 230% more hydration and proven skin relief." COSRX states elsewhere on the same page that "all clinical studies were conducted by Dermacosmetic Skin Science Laboratory (Korea) from May 19 to June 16, 2025, on 20 adult participants."[^1] A single small brand-run test on this product, with no independently published protocol or results.
