Avène Mineral Ultra-Light Hydrating Sunscreen Lotion SPF50+ is an all-mineral facial sunscreen from [[avene|Avène]], sold in Europe and rated SPF 50+ on a titanium dioxide 4% and zinc oxide 6% filter combination.[^lf][^ls]

> An all-mineral SPF 50+ facial sunscreen using titanium dioxide (4%) and zinc oxide (6%) as its only UV filters. Broad-spectrum sunscreen's health case, slowing photoaging and lowering skin-cancer risk, is well evidenced at the category level and set out on [[sunscreen-uv-filters]]; both filters here are photostable and genuinely broad-spectrum, though the combined 10% active load sits below higher-concentration single-filter mineral sunscreens such as [[prequel-sun-barrier-mineral-sunscreen]] (18% zinc oxide alone). This is an EU-rated product: the "50+" is the EU/international testing standard's top SPF band, not an FDA-tested US Broad Spectrum rating, a distinction covered on [[sunscreen-uv-filters]].[^lf][^ls]

## Summary

Mineral Ultra-Light Hydrating Sunscreen Lotion is Avène's oil-free, mineral-only facial sunscreen, sold through European and, previously, some US specialty retailers rather than under this name on Avène's current US site. Its core purpose is the same as any sunscreen's: broad-spectrum UV protection is the best-evidenced way to slow photoaging and lower the risk of sun-related skin cancer, a case with randomized-trial support set out on [[sunscreen-uv-filters]].

What distinguishes it from a plainer sunscreen is that it relies entirely on mineral (inorganic) filters rather than organic (chemical) ones. An Irish retailer's listing states its active ingredients as titanium dioxide 4% and zinc oxide 6%, rated SPF50+, describing the formula as "100% mineral sun filters"; a US retailer's listing for the same product independently corroborates the SPF50+ rating and describes it as "chemical-free" with "non-nano mineral particles."[^lf][^ls] [INCIdecoder's ingredient listing for this product](https://incidecoder.com/products/avene-ultra-light-mineral-lotion-spf-50-face) records the same two actives at the same 4%/6% split, with a fuller INCI order for the base ingredients than the retailer listing shows. Both zinc oxide and titanium dioxide are photostable mineral filters, covered generally on [[zinc-oxide]] and [[titanium-dioxide]]; between the two, zinc oxide reaches further into long-wave UVA. The combined 10% mineral load here is lower than the concentration used in some higher-percentage single-filter mineral sunscreens, such as [[prequel-sun-barrier-mineral-sunscreen]] (18% zinc oxide alone).

The product carries an SPF50+ rating from the EU sunscreen testing standard rather than an FDA-tested US Broad Spectrum claim. Both retailer listings describe it as fragrance-free, paraben-free, water-resistant, and enriched with Avène Thermal Spring Water; the US listing additionally lists vitamin E and states 80 minutes of water resistance, versus up to 40 minutes on the Irish listing, and notes the product is no longer available for purchase there.[^lf][^ls] It is priced at EUR 24.21 for 1.7 fl oz (50 mL) on the Irish retailer listing checked for this profile, the only listing found with a current price (see [Where to Buy](#where-to-buy), accessed 2026-08-07).[^lf]

## The Evidence

Whether regular sunscreen use works, and how the SPF (UVB) and UVA axes differ, is covered on [[sunscreen-uv-filters]]. No independent study of this specific product was located; what is specific to it is its filter composition and rating.

An Irish retailer's listing gives the active ingredients as titanium dioxide 4% and zinc oxide 6%, at an SPF50+ rating; a US retailer's independent listing for the same product corroborates the SPF50+ rating and the all-mineral, "chemical-free" formulation.[^lf][^ls] Both are mineral filters: [[titanium-dioxide]] and [[zinc-oxide]] are photostable and provide genuine broad-spectrum coverage, with zinc oxide reaching further into long-wave UVA than titanium dioxide. This SPF50+ figure comes from the EU sunscreen testing standard, not the FDA's US OTC Broad Spectrum monograph; the two testing systems use different methods and are not directly interchangeable, a distinction set out generally on [[sunscreen-uv-filters]].

> **Bottom line.** Two independent retailer listings agree the product is an all-mineral SPF50+ sunscreen rated under the EU testing standard, with titanium dioxide and zinc oxide as its only actives.[^lf][^ls] Both filters are photostable mineral UV absorbers with real broad-spectrum coverage, though the combined 10% active load reported by the Irish listing is lower than some higher-concentration single-filter mineral sunscreens.

## What's In It

UV filters (actives), per an Irish retailer's listing:[^lf]

- [[titanium-dioxide]] 4%, a mineral (physical) UV filter
- [[zinc-oxide]] 6%, a mineral (physical) broad-spectrum filter

Other functional/notable ingredients: tocopheryl acetate (a form of [[tocopherol]], vitamin E), per [INCIdecoder's ingredient listing for this product](https://incidecoder.com/products/avene-ultra-light-mineral-lotion-spf-50-face), which carries a fuller INCI order than the retailer listing above and is not itself enumerated on the Irish retailer's partial ingredient list.[^lf][^inci]

Base, texture, and preservative system, per the same listing: water, butyloctyl salicylate, cetyl dimethicone, dimethicone, octyldodecyl neopentanoate, C12-15 alkyl benzoate, trimethylsiloxysilicate, styrene acrylates copolymer, dimethicone/PEG-10/15 crosspolymer, dimethicone PEG-8 laurate, polymethylmethacrylate, glycerin, and butylene glycol, plus additional stabilizers and preservatives.[^lf]

Not in the formula: no organic (chemical) UV filter, and no added fragrance.[^lf][^ls]
