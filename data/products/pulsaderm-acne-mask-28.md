The Pulsaderm Acne Mask 28 is a wearable blue-and-red LED mask from Pulsaderm, LLC of Fort Myers, Florida, cleared by the FDA in May 2017 under 510(k) number K170111, which also covers the 37-diode version.[^fda] Its cleared indication is that the masks "are intended for over the counter use for the treatment of mild to moderate acne".[^fda] The device category is on [[home-led-light-therapy]].

> **This is the device the Neutrogena mask was judged equivalent to, and it holds no acne trial either. It points at a third company.** [[neutrogena-light-therapy-acne-mask-plus]] rests on equivalence to this clearance. This clearance in turn names its own predicate as the "Illumask Acne Light Therapy System K123999" from La Lumiere, LLC.[^fda] Its testing section is headed "Non-clinical-testing", and the human study it describes measured whether people could **read the instructions**, not whether their acne improved.[^fda]

## Summary

Class II devices using "Light Emitting Diodes to apply low power monochromatic and non-coherent light in the red and blue range, to treat mild to moderate acne", rechargeable, in 28-diode and 37-diode versions.[^fda] Wavelengths are red at 630 plus or minus 5 nm and blue at 440 plus or minus 5 nm; the protocol is "10 minutes everyday" with "Total Treatments required 30"; total energy delivered per session is 20.44 J/cm2 for the 28 and 22.72 J/cm2 for the 37.[^fda] That energy figure is the most specific dosing number any home LED device on this site publishes.

## The Rubric

**Mild to moderate acne**
- **Effect size: modest, and carried by the category rather than this device.** Red 630nm with blue 440nm is the blue-red pairing the 2026 GRADE review graded Level B with a Grade I recommendation, the best grade in its acne section.[^home][^fda] No acne outcome was measured in this clearance.
- **Evidence quality: preliminary for the device, mixed at the category level.** The file's testing is conformance to IEC standards plus biocompatibility, and a usability study.[^fda]

**Knowing the dose you are getting**
- **Effect size: not an efficacy question; this is a disclosure point in the device's favour.** Unlike the other home LED clearances profiled here, this one publishes energy per session in J/cm2 alongside diode counts and a defined 30-treatment course.[^fda] Whether those figures correspond to the doses used in the trials behind the Level B grade is not something any source here establishes.
- **Evidence quality: manufacturer specification from a published 510(k) Summary.**

## The Evidence

**What the human study actually measured.** Under the heading "Non-clinical-testing", the summary records that "A lay-user study and self-selection study was conducted with Institutional Review Board (IRB) approval and oversight to determine if lay users could read the product labeling and then self-assess if the Pulsaderm Acne Mask 28 and 37 would be beneficial for them to use", and that the data "demonstrated that the intended users of the device could successfully follow the instructions and use the device as intended".[^fda] That is a real study with real ethical oversight, and its endpoint is comprehension. A reader who hears "IRB-approved study" and pictures an acne trial would be picturing the wrong thing.

**What the rest of the testing covered.** "Performance testing was conducted and confirm compliance to design specifications; similar wavelength, energy type and safety characteristics", with conformance to IEC 60601-1, IEC 60601-1-2 and IEC 62471, plus biocompatibility and a risk system conforming to ISO 14971.[^fda] Electrical safety, optical safety and materials. None of it measures a lesion.

**The chain, now three companies deep.** Neutrogena's 2018 clearance rests on this 2017 one; this one rests on La Lumiere's 2013 Illumask.[^fda] On the reVive side the chain ran three generations inside a single manufacturer, documented on [[revive-light-therapy-essentials]]. Here it crosses between firms, which is the form substantial equivalence is designed to take, and the result is the same: no acne trial appears at any step this site has read.

## Common Marketing Claims

- **"FDA cleared"**. Accurate.[^fda] It means the FDA agreed this mask is substantially equivalent to the Illumask, which the public file does not show to have been trialled either.
- **"for the treatment of mild to moderate acne"**. The cleared indication verbatim, so legitimate labelling.[^fda] It states what the device may be sold for.
- **Dose and wavelength figures**. Accurate as published specifications, and more specific than most.[^fda] A J/cm2 figure is an engineering quantity, not a demonstrated outcome.
