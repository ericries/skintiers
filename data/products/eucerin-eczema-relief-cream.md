Eucerin Eczema Relief Cream is an over-the-counter eczema moisturizer built around colloidal oatmeal 1%, an FDA skin-protectant active, in a glycerin-and-ceramide base.[^1]

> An OTC skin-protectant drug: the Drug Facts label lists the active as "Colloidal Oatmeal 1%," which "temporarily protects and helps relieve minor skin irritation and itching due to rashes, eczema."[^1] Colloidal oatmeal is a genuine, if modest, eczema active, with its evidence set out on [[colloidal-oatmeal]] and [[atopic-dermatitis]]; the base adds licorice root extract and a ceramide.

## Summary

This is Eucerin's eczema-labeled daily cream. It is regulated as an OTC drug: the label states the active as "colloidal oatmeal 1%," the purpose as skin protectant, and the use as temporarily protecting and relieving minor skin irritation and itching due to rashes and eczema, applied as needed.[^1]

Colloidal oatmeal's evidence lives on [[colloidal-oatmeal]]: it is an FDA-monograph skin protectant with modest but real support for calming the itch and irritation of eczema-prone skin, used as a maintenance moisturizer alongside (not instead of) anti-inflammatory treatment on [[atopic-dermatitis]]. No trial of this exact cream was located, so the grade rests on that active plus its monograph status.

## The Evidence

The defensible facts are the active (colloidal oatmeal 1%, an FDA skin-protectant monograph active) and the emollient base.[^1] For eczema-prone skin, a colloidal-oatmeal moisturizer helps by protecting and hydrating the barrier and easing itch; that is genuine maintenance value, but it is a supportive moisturizer, not a substitute for the corticosteroids or other anti-inflammatory treatment that address flares, covered on [[atopic-dermatitis]].

> **Bottom line.** A colloidal oatmeal 1% eczema moisturizer, a reasonable, well-evidenced-at-the-ingredient-level daily cream for dry, eczema-prone, itchy skin.[^1] Judge it as barrier-supporting maintenance care; see [[atopic-dermatitis]] for what treats flares.

## What's In It

Active ingredient: "Colloidal Oatmeal 1%."[^1] Inactive ingredients, from the label: water, glycerin, castor seed oil, mineral oil, cetyl alcohol, glyceryl stearate, caprylic/capric triglyceride, octyldodecanol, cetyl palmitate, PEG-40 stearate, licorice (Glycyrrhiza inflata) root extract, ceramide 3, 1,2-hexanediol, and phenoxyethanol, among others.[^1] The base combines humectants and emollients with a ceramide and a licorice-root extract.

## Common Marketing Claims

- **"Eczema relief."** Label-supported at the skin-protectant level: colloidal oatmeal 1% temporarily protects and relieves minor irritation and itching due to eczema.[^1] It is maintenance care, not flare treatment.
- **Long-lasting hydration.** Consistent with the emollient base; a moisturizer benefit, not a disease-modifying claim.[^1]
