Black Girl Sunscreen SPF 30 is a moisturizing, Broad Spectrum SPF 30 chemical facial and body sunscreen from Black Girl Sunscreen, a brand founded in 2016 by Shontay Lundy and marketed to blend into deeper skin tones with no white cast.[^1][^3]

> A Broad Spectrum SPF 30 sunscreen built on four organic UV filters, avobenzone, homosalate, octisalate, and octocrylene, the same US filter family used in several other chemical sunscreens.[^1][^2] Broad-spectrum sunscreen's health case, slowing photoaging and lowering skin-cancer risk, is well evidenced at the category level and set out on [[sunscreen-uv-filters]]. SPF 30 is a solid but not top-tier UVB rating, and the UVA side rests on avobenzone stabilized by a comparatively small 2.75% octocrylene share. The brand's "no white cast" and "blends in" positioning is its own cosmetic claim, not an independently tested finding.[^1][^3]

## Summary

Black Girl Sunscreen SPF 30 is the flagship product of Black Girl Sunscreen (BGS), a US suncare brand founded in 2016 by Shontay Lundy, who has said she started the company after struggling to find a sunscreen that did not leave a visible cast on deeper skin tones.[^3] It is sold as a facial and body lotion in 2 fl oz and 3 fl oz sizes.[^1]

Per the Drug Facts panel, confirmed independently on both Black Girl Sunscreen's own product page and Target's listing, the actives are avobenzone 3%, homosalate 10%, octisalate 5%, and octocrylene 2.75%, an all-organic (chemical) filter set rated Broad Spectrum SPF 30.[^1][^2] That is the same four-filter family used in other chemical sunscreens, including [[la-roche-posay-anthelios-melt-in-milk-spf-60]] and [[supergoop-unseen-sunscreen-spf-50]], though at a lower SPF and, compared with those two, a markedly lower octocrylene share.[^1][^2] Broad-spectrum protection against UV, the part of any sunscreen's case with the strongest evidence, is set out at the category level on [[sunscreen-uv-filters]].

Both sources describe the formula as free of oxybenzone and octinoxate; Target's listing further describes it as paraben-free, fragrance-free, silicone-free, aluminum-free, vegan, and reef safe.[^2] The brand markets it as drying "completely clear" and formulated for melanin-rich skin with no white cast, positioned as a moisturizer as well as a sunscreen, for use "before any outdoor activity or even under your favorite makeup products."[^1] That cosmetic-elegance positioning is the brand's own claim, addressed separately under [Common Marketing Claims](#common-marketing-claims) and not part of the graded UV-protection evidence below.

## The Evidence

Regular use of a broad-spectrum sunscreen has randomized-trial support for slowing photoaging and reducing melanoma and squamous-cell tumor burden, among the strongest clinical evidence in skincare; that evidence, and how the SPF (UVB) and UVA axes differ, is set out on [[sunscreen-uv-filters]]. No independent study of this specific product was located; the product-specific facts are its filter composition and label claims.

Black Girl Sunscreen's own product page and Target's Drug Facts listing agree on the active ingredients: avobenzone 3%, homosalate 10%, octisalate 5%, and octocrylene 2.75%, at Broad Spectrum SPF 30.[^1][^2] SPF is a UVB (sunburn) number; the SPF 30 rating certifies UVB strength and does not by itself certify UVA (photoaging) protection, a separate axis described on [[sunscreen-uv-filters]].

Three of the four filters, homosalate, octisalate, and octocrylene, are UVB absorbers; UVA coverage rests on avobenzone alone.[^1][^2] [[avobenzone]] at 3% provides genuine UVA coverage once photostabilized, but it degrades under sunlight unless a co-filter holds it stable. [[octocrylene]] serves that stabilizing role here, at 2.75% of the formula, a smaller share than the 7% and 9% used to stabilize avobenzone in [[la-roche-posay-anthelios-melt-in-milk-spf-60]] and [[supergoop-unseen-sunscreen-spf-50]].[^1][^2] Measured against the global field, this is the older US filter set: avobenzone is a weaker, less photostable UVA filter than the modern organic filters (bemotrizinol/Tinosorb S, bisoctrizole/Tinosorb M, the Mexoryl filters) standard in the EU, Korea, and Australia, a comparison detailed on [[sunscreen-uv-filters]].

> **Bottom line.** The Broad Spectrum SPF 30 rating reflects a genuine, moderate US chemical filter set: homosalate, octisalate, and octocrylene carry the UVB side at a solid but not top-tier SPF, and avobenzone carries the UVA side, stabilized by a comparatively small octocrylene share.[^1][^2] That UVA coverage is real but older, and less photostable than the modern-filter products sold outside the US.

How much protection any SPF delivers in practice depends on applying enough product, a point covered generally on [[sunscreen-uv-filters]].

## What's In It

The active filters as declared on the Drug Facts panel, confirmed on both Black Girl Sunscreen's own product page and Target's listing, plus the inactive-ingredient list from Black Girl Sunscreen's page.[^1][^2] Ingredients with a functional role are linked to their own pages; the rest are the base, texture, and preservative system.

UV filters (Drug Facts actives):

- [[avobenzone]] 3%, the formula's only dedicated UVA filter
- [[homosalate]] 10%, a UVB filter
- [[octisalate]] 5%, a weak, secondary UVB filter
- [[octocrylene]] 2.75%, a UVB filter that also helps stabilize avobenzone

Other functional/notable ingredients: Aloe Barbadensis (Aloe Vera) Leaf Juice, Persea Gratissima (Avocado) Oil, Simmondsia Chinensis (Jojoba) Seed Oil, Theobroma Cacao (Cocoa) Seed Butter, Daucus Carota Sativa (Carrot) Seed Oil, Helianthus Annuus (Sunflower) Seed Oil, and [[tocopherol|Tocopheryl Acetate]] (vitamin E), an antioxidant.[^1]

Base, texture, and preservative system: water, acrylates/C10-30 alkyl acrylate crosspolymer, acrylates/C12-22 alkyl methacrylate copolymer, butylphthalimide, carbomer, disodium EDTA, ethylhexylglycerin, hydroxypropyl methylcellulose, isopropylphthalimide, lecithin, phenoxyethanol, propylene glycol, sodium hydroxide, and sorbitan oleate.[^1]

Not in the formula: no oxybenzone and no octinoxate, two organic filters the brand markets itself as free of; no added fragrance appears on the ingredient list.[^1][^2]
