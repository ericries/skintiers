SkinMedica HA5 Rejuvenating Hydrator is a hydrating facial serum from [[skinmedica]], sold in a 2 fl. oz. (56.7 g) bottle and described by Dermstore's listing as blending "five types of hydrators" to replenish the skin's hyaluronic acid, for "all skin types."[^dermstore]

> Moisturizing with a humectant is a well-evidenced way to raise a skin's hydration reading, and hyaluronic acid specifically has solid trial evidence for that use, covered on [[hyaluronic-acid]]. This serum supplies that hydration through several hyaluronic-acid forms plus glycerin, layered over a dimethicone/silicone base that sits ahead of the named hyaluronic-acid ingredients in the ingredient list, at a price several times that of a plain hyaluronic-acid serum.[^dermstore]

## Summary

HA5 Rejuvenating Hydrator is a leave-on serum, applied as a step before moisturizer and sunscreen, built around hyaluronic acid, the well-evidenced humectant covered on [[hyaluronic-acid]].[^dermstore] Dermstore's directions for the product read: "Apply twice daily as the last treatment product in your skin care regimen and immediately before applying a moisturizer (AM/PM) and before sunscreen (AM). Apply to face, neck and decollete or any area where fine lines and wrinkles primarily exist."[^dermstore]

What distinguishes it from a single-ingredient hyaluronic-acid serum is formula breadth, not a proprietary hyaluronic-acid technology: the ingredient list carries three named hyaluronic-acid forms (sodium hyaluronate crosspolymer, hydrolyzed hyaluronic acid, and sodium hyaluronate) plus glycerin, but they sit behind Dimethicone, a silicone, which is the second-largest ingredient after water, and behind two more silicones (Bis-PEG-8 Dimethicone, Polysilicone-11) further down the list, ahead of any of the named hyaluronic-acid ingredients ([full list below](#whats-in-it)).[^dermstore] A third-party review of the product describes the formula as containing "sodium hyaluronate, hydrolyzed HA, acetylated HA, free HA, and HA crosspolymer" and reports that the manufacturer's own studies found "improvements in hydration measures, fine wrinkle appearance, and skin smoothness over 4-8 weeks of use," without giving the underlying figures.[^dermapproved]

Dermstore lists the 2 fl. oz. bottle at $184.00, a price similar to the $178.00 a third-party review site separately lists for the same size.[^dermstore][^dermapproved] That is several times the price of a plain hyaluronic-acid serum, such as [[the-ordinary-multi-peptide-ha-serum]] at about $19.90 for 30 ml.

## The Evidence

Hyaluronic acid has solid trial evidence as a topical humectant: a placebo-controlled trial found hyaluronic-acid creams raised skin-hydration and elasticity readings above placebo across the molecular weights tested, covered on [[hyaluronic-acid]]. No product-specific trial of HA5 Rejuvenating Hydrator was found in the sources fetched for this page. A third-party review reports that SkinMedica has run its own studies of the product and that those studies found improvements in hydration, fine-wrinkle appearance, and skin smoothness over four to eight weeks, but does not quote the underlying numbers.[^dermapproved]

> **Bottom line.** The category evidence for hyaluronic acid as a topical humectant is solid; this specific product's evidence, beyond that category case, rests on unspecified manufacturer-sponsored studies relayed by a third-party review rather than a verified independent trial.[^dermapproved]
