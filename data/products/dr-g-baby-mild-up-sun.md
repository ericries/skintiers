Dr.G Baby Mild Up Sun SPF35/PA+++ (Dr.G) is a South Korean mineral sunscreen built on zinc oxide as its only UV filter, marketed for sensitive and baby skin.[^monodist]

> A zinc-oxide-only mineral sunscreen rated SPF35/PA+++ for the Korean market, a rating tested to Korean, not FDA, protocols. Broad-spectrum sunscreen's health case, slowing photoaging and lowering skin-cancer risk, is well evidenced at the category level and set out on [[sunscreen-uv-filters]]. Zinc oxide itself is a photostable filter with real coverage across both UVB and UVA, detailed on [[zinc-oxide]]; this formula does not disclose what percentage of zinc oxide it uses. It pairs the filter with a ceramide-cholesterol-fatty acid barrier blend and carries no listed fragrance ingredient.[^monodist]

## Summary

Baby Mild Up Sun is a facial sunscreen from Dr.G, a Korean dermatologist-founded brand (Gowoonsesang Cosmetics, acquired by L'Oréal Groupe in a deal announced December 2024) positioned around hypoallergenic, sensitive-skin formulas.[^loreal] Its own retail description frames it as "an easy-to-remove, gentle mineral sunscreen designed for delicate skin," with a "soothing, hypoallergenic creamy formula."[^monodist]

The formula's single active is [[zinc-oxide]], the only UV filter on its ingredient list; no titanium dioxide or organic filters are added.[^monodist] Zinc oxide's broad-spectrum, photostable coverage across UVB and UVA is well evidenced at the ingredient level, but this formula does not disclose a zinc oxide percentage, so the strength of protection it delivers cannot be read off the label the way an FDA Drug Facts panel would show it. The product carries a Korean SPF35/PA+++ rating, tested to Korean, not FDA, protocols.[^monodist] Besides the filter, the notable ingredients are a ceramide-cholesterol-fatty acid combination (Ceramide NP, cholesterol, palmitic acid) associated with skin-barrier support, and Pinus Pinaster (maritime pine) bark extract.[^monodist]

Dr.G's own current global storefront no longer lists Baby Mild Up Sun among its sunscreens; its present sunscreen lineup consists of different named products (Green Mild Up Defense Mineral Sunscreen, R.E.D Blemish Soothing Up Moisture Sunscreen, and others).[^drg] The 60 ml size and 25,000 KRW original retail price come from The Monodist's listing of the formula.[^monodist]

## The Evidence

Regular use of a broad-spectrum sunscreen has randomized-trial support for slowing photoaging and reducing skin-cancer tumor burden, among the strongest clinical evidence in skincare; that evidence, and how SPF (UVB) and PA/UVA ratings differ, is set out on [[sunscreen-uv-filters]]. No independent study of this specific product was located; the product-specific facts are its single-filter composition and its Korean SPF/PA rating.

Zinc oxide is a photostable mineral filter with genuine coverage across both UVB and UVA, including further into long-wave UVA than titanium dioxide, evidence covered in full on [[zinc-oxide]]. This formula uses zinc oxide as its sole listed UV filter, with no titanium dioxide or organic co-filters.[^monodist] Its SPF35/PA+++ rating is tested to Korean, not FDA, protocols; the general point that filter sets and testing regimes standard outside the US are not equivalent to the US OTC monograph is set out on [[sunscreen-uv-filters]].

> **Bottom line.** The formula relies entirely on zinc oxide, a broad-spectrum, photostable mineral filter, for its SPF35/PA+++ rating, a rating earned under Korean testing protocols rather than the FDA's.[^monodist]

## What's In It

The full ingredient list as published in a Monodist product listing.[^monodist]

- [[zinc-oxide]] (CI 77947), the formula's sole UV filter
- Ceramide NP, Cholesterol, Palmitic Acid: a barrier-lipid combination
- Pinus Pinaster Bark Extract: a plant antioxidant extract

Base, texture, and preservative system: water, coco-caprylate/caprate, propanediol, glycerin, pentylene glycol, polyhydroxystearic acid, beheneth-25, methyl methacrylate crosspolymer, silica, triethoxycaprylylsilane, cetearyl olivate, sorbitan olivate, hydroxyethyl acrylate/sodium acryloyldimethyl taurate copolymer, glyceryl stearate, ammonium acryloyldimethyltaurate/VP copolymer, caprylyl glycol, polyacrylate crosspolymer-6, 1,2-hexanediol, disodium EDTA, xanthan gum.[^monodist]

Not in the formula: no titanium dioxide, no organic (chemical) UV filters, and no fragrance/parfum on the listed ingredients.[^monodist]
