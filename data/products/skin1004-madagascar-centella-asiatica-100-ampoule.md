SKIN1004 Madagascar Centella Ampoule (SKIN1004), marketed as the Madagascar Centella Asiatica 100 Ampoule, is a seven-ingredient, fragrance-free leave-on serum built around Madagascar-grown centella asiatica extract.[^1][^2]

> A simple, inexpensive centella ("cica") ampoule: seven ingredients, no fragrance, and a short list led by water, glycerin, and butylene glycol, with centella asiatica extract fourth and its concentration undisclosed.[^1][^2] No trial isolates this product. Centella's best-documented human evidence is for wound healing rather than everyday soothing; the "cica" anti-redness case is [mechanistically plausible but not established in a controlled human trial](#the-evidence), and glycerin, the second-listed ingredient, is a well-evidenced leave-on humectant.

## Summary

SKIN1004 Madagascar Centella Ampoule is a lightweight, fragrance-free serum whose [full ingredient list](#whats-in-it) is just seven items: water, glycerin, butylene glycol, centella asiatica extract, 1,2-hexanediol, cellulose gum, and ethylhexylglycerin, matched identically on the brand's own listing and on a Target listing for the smaller size.[^1][^2] It is positioned by the brand to "soothe visible redness and comfort sensitive-looking skin" and to support the skin barrier.[^1] The name references the product line rather than a 100% concentration: centella extract is the fourth-listed ingredient, after water, glycerin, and butylene glycol, and neither the brand nor the retailer discloses what percentage of the formula it makes up.[^1][^2]

Centella asiatica's clinical evidence is strongest for wound healing and much thinner for everyday redness/soothing ("cica") use, where the anti-inflammatory case comes from lab and cell studies rather than an isolating human trial; no trial isolates this product, and the full graded picture is on [[centella-asiatica]]. Glycerin, the second-listed ingredient, is a well-evidenced humectant for stratum corneum hydration and barrier support in leave-on formulas, covered on [[glycerin]]; because this is a leave-on ampoule rather than a rinse-off product, that evidence applies more directly than it would in a cleanser.

It is inexpensive for the category: the brand's own site lists the 100ml size at $19.80 on sale (regular $22.00), and Target separately sells a 55ml size of the same seven-ingredient formula at $15.99 ([see Where to Buy](#where-to-buy), accessed 2026-08-07).[^1][^2] It carries no drug active and no product-specific clinical trial; its case rests on a short, recognizable ingredient list rather than a measured outcome.

## The Evidence

No trial isolates this product: it is a single-active formula, with centella asiatica extract the sole active, listed fourth after water, glycerin, and butylene glycol. See [[centella-asiatica]] for the full graded picture of centella's wound-healing and anti-inflammatory evidence. Glycerin has solid evidence as a leave-on humectant for stratum corneum hydration and barrier function, graded on [[glycerin]]; this is a leave-on ampoule, so that evidence transfers more directly than it would to a rinse-off product, though the brand does not disclose glycerin's concentration here.

## What's In It

The full ingredient list (INCI), in order, as declared by the manufacturer and matched on Target's listing for the same formula.[^1][^2]

- water
- [[glycerin]], a humectant, second-listed
- butylene glycol (solvent/humectant)
- [[centella-asiatica]] extract, fourth-listed; concentration not disclosed
- 1,2-hexanediol (preservative booster)
- cellulose gum (thickener)
- ethylhexylglycerin (preservative booster/emollient)

Not in the formula: no added fragrance and no ingredients beyond these seven.[^1][^2]
