Aestura Atobarrier 365 Cream Mist is a South Korean facial spray from the ceramide-focused Atobarrier 365 line, sold in a 120 mL[^aestura] / 4.05 fl oz[^target] bottle.

> A spray-on companion to Aestura's [[aestura-atobarrier-365-cream|ATOBARRIER365 Cream]], marketed on a "10,000 ppm Ceramide" claim, but its own declared ingredient list names no ceramide; it lists cholesterol and behenic acid, two of the three physiologic barrier lipids, without the third ([see What's In It](#whats-in-it)). Its most reliably active ingredient by ingredient-list position is glycerin, a well-evidenced humectant, second only to water. As a fine mist rather than a leave-on cream, it also delivers less product per application than the line's namesake cream.[^aestura]

## Summary

Aestura Atobarrier 365 Cream Mist is a facial spray from [[aestura|Aestura]]'s Atobarrier 365 line, the same ceramide-focused family as the brand's [[aestura-atobarrier-365-cream|ATOBARRIER365 Cream]]. Aestura markets it as an "On-The-Go Moisturizer with 10,000 ppm Ceramide" that "forms a strong moisture film using highly concentrated ceramide with a special emulsifying process," and its own directions say to "hold the bottle approximately 30cm from your face and spray when skin feels dry," describing it as usable "as a hydrating toner AM & PM, or throughout the day as needed."[^aestura]

The declared INCI, however, names no ingredient with "ceramide" in it ([see What's In It](#whats-in-it)).[^aestura] What it does contain, further down the list, are cholesterol and behenic acid, a fatty acid, the other two of the three physiologic lipids that make up the skin barrier alongside ceramides; the ATOBARRIER365 Cream's own ingredient list, by contrast, opens its actives with Ceramide NP.[^aestura] The mist's most prominent functional ingredient by list position is glycerin, second only to water, a longer-established, well-evidenced humectant ([see The Evidence](#the-evidence)).[^aestura]

Target lists the 4.05 fl oz bottle at $35.99 under a listing titled "AtoBarrier 360 Cream Mist," matching Aestura's own product by size (120 mL / 4.05 fl oz) and its "10,000 ppm ceramide" marketing claim, as of 2026-08-07 ([see Where to Buy](#where-to-buy)).[^target]

## The Evidence

No published trial of this mist, or of the ATOBARRIER365 line generally, is cited here. Cholesterol is a genuine structural component of the skin barrier, but the cited barrier-repair experiments on [[cholesterol]] found that applying cholesterol alone, or paired with only one of the other two physiologic lipids, delayed rather than aided barrier recovery; only a complete, correctly proportioned mix of ceramides, cholesterol, and fatty acids restored it. This mist's declared INCI supplies cholesterol and one fatty acid (behenic acid) but no ceramide, so it does not match that complete mix. Glycerin and other humectants have solid, well-established category-level hydration evidence, set out on [[moisturizing]]; glycerin's position second on the ingredient list, ahead of every other named ingredient, is consistent with it doing a meaningful share of the formula's hydrating work.[^aestura]

## What's In It

The full ingredient list (INCI) as declared on Aestura's own product page, grouped by function below; the footnote preserves the verbatim declared order.[^aestura]

- Water, Glycerin, Butylene Glycol
- Caprylic/Capric Triglyceride, Hydrogenated Poly(C6-14 Olefin), Dimethicone, Cetyl Ethylhexanoate, emollients
- Hydroxypropyl Bislauramide MEA
- 1,2-Hexanediol, Sodium Surfactin, Glyceryl Caprylate, Ethylhexylglycerin, solvent/preservative-system components
- Cholesterol, a physiologic barrier lipid ([[cholesterol]])
- Disodium EDTA, a chelating agent
- Behenic Acid, a fatty acid
- Tocopherol (vitamin E)

The ingredient list declares no ingredient with "ceramide" in its INCI name.[^aestura] The brand states the formula is free of parabens, formaldehyde and formaldehyde-releasing agents, phthalates, mineral oil, retinyl palmitate, oxybenzone, coal tar, hydroquinone, SLS/SLES sulfates, triclocarban, and triclosan, and contains less than 1% synthetic fragrance.[^aestura]

## Common Marketing Claims

Label and marketing claims not established by the declared ingredient list above:

- **"10,000 ppm Ceramide"** and a formula that "forms a strong moisture film using highly concentrated ceramide with a special emulsifying process that binds finely dissected ceramide to moisture."[^aestura] The declared INCI names no ingredient with "ceramide" in it ([see What's In It](#whats-in-it)).
- **Multi-Lamellar Emulsion (MLE) technology that "mimics the structure of natural skin lipids" for "deeper penetration."**[^target] A brand/retailer-stated delivery-technology claim with no independently published test cited.
- **"Reduce redness and dryness caused by external aggressors."**[^target] An unproven marketing claim; no study is cited.
