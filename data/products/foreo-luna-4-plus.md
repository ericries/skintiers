The LUNA 4 plus is one of only two FOREO devices whose FDA file you can actually read, and reading it answers a question the rest of this brand's pages could not. **Bottom line:** a 510(k) Summary buys you transparency, not evidence. You learn the predicates, the wavelengths and the standards, and you also learn that the testing was explicitly non-clinical.[^k241102] Category evidence is on [[home-led-light-therapy]]; the maker on [[foreo]].

> **Three functions, and the headline one has no cleared claim.** The indications line reads: "Luna 4 plus is a Near-Infrared Heated Cleansing Device With Red LED/NIR light & Microcurrent Massage." Then the actual indications: "Microcurrent: indicated for facial stimulation" and "RED+NIR: indicated to treat periorbital wrinkles".[^k241102] Cleansing is in the device's name and its description, and nowhere in what it is cleared to do.

## Summary

Cleared 2024-08-20 as K241102, one of FOREO's two Summary filings out of ten clearances.[^openfda] It emits "visible red/IR light (Red: 630nm +/- 10nm, NIR 830nm +/-10nm.) for elevating tissue temperature in improving appearance of facial wrinkles", and carries "Two microcurrent pins at the back for a targeted toning massage".[^k241102] Note the stated mechanism for the light: elevating tissue temperature. No price is quoted here; no brand page was fetched and no metered lookup was spent.

## The Rubric

**Periorbital wrinkles, with red plus near-infrared**
- **Effect size: minimal.** The claim is scoped to the eye area, not the face, which is the second device this site has covered whose wrinkle clearance stops at periorbital; the other is on [[home-fractional-nonablative-laser]].[^k241102] LED for facial rejuvenation is Level B in the 2026 review, behind fractional nonablative lasers at Level A.[^grade]
- **Evidence quality: preliminary.** The file's testing section is headed "Non-Clinical Testing" and lists electrical safety, EMC, biocompatibility, bench characterisation of the electrical stimulation output, and usability studies.[^k241102] No clinical outcome appears.

**Facial stimulation, by microcurrent**
- **Effect size: none that can be stated.** "Microcurrent: indicated for facial stimulation" describes the current, not the skin.[^k241102] A cleared indication phrased this way commits to no visible change, which is worth recognising when marketing for this category promises lifting or toning. Microcurrent evidence is graded on [[nuface-trinity-microcurrent-device]].
- **Evidence quality: preliminary.** Same non-clinical basis.[^k241102]

**Cleansing**
- **Effect size: not gradeable; there is no claim.** The word appears in the device name and description but not among the indications.[^k241102]
- **Evidence quality: anecdotal.** Nothing to assess.

**No blue light at all.** The emitters are red and near-infrared.[^k241102] The best-graded home modality for acne is blue, or blue combined with red, so nothing here speaks to acne, and the device makes no acne claim.[^grade]

## The Evidence

**What the Summary actually discloses, and it is a real improvement.** Three predicates are named with their product codes: K162652 from Guangzhou LETA Testing Technology Co., Ltd under codes OHS, NFO and OLP; K201906 from "Carol Cole Company dba NuFACE" under code NFO; and K212155 from Theragun, Inc. under codes OHS, OLP and IRO.[^k241102] Compare [[foreo-espada-blue-light-pen]], a Statement filing where none of that is public. Credit where it is due: on this device, FOREO showed its work.

**And what showing its work reveals.** The equivalence argument rests on standards compliance and bench testing, under a heading that says so: "Non-Clinical Testing". The file states that "LUNA 4 Plus underwent lab bench performance testing to characterize its electrical stimulation output, including performance testing of the Anti-Shock System and performance under overload conditions", and that "Usability studies conducted demonstrate that the device is substantially equivalent to the predicate given the new indications".[^k241102] Usability means people could operate it, not that their skin changed. So the Summary answers the question this brand's other pages had to leave open: disclosure changes what a reader can verify, not what the device has been shown to do.

**It is built on NuFACE's clearance.** K201906 is the NuFACE Trinity ELE Plus Pro, from a firm whose 14 clearances are all Summaries.[^openfda] The microcurrent lineage here runs through that device, which is graded on [[nuface-trinity-microcurrent-device]] and whose maker is on [[nuface]].

**Medical note.** This is educational content, not a substitute for a clinician. A device that works by "elevating tissue temperature" near the eyes is one to use per its instructions; the cleared wrinkle indication is periorbital, and nothing here is a treatment for acne or any skin disease.
