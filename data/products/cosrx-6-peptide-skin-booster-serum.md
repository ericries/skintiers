COSRX The 6 Peptide Skin Booster Serum is a lightweight, watery leave-on serum whose ingredient list combines six peptides (led by acetyl hexapeptide-8 and copper tripeptide-1) with niacinamide, humectants (glycerin and sodium hyaluronate), and a set of skin-identical amino acids.[^1]

> Whether topical peptides visibly reduce lines or firm skin is a preliminary question, covered on [[peptides]]. For the peptide this serum leads with, acetyl hexapeptide-8, a 2025 review found its penetration through the skin barrier is limited and its delivery to the sites it targets "remains uncertain."[^2] The serum's humectant base has a clearer basis for short-term hydration (see [[moisturizing]]).[^1]

## The Evidence

Whether topical peptides prevent or reverse signs of aging is covered on [[peptides]]. The evidence relevant to this serum is at the ingredient level.

Griffiths, Watson and Langton (2023) review topical treatments for aging skin and call topical tretinoin "a prescription-only medicine considered to be the clinical 'gold standard'." They note that a "cosmeceutical" category "at the midpoint of the spectrum of cosmetics and pharmaceutical" has emerged, among which they review retinoids, peptides, and antioxidants.[^4]

> **Bottom line.** In the dermatology literature's own framing, the strongest topical anti-aging evidence is for tretinoin; peptides sit among the reviewed cosmeceutical-tier options, not the established treatments.

Acetyl hexapeptide-8 (Argireline) is the "botox-like" peptide this serum names first. Zdrada-Nowak and colleagues (2025) reviewed its skin permeability and efficacy and reported that, "due to its hydrophilic nature and relatively large molecular size, AH-8 faces limited permeability through the lipophilic stratum corneum, making effective dermal delivery challenging," that its ability to reach neuromuscular junctions "remains uncertain," and that the mechanism behind its effects "remain[s] incompletely understood." They wrote that "preclinical and clinical studies indicate that AH-8 may reduce wrinkle depth, improve skin elasticity, and enhance hydration."[^2]

> **Bottom line.** Even for the peptide this serum leads with, the 2025 review found topical delivery is limited and the anti-wrinkle evidence is preliminary and conditional ("may reduce wrinkle depth").

Gorouhi and Maibach (2009) group topical cosmetic peptides into four types (signal, enzyme-inhibitor, neurotransmitter-inhibitor, and carrier peptides) and review the controlled ex vivo and in vivo efficacy studies behind them.[^3] This serum draws from several of those types.

> **Bottom line.** Topical peptides are organized by their proposed mechanism, and this serum combines peptides from several of those groups; the review surveys the category rather than singling out an established anti-aging peptide.

The serum's base is built on humectants: glycerin, sodium hyaluronate, and skin-identical amino acids and natural-moisturizing-factor components (serine, glycine, sodium PCA, sodium lactate).[^1] Humectant hydration is well-established and is covered on [[moisturizing]].

## What's In It

The full ingredient list (INCI) as declared by the manufacturer.[^1] The ingredients with a functional role are linked to their own pages; the rest are the base, texture, and preservative system.

Peptides and actives:

- six [[peptides]]: acetyl hexapeptide-8 (Argireline), copper tripeptide-1 (GHK-Cu), sh-polypeptide-121, dipeptide diaminobutyroyl benzylamide diacetate, oligopeptide-68, and palmitoyl tripeptide-8
- [[niacinamide]], sixth on the ingredient list
- sodium hyaluronate ([[hyaluronic-acid]]) and [[glycerin]], humectants
- acetyl glucosamine (NAG), allantoin, and adenosine
- skin-identical amino acids and natural-moisturizing-factor humectants: serine, alanine, glycine, threonine, arginine, proline, betaine, sodium PCA, sodium lactate, and PCA
- [[tocopherol]] (vitamin E), an antioxidant

Base, texture, and preservative system: water, dipropylene glycol, pentylene glycol, 1,2-hexanediol and butylene glycol (solvents and humectant-solvents), ammonium acryloyldimethyltaurate/VP copolymer, polyacrylate crosspolymer-6 and xanthan gum (thickeners), dextran, hydrogenated lecithin, glycine soja (soybean) oil, polyquaternium-51, sodium oleate, disodium EDTA (chelator), citric acid (pH), and ethylhexylglycerin, caprylyl glycol, t-butyl alcohol, and potassium sorbate (preservatives).

The ingredient list declares no added fragrance or essential oils.[^1]
