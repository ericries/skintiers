Head & Shoulders Clinical Strength Dandruff Defense (Intensive Itch Relief) is an over-the-counter medicated shampoo built around selenium sulfide 1%, the same antifungal class as [[selsun-blue-selenium-sulfide-shampoo|Selsun Blue's selenium sulfide shampoo]], in a cooling menthol-and-peppermint base.[^1]

> An OTC drug, not a cosmetic: the FDA Drug Facts label lists the active as "Selenium Sulfide 1%" with the purpose "Anti-dandruff, anti-seborrheic dermatitis."[^1] Selenium sulfide is one of the best-evidenced antidandruff antifungals, ranked strong on [[anti-dandruff-seborrheic-dermatitis-treatments-by-evidence]]. This particular formula adds menthol and peppermint oil for a cooling "itch relief" sensation, which is sensory, not extra antifungal power, and it uses a standard sulfate (SLS) surfactant base.

## Summary

This is the selenium sulfide member of the Head & Shoulders line, distinct from the brand's more familiar pyrithione zinc shampoos. It is regulated as an OTC drug: the Drug Facts label states the active as "Selenium Sulfide 1%," the purpose as "Anti-dandruff, anti-seborrheic dermatitis," and the use as helping "prevent recurrence of flaking, itching, irritation, scaling and redness associated with dandruff and seborrheic dermatitis."[^1]

Selenium sulfide's evidence sits at the ingredient-class level, set out on [[seborrheic-dermatitis]] and ranked on [[anti-dandruff-seborrheic-dermatitis-treatments-by-evidence]], where it is a first-line topical antifungal alongside [[ketoconazole]]. No trial of this exact shampoo was located, so the dandruff grade rests on that active plus its FDA monograph status, not on a product-specific study. The menthol and peppermint oil give the "intensive itch relief" cooling feel but do not add antifungal effect.

## The Evidence

Two health uses, graded above:

**Dandruff and seborrheic dermatitis (the labeled use).** This is what the product is approved and labeled for. Selenium sulfide is an FDA OTC-monograph anti-dandruff and anti-seborrheic-dermatitis active, and it is one of the best-evidenced options in its class: a 2026 randomized trial found a 0.6% selenium disulfide shampoo statistically matched 2% ketoconazole, the detail carried on [[farris-2026-beyond-efficacy-0-6-selenium-disulfide-shampoo-matches-2-k]].[^1] The caveat specific to this product is that no trial tested this exact 1% formulation.

**Fungal acne (off-label, mechanistic).** Malassezia folliculitis responds to antifungals, and selenium sulfide is one of the better off-label washes for it, but that use is not on this product's label and the general [[fungal-acne]] evidence is what supports it, not a study of this shampoo.

> **Bottom line.** A well-formulated selenium sulfide 1% antidandruff shampoo whose labeled dandruff and seborrheic-dermatitis use is strongly supported at the ingredient level.[^1] The cooling menthol base is a comfort feature, not added treatment; judge it as a solid, first-line-class antidandruff option, interchangeable with other selenium sulfide shampoos on the active.

## What's In It

As an OTC drug the label separates the active from everything else. Active ingredient: "Selenium Sulfide 1%."[^1]

Inactive ingredients, verbatim from the Drug Facts label: "Water, sodium lauryl sulfate, glycol distearate, sodium chloride, cocamidopropyl betaine, sodium citrate, sodium xylenesulfonate, dimethicone, menthol, fragrance, citric acid, sodium benzoate, tetrasodium EDTA, hydroxypropyl methylcellulose, mentha piperita (peppermint) oil, mentha arvensis leaf oil."[^1]

The base uses a standard sulfate surfactant (sodium lauryl sulfate) rather than a sulfate-free system, plus menthol and two mint oils that produce the cooling "itch relief" sensation and the added fragrance.[^1]

## Common Marketing Claims

- **"Clinical Strength" / "Intensive Itch Relief."** The active is selenium sulfide 1%, a standard OTC-monograph concentration; the "itch relief" is driven by menthol and peppermint (a cooling sensation), supported for the labeled antidandruff use at the active level, not by a trial of this product.[^1]
- **Off-label "fungal acne" use.** Plausible on mechanism (selenium sulfide is antifungal) but not on the product's OTC label and documented on [[fungal-acne]] at the ingredient level, not for this shampoo.
