BYOMA Moisturizing Rich Cream is a leave-on facial moisturizer from the UK skincare brand BYOMA, sold in a 50 mL / 1.69 fl oz pump bottle for $15.99.[^byoma]

> BYOMA markets this as a richer, more occlusive companion to its original Moisturizing Cream, built around what it calls a "Tri-Ceramide Complex (ceramides, cholesterol, fatty acids)."[^byoma] Its own declared ingredient list backs that up: Ceramide NP, cholesterol, and multiple fatty acids (palmitic acid, stearic acid, oleic acid) are all present, the same three-lipid combination shown to restore a damaged skin barrier in the cited experiments on [[ceramides]] and [[cholesterol]] ([see What's In It](#whats-in-it)). Glycerin, a well-evidenced humectant, sits second on the list after water. Bakuchiol, the ingredient BYOMA highlights alongside shea in its marketing copy, appears much further down the list, after several stabilizers and solvents.[^byoma]

## Summary

BYOMA Moisturizing Rich Cream is positioned by the brand as a cream that "melts into skin to help soothe and intensely hydrate from first use while protecting the skin barrier," built on what BYOMA calls its "Tri-Ceramide Complex (ceramides, cholesterol, fatty acids)" and "enriched with soothing, restorative bakuchiol and shea extracts."[^byoma] The brand also describes it as "dermatologist tested and approved, non-comedogenic, alcohol and fragrance-free, vegan and cruelty free."[^byoma]

BYOMA's own product-page directions say to apply "1-2 pumps on cleansed skin following serum application AM + PM" and to smooth it onto skin until fully absorbed.[^byoma]

The declared INCI supports the barrier-lipid part of the marketing story more directly than most single-ceramide "barrier cream" competitors: it lists Ceramide NP, Cholesterol, and several fatty acids, the complete three-lipid set that the cited barrier-repair literature associates with restoring, rather than merely slowing, moisture loss ([see The Evidence](#the-evidence)). Glycerin, a longer-established humectant, is the second-listed ingredient overall, ahead of every active. Bakuchiol, which BYOMA calls out by name in its marketing copy, is listed well down the formula, grouped with antioxidants alongside tocopherol and olive fruit oil rather than near the top of the list ([see What's In It](#whats-in-it)).[^byoma]

BYOMA's own site lists the 50 mL / 1.69 fl oz bottle at $15.99, as of 2026-08-23 ([see Where to Buy](#where-to-buy)).[^byoma]

## The Evidence

No published clinical trial of this specific product is cited here. The general case for a combined ceramide/cholesterol/fatty-acid approach to barrier repair, including the barrier-repair experiments showing that a complete three-lipid mix restores barrier function better than any one or two lipids alone, is set out on [[ceramides]] and [[cholesterol]]; this formula's declared INCI supplies all three components of that mix. Glycerin's category-level hydration evidence is set out on [[glycerin]]. Bakuchiol's evidence, mostly from studies using it as a retinol-alternative at specified concentrations for antiaging and antioxidant effects, is set out on [[bakuchiol]]; because BYOMA does not disclose a concentration and the ingredient sits well down this formula's list, the evidence base on that page describes a different context (higher, disclosed concentrations) than what a low-ranked listing here implies.

## What's In It

The full ingredient list (INCI), grouped by the function labels BYOMA itself uses on its product page; the footnote preserves the verbatim declared order.[^byoma]

- Water (carrier)
- Glycerin (humectant)
- Caprylic/Capric Triglyceride, Butyrospermum Parkii (Shea) Butter, Caprylyl Glycol, Ethylhexylglycerin, Ceramide NP, Cholesterol, Phytosphingosine, Dipotassium Glycyrrhizate (skin-conditioning agents, per BYOMA's own labeling; includes the barrier lipids [[ceramides|Ceramide NP]] and [[cholesterol]])
- Cetearyl Alcohol (emulsion stabilizer)
- Cetearyl Olivate, Sorbitan Olivate, Palmitic Acid, Stearic Acid, Sorbitan Isostearate, Hydrogenated Lecithin (surfactants)
- Dipropylene Glycol (solvent)
- Hydroxyethyl Acrylate/Sodium Acryloyldimethyl Taurate Copolymer, Acrylates/C10-30 Alkyl Acrylate Crosspolymer (viscosity controllers)
- Hydroxyacetophenone, Olea Europaea (Olive) Fruit Oil, [[bakuchiol|Bakuchiol]], Tocopherol (antioxidants, per BYOMA's own labeling)
- Tromethamine, Lactic Acid (pH adjusters)
- Disodium EDTA, Caprylhydroxamic Acid (chelating agents)
- Oleic Acid (emollient)

BYOMA states the formula is alcohol-free and fragrance-free, non-comedogenic, vegan, and cruelty-free.[^byoma]
