The INKEY List Oat Cleansing Balm is an oil-to-milk cleansing balm built on sweet almond oil, meant as the first step of a [[double-cleansing|double cleanse]] to dissolve makeup and sunscreen before a water-based wash. The brand lists the 150 mL jar at $17.00.[^inkey]

> A cleansing balm is judged by how well it removes what a water-based cleanser leaves behind, and an oil-based balm like this is genuinely the right tool for the job: the trial evidence for oil cleansers clearing water-resistant sunscreen and long-wear makeup is on [[double-cleansing]]. The oat here, oat kernel oil and oat kernel flour, is colloidal oatmeal, a recognized skin soother (see [[colloidal-oatmeal]]), which is a reasonable touch for sensitive skin but not the reason to buy a cleanser. Read the brand's "removes 100% of waterproof makeup and SPF in 30 seconds" and "clinically proven 12hr hydration" as its own unpublished testing, and note that the current "new and improved" version the brand advertises with 5% oat kernel oil differs from the 3% listed on the ingredient breakdown below.

## Summary

The INKEY List markets this as a balm that "melts away makeup & SPF in 30 seconds," is "clinically proven" for "12hr hydration," and is "gentle on sensitive skin," with a headline claim that it "removes 100% of waterproof makeup and SPF in 30 seconds."[^inkey] It is a solid balm that warms and emulsifies on contact with water, rinsing off as a milk.

The formula leads with sweet almond oil, cetearyl alcohol, and two PEG glyceride emulsifiers (PEG-6 caprylic/capric glycerides and PEG-60 almond glycerides) that are what let an oil balm rinse clean rather than leaving a greasy film; candelilla wax gives it the solid balm texture, and it carries oat kernel oil and oat kernel flour as [[colloidal-oatmeal|colloidal oatmeal]] plus vitamin E and sunflower oil. It is listed at $17.00 for the 150 mL jar at the brand site ([see Where to Buy](#where-to-buy), accessed 2026-09-24).[^inkey]

## The Evidence

The product-specific claim worth checking is the removal one, and it is directionally right for the format: as set out on [[double-cleansing]], an oil-based cleanser removes water-resistant sunscreen that a foaming water-based cleanser leaves largely behind, so an oil balm is a sound first step on heavy-sunscreen or long-wear-makeup days (and unnecessary on a bare day). The brand's specific 100-percent, 30-second removal and 12hr-hydration figures are its own testing, not independently published.[^inkey] The oat content is colloidal oatmeal, an over-the-counter skin protectant with genuine soothing evidence set out on [[colloidal-oatmeal]], but it sits in a rinse-off product, so its leave-on soothing benefit is limited by contact time.

> **Bottom line.** A competent, oil-based first-step cleansing balm whose real value is dissolving makeup and sunscreen, a job the double-cleanse evidence supports, with colloidal oat as a mild soothing extra. It is a cleanser, not a treatment; the 12hr-hydration and 100-percent-removal numbers are brand claims,[^inkey] and if you do not wear water-resistant sunscreen or long-wear makeup, a single gentle cleanse does the same job.

## What's In It

The full ingredient list (INCI), as decoded by incidecoder from the product's packaging. Sweet almond oil leads, with the oat actives and emulsifiers following.

Prunus Amygdalus Dulcis (Sweet Almond) Oil, Cetearyl Alcohol, PEG-6 Caprylic/Capric Glycerides, Avena Sativa (Oat) Kernel Oil 3.0%, Candelilla Cera (Cire De Candelilla), Silica, Sorbitan Stearate, Tribehenin, PEG-60 Almond Glycerides, Avena Sativa (Oat) Kernel Flour 1.0%, Aqua (Water/Eau), Benzyl Alcohol, Phenoxyethanol, Lecithin, 1,2-Hexanediol, Ascorbyl Palmitate, Tocopherol, Biosaccharide Gum-4, Helianthus Annuus (Sunflower) Seed Oil.

Notable: sweet almond oil is the emollient base; PEG-6 caprylic/capric glycerides and PEG-60 almond glycerides are the emulsifiers that let the oil rinse off; oat kernel oil (3.0%) and oat kernel flour (1.0%) are the [[colloidal-oatmeal|colloidal oatmeal]]; candelilla wax sets the balm texture; ascorbyl palmitate and tocopherol are antioxidants. The brand's current "new and improved" version advertises 5% oat kernel oil, above the 3% on this decoded list, so the exact oat percentage depends on the version.
