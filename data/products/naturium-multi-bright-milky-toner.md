Naturium Multi-Bright Milky Toner is a mass-market leave-on toner built around potassium azeloyl diglycinate, a water-soluble azelaic acid derivative, paired with a fermented grape extract and rice-derived ingredients, sold in one 4.0 fl oz (120 ml) size directly from the brand and at Walmart.[^naturium][^walmart]

> A budget "brightening" toner whose named active is not azelaic acid itself but a derivative salt of it, potassium azeloyl diglycinate, that has its own small independent trials for facial redness and melasma, always in combination with other actives and never at the concentration or in the format used here (the brand discloses no percentage). The general azelaic acid case, which does not directly transfer to this derivative, is set out on [[azelaic-acid]]. No published trial tests this exact product.[^naturium]

## Summary

Multi-Bright Milky Toner is Naturium's toner-format entry in its "Multi-Bright" line, led by potassium azeloyl diglycinate, described on the brand's product page as an "azelaic acid derivative," alongside a fermented grape extract complex, the amino acid arginine, a rice milk blend, and licorice root extract.[^naturium] It is positioned for "gentle yet effective exfoliation," smoother texture, and reduced appearance of dark spots, applied after cleansing and left on the skin.[^naturium]

Potassium azeloyl diglycinate (also called azeloglycine or PAD) is chemically related to but distinct from [[azelaic-acid|azelaic acid]], the ingredient with controlled trial evidence for rosacea, acne, and melasma at prescription strengths set out on that page. PAD has its own, much smaller published evidence base: an independent placebo-controlled pilot trial found a 5% PAD cream combined with 1% hydroxypropyl chitosan reduced facial stinging, burning, and erythema in patients with rosacea (stages I and II), and a separate randomized trial found a combination product containing PAD plus niacinamide and another active outperformed a control emulsion on melasma severity after 6 to 8 weeks.[^pad-rosacea][^pad-melasma] Naturium's toner does not disclose what percentage of PAD it contains, does not include hydroxypropyl chitosan, and is not marketed for rosacea, so those trial results do not transfer directly to this product ([see The Evidence](#the-evidence)).

The formula also contains a fermented grape/yeast extract complex with arginine, rice extract and rice bran oil, and licorice root extract, ingredients the brand ties to smoothing texture and conditioning the skin barrier rather than to a specific studied active.[^naturium] The brand lists it at $17 for the 4.0 fl oz size; Walmart independently lists the same product, in the same size, at $11.99 as of 2026-08-23.[^naturium][^walmart]

## The Evidence

Azelaic acid itself has controlled clinical evidence for papulopustular rosacea, acne, and melasma at prescription strengths, detailed on [[azelaic-acid]]. Potassium azeloyl diglycinate is a related but chemically distinct salt (a diglycine conjugate of azelaic acid), and its published evidence is smaller and separate: a placebo-controlled pilot trial used a cream with 5% PAD combined with 1% hydroxypropyl chitosan and found it reduced erythema, stinging, and burning in patients with rosacea (stages I and II), and a randomized controlled trial found a combination emulsion containing PAD along with niacinamide and a third compound reduced melasma severity scores more than a control emulsion over 6 to 8 weeks.[^pad-rosacea][^pad-melasma] In each case PAD was tested as part of a fixed combination with other actives, never alone, and always at a disclosed 5%.

Naturium's toner does not state what percentage of PAD it contains, places it seventh on the ingredient list behind five texture and emollient ingredients, and does not contain hydroxypropyl chitosan or niacinamide alongside it.[^naturium] No published, independent trial tests this exact toner. Given the undisclosed concentration and the absence of the specific co-ingredients used in the published PAD trials, the grade above rests on preliminary, indirect evidence rather than a demonstrated effect for this formula.

## What's In It

The full ingredient list (INCI) as declared on the manufacturer's product page.[^naturium]

Named actives:

- Potassium azeloyl diglycinate, described by the brand as "an azelaic acid derivative" (see [[azelaic-acid]] for the parent compound's evidence)
- Saccharomyces ferment and Vitis vinifera (grape) fruit extract, the brand's "fermented grape extract complex"
- Arginine, an amino acid the brand pairs with the grape/yeast complex
- Oryza sativa (rice) extract and rice bran oil, the brand's "rice milk blend"
- Glycyrrhiza glabra (licorice) root extract

Base, texture, and preservative system: water, isopropyl palmitate, sorbitol, triethylhexanoin, methyl glucose dioleate, glycerin, propanediol, squalane, hydrogenated polyisobutene, hydrogenated lecithin, gluconolactone, corn starch, lauryl glucoside, calcium gluconate, tocopherol (vitamin E), acacia senegal gum, polyquaternium-39, polyglyceryl-6 laurate, polyglyceryl-4 caprate, myristyl glucoside, acrylates/C10-30 alkyl acrylate crosspolymer, polyquaternium-51, xanthan gum, tetrasodium glutamate diacetate, ethylhexylglycerin, potassium sorbate, sodium benzoate, sodium hydroxide, and phenoxyethanol.[^naturium]

The manufacturer lists the product's pH as 4.75 to 5.25 and states it is cruelty free, paraben free, vegan, gluten free, and fragrance free.[^naturium]

## Common Marketing Claims

Label and marketing language not established by the graded evidence above:

- **"Gentle yet effective exfoliation suitable for all skin types."** No independent test of this formula's exfoliating effect was found; the ingredients cited for texture (fermented grape/yeast extract, rice extract) are not established chemical exfoliants in the way an AHA or BHA is.[^naturium]
- **"Helps improve the appearance of uneven tone & texture"** and **"reduce the look of dark spots."** These rest on potassium azeloyl diglycinate at an undisclosed percentage; the ingredient's own published trials, described in [The Evidence](#the-evidence), used a disclosed 5% dose combined with other actives this toner does not contain.[^naturium]
- **"Hydrates & calms for soft, glowing skin."** No independent test of this formula's hydration or calming effect on skin was found.[^naturium]
