Vanicream Moisturizing Cream for Dry, Sensitive Skin (Vanicream) is a fragrance-free, petrolatum-based cream positioned for dry, sensitive skin, including skin associated with eczema, psoriasis, and ichthyosis.[^1]

> A plain, fragrance-free occlusive moisturizer built to avoid common irritants: petrolatum, the strongest-rated occlusive on [[moisturizing]], is the second-listed ingredient, and the cream carries the National Eczema Association Seal of Acceptance.[^1][^2] No trial isolates this exact cream. Moisturizing this way is one of dermatology's better-evidenced routines for dry and eczema-prone skin, covered on [[moisturizing]] and [[atopic-dermatitis]]; this cream is a faithful, plain example of that category rather than a formula with an added treatment active.

## Summary

Vanicream Moisturizing Cream is a fragrance-free cream made for dry, sensitive skin, including skin managing eczema, psoriasis, ichthyosis, and "winter itch," and the manufacturer states it is appropriate for all ages, including children and infants.[^1] Its [ingredient list](#whats-in-it) is short and occlusive-forward: water, petrolatum, sorbitol, cetearyl alcohol, propylene glycol, ceteareth-20, simethicone, glyceryl stearate, PEG-30 stearate, and sorbic acid.[^1][^2] Target's listing confirms the same ingredient list and adds that the product is free of "dyes, fragrance, masking fragrance, lanolin, parabens, and formaldehyde releasers."[^2]

What distinguishes it from a scented drugstore lotion is again mainly what it leaves out: no fragrance or masking fragrance, no dye, no lanolin, no parabens, no formaldehyde-releasing preservatives.[^1][^2] Unlike [[cerave-moisturizing-cream]], this formula carries no added ceramides or hyaluronic acid; the barrier-support job here rests on petrolatum plus the humectants sorbitol and propylene glycol.[^1][^2]

The cream has been awarded the National Eczema Association Seal of Acceptance and is described by the manufacturer as "dermatologist tested."[^1] Target's listing also carries the brand-wide claim "#1 Dermatologist-Recommended Brand for Sensitive Skin."[^2] These are formulation-review designations and manufacturer/retailer claims, not a clinical trial of this product; no product-specific trial is [cited below](#the-evidence). Moisturizing dry and eczema-prone skin is a well-evidenced dermatology routine covered on [[moisturizing]] and [[atopic-dermatitis]], where a Cochrane review and the mechanism for occlusives like [[petrolatum]] are set out.

It is inexpensive. Target lists the 16 oz pump at $13.19 ([see Where to Buy](#where-to-buy), accessed 2026-08-07).[^2] It carries no drug active and no added treatment ingredient beyond the occlusive/humectant base; its positioning is tolerability and barrier support rather than a targeted cosmetic or medical claim.

## The Evidence

No trial isolates this cream. Its composition matches what the general moisturizing evidence covers: a leave-on cream led by [[petrolatum]], the strongest-rated occlusive on that page, plus the humectants sorbitol and propylene glycol.[^1][^2] Moisturizing dry, barrier-impaired, and eczema-prone skin this way is one of the better-evidenced routines in dermatology, reducing flares and topical corticosteroid use per a Cochrane review; that evidence and the occlusive/humectant mechanism are set out on [[moisturizing]] and [[atopic-dermatitis]]. The manufacturer states the cream was awarded the National Eczema Association Seal of Acceptance and describes it as "dermatologist tested," but no independent trial data, methodology, or review criteria for that designation are reported on the pages cited here.[^1]

## What's In It

The full ingredient list (INCI) as declared by the manufacturer and matched on Target's listing.[^1][^2]

- water
- [[petrolatum]], an occlusive and the second-listed ingredient
- sorbitol (humectant)
- cetearyl alcohol (emollient/thickener)
- propylene glycol (humectant)
- ceteareth-20 (emulsifier)
- simethicone (skin-protectant/smoothing agent)
- glyceryl stearate (emulsifier)
- PEG-30 stearate (emulsifier)
- sorbic acid (preservative)

Not in the formula: no added fragrance or masking fragrance, no dyes, no lanolin, no parabens, and no formaldehyde-releasing preservatives.[^1][^2]
