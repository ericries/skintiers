La Roche-Posay Toleriane Double Repair is a fragrance-free, oil-free daily face moisturizer built on glycerin, niacinamide, and ceramide NP in the brand's prebiotic thermal water base. Ulta, an authorized La Roche-Posay retailer, lists the 3.38 oz size at a regular price of $25.99 (recently discounted to $19.49).

> The "Double Repair" name points to the two barrier-supporting actives the formula leads with: [[niacinamide]] (fifth on the ingredient list) and ceramide NP (see [[ceramides]]), on top of [[glycerin]], a humectant that sits second. Those three are genuinely well-evidenced for hydration and supporting the skin barrier, which is what this product is for; it is a solid, unfragranced basic moisturizer, not an anti-aging treatment. The brand's headline claims of 48-hour hydration and barrier repair within an hour are its own unpublished testing, and no concentration is disclosed for any active, so the ingredient-level evidence on the linked pages is the honest guide rather than the label numbers.

## Summary

La Roche-Posay markets Toleriane Double Repair as an oil-free, prebiotic daily face moisturizer for sensitive skin that provides 48-hour hydration, restores the skin barrier within an hour, and offers prebiotic benefits from a high concentration of its thermal spring water; it is described as non-comedogenic and dermatologist, allergy, and sensitive-skin tested (brand claims, per the Ulta listing, accessed 2026-09-24). It is fragrance-free, oil-free, and paraben-free.

Read against the ingredient list, the formula leads with water (the brand's prebiotic thermal water), glycerin, dimethicone, and hydrogenated polyisobutene, then niacinamide fifth, with ceramide NP further down alongside fatty acids (stearic, myristic, palmitic) and vitamin E (tocopherol) at the end. The genuinely evidence-backed actives are the humectant [[glycerin]], [[niacinamide]], and the barrier lipid in [[ceramides]]; the silicone dimethicone acts as an occlusive that slows water loss. It is listed at $25.99 for the 3.38 oz size at Ulta ([see Where to Buy](#where-to-buy), accessed 2026-09-24).

## The Evidence

No independently published controlled trial of this exact moisturizer is cited here; the brand's 48-hour hydration and 1-hour barrier-repair figures come from testing La Roche-Posay has not published for outside review. What the product has going for it is ingredient-level evidence, set out on the linked pages: [[glycerin]] is a well-established humectant, [[niacinamide]] has mid-tier evidence for barrier support and soothing, and the ceramide it contains is one of the skin's own barrier lipids (see [[ceramides]]). None of these concentrations is disclosed, so the strength of that general evidence cannot be assumed to transfer at whatever dose is used here.

> **Bottom line.** A well-formulated, fragrance-free daily moisturizer whose barrier-supporting actives (glycerin, niacinamide, ceramide) are genuinely evidence-backed for hydration and barrier support, at undisclosed concentrations. It is a sound basic moisturizer, not an anti-aging product; for the best-evidenced anti-aging active see [[retinoids]], which this does not contain.
