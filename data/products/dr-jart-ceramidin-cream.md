## Summary

Dr. Jart+ Ceramidin Cream is a face moisturizer from the South Korean brand [[dr-jart|Dr. Jart+]], sold at Target under the title "Dr. Jart + Ceramidin Skin Barrier Moisturizing Cream For Dry Skin," in a 1.69 oz jar.[^target] Target's listing describes it as "packed with a powerful blend of five ceramides that work synergistically to deeply hydrate and fortify your skin's natural moisture barrier," naming Ceramide NP, NS, AS, EOP, and AP, combined with panthenol and hyaluronic acid.[^target]

Ceramides are structural lipids of the skin's outer barrier, and topical ceramide moisturizers have been shown in a controlled trial to lower transepidermal water loss and raise hydration measurements compared with a matched base, without a measurable edge on eczema severity itself; that general case, with its numbers and limits, is set out on [[ceramides]]. Hyaluronic acid separately has a well-evidenced case for raising a measured skin-hydration reading, covered on [[hyaluronic-acid]]. Neither ingredient's evidence is specific to this product: the Target listing used here names the actives but does not publish a full declared ingredient (INCI) list, so [the formula's actual order and concentration of ingredients cannot be independently confirmed from the source cited here](#whats-in-it), and no independently published trial of this exact cream is cited.

Target's listing also carries cosmetic language, describing "a noticeable improvement in your skin's texture and resilience" and calling the cream suitable for both morning and night use; those are [reported below as retailer marketing copy, not independently verified findings](#common-marketing-claims).[^target]

It sits at the premium end for a 1.69 oz face cream. Target lists it at $51.99, discounted from a regular price of $68.00 (see [Where to Buy](#where-to-buy), accessed 2026-08-07).[^target]
