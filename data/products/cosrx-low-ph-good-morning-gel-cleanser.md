COSRX Low pH Good Morning Gel Cleanser is a South Korean gel facial cleanser built around a mildly acidic, manufacturer-labeled pH and mild surfactants, sold in 150 mL and 50 mL sizes.[^1]

> A low-pH, sulfate-free gel cleanser from the same brand as the [[cosrx-advanced-snail-96-mucin-power-essence|Advanced Snail 96 Mucin Power Essence]], positioned mainly as a gentle daily wash rather than a treatment product: the tea tree oil and betaine salicylate on its ingredient list sit near the bottom, far below the mild betaine and isethionate surfactants that do the actual cleansing.[^1][^2]

## Summary

COSRX Low pH Good Morning Gel Cleanser is a gel-textured facial wash that COSRX describes as a "gentle gel type cleanser with mildly acidic pH level," stating it will "help cleanse with no stripping" while "refining the skin texture."[^1] COSRX's own product page states a pH of "5.31±1.00"; Target's listing independently states a pH range of 5.3 to 6.3 for the same product, a corroborating range.[^1][^2] It is sold on COSRX's own site in 5.07 fl oz / 150 mL and 1.69 fl oz / 50 mL sizes; Target lists the 150 mL size at $14.00, matching the $14.00 shown on the brand's own site ([see Where to Buy](#where-to-buy), accessed 2026-08-07).[^1][^2]

The formula's surfactant base is cocamidopropyl betaine and sodium lauroyl methyl isethionate, both mild cleansing agents, with no sulfate surfactant on the declared ingredient list, consistent with Target's description of the product as "sulfate-free."[^1][^2] That composition, together with the labeled low pH, is the product's main distinguishing feature against a plainer, higher-pH gel or foam cleanser; no independent trial of this cleanser measures barrier or irritation outcomes, so that comparison rests on composition, not a clinical result ([see The Evidence](#the-evidence)).

Further down the ingredient list, the formula also declares tea tree leaf oil (Melaleuca Alternifolia) and betaine salicylate, a salicylic-acid derivative that Target's listing separately calls out as a "Beta Hydroxy Acid (BHA)."[^1][^2] Betaine salicylate sits nineteenth of twenty-five ingredients, immediately after the preservative boosters Caprylyl Glycol and Ethylhexylglycerin and before the citric acid/preservative system, and neither source discloses its concentration.[^1][^2] [[salicylic-acid|Salicylic acid]] has real but second-tier evidence as an acne active and stronger evidence as a keratolytic ([set out on that page](#the-evidence)), but this formula's use of a derivative, at an undisclosed and list-position-implied low concentration, in a rinse-off product, is a materially weaker case than a leave-on treatment at the 0.5-2% OTC range.

Between the surfactants and the tea tree/BHA pair, the ingredient list carries a run of botanical extracts (Cryptomeria Japonica, lotus, pine, elm, evening primrose, kudzu) and allantoin, a skin-conditioning ingredient; general evidence for moisturizing and barrier-supporting ingredients is covered on [[moisturizing]]. A rinse-off cleanser gives all of these only brief contact with skin.

## The Evidence

No published trial of this cleanser, or of a betaine-salicylate-plus-tea-tree gel cleanser formula generally, is cited on [[salicylic-acid]]; the controlled evidence collected there is for free salicylic acid at OTC acne (0.5-2%) and keratolytic/wart-removal concentrations, not for the betaine ester used here or for a rinse-off contact time.[^1]

## What's In It

The full ingredient list (INCI) as declared by the manufacturer, in order.[^1]

- Water, Cocamidopropyl Betaine and Sodium Lauroyl Methyl Isethionate, mild cleansing surfactants
- Sodium Chloride and Polysorbate 20, thickening/emulsifying agents
- Styrax Japonicus Branch/Fruit/Leaf Extract, Butylene Glycol
- Saccharomyces Ferment
- Cryptomeria Japonica Leaf Extract, Nelumbo Nucifera (Lotus) Leaf Extract, Pinus Palustris (Pine) Leaf Extract, Ulmus Davidiana Root Extract, Oenothera Biennis (Evening Primrose) Flower Extract, Pueraria Lobata (Kudzu) Root Extract, botanical extracts
- Melaleuca Alternifolia (Tea Tree) Leaf Oil
- Allantoin, a skin-conditioning ingredient (general evidence on [[moisturizing]])
- Caprylyl Glycol and Ethylhexylglycerin, preservative boosters
- Betaine Salicylate ([[salicylic-acid|a salicylic-acid derivative]]), nineteenth of twenty-five ingredients, no concentration disclosed
- Citric Acid, a pH adjuster
- Ethyl Hexanediol, 1,2-Hexanediol, Trisodium Ethylenediamine Disuccinate, Sodium Benzoate and Disodium EDTA, preservative/chelating system

The ingredient list declares no added fragrance and no sulfate surfactant.[^1]
