Eucerin Advanced Repair Cream, made by [[eucerin|Eucerin]], is a fragrance-free body cream built around 5% urea, glycerin, and ceramide NP, marketed for dry or very dry skin.[^pdp][^target]

> A fragrance-free, drugstore-priced barrier cream whose actives are 5% urea and glycerin, both confirmed in the ingredient list Target declares for this SKU, plus a ceramide (ceramide NP, called "Ceramide-3" on Eucerin's own product page).[^pdp][^target] Moisturizing dry or barrier-impaired skin this way has a well-evidenced general case, covered on [[moisturizing]]; no independently reported trial isolates this exact cream, and Eucerin's "48 hour moisture" claim is [reported here as marketing, not independently verified](#common-marketing-claims).[^pdp][^target]

## Summary

Eucerin Advanced Repair Cream is a fragrance-free body cream that Eucerin markets for dry or very dry skin, sold in 16 oz and 8 oz (tube) sizes.[^pdp] Eucerin's own product page describes it as helping to "repair dry or very dry skin" and providing "48 hour moisture."[^pdp] The [full ingredient list](#whats-in-it), as declared on Target's listing, leads with water, then glycerin, then urea, ahead of emollients such as shea butter, a ceramide (ceramide NP), and several components of the skin's own natural moisturizing factor (sodium PCA, sodium lactate, arginine HCl, glycine, alanine).[^target]

The two actives that anchor the formula are urea, stated at 5% on Target's listing, and glycerin, both humectants that draw and hold water in the skin. Eucerin's own product page separately names "Ceramide-3" among its key ingredients, and Target's declared INCI list includes ceramide NP; each is confirmed on its own source cited here.[^pdp][^target] Low-concentration urea and glycerin both have controlled-trial support for easing dryness in [[atopic-dermatitis]]-prone skin, and ceramides are a real structural component of the skin barrier that measurably moves barrier markers, covered respectively on [[urea]], [[glycerin]], and [[ceramides]]. The well-evidenced case for moisturizing dry and barrier-impaired skin generally, which this formula's combination of humectants and a barrier lipid fits into, is set out on [[moisturizing]].

Eucerin's own product page and Target's listing both describe the cream as fragrance-free, dye-free, and paraben-free.[^pdp][^target]

It sits at the inexpensive end of the category. Target lists the 16 oz jar at $13.49, or $0.84 per ounce (see [Where to Buy](#where-to-buy), accessed 2026-08-07).[^target]

## The Evidence

No published, independently reported trial of this exact cream is cited on the two sources reviewed here. Eucerin's own product page claims the cream "strengthens the skin's moisture barrier to support its ability to lock in moisture" and provides "48 hour moisture," and Target's listing separately states "48 hours of hydration," but neither page discloses a study design, sample size, or methodology behind those figures.[^pdp][^target]

> **Bottom line.** No independently reported study isolates this exact cream's own effect. The formula's case rests on its confirmed 5% urea and glycerin content[^target] plus a declared ceramide (ceramide NP / "Ceramide-3")[^pdp][^target], which fits the general, well-evidenced pattern for moisturizing dry and barrier-impaired skin covered on [[moisturizing]]; Eucerin's own "48 hour moisture" claim carries no disclosed data.
