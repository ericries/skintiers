Isntree Hyaluronic Acid Toner is a Korean hydrating toner from [[isntree]] built on a water, propanediol, and pentylene glycol base, declaring five hyaluronic-acid-related ingredients on its label and sold in a 200 mL bottle.[^brand]

> A watery, humectant-led toner rather than a serum with a stated active concentration. The label lists five forms of hyaluronic acid (sodium hyaluronate, hyaluronic acid, hydrolyzed sodium hyaluronate, hydrolyzed hyaluronic acid, and sodium hyaluronate crosspolymer) but no percentage or molecular weight for any of them, and the first, sodium hyaluronate, ranks fifth on a 20-ingredient panel, after water and three texture/solvent ingredients. The general case for topical hyaluronic acid's hydration benefit is set out on [[hyaluronic-acid]].

## Summary

Hyaluronic Acid Toner is Isntree's entry-level hydrating toner: a water-propanediol-pentylene glycol base carrying five hyaluronic-acid-related ingredients, sold in a single 200 mL size.[^brand] The brand's own product page describes it as "a hydrating toner that contains five types of hyaluronic acid" that "quickly replenishes moisture to the dry skin and absorbs without stickiness."[^brand] The general case for topical hyaluronic acid, reliable in placebo-controlled trials for raising a skin-hydration reading, thinner for wrinkle-appearance and dependent on molecular weight, is set out on [[hyaluronic-acid]].

This toner does not declare a concentration or molecular weight for any of its five hyaluronic-acid forms, and the first of them, sodium hyaluronate, is fifth on the 20-ingredient panel, behind water, propanediol, pentylene glycol, and 1,2-hexanediol.[^brand] It is listed at $16.02 for the 200 mL bottle on Isntree's own store.[^brand]

## The Evidence

Topical hyaluronic acid consistently raises a skin-hydration measurement in placebo-controlled testing, with a smaller and molecular-weight-dependent effect on fine-wrinkle appearance; the full case, including the split between low- and high-molecular-weight forms, is set out on [[hyaluronic-acid]]. Nothing specific to this product was found: no published trial tests Isntree's exact formula, and the label does not state a concentration or molecular weight for any of the five declared hyaluronic-acid ingredients, so it is not possible to say whether this toner's hyaluronic acid is the smaller, skin-penetrating type shown to affect wrinkle depth or the larger, surface-sitting type shown mainly to raise hydration readings.[^brand]

> **Bottom line.** No trial isolates this product. Its hydration grade rests on hyaluronic acid's general, evidence-backed but concentration- and molecular-weight-dependent case, undermined here by the label not declaring a percentage or molecular weight for any of the five hyaluronic-acid ingredients it lists.
