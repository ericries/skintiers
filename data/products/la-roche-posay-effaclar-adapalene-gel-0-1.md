La Roche-Posay Effaclar Adapalene Gel 0.1% is a once-daily, over-the-counter [[adapalene]] gel for acne, sold by [[la-roche-posay]] in a 1.6 oz (45 g) tube for $39.99 as listed on Target.[^target]

> Adapalene is a synthetic retinoid that the FDA classifies as an acne treatment in its own right, with a solid evidence base at the 0.1% strength used here ([[adapalene]]). This tube's Drug Facts label lists that same active, "adapalene usp (retinoid)(0.1%)," for the purpose "Acne Treatment," so the product is a standard-strength, standard-formula delivery of an already well-evidenced OTC active rather than a novel or diluted version of it.[^target]

## Summary

This is a leave-on gel treatment, not a cleanser or spot treatment, meant to be applied as a thin layer over the whole affected area rather than dabbed on individual spots. Its Drug Facts label states: "Use once daily. Clean the skin gently and pat dry before applying the product. Cover the entire affected area with a thin layer."[^target] The label also warns that it "may cause irritation, redness, itching, dryness, or burning," that acne "may worsen initially," and that results "may take up to 3 months," and it advises limiting sun exposure and using sunscreen.[^target]

The formula's single active is [[adapalene]] at 0.1%, an FDA-approved topical retinoid for acne with a solid evidence base built on a Cochrane review and a pooled meta-analysis of comparative trials against benzoyl peroxide and tretinoin (see [[adapalene]] for that evidence). Because the OTC 0.1% strength sold here is the same molecule and concentration as the original prescription gel, that evidence base applies directly rather than by extrapolation.[^target] The rest of the formula is a short, largely functional base: no fragrance, and no other treatment actives beyond adapalene.[^target]

This page found no fetchable La Roche-Posay page or clinical study specific to this SKU (see [What's In It](#whats-in-it) and [Sources](#sources) for what was verifiable); the grade above rests on the general adapalene evidence, not on a trial of this exact product.

## What's In It

Active ingredient, per the Target Drug Facts label:

- Adapalene USP 0.1% ([[adapalene]]), a synthetic retinoid, the sole treatment active[^target]

Inactive ingredients, per the same label: "carbomer homopolymer, edetate disodium, phenoxyethanol, poloxamer 182, propylene glycol, purified water and sodium hydroxide."[^target] The listing describes the product as "oil-free, non comedogenic, fragrance-free."[^target]
