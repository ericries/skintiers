Vanicream Daily Facial Moisturizer for Sensitive Skin (Vanicream) is a fragrance-free facial lotion built around hyaluronic acid and five named ceramide types, positioned for day-or-night use under makeup.[^1]

> A lightweight, fragrance-free facial lotion led by water and squalane, with hyaluronic acid and five ceramide types (EOP, NG, NP, AS, AP) among its declared ingredients, and no botanical extracts, essential oils, dyes, or parabens.[^1][^2] No trial isolates this exact lotion. Moisturizing dry and sensitive skin this way is one of dermatology's better-evidenced routines, covered on [[moisturizing]], where the general evidence for hyaluronic acid and ceramides is set out.

## Summary

Vanicream Daily Facial Moisturizer is described by the manufacturer as "a rich, but lightweight lotion that provides effective moisturization for day or night," formulated with hyaluronic acid and five key ceramides "to hydrate and retain the skin's moisture," and marketed as suitable to wear under makeup.[^1] Its [full ingredient list](#whats-in-it) is: water, squalane, glycerin, pentylene glycol, polyglyceryl-2 stearate, glyceryl stearate, stearyl alcohol, hyaluronic acid, ceramide EOP, ceramide NG, ceramide NP, ceramide AS, ceramide AP, carnosine, hydrogenated lecithin, phytosterols, caprylyl glycol, polyacrylate crosspolymer-11, and 1,2-hexanediol.[^1]

What distinguishes it from a scented drugstore lotion is again mainly what it leaves out: the manufacturer states it contains no fragrance, dyes, botanical extracts, or essential oils, and lists it as gluten-free, non-comedogenic, and pH balanced.[^1] Target's listing corroborates the fragrance-free, paraben-free, formaldehyde-free, dye-free, and vegan claims and confirms the National Eczema Association Seal of Acceptance.[^1][^2] Unlike [[vanicream-moisturizing-cream]], which is a plainer petrolatum-forward cream without added ceramides or hyaluronic acid, this lotion adds both, closer in composition to [[cerave-pm-facial-moisturizing-lotion]].[^1][^2]

The lotion has been awarded the National Eczema Association Seal of Acceptance and is described by the manufacturer as "dermatologist tested."[^1] These are formulation-review designations and manufacturer claims, not a clinical trial of this product; no product-specific trial is [cited below](#the-evidence). Moisturizing dry and sensitive skin is a well-evidenced dermatology routine covered on [[moisturizing]], where the general evidence for hyaluronic acid, ceramides, and squalane is set out.

## The Evidence

No trial isolates this lotion. Its composition matches what the general moisturizing evidence covers: a water- and squalane-based lotion with added [[hyaluronic-acid]] and [[ceramides]], both graded on their own pages for general barrier-support and hydration evidence, plus [[squalane]] as an emollient. Moisturizing dry, barrier-impaired, and sensitive skin this way is one of the better-evidenced routines in dermatology; that category-level evidence is set out on [[moisturizing]]. The manufacturer states the lotion was awarded the National Eczema Association Seal of Acceptance and describes it as "dermatologist tested," but no independent trial data, methodology, or review criteria for that designation are reported on the pages cited here.[^1]
