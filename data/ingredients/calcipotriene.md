Calcipotriene (INN calcipotriol, IUPAC name derived from the parent compound with molecular formula C27H40O3) is a synthetic vitamin D3 analog used as a first-line prescription topical for [[psoriasis]], most often at 0.005% (50 μg/g) either alone or fixed-combined with the corticosteroid betamethasone dipropionate.[^pubchem][^kaufmann] Its evidence base is one of the largest in topical dermatology: in a 1,603-patient randomized trial, once-daily calcipotriol monotherapy for 4 weeks outperformed vehicle but was clearly inferior to betamethasone and to the calcipotriol/betamethasone combination, and that combination is now the standard of care because it out-treats either component alone.[^kaufmann]

> In a randomized, double-blind trial of 1,603 patients with psoriasis vulgaris treated once daily for 4 weeks, mean percentage PASI change was -71.3 for the calcipotriol/betamethasone combination, -57.2 for betamethasone alone, -46.1 for calcipotriol alone, and -22.7 for vehicle; the combination beat calcipotriol alone by a mean difference of -25.3 points (95% CI -28.7 to -21.9, p<0.001).[^kaufmann]

## The Rubric

**Plaque psoriasis, calcipotriol as monotherapy (topical)**
- **Effect size: real but modest, and clearly the weakest of the active options tested head to head.** In the Kaufmann trial, calcipotriol alone reduced PASI by a mean of 46.1% versus 22.7% for vehicle over 4 weeks, a genuine drug effect, but it was outperformed by betamethasone alone (-57.2%) and by far by the fixed combination (-71.3%).[^kaufmann] Calcipotriol monotherapy also carried the highest local adverse-reaction rate of the four arms (11.4%, versus 4.9% for betamethasone, 6.0% for the combination, and 13.6% for vehicle), reflecting its known irritant potential.[^kaufmann]
- **Evidence quality: high.** This is a large (n=1,603), randomized, double-blind, four-arm trial with a placebo (vehicle) arm and an active corticosteroid comparator, the strongest single-trial design available for a topical psoriasis agent, and its findings are consistent with decades of prior calcipotriol monotherapy trials cited across the dermatology literature.[^kaufmann]

**Plaque psoriasis, fixed-dose calcipotriol/betamethasone combination (topical, short-course)**
- **Effect size: large, and superior to either active ingredient alone.** Beyond the Kaufmann registration trial, a second randomized, double-blind trial of 501 patients found that combination treatment for 4 weeks followed by calcipotriol alone for 4 more weeks produced significantly greater PASI reduction than an active comparator (tacalcitol) at both week 4 (65.0% vs 33.3%) and week 8 (59.0% vs 38.4%, p<0.001 for both).[^ortonne] Because these trials compare the combination to another active vitamin D analog or to calcipotriol alone rather than to betamethasone plus vehicle in every design, the effect should be read as "combination beats monotherapy of either component," which is well established, more than as a novel mechanism.
- **Evidence quality: high, though industry-dependent.** Both major trials cited here, and most of the combination-product literature, were sponsored by LEO Pharma (the product's manufacturer), which does not invalidate the randomized, double-blind, vehicle- or active-controlled designs but means most calcipotriol/betamethasone efficacy data comes from a single interested sponsor rather than independent academic replication.[^kaufmann][^ortonne]

**Plaque psoriasis, long-term proactive maintenance with the combination foam (topical)**
- **Effect size: meaningful reduction in relapse, not a cure.** In the phase 3 PSO-LONG trial, patients whose psoriasis had cleared with 4 weeks of once-daily combination foam were randomized to twice-weekly combination foam or twice-weekly vehicle for 52 weeks; proactive (combination) maintenance reduced the hazard of first relapse by a hazard ratio of 0.56 (p<0.001) and cut the overall rate of relapses by a rate ratio of 0.52 (p<0.001) versus reactive (vehicle, treat-only-on-relapse) management, with the benefit larger in patients with more severe baseline disease.[^lebwohl] This is a maintenance/relapse-prevention effect on a chronic disease, not evidence the drug resolves psoriasis permanently.
- **Evidence quality: high for a maintenance design, industry-funded.** This is a randomized, double-blind, vehicle-controlled, 52-week phase 3 trial with a hard clinical endpoint (time to relapse), a rigorous design for long-term topical therapy; it was funded and co-authored by LEO Pharma.[^lebwohl]

## How It Works

Calcipotriene is a synthetic analog of the active form of vitamin D (calcitriol) engineered to retain vitamin D receptor (VDR) binding and its anti-proliferative, pro-differentiating effects on keratinocytes while carrying a much lower risk of the hypercalcemia that limits calcitriol itself, which is the basis for its use in psoriasis, a disease of keratinocyte hyperproliferation and abnormal differentiation.[^pubchem][^kraggballe-cutis] In ex vivo psoriatic skin and in vitro immune-cell cultures, a LEO Pharma research group found that calcipotriol/betamethasone combination inhibited secretion of IL-17A and TNF-α in psoriatic skin, that the two components showed additive inhibition of IL-23 and TNF-α secretion by inflammatory dendritic cells and of IL-17A and TNF-α by CD4+ and CD8+ T cells, and that calcipotriol increased IL-10 (an anti-inflammatory cytokine) in psoriatic skin and human T cells, directly targeting the IL-23/Th17 inflammatory axis that drives psoriatic plaques.[^lovato] Because calcipotriol and topical corticosteroids act through largely separate mechanisms (VDR-mediated keratinocyte and immune modulation versus broad corticosteroid anti-inflammatory and vasoconstrictive effects), combining them produces additive rather than purely overlapping efficacy, which is the pharmacologic rationale for the fixed-combination product.[^kraggballe-cutis][^lovato]

## The Evidence

**Calcipotriol monotherapy versus betamethasone, combination, and vehicle (topical, 4 weeks).** A randomized, double-blind trial enrolled 1,603 patients with psoriasis vulgaris to one of four once-daily treatments for 4 weeks: calcipotriol/betamethasone dipropionate combination ointment, betamethasone dipropionate alone, calcipotriol alone, or vehicle. Mean percentage PASI change was -71.3 (combination), -57.2 (betamethasone), -46.1 (calcipotriol), and -22.7 (vehicle); the combination beat betamethasone alone by a mean difference of -14.2 points (95% CI -17.6 to -10.8, p<0.001) and beat calcipotriol alone by -25.3 points (95% CI -28.7 to -21.9, p<0.001). Local adverse reactions occurred in 6.0% (combination), 4.9% (betamethasone), 11.4% (calcipotriol), and 13.6% (vehicle) of patients.[^kaufmann] *This large, well-controlled trial establishes that calcipotriol has a real but modest monotherapy effect, that it irritates skin more than either the steroid or the combination, and that the fixed combination is meaningfully better than either ingredient used alone.*

**Combination followed by calcipotriol versus tacalcitol (topical, 8 weeks).** A randomized, double-blind trial in 501 patients with stable psoriasis vulgaris compared the calcipotriol/betamethasone combination for 4 weeks followed by calcipotriol alone for 4 weeks, against an active vitamin D comparator, tacalcitol, for the full 8 weeks. Mean percentage PASI reduction was 65.0% versus 33.3% at week 4, and 59.0% versus 38.4% at week 8 (p<0.001 for both comparisons).[^ortonne] *This trial shows the combination-then-calcipotriol regimen clearing psoriasis roughly twice as much as another topical vitamin D analog used alone, though it does not isolate calcipotriol's contribution from betamethasone's.*

**Long-term proactive combination-foam maintenance versus reactive treatment (topical, 52 weeks).** The phase 3 PSO-LONG trial began with a 4-week open-label run-in of once-daily calcipotriene 0.005%/betamethasone dipropionate 0.064% foam; responders were then randomized to twice-weekly combination foam (proactive management) or twice-weekly vehicle foam (reactive management, with 4-week rescue combination-foam courses if relapse occurred) for up to 52 weeks. Proactive management reduced the hazard of first relapse (hazard ratio 0.56, p<0.001) and the overall relapse rate (rate ratio 0.52, p<0.001) compared with reactive management, and the size of the benefit increased with greater baseline disease severity (Physician Global Assessment and modified PASI).[^lebwohl] *In patients who had already cleared, twice-weekly maintenance treatment roughly halved the rate of psoriasis coming back over a year, evidence for a real relapse-prevention effect rather than proof the underlying disease is resolved.*

## Common Marketing Claims

- **"Steroid-free psoriasis treatment."** True only for calcipotriol used alone, and that framing undersells the tradeoff: monotherapy calcipotriol is clearly less effective than betamethasone alone or the combination in the same head-to-head trial (46.1% vs 57.2% vs 71.3% PASI reduction) and caused more local irritation than either.[^kaufmann] "Steroid-free" is accurate but not a claim of superior efficacy.
- **"Clinically proven for long-term psoriasis control."** Fair for the fixed-combination foam specifically: a year-long, randomized, vehicle-controlled trial with a hard relapse endpoint backs up long-term maintenance use.[^lebwohl] It does not extend to calcipotriol monotherapy, for which the cited long-term data concern the combination product, not calcipotriol alone.
