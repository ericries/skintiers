Galactomyces ferment filtrate (GFF) is the liquid left over after fermenting the yeast *Galactomyces* (marketed by Procter & Gamble's SK-II as Pitera), an ingredient P&G describes as tracing to "the empirical observation that the hands of sake brewers who deal with yeast fermentation retain a beautiful and youthful appearance."[^yan] Almost every peer-reviewed study of GFF identified here, mechanistic and clinical alike, was authored or co-authored by P&G employees or a P&G-paid consultant, and no independent placebo-controlled human trial isolating GFF by itself was found.

> Cell-culture work from P&G's own labs shows GFF changes gene expression linked to the skin barrier and inflammation in cultured keratinocytes, a plausible mechanism rather than a measured effect on real skin.[^nakajima] The one 12-month improvement in wrinkles, spots, and roughness attributed to GFF came from an open-label study with no untreated comparison group, run and authored almost entirely by P&G researchers testing three SK-II products at once, so GFF's individual contribution cannot be separated from the rest of each formula.[^miyamoto12mo] The only independent randomized trial that included GFF tested it as one of three actives in a combination serum, so it cannot credit GFF specifically either.[^anwar]

## The Rubric

**Skin barrier and antioxidant/anti-inflammatory gene expression (in vitro).**
- **Effect size: unclear at the human-skin level.** In cultured human keratinocytes, GFF treatment consistently changed the expression of 99 genes, upregulating ones tied to skin barrier function (including claudins and small proline-rich proteins) and wound healing while downregulating inflammation-linked genes such as CXCL14 and IL6R.[^nakajima] This is a dish-based transcriptomic readout, not a measured change in barrier function or inflammation on a person's face. "Plausible cell-level mechanism from P&G's own research group; no independent human-skin measurement of the same endpoints."
- **Evidence quality: preliminary and entirely company-run.** The transcriptomic study's authors were P&G Innovation GK and Procter & Gamble Company employees, with one Kyushu University co-author; no independent lab has published a replication.[^nakajima]

**Facial pore size, roughness, and redness over weeks (open-label, no untreated control).**
- **Effect size: modest, self-comparison only.** Two studies in young Japanese women (47 and 57 participants) tracked facial pore area, roughness, and redness by portable imaging device across a 4-week baseline and 4 weeks of twice-daily Pitera/GFF use, and reported the treatment "significantly reduced the net values and delta fluctuation of pore area, roughness, and redness" compared with each subject's own baseline.[^miyamotofluc] There was no untreated or placebo comparison group, so a normal seasonal or measurement-familiarity trend cannot be ruled out. "Modest change from baseline in a company-run study; no placebo or untreated control arm."
- **Evidence quality: preliminary.** Authors were P&G Innovation GK and P&G International Operations employees plus Kyushu University dermatology co-authors; imaging-device output, not a clinician-graded outcome.[^miyamotofluc]

**Facial wrinkles, pigmented spots, and roughness over 12 months (open-label, historical comparison).**
- **Effect size: reported as a reversal of about a decade of visible aging, but confounded by product and design.** 86 women used three GFF-containing SK-II products (a treatment essence, an essence, and a cream) twice daily for 12 months, with wrinkles, pigmented spots, and roughness compared both to their own baseline and to a separate cohort of women measured in 1999.[^miyamoto12mo] Skin hydration rose and transepidermal water loss fell over the 12 months.[^miyamoto12mo] Because three multi-ingredient products were used together with no untreated arm, none of this can be attributed to GFF specifically rather than to the products' other actives or to twelve months of daily moisturizing. "Positive company-run finding for a three-product SK-II regimen; not evidence that GFF alone did it, and not tested against an untreated or placebo group."
- **Evidence quality: preliminary and company-funded.** "Masutaka Furue is a consultant of P&G Innovation GK. The other authors are employees of P&G Innovation GK."[^miyamoto12mo]

**Post-acne hyperpigmentation, only as part of a three-active combination serum (independent RCT).**
- **Effect size: modest, but not attributable to GFF alone.** An Indonesian randomized, controlled trial gave 51 participants with Fitzpatrick skin types IV-V a serum combining GFF, dexpanthenol, and [[centella-asiatica]] or a placebo, twice daily for 8 weeks. The treated group had a significantly lower melanin index by week 8 in skin type IV and a significantly higher lightness (L*) score by weeks 4-6 in skin type V (P<0.05 for both).[^anwar] Because the tested product combines three actives, the trial cannot isolate what GFF itself contributed. "Modest combination-serum benefit for post-acne dark marks in one independent trial; confounded with dexpanthenol and centella asiatica, so it is not evidence for GFF in isolation."
- **Evidence quality: preliminary and confounded.** One small independent RCT (51 participants) of a fixed three-active combination, not the isolated ingredient.[^anwar]

## The Evidence

**Gene expression in cultured keratinocytes.** Nakajima and colleagues, all P&G Innovation GK, Procter & Gamble Company, and Kyushu University-affiliated, ran transcriptomic profiling of human keratinocytes treated with GFF and reported it "consistently modulated 99 (22 upregulated and 77 downregulated) genes," upregulating barrier-related genes (claudins, small proline-rich proteins) and wound-healing genes while downregulating the inflammation-associated genes CXCL14 and IL6R.[^nakajima] The paper describes GFF (branded Pitera) as "a cosmetic ingredient known to have multiple skin care benefits, such as reducing redness and pore size via the topical application of its moisturizer form."[^nakajima]

**Facial pore, roughness, and redness fluctuation.** Miyamoto and colleagues (P&G Innovation GK, P&G International Operations, and Kyushu University) used a portable imaging device to photograph young Japanese women's faces three times daily across two studies (n=47 and n=57), comparing a 4-week baseline period to 4 weeks of twice-daily Pitera/GFF application. GFF treatment "significantly reduced the net values and delta fluctuation of pore area, roughness, and redness" across both studies, with pore area and roughness peaking in the morning after waking and redness peaking in the evening after washing.[^miyamotofluc]

**12-month facial aging study.** The same P&G Innovation GK/Kyushu University group enrolled 86 women in twice-daily use of three GFF-containing SK-II products (a facial treatment essence, an essence, and a cream) for 12 months, comparing their wrinkles, pigmented spots, and roughness both to baseline and to a separate cohort measured in 1999, and reported "significant reversal" of facial wrinkle, pigmented spot, and roughness scores alongside rising hydration and falling transepidermal water loss over the 12 months.[^miyamoto12mo] The paper discloses: "Masutaka Furue is a consultant of P&G Innovation GK. The other authors are employees of P&G Innovation GK."[^miyamoto12mo]

**Post-acne hyperpigmentation, combination serum.** Anwar and colleagues randomized 51 participants with Fitzpatrick skin types IV and V and post-acne hyperpigmentation to a serum combining GFF, dexpanthenol, and centella asiatica, or placebo, applied twice daily for 8 weeks, measuring melanin index and lightness (L*) biweekly.[^anwar] By week 8, the melanin index was significantly lower with the active serum in skin type IV, and by weeks 4 through 6 the L* score was significantly higher in skin type V (P<0.05).[^anwar] The tested product is a three-active combination, so the trial does not isolate GFF's own contribution.

## Common Marketing Claims

- **"Miracle water" / crystal-clear, youthful-looking skin from a sake-fermentation discovery.** The sake-brewers'-hands origin story is P&G's own account, published by a P&G Science Communications author, not an independently verified historical record.[^yan]
- **Anti-aging, wrinkle reduction.** The only long-term human result rests on an open-label, no-control 12-month study of three SK-II products used together, authored and funded by P&G, so it cannot be attributed to GFF alone.[^miyamoto12mo]
- **Brightening and even skin tone.** The single independent randomized trial that reported a pigmentation benefit tested GFF only inside a three-active combination serum, not by itself.[^anwar]
- **Pore-minimizing and redness-reducing.** Based on open-label, self-comparison imaging studies with no untreated or placebo group.[^miyamotofluc]
