Palmitoyl tetrapeptide-7 is a synthetic signal peptide, a four-amino-acid sequence (glycine-glutamine-proline-arginine) with a fatty palmitoyl tail attached, molecular formula C34H62N8O7.[^pubchem] It is added to leave-on anti-aging serums and creams on its own and, more often, as one half of the Sederma trade-named blend Matrixyl 3000; no published trial has tested it alone against a placebo.

> No independent, placebo-controlled human trial isolates palmitoyl tetrapeptide-7 from the blends it is sold in. Every human study found tests it mixed with at least one other peptide or active: paired with palmitoyl oligopeptide as Matrixyl 3000 in a Sederma-authored study,[^mondon] or combined with three other actives in a small, open-label, manufacturer-run eye-cream trial.[^yang] A 2025 wound-healing study reports the peptide reduces inflammatory cytokines, but only when grafted onto a chitosan hydrogel and tested in diabetic mice, a delivery form and use case unrelated to a cosmetic serum.[^xing]

## The Rubric

**Improvement in fine lines, firmness, or elasticity (in blended anti-aging formulas)**
- Effect size: unclear and confounded. A 2024 open-label eye-cream trial in 37 women tested an "active complex" of 0.0001% palmitoyl tetrapeptide-7 alongside 0.0002% palmitoyl tripeptide-1, a yeast/rice fermentation filtrate, and N-acetylneuraminic acid, applying the cream to one side of the face with the other side left as an untreated comparison for 12 weeks; the treated side showed hydration "28.1%" higher, elasticity parameters "14.1%, 20.9%, and 28.1%" higher, and collagen density "55.0%" higher (all p<0.001).[^yang] The trial does not isolate palmitoyl tetrapeptide-7's own contribution from the other three actives. A separate 2015 study of the Matrixyl 3000 blend (palmitoyl oligopeptide plus palmitoyl tetrapeptide-7) reported by echography that "the peptide blend reduced subepidermal low-echogenic band thickness and improved its density," in a paper its own authors call "preliminary"; see [[palmitoyl-pentapeptide-4-matrixyl]] for the full record on that trade name.[^mondon]
- Evidence quality: preliminary. The eye-cream trial was open-label with no vehicle placebo, small (37 participants), and run by the cream's manufacturer.[^yang] The Matrixyl 3000 blend study is authored by Sederma, the peptide's own supplier.[^mondon] A 2021 independent review of peptides in sensitive-skin cosmetics found that, across the peptides it surveyed including palmitoyl tetrapeptide-7, "the available data is mostly found in patents and supplier brochures, and not in randomized placebo-controlled studies."[^resende]

**Anti-inflammatory / IL-6-suppressing mechanism (the marketed rationale)**
- Effect size: shown only in a wound-dressing hydrogel, not a cosmetic formulation. Independent materials-science researchers in China grafted the peptide onto a chitosan hydrogel (not a leave-on cream) and, in streptozotocin-induced diabetic mice, reported the peptide-hydrogel-stem-cell complex reduced wound IL-6 expression by "around 81%" and TNF-α by "approximately 75%" while improving wound healing.[^xing] This is a tissue-engineering wound-healing study in mice, not a test of the peptide as used in a retail cosmetic serum, and it does not establish that a leave-on formulation delivers a comparable anti-inflammatory effect to human skin.
- Evidence quality: preclinical and off-context. A 2024 clinical paper describes palmitoyl tetrapeptide-7 as "known for its ability to reduce the interleukin-6 (IL-6) secretion and prevent UVB irradiation-induced inflammatory response," citing a prior review rather than reporting its own independent test of that mechanism.[^yang] No cited source here independently confirms IL-6 suppression from a topical cosmetic use of this peptide.

## How It Works

Palmitoyl tetrapeptide-7 is the tetrapeptide Gly-Gln-Pro-Arg with a 16-carbon palmitoyl fatty acid attached to its N-terminus, giving the molecular formula C34H62N8O7.[^pubchem] The palmitoyl tail is a common design choice among cosmetic signal peptides, intended to help a small, otherwise hydrophilic peptide associate with the skin's lipid barrier. It is most often sold combined with palmitoyl oligopeptide under Sederma's Matrixyl 3000 trademark; the marketing and evidence history of that trade name, including which retail products use which peptide blend, is covered on [[palmitoyl-pentapeptide-4-matrixyl]]. Cosmetic peptides as a class are well-tolerated, with a modest and mostly preliminary effect on the appearance of aging that is larger for ingested than topical forms; the class-level evidence is set out on [[peptides]].

## The Evidence

**Manufacturer eye-cream trial (blend, not isolated).** Yang and colleagues at Mageline Biology Tech Co., the eye cream's manufacturer, ran a 12-week, open-label, split-face trial in 37 women aged 20-55, applying an eye cream to one side of the periorbital area with the other side serving as an untreated comparison. The cream's active complex combined "0.0002% palmitoyl tripeptide-1 and 0.0001% palmitoyl tetrapeptide-7" with a yeast/rice fermentation filtrate and N-acetylneuraminic acid. The treated side showed hydration up "28.1%," elasticity parameters up "14.1%, 20.9%, and 28.1%," and collagen density up "55.0%" versus baseline (p<0.001 for each).[^yang] *A small, open-label, manufacturer-run trial of a four-ingredient blend reports large improvements, but with no vehicle placebo and no data isolating palmitoyl tetrapeptide-7 from the other three actives.*

**Matrixyl 3000 blend study (supplier-authored).** Mondon and colleagues at Sederma tested "a blend of palmitoyl oligopeptide and palmitoyl tetrapeptide-7" (the Matrixyl 3000 blend) using echography, in vivo confocal microscopy, and MALDI mass-spectrometric imaging, and found by echography that "the peptide blend reduced subepidermal low-echogenic band thickness and improved its density" and by confocal microscopy that "the peptides improved the ECM structure vs. placebo," describing their own methodology paper as a "preliminary" study.[^mondon] *The peptide's supplier reports a benefit for the two-peptide blend, not for palmitoyl tetrapeptide-7 alone, in a study its own authors call preliminary; full detail on [[palmitoyl-pentapeptide-4-matrixyl]].*

**Wound-hydrogel study (different delivery form, animal model).** Xing and colleagues at the University of Science and Technology Beijing grafted palmitoyl tetrapeptide-7 ("Pal-7") onto a chitosan hydrogel loaded with stem cells and tested it on streptozotocin-induced diabetic mouse wounds. The complex "significantly improved wound healing with over 95% wound closure," reduced "tumor necrosis factor-α (TNF-α) expression by approximately 75% and Interleukin-6 (IL-6) expression by around 81%," and increased IL-10 expression, alongside increased angiogenesis markers.[^xing] *An independent tissue-engineering study finds anti-inflammatory and wound-healing effects for the peptide chemically bonded into a hydrogel wound dressing in diabetic mice, a use case and delivery form unrelated to a cosmetic leave-on cream.*

**Independent review of peptides in sensitive-skin cosmetics.** Resende and colleagues reviewed 88 facial cosmetics for sensitive skin and the synthetic peptides in them, including palmitoyl tetrapeptide-7, and found that "only five peptides present evidence supporting their use in sensitive skin, with only one clinical study including volunteers having this condition," and that "the available data is mostly found in patents and supplier brochures, and not in randomized placebo-controlled studies."[^resende] *An independent survey of the peptide field concludes the supporting evidence for ingredients like this one is largely unpublished supplier material rather than controlled clinical trials.*

## Common Marketing Claims

- **"Reduces IL-6 / calms inflammation."** This claim traces to a wound-hydrogel study in diabetic mice using the peptide chemically grafted onto chitosan, not a topical cosmetic formulation,[^xing] and to a 2024 clinical paper's description of the peptide as "known for its ability to reduce" IL-6, which cites a prior review rather than an independent trial of a cosmetic product.[^yang]
- **"Matrixyl 3000 / next-generation Matrixyl."** This trade name covers palmitoyl tetrapeptide-7 combined with palmitoyl oligopeptide, evaluated only in a preliminary, supplier-authored study; see the full marketing-claims record on [[palmitoyl-pentapeptide-4-matrixyl]].[^mondon]
