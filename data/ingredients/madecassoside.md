Madecassoside is a pentacyclic triterpenoid glycoside purified from [[centella-asiatica]] (gotu kola) and used on its own INCI line, "Madecassoside," in soothing and post-procedure skincare rather than as part of a whole-plant extract.[^review] The molecule is one of the two marker triterpene glycosides of the plant, alongside asiaticoside, and the Chinese Pharmacopoeia uses both as identity markers for Centella asiatica.[^review]

Isolated madecassoside raises collagen synthesis and dampens inflammatory signaling in cultured skin cells, and human trials of madecassoside-containing creams show comparable results to an active steroid comparator after laser resurfacing, but no cited trial isolates madecassoside from the other actives in its formulas.[^bonte][^shen][^lueangarun][^haftek]

> The cell and animal data for isolated madecassoside are extensive and consistently positive for collagen stimulation, anti-inflammatory signaling, and UV-damage protection, but the human clinical record rests on combination products (madecassoside plus panthenol and trace minerals, or madecassoside plus vitamin C), so no cited trial shows what madecassoside alone does in human skin.[^bonte][^shen][^lueangarun][^haftek]

## What It Is and How It Works

Madecassoside is the glycoside of madecassic acid, one of four related triterpenes found in Centella asiatica (the others being asiaticoside, asiatic acid, and madecassic acid itself); see [[centella-asiatica]] for the whole-plant story and its wound-healing tradition.[^review] As a purified compound it appears on ingredient lists by its own INCI name, distinct from "Centella Asiatica Leaf Extract."

In cultured human dermal fibroblasts, madecassoside significantly increased type III collagen secretion, and both it and asiaticoside raised type I collagen secretion by roughly 25 to 30%, in a study run at a cosmetics-industry laboratory (LVMH Recherche).[^bonte] Separately, an in vitro study using Propionibacterium acnes-stimulated human monocytic cells found that madecassoside "significantly inhibited the pro-inflammatory cytokine IL-1β, TLR2 and nuclear translocation of NF-κB," and in cultured keratinocytes and fibroblasts it increased markers tied to skin hydration, including aquaporin-3, loricrin, involucrin, and hyaluronan secretion.[^shen] A 2023 review covering the compound's pharmacology across tissue types describes cosmetic-relevant roles in "anti-ageing, skin hydration, collagen synthesis, UV protection and curing scars," drawing on studies published between 2005 and 2022.[^review] These are cell-culture and animal findings; the human clinical picture is narrower, below.

## The Evidence

**Post-laser wound recovery.** A double-blinded, split-face, randomized controlled trial in 20 subjects compared a moisturizer containing 5% panthenol, madecassoside, and copper-zinc-manganese against 0.02% triamcinolone acetonide cream after ablative fractional CO2 laser resurfacing.[^lueangarun] Both creams "significantly reduce postlaser downtime including swelling, redness, crusting, and scaling in 5-7 days, with comparable efficacies in decreasing downtime and adverse reactions, as well as wound healing improvement and lower PIH without statistically significant difference between the two treatments," and post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation occurred in 60% of the madecassoside-cream side, described as "minimal intensity."[^lueangarun] The trial demonstrates that this multi-ingredient moisturizer performs comparably to a topical steroid after laser injury; it does not isolate madecassoside's own contribution from panthenol or the trace minerals in the same formula.

**Photoaging (cosmetic).** The same randomized, double-blind trial of 20 women applying 5% vitamin C plus 0.1% madecassoside for six months that is described on the [[centella-asiatica]] page reported improved wrinkle scores, firmness, and hydration, with dermal elastic-fibre remodelling on histology.[^haftek] Because vitamin C was co-formulated at a well-evidenced concentration (see [[ascorbic-acid-vitamin-c]]), madecassoside's individual contribution cannot be separated out.

**UV and pollution protection, and anti-inflammatory action.** More recent cell and animal work reports that madecassoside protects keratinocytes from UVB-induced damage and PM2.5 particulate exposure, and reduces UVB-induced ferroptosis in skin cells, but these findings have not yet been confirmed in a cited human trial.[^review]

## The Rubric

Grades differ by use. Framing below is relative and names comparators.

**Post-procedure redness and wound recovery (health-adjacent, cosmetic formulas)**
- **Effect size: modest, but from combination products.** A 20-subject split-face RCT found a madecassoside-panthenol-mineral moisturizer performed comparably to a topical corticosteroid for post-laser downtime and healing.[^lueangarun] "Encouraging as a steroid-sparing option in this small trial, but the formula also contains panthenol, so madecassoside's own contribution is not isolated. Petrolatum-based occlusives remain the most extensively evidenced post-procedure barrier support (see [[petrolatum]])."
- **Evidence quality: preliminary.** One small RCT of a combination product against an active comparator, no placebo arm, no isolated madecassoside arm.[^lueangarun]

**Skin soothing, hydration, and anti-inflammatory action (cosmetic)**
- **Effect size: not clinically established.** In vitro work shows madecassoside inhibits inflammatory signaling (IL-1β, TLR2, NF-κB) and raises hydration markers (aquaporin-3, loricrin, hyaluronan) in cultured skin cells.[^shen] "Mechanistically plausible but unproven in a human trial isolating the compound. [[niacinamide]] and [[ceramides]] carry stronger direct human evidence for barrier support and hydration."
- **Evidence quality: preliminary.** In vitro mechanism only; no cited human trial isolates madecassoside for this use.[^shen]

**Photoaging / wrinkles (cosmetic)**
- **Effect size: not established for madecassoside alone.** The one supporting RCT (n=20) delivered madecassoside together with 5% vitamin C, so the benefit cannot be attributed to madecassoside.[^haftek] "Unresolved on its own. Prescription [[tretinoin]] and the [[retinoids]] class have much stronger, isolated anti-aging evidence."
- **Evidence quality: preliminary.** A single small confounded trial.[^haftek]

## Common Marketing Claims

This section is quarantined and discounted; treat everything here as unproven or overstated unless it appears in The Evidence above.

- **"Heals and soothes skin as well as steroid creams."** One 20-subject split-face RCT found a madecassoside-containing moisturizer comparable to 0.02% triamcinolone acetonide for post-laser downtime, without a statistically significant difference between the two,[^lueangarun] but this is one small trial of a multi-ingredient formula, not an isolated madecassoside result, and post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation still occurred in 60% of the madecassoside-cream side.[^lueangarun]
- **"Boosts collagen."** Madecassoside raised type I collagen secretion in cultured human fibroblasts by roughly 25 to 30% (with a rise in type III as well),[^bonte] which is a cell-culture result, not a demonstrated effect on collagen in living human skin.
- **"Clinically proven anti-aging."** The only human anti-aging trial paired madecassoside with 5% vitamin C and reported cosmetic endpoints, so it cannot show madecassoside reverses aging on its own.[^haftek]
