Piroctone olamine (INCI name, trade name Octopirox) is an antifungal active used in anti-dandruff shampoos and scalp products, and increasingly in leave-on skincare marketed as "fungal-acne-safe," because it suppresses the *Malassezia* yeast behind dandruff and seborrheic dermatitis.[^tang][^schmidt] It is the monoethanolamine salt of piroctone, a hydroxypyridone antifungal.[^tang]

> Piroctone olamine is a real, effective anti-*Malassezia* antifungal, but its isolated evidence is thinner than the field leaders. The cleanest trial pairs it with a second antifungal (climbazole) and shows it roughly matches zinc pyrithione for dandruff rather than beating it, and it is largely industry-run cosmetic-science work rather than independent clinical trials.[^schmidt] Judge it as a solid, well-tolerated dandruff and seborrheic-dermatitis active in the same tier as zinc pyrithione, below the best-evidenced options (ketoconazole, selenium sulfide) ranked on [[seborrheic-dermatitis]]; it is not a stronger or better-proven ingredient just because it is newer or marketed as fungal-acne-safe.

## The Rubric

Piroctone olamine is graded on the one thing it is actually used and tested for: controlling dandruff and seborrheic dermatitis by suppressing *Malassezia*.

**Dandruff and seborrheic dermatitis (health)**
- **Effect size: modest to notable.** In a controlled comparison, a shampoo with 0.5% piroctone olamine plus 0.45% climbazole matched a 1% zinc pyrithione shampoo: the authors reported "a comparable anti-fungal effectiveness" in vitro, and in an in-vivo split-head study "shampoo 1 was shown to be equally effective in reducing the amount of dandruff on the scalp compared with shampoo 2," with 90% of volunteers agreeing it decreased scalp itching after 4 weeks.[^schmidt] So it works about as well as zinc pyrithione, a real but not class-leading effect.
- **Evidence quality: limited.** The strongest efficacy data comes from a combination shampoo (piroctone olamine plus climbazole, so the isolated contribution of piroctone olamine is not separated) run by a manufacturer (Beiersdorf), and the ingredient is described in pharmacy literature as "an antifungal agent widely used for the management of dandruff" without an independent randomized trial of the isolated ingredient at its heart.[^schmidt][^tang]

## The Evidence

The honest profile is an effective, well-tolerated antifungal with a limited, industry-weighted evidence base. Its most-cited clinical study is a head-to-head against the reference over-the-counter antidandruff active, zinc pyrithione: the piroctone olamine plus climbazole shampoo delivered "comparable anti-fungal effectiveness" and equal dandruff reduction, plus "significantly higher anti-mycotics substantivity" (it stayed on the scalp longer between washes).[^schmidt] Pharmacy characterization work confirms its role as a widely used antidandruff antifungal but is formulation science rather than clinical efficacy.[^tang] Because *Malassezia* also drives [[fungal-acne]] (Malassezia folliculitis), piroctone olamine is often promoted as a leave-on option for it, but that specific use rests on its antifungal mechanism rather than dedicated trials; the condition-level evidence and the better-proven treatments are on [[seborrheic-dermatitis]] and [[fungal-acne]] and are not repeated here.

## How It Works

Piroctone olamine is a hydroxypyridone antifungal (the monoethanolamine salt of piroctone) that suppresses *Malassezia*, the lipophilic yeast that colonizes sebum-rich skin and scalp and drives dandruff and seborrheic dermatitis.[^tang] Reducing the yeast load lowers the scaling, itch, and inflammation of those conditions, which is why it is formulated into shampoos and, more recently, leave-on scalp and face products. It is generally well tolerated at the low concentrations used in cosmetics.

## Common Marketing Claims

- **"Fungal-acne-safe" / clears fungal acne.** Grounded in a real anti-*Malassezia* mechanism, but the specific fungal-acne (Malassezia folliculitis) use rests on that mechanism rather than dedicated trials; better-proven options are on [[fungal-acne]].[^tang]
- **"Gentle, sulfate-free alternative to medicated dandruff shampoos."** Fair as a tolerability point; on efficacy it roughly matches zinc pyrithione rather than exceeding it, and sits below ketoconazole and selenium sulfide on the evidence ranked on [[seborrheic-dermatitis]].[^schmidt]
