Palmitoyl Tripeptide-1 (INCI name; chemically palmitoyl-glycyl-histidyl-lysine, Pal-GHK, C30H54N6O5) is a synthetic "matrikine" signal peptide, a fatty-acid-conjugated version of the naturally occurring wound-healing tripeptide GHK, marketed in anti-aging serums and creams as the fibroblast-stimulating half of the "Matrixyl 3000" complex alongside [[palmitoyl-tetrapeptide-7]].[^pubchem] Its mechanistic rationale traces to a single 1988 in vitro finding that the unpalmitoylated, copper-bound version of GHK stimulates collagen synthesis in cultured fibroblasts at picomolar to nanomolar concentrations, but a 2024 independent pharmaceutics review of topical GHK peptides concludes there is "a surprising absence of clinical studies" of Pal-GHK itself, and every clinical trial identified here tested Pal-GHK only as one ingredient in a multi-active formulation, never in isolation against a vehicle control.[^maquart][^mortazavi][^yang][^wang][^trookman]

> A 2024 independent review of topical GHK-based peptides for wrinkle reduction, covering both the copper complex (GHK-Cu) and the palmitoylated form used in cosmetics (Pal-GHK), states plainly that "there is a surprising absence of clinical studies using them," despite both being "widely used in anti-wrinkle products available on the cosmetic market."[^mortazavi]

## The Rubric

**Fine lines and wrinkles / collagen support (topical, as marketed)**
- **Effect size: not established for the isolated ingredient.** No identified trial applied Pal-GHK alone against a vehicle control and measured a skin endpoint; every clinical use found here bundles it with 3 to 5 other actives (yeast/rice ferment filtrate, N-acetylneuraminic acid, palmitoyl tetrapeptide-7, human growth factors, hyaluronic acid, niacin, baicalin) in formulas whose overall improvement cannot be attributed to Pal-GHK specifically.[^yang][^trookman][^wang]
- **Evidence quality: weak.** The strongest direct statement on the evidence base is a 2024 independent (no stated conflicts of interest) pharmaceutics review concluding that clinical data on Pal-GHK are essentially absent, a gap the review attributes partly to the peptide's poor inherent skin permeability.[^mortazavi] The combination studies that do exist are industry-run (cosmetic-ingredient manufacturers or the eye-cream/lip-treatment brand itself), open-label or uncontrolled, and none isolates Pal-GHK's contribution.[^yang][^trookman][^wang]

## How It Works

GHK (glycyl-L-histidyl-L-lysine) is a naturally occurring tripeptide first isolated from human plasma and proposed to be liberated at wound sites by proteolysis of type I collagen's alpha-2 chain, which contains a GHK sequence; the original 1988 report found that the copper-bound form, GHK-Cu, stimulated collagen synthesis in cultured fibroblasts, with the effect beginning between 10^-12 and 10^-11 M and maximal around 10^-9 M, independent of any change in cell number.[^maquart] Palmitoyl Tripeptide-1 attaches a 16-carbon palmitic acid chain to GHK instead of copper, a modification intended to improve lipophilicity and skin penetration for topical cosmetic use, and it is marketed as a "matrikine," a signal peptide meant to mimic fragments generated during natural collagen breakdown and thereby prompt fibroblasts to synthesize new matrix.[^mortazavi] A 2024 review notes that although cellular studies support GHK's general capacity to stimulate collagen and glycosaminoglycan production, the palmitoylated cosmetic form's actual skin permeability and downstream efficacy remain insufficiently characterized in the literature, and permeation-enhancement strategies (chemical modification, cell-penetrating peptide conjugation, microneedling) are still being investigated to get more of the peptide into viable skin.[^mortazavi]

## The Evidence

**Original fibroblast collagen-synthesis finding, GHK-Cu (in vitro, not the cosmetic form).** Maquart and colleagues treated cultured human fibroblasts with the copper complex of GHK and found collagen synthesis was stimulated starting at 10^-12 to 10^-11 M and peaked near 10^-9 M, without a change in cell number.[^maquart] *This is the foundational mechanistic result behind GHK's collagen-stimulating reputation, but it used the copper-bound peptide, not the palmitoylated Pal-GHK sold in cosmetics, and it is a cell-culture result with no skin-application data.*

**Independent review of topical GHK peptides (narrative review, 2024).** A pharmaceutics and drug-delivery review from Shahid Beheshti University of Medical Sciences examined the published literature on GHK-Cu and Pal-GHK as anti-wrinkle actives. It affirms that cellular studies support GHK's tissue-regenerative and collagen-stimulating biology, but states directly that despite widespread commercial use, "there is a surprising absence of clinical studies using them," and separately flags that GHK's hydrophilic, low-permeability nature limits how much reaches its site of action without enhancement.[^mortazavi] *An independent group that reviewed the literature specifically to answer whether GHK peptides work on skin concluded the clinical evidence simply is not there yet, for either the copper or palmitoylated form.*

**Multi-active eye cream containing Pal-GHK plus palmitoyl tetrapeptide-7 (topical, combination, industry).** A 12-week clinical trial from a cosmetic-ingredient manufacturer (Mageline Biology Tech) tested a periorbital eye cream whose active complex was yeast/rice fermentation filtrate, N-acetylneuraminic acid, palmityl tripeptide-1, and palmitoyl tetrapeptide-7. In fibroblast culture the complex increased collagen- and elastin-related gene and protein expression; clinically, hydration rose 28.12%, elasticity 18.81%, and ultrasound-measured collagen density 54.99% after 12 weeks, with dermatologist- and self-rated improvement in fine lines by week 8.[^yang] *A real improvement was measured, but the formula combines four actives and the trial had no vehicle-only arm, so none of the result can be assigned to Pal-GHK specifically, and the sponsor is an ingredient supplier with a direct commercial interest in the complex.*

**Circadian-timed Pal-GHK plus baicalin regimen (topical, combination, industry).** A 2026 study from a Chinese cosmetic-biotechnology company proposed applying baicalin by day and Pal-GHK by night to match circadian gene-expression rhythms in skin fibroblasts, then ran an 8-week mouse study and a 30-woman clinical trial of the paired regimen. The clinical trial reported skin luminance +16.29%, nasolabial fold depth -36.35%, and firmness (R2) +24.35% after the combination regimen.[^wang] *Both actives were applied together in every clinical arm reported, so this cannot show what Pal-GHK alone does; it is also a small (n=30), industry-funded trial of a paired day/night product concept rather than an isolated-ingredient test.*

**Lip treatment containing a "tripeptide palmitoyl-glycyl-histidyl-lysine complex" (topical, combination, open-label).** A 4-week, open-label study in 32 women tested a two-step lip product; step one combined human growth factors, hyaluronic acid, marine filling spheres, emollients, and the Pal-GHK complex. Lip scaling, cracking, fine lines, texture, and overall condition improved significantly (P<0.001) by week 2 and week 4, with corneometer-confirmed hydration and caliper-confirmed plumping.[^trookman] *An open-label study with no control group and several other active ingredients in the same formula; it demonstrates the product worked, not that Pal-GHK contributed to that result.*

## Common Marketing Claims

- **"Stimulates collagen production" / "signals your skin to make more collagen."** The mechanistic story is real for GHK biology in general, a 1988 in vitro study showed the copper-bound tripeptide stimulates fibroblast collagen synthesis at very low concentrations, but that experiment used GHK-Cu, not the palmitoylated Pal-GHK actually used in cosmetics, and no cited trial isolates Pal-GHK's own contribution to a skin collagen outcome.[^maquart][^mortazavi] Treat this as a plausible-but-unproven extrapolation, not a demonstrated effect of the specific ingredient on the specific tissue.
- **"Clinically proven anti-aging peptide," as seen on products built around "Matrixyl 3000."** Every clinical trial identified here tested Pal-GHK only as one ingredient among several, with no vehicle-only comparison arm, and an independent 2024 review of the literature explicitly found a "surprising absence" of clinical studies on the peptide itself.[^yang][^wang][^trookman][^mortazavi] "Clinically proven" claims on Pal-GHK products describe the whole formula's performance, not the peptide's isolated effect.
