Collagen is the protein skin ageing is a story about, and putting it on skin does not put it back. **Bottom line:** the molecule is roughly 300 kDa, and the working limit for crossing the stratum corneum is 500 Da, so topical collagen is about six hundred times too large to get where it would need to go. What it can do is sit on the surface and hold water. The ingredients that do act on collagen production are at [[peptides]] and [[retinoids]].

> **Two numbers, from two sources, settle most of the argument.** A 2023 review of collagen delivery states that "the topical administration of collagen is limited by poor permeability due to high molecular weight (±300 kDa)".[^delivery] The 500 Dalton rule, argued in Experimental Dermatology in 2000, holds that "the molecular weight (MW) of a compound must be under 500 Dalton to allow skin absorption. Larger molecules cannot pass the corneal layer."[^500da] Those are not in tension. They are the same fact from either end.

## The Rubric

- **Effect size: minimal, for the thing people buy it for.** Replacing or rebuilding dermal collagen from a leave-on product is not what this ingredient does, because it cannot reach the dermis. The 2023 review's own conclusion is that further research is needed "to increase its penetration either by physical modification or entrapment into suitable carrier", which is a statement that current topical delivery does not work.[^delivery]
- **Effect size: modest, for what it actually does.** A large hydrophilic protein film on the surface holds water, and surface hydration makes skin look temporarily smoother and plumper. That is a real cosmetic effect and it is the same mechanism any humectant film-former provides; it is not specific to collagen and does not justify a premium for the word.
- **Evidence quality: preliminary.** The penetration limit is well sourced. What is thin is evidence that a collagen-containing product outperforms an equivalent humectant without it, which would be the test that matters, and no such comparison is cited here because none was found.
- **The hydrolysed version is smaller, and the file does not say how much smaller.** The same review names "hydrolyzed collagen" among the strategies for improving delivery, alongside entrapment in a carrier.[^delivery] It gives no molecular weight for the hydrolysate, so this page gives none either. Smaller than 300 kDa is not the same as under 500 Da, and the gap between those is where the marketing lives.
- **Scope note: the oral question is deferred, not answered.** The same review reports that oral collagen "has an effective biological activity but requires large doses (up to 5 g daily)".[^delivery] Oral supplements are outside what this site currently covers, so nothing here evaluates that claim either way.

## The Evidence

The product-level arguments already live on the pages that need them: [[medicube-triple-collagen-serum]], [[biodance-bio-collagen-real-deep-mask]], [[medicube-collagen-jelly-cream]] and [[biodance-collagen-cleansing-oil]]. This page exists so those pages have one place to point at rather than each re-deriving the same physics.

What does raise the skin's own collagen is a different set of ingredients and a different mechanism, argued at [[retinoids]] and [[peptides]]. A signal peptide is small enough to penetrate and is meant to instruct; a collagen molecule is neither. [[microneedling]] is the procedural route that bypasses the barrier entirely.

## Common Marketing Claims

> One real example, quoted from a product page on this site where it is sourced to the brand, plus the framings that recur. The recurring ones are described rather than quoted, because they are patterns across the category and not a sentence any one brand is on record for.

- **A real one.** Medicube markets its serum on a "Triple Collagen Complex" it describes as "1000x fortified collagen, hydrolyzed collagen, atelocollagen, and soluble collagen", set out with its source on [[medicube-triple-collagen-serum]]. Four forms of a molecule that cannot cross the stratum corneum is still four forms of a molecule that cannot cross the stratum corneum, and the brand attaches no molecular weight to any of them.
- **Replenishing or restoring collagen** is the standard framing and it is the one the two sources above rule out for a leave-on product. Any visible change is at the surface.
- **Depth claims** are the specific thing molecular weight forecloses.
- **Marine, bovine or vegan sourcing** as a point of difference does not change the size of the protein, and size is the constraint.
