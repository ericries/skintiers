Ranks the peptides the site profiles by how much independent human evidence supports a visible skin benefit. Peptides are one of the most heavily marketed anti-aging ingredient classes, but the honest picture is thin: most claims rest on manufacturer studies or in-vitro data, and none reach the evidence level of retinoids or vitamin C. The general class story (signal, carrier, and neurotransmitter peptides) lives on [[peptides]]; this list is only the evidence ranking of the specific peptides profiled here.

## How to read the tiers

**Moderate** means there is some independent human data for a modest, real effect. **Weak** means the evidence is limited or comes mostly from the ingredient's manufacturer or from in-vitro (test-tube) work that does not establish a benefit on real skin. No peptide profiled here earns a strong rating, which is itself the point: this is a category where marketing runs well ahead of the evidence.
