## The tier list

These are 2% [[zinc-pyrithione|pyrithione zinc]] cleansing bars used on the face and body for [[seborrheic-dermatitis]] and everyday [[dandruff]]-prone skin, the wash-off counterpart to the shampoo-focused rankings on [[anti-dandruff-seborrheic-dermatitis-treatments-by-evidence]]. All three deliver the same 2% active at the same brief rinse-off exposure, so the ingredient-level evidence on [[zinc-pyrithione]] applies equally to each and none has been tested head to head against the others.

The one real differentiator is regulatory footing: the [[vanicream-z-bar-pyrithione-zinc|Vanicream Z-Bar]] is registered as an FDA over-the-counter drug with a Drug Facts label listing "pyrithione zinc 2%" and the purpose "Anti-dandruff, Anti-seborrheic dermatitis,"[^dailymed] while the [[noble-formula-2-pyrithione-zinc-bar-soap|Noble Formula]] and [[dermaharmony-2-pyrithione-zinc-bar-soap|DermaHarmony]] bars are sold as cosmetics carrying the same 2% active without their own Drug Facts registration. Because a rinse-off bar's benefit depends on consistent use rather than the specific base, pick the one whose price, fragrance, and feel you will actually keep using.
