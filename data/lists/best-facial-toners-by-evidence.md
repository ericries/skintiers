"Toner" is a legacy word. The old astringent, alcohol-based, pore-stripping toner meant to undo the damage of harsh bar-soap cleansing is mostly gone from modern formulas; what is sold as a toner today is either a humectant hydrating step (glycerin, hyaluronic acid, soothing plant extracts) or a diluted acid exfoliant in liquid form. A toner is only as good as what is actually in it, so this list ranks each product by its real category rather than by the marketing name, and links out to the ingredient pages that own the evidence.

## The tier list

**Hydrating and soothing toners** do one job well: add water-binding humectants back to the skin after cleansing, before the rest of a routine. None of these are treatments; they are a comfort and hydration layer.

**[[isntree-hyaluronic-acid-toner|Isntree Hyaluronic Acid Toner]]** and **[[haruharu-wonder-black-rice-hyaluronic-toner|Haruharu Wonder Black Rice Hyaluronic Toner]]** both lead with hyaluronic acid, the best-evidenced humectant for surface hydration; see [[hyaluronic-acid]].

**[[anua-heartleaf-77-soothing-toner|Anua Heartleaf 77% Soothing Toner]]** is built around Houttuynia cordata (heartleaf) extract at a high stated concentration for a calming, non-active hydrating step; see [[houttuynia-cordata]].

**[[anua-rice-70-glow-milky-toner|Anua Rice 70 Glow Milky Toner]]** pairs a rice-extract base with niacinamide, giving it a mild brightening angle on top of hydration; niacinamide's evidence is on [[niacinamide]].

**[[beauty-of-joseon-ginseng-essence-water|Beauty of Joseon Ginseng Essence Water]]** is an essence-toner hybrid combining niacinamide with panax ginseng root water; the niacinamide is the better-evidenced part, per [[niacinamide]].

**Exfoliating acid toners** are genuine actives, not merely prep steps, and belong in the same evidence tier as any other leave-on exfoliant.

**[[cosrx-aha-bha-clarifying-treatment-toner|COSRX AHA/BHA Clarifying Treatment Toner]]** combines glycolic acid and salicylic acid, stacking a well-evidenced AHA with a well-evidenced BHA; see [[glycolic-acid]] and [[salicylic-acid]].

**[[the-ordinary-glycolic-acid-7-toning-solution|The Ordinary Glycolic Acid 7% Toning Solution]]** is a straightforward 7% glycolic acid liquid exfoliant labeled as a toner; the AHA evidence is on [[glycolic-acid]].

**[[naturium-multi-bright-milky-toner|Naturium Multi-Bright Milky Toner]]** lists an azelaic acid derivative alongside niacinamide but discloses no percentage for the azelaic ingredient, so it ranks last: a plausible brightening blend with the least verifiable dosing of the group. See [[azelaic-acid]] and [[niacinamide]].

If a toner's only ingredients are water, glycerin, and fragrance, it is not doing anything an inexpensive moisturizer or the following serum step would not already cover. The value here is in the specific active, not the format.
