This is not a haul. It is the minimum routine that the evidence actually supports, one product per step, morning and night. The two steps that matter most are the two people skip: sunscreen every morning and giving a retinoid, if you use one, weeks to work before judging it. Everything else here is optional polish.

**Morning:** gentle cleanse, an optional vitamin C serum, then sunscreen. Sunscreen is the one non-negotiable; a randomized trial of daily broad-spectrum sunscreen use over 4.5 years slowed measurable skin aging (photoaging) versus discretionary use, and that same cohort later showed fewer melanomas.[^nambour] The evidence case is on [[sunscreen-uv-filters]].

**Evening:** cleanse again, a retinoid if your skin tolerates it, then moisturizer to buffer irritation. Retinoids are the best-evidenced anti-aging and anti-acne active in this routine, covered on [[retinoids]]; start low and infrequent (two or three nights a week) and build up.

## The tier list

Five products, five steps, in the order to build them:

**[[cerave-hydrating-facial-cleanser|CeraVe Hydrating Facial Cleanser]].** A fragrance-free, non-stripping cleanser for both ends of the routine; it will not undo an irritated barrier the way a foaming or scrubbing cleanser can. Use morning and night as step one.

**[[la-roche-posay-pure-vitamin-c-12-serum|La Roche-Posay Pure Vitamin C12 Serum]].** Optional AM add-on after cleansing. Vitamin C (ascorbic acid) has real but modest evidence for brightening and antioxidant support; the strength case and how to judge stability are on [[ascorbic-acid-vitamin-c]]. Skip it before you have the first two steps dialed in.

**[[eltamd-uv-clear-broad-spectrum-spf-46|EltaMD UV Clear Broad-Spectrum SPF 46]].** The last AM step, every day, rain or shine. This is the step with the strongest long-term evidence in the whole routine.[^nambour]

**[[differin-adapalene-gel-0-1|Differin Gel (adapalene 0.1%)]].** The one active in this routine, used PM only. An over-the-counter adapalene retinoid whose Drug Facts label lists it as an acne treatment, and the entry point most people tolerate better than prescription tretinoin.[^adapalene] Introduce it after cleanser and before moisturizer, a few nights a week at first.

**[[cerave-daily-moisturizing-lotion|CeraVe Daily Moisturizing Lotion]].** Closes out both AM and PM. Moisturizer does not treat anything on its own, but it keeps the barrier intact while a retinoid is doing its work; the barrier case is on [[moisturizing]].

Add actives one at a time, not all at once: if something starts stinging or peeling, it is easier to find the culprit when you only changed one thing. A retinoid and a vitamin C serum can usually coexist, but if either one irritates you, cut back before adding the next step.
