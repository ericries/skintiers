This ranks finished, over-the-counter sunscreens whose only active ingredients are zinc oxide and/or titanium dioxide, no organic (chemical) filters like avobenzone, octinoxate, or octocrylene. The general case for broad-spectrum sunscreen, cutting skin-cancer risk and slowing photoaging, and the chemistry behind these two mineral filters, are covered on [[sunscreen-uv-filters]]; this list does not re-argue that case, it ranks how well each product delivers it.

## The tier list

Zinc oxide and titanium dioxide cover different parts of the UV spectrum, so products that pair both filters at a high combined percentage rank near the top; a high single-filter zinc load can still outrank a low-load combo, and lower-load formulas rank below. Products tested only to a non-US standard (no FDA Drug Facts label) rank lowest, since the label itself is one of the independent facts this site can verify. Several entries are tinted with iron oxides, which unlike UV-only filters also block visible light, a real bonus for melasma and post-inflammatory pigmentation covered on [[hyperpigmentation]].

**[[eltamd-uv-aox-elements-tinted-mineral-spf-50|EltaMD UV AOX Elements SPF 50]].** FDA Drug Facts actives are titanium dioxide 4.4% and zinc oxide 13.5%, the highest zinc load of any combo product here, plus an iron-oxide tint.

**[[cerave-hydrating-mineral-sunscreen-spf-50|CeraVe Hydrating Mineral Sunscreen SPF 50]].** Titanium dioxide 9%/zinc oxide 7%, a high combined mineral load at a budget price point.

**[[cetaphil-sheer-mineral-sunscreen-spf-50|Cetaphil Sheer Mineral Sunscreen SPF 50]].** The same titanium dioxide 9%/zinc oxide 7% load as CeraVe's SPF 50, in lotion format.

**[[eltamd-uv-elements-tinted-mineral-sunscreen-spf-44|EltaMD UV Elements SPF 44]].** Zinc oxide 10.0%/titanium dioxide 5.7%, tinted; the older SPF 44 predecessor to EltaMD's AOX formula above.

**[[prequel-sun-barrier-mineral-sunscreen|Prequel Sun Barrier Mineral Sunscreen]].** Zinc oxide 18.0% as the sole active, roughly double most single-filter competitors' load, but no titanium dioxide to add UVB efficiency.

**[[isdin-eryfotona-ageless-tinted-mineral-spf-50|ISDIN Eryfotona Ageless Tinted Mineral SPF 50]].** Zinc oxide 10.7% alone, tinted, no titanium dioxide.

**[[avene-mineral-ultra-light-spf-50|Avène Mineral Ultra-Light SPF 50+]].** Titanium dioxide 4%/zinc oxide 6%, a lower combined load than the EltaMD/CeraVe/Cetaphil formulas above, and rated under the EU standard rather than FDA-tested.

**[[cerave-hydrating-mineral-sunscreen-spf-30-face|CeraVe Hydrating Mineral Sunscreen SPF 30 (Face)]].** Titanium dioxide 6%/zinc oxide 5%, the lower-SPF sibling of CeraVe's SPF 50 formula.

**[[cetaphil-sheer-mineral-sunscreen-stick-spf-50|Cetaphil Sheer Mineral Sunscreen Stick SPF 50]].** Titanium dioxide 6%/zinc oxide 4.7%, a lower filter load than Cetaphil's own lotion, in stick format.

**[[vanicream-facial-moisturizer-spf-30|Vanicream Facial Moisturizer SPF 30]].** Zinc oxide 19.5% alone, the highest single-filter load here, but no titanium dioxide and a plain moisturizer base rather than a dedicated sunscreen formula.

**[[la-roche-posay-anthelios-mineral-tinted-spf-50|La Roche-Posay Anthelios Tinted Mineral SPF 50]].** Titanium dioxide 11% is the sole active in this shade, no zinc oxide, giving it a narrower UVA curve than the combo products above.

**[[aestura-derma-uv365-barrier-hydro-mineral-sunscreen|Aéstura Derma UV365 Barrier Hydro Mineral Sunscreen]].** Zinc oxide (nano) alone, no independently verified FDA Drug Facts label, ranking it last on verification grounds even though zinc oxide itself is a real filter.

Sunscreen only works with correct amount and reapplication; even the top formula here underperforms if applied too thin or skipped after swimming or sweating. Nothing here is medical advice.
