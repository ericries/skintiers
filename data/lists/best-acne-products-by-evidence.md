This ranks the [[acne]] products covered on this site by the strength of the evidence behind their labeled active, drawn from each product's own page grade. It is not a head-to-head trial of the finished formulas; it is a way to see, at a glance, which products are built on the best-evidenced acne actives.

> The strongest acne products are topical retinoids ([[adapalene]], [[tretinoin]], [[tazarotene]], trifarotene) and fixed retinoid-plus-[[benzoyl-peroxide]] combinations, which have the deepest trial support and anchor dermatology guidelines. [[azelaic-acid]] and [[salicylic-acid]] products are solid second-line choices. Older [[sodium-sulfacetamide]] and [[sulfur]] products carry a real acne indication but the thinnest modern trial evidence, so they rank last.

For the full active-by-active picture, including oral options like [[isotretinoin]] that are not a single product, see the [[acne]] hub. Each product's grade and caveats live on its own page; this list only orders them by that evidence, so a high rank means a well-evidenced active, not that the specific formula was proven better than the next one in a trial.

## Sources

No product-specific trial statistics are cited beyond what is already sourced and verified on each linked product page and on the underlying active pages ([[adapalene]], [[tretinoin]], [[tazarotene]], [[benzoyl-peroxide]], [[azelaic-acid]], [[salicylic-acid]], [[sodium-sulfacetamide]], [[sulfur]]). The ranking follows the evidence ordering those pages and the [[acne]] hub already establish.
