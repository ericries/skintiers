A Korean-brand-led routine for oily and combination skin, drawn only from products already graded on the site. It leans on niacinamide, the ingredient with the most oil-relevant evidence, plus lightweight, oil-free hydration and mattifying steps, so oily skin gets treated without stripping it (which tends to backfire and drive more sebum). Two picks, the cleanser and the mattifying daytime moisturizer, are Western staples chosen for function rather than origin, and are noted as such below.

## What the routine is built from

The single treatment active is niacinamide, which is [both cosmetic and evidenced](#the-evidence) for oily skin; everything else is gentle cleansing, oil-free hydration, and sun protection.

- [[some-by-mi-yuja-niacin-serum|Some By Mi Yuja Niacin Serum]] is the niacinamide step, used morning and night. Topical [[niacinamide]] has the best evidence among the routine's actives for reducing sebum and evening tone on oily skin.

The supporting products keep oil in check without over-drying.

- [[cerave-foaming-facial-cleanser|CeraVe Foaming Facial Cleanser]] is a gel-foaming cleanser suited to oilier skin (a non-Korean staple, included for function); it still carries [[ceramides]] so it cleans without stripping the barrier.
- [[la-roche-posay-effaclar-mat|La Roche-Posay Effaclar Mat]] is the daytime mattifying moisturizer, built around [[niacinamide]] and a sebum-controlling texture (the routine's other non-Korean pick).
- [[innisfree-no-sebum-mineral-powder|Innisfree No Sebum Mineral Powder]] is a [[silica]]-based finishing powder that absorbs surface oil through the day.
- [[cosrx-oil-free-ultra-moisturizing-lotion-birch-sap|COSRX Oil-Free Ultra-Moisturizing Lotion (Birch Sap)]] is the nighttime moisturizer: oil-free, lightweight hydration from [[niacinamide]] and [[hyaluronic-acid]] for skin that does not want a heavy cream.
- [[beauty-of-joseon-relief-sun-rice-probiotics-spf50|Beauty of Joseon Relief Sun SPF50]] is a lightweight chemical sunscreen ([[bisoctrizole]], [[ethylhexyl-triazone]]) that suits oily skin better than heavier formulas; the filter evidence sits on [[sunscreen-uv-filters]].
