This ranks on-site sunscreens whose tint comes from iron oxides, the pigment that adds visible-light protection on top of ordinary UV filtering. That extra layer matters for [[melasma]] and [[hyperpigmentation]], where visible light, beyond UV alone, can trigger and worsen pigment darkening, and is a separate list from [[best-mineral-sunscreens-by-evidence]], which ranks plain mineral sunscreens on UV protection alone.

Be honest about the strength split here: broad-spectrum UV protection is well established category-wide, on [[sunscreen-uv-filters]]. The visible-light case for iron oxides is real but thinner, one randomized trial showing a modest edge for iron-oxide sunscreen over UV-only sunscreen (both paired with hydroquinone) in melasma, plus supporting irradiation studies, all detailed on [[iron-oxides]].[^castanedo][^lyons] None of the products below disclose an iron oxide percentage, so this list ranks by the two things that are disclosed and verifiable: how strong and broad-spectrum the mineral filtering is, and whether the base is 100% mineral or a chemical hybrid.

## The tier list

The top three are 100% mineral, dual-filter (zinc oxide plus titanium dioxide) formulas from one brand's lineup, ordered by combined active-ingredient percentage and SPF. Next come two single-mineral-filter tinted sunscreens, then a chemical-mineral hybrid, then two products where the tint itself is lighter or the filter set is entirely chemical.
