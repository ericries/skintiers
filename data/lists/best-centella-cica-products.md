This ranks on-site "cica" products by how much centella asiatica they actually contain and how clearly it's dosed, not by how loudly the packaging says "cica." The honest framing lives on [[centella-asiatica]]: the plant's triterpenes (asiaticoside, madecassoside, asiatic acid, madecassic acid) have a real, well-mapped mechanism for wound healing and collagen support, and a small human trial record for wounds, but the redness-soothing and anti-aging claims that sell most K-beauty "cica" products rest on lab mechanism, not controlled human trials. It's a better-than-most botanical, not a proven anti-inflammatory. General barrier-support and moisturizing context is on [[skin-barrier-repair]].

## The tier list

The order below runs from formulas built around centella as the headline single active to formulas where it's one supporting ingredient among several, per each product's own ingredient list and marketing.

**[[skin1004-madagascar-centella-asiatica-100-ampoule|SKIN1004 Madagascar Centella Asiatica 100 Ampoule]].** The most centella-forward product on site: a single-active formula built around centella asiatica extract, the closest thing here to testing the ingredient on its own.

**[[beauty-of-joseon-light-on-serum-centella-vita-c|Beauty of Joseon Light On Serum: Centella + Vita C]].** Centella asiatica extract is one of the two headline actives (alongside vitamin C), positioned for soothing; concentration undisclosed.

**[[purito-cica-clearing-bb-cream|Purito Cica Clearing BB Cream]].** A cica-branded tinted BB cream: "cica" carries the product name, but on the actual ingredient list centella asiatica extract sits low, near the preservative system, behind the silicone base and niacinamide, at an undisclosed concentration. It ranks here for the cica branding, not for centella prominence in the bottle.

**[[skin1004-hyalu-cica-water-fit-sun-serum-uv|SKIN1004 Hyalu-Cica Water-Fit Sun Serum UV]].** A sunscreen that also carries centella asiatica; the SPF is the primary job, the cica is a soothing add-on, covered generally on [[sunscreen-uv-filters]].

**[[skin1004-madagascar-centella-air-fit-suncream|SKIN1004 Madagascar Centella Air-Fit Suncream]].** Same brand pairing of sunscreen plus centella positioning; again, evaluate the SPF performance first and the cica claim as a bonus.

**[[iope-stem-3-clinical-recovery-serum|IOPE Stem 3 Clinical Recovery Serum]].** Lists three centella triterpenes (asiaticoside, madecassic acid, asiatic acid), but per [[centella-asiatica]] the "recovery"/soothing framing this product leans on is not clinically established, only mechanistically plausible.

**[[la-roche-posay-cicaplast-baume-b5|La Roche-Posay Cicaplast Baume B5]].** Includes centella asiatica leaf extract and madecassoside on the ingredient list, but the formula and evidence case are built around 5% panthenol, not centella; it's here as a barrier-repair balm that happens to also contain cica actives, not a cica product proper. Panthenol's own evidence is on [[panthenol]].

Left off this list: products marketed with "cica" in the name or a heartleaf (Houttuynia cordata) base where centella asiatica is a minor, undosed ingredient far down the list, since ranking those by centella evidence would overstate what's actually in the bottle.
