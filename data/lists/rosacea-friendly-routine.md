A low-irritant routine for rosacea, built only from products already graded on the site: two gentle, barrier-supporting steps around a single evidence-backed active, azelaic acid, plus the daily sunscreen that matters more for rosacea than almost any other condition. It deliberately leaves out the exfoliating acids, high-strength retinoids, and fragrance that commonly flare rosacea.

## What the routine is built from

Rosacea care is mostly about doing less, gently, and being consistent with the one or two things that have real evidence.

- [[cos-de-baha-az20-azelaic-acid-20-serum|Cos De BAHA AZ20 Azelaic Acid 20% Cream]] is the only active in the routine. Azelaic acid is the topical with the best controlled evidence for papulopustular rosacea, where the 15% prescription gel is FDA-approved; the ingredient case is on [[azelaic-acid]] and the condition itself on [[rosacea]] and [[rosacea-papulopustular]]. It also calms the redness of [[rosacea-erythematotelangiectatic|the erythematotelangiectatic type]] for some people. This is a 20% cosmetic version, above the studied gel strength, so it is placed on already-cleansed skin and paired with a plain moisturizer to limit stinging.

The rest of the routine is chosen to support the barrier and avoid triggers, not to add more actives.

- [[la-roche-posay-toleriane-hydrating-gentle-cleanser|La Roche-Posay Toleriane Hydrating Gentle Cleanser]] is a non-foaming, fragrance-free cleanser with [[ceramides]] and [[niacinamide]] for both washes; harsh foaming cleansers are a common rosacea aggravator.
- [[la-roche-posay-toleriane|La Roche-Posay Toleriane]] is a plain barrier moisturizer that offsets any dryness from the azelaic acid, again without competing actives.
- [[eltamd-uv-clear-spf-46|EltaMD UV Clear SPF 46]] is the daytime sunscreen. Ultraviolet exposure is one of the most frequently reported rosacea triggers, so daily sun protection is both a cosmetic and a flare-prevention step; its [[niacinamide]] content also suits reactive skin. The filter evidence sits on [[sunscreen-uv-filters]].
