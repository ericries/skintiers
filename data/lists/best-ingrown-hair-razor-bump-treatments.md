This list ranks the finished products on this site for ingrown hairs and razor bumps, the condition hubs for which are [[ingrown-hairs]] and [[pseudofolliculitis-barbae]]. It is deliberately narrow: those hubs own the evidence for the actives and for the non-product options, and this page only sorts what you can actually buy.

Two things have to be said before any product ranking, because both outrank everything below. A treatment review of pseudofolliculitis barbae concluded that "effective treatment for PFB requires a multifaceted approach that targets various aspects of the pathogenesis," listing preventive measures alongside keratolytics and laser, and found that "topical therapies are currently the mainstay treatment. However, laser hair removal has become a potential long-term treatment option."[^dalia] So the foundation is changing how you remove the hair, which costs nothing, and laser is the long-term option, covered on [[laser-hair-removal]]. The keratolytics are the mainstay topical add-on; their evidence is set out on [[ingrown-hairs]], which grades them as comparably evidenced rather than ranked against one another, so this list does not invent a winner among the actives.

> No product on this list has a published trial of its own. Every grade below is an inference from the disclosed active and the format, not a measured result, so the honest comparison is "which of these delivers a mechanism the evidence supports" rather than "which works best." A product with an exfoliating acid has that mechanism; an oil or a shave gel does not.

## The tier list

Each product is graded on the keratolytic it discloses and how its format delivers it. The note names what is actually in it and where its full grading lives.
