This ranks topical treatments for plaque [[psoriasis]] by the strength of their trial evidence, prescription options included. That is a deliberately wider scope than the psoriasis page's own tier list, which is OTC-only; here, potent corticosteroids and vitamin D analogs sit at the top because that is where the evidence actually is, not because they are what a drugstore shelf offers.

The honest framing: psoriasis is a chronic, relapsing autoimmune-driven disease, and no topical cures it. Corticosteroids and the calcipotriene/betamethasone combination have the longest track record and the largest effect sizes,[^psofast] tapinarof and roflumilast are newer steroid-free options with strong phase 3 data of their own,[^psoaring][^dermis] coal tar is an older adjunct with real but thinner modern evidence, and salicylic acid only manages scale rather than the disease itself.

Ranked from the best-evidenced first-line actives down to adjuncts that manage symptoms rather than the underlying disease.

Full citation on [[tapinarof]]. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34879448/ (accessed 2026-08-30).
