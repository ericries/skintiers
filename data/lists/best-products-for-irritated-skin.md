This ranks finished, over-the-counter products for irritated, over-exfoliated, or barrier-compromised skin, the kind that stings, flakes, or looks shiny-red after too many actives or too much sun and wind. The honest framing is on [[skin-barrier-repair]]: the fix isn't a new active, it's the opposite. Stop retinoids, acids, and scrubs, and lean on a plain occlusive-plus-humectant moisturizer while the barrier rebuilds itself over days to weeks. Every product below fits that job; none of them is a treatment for the irritation's cause, so a rash that doesn't settle in a couple of weeks is a reason to see a dermatologist rather than layer on more products. See also [[sensitive-skin]] and [[moisturizing]].

## The tier list

Petrolatum is the single best-evidenced occlusive for cutting water loss and letting a barrier recover,[^petrolatum] so the petrolatum-forward ointments and creams rank first, followed by ceramide- and humectant-based creams, then panthenol- and centella-based soothers, which help modestly but don't rebuild the lipid matrix itself.[^panthenol]

None of these carry an independent clinical trial of the exact finished formula; the grades on each page rest on the category-level evidence for occlusives, humectants, and barrier lipids, detailed on [[skin-barrier-repair]], [[petrolatum]], [[ceramides]], and [[panthenol]].
