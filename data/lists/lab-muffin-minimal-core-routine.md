A deliberately minimal routine that follows the core message cosmetic chemist [[michelle-wong|Michelle Wong]] (Lab Muffin Beauty Science) sets out in her published basic-skincare guidance: the products that matter most are the least glamorous ones, a gentle cleanser, a moisturizer, and daily sunscreen, and those cornerstones do more for most skin than any trending active. It is built only from products already graded on the site.

## What the routine is built from

Wong's stated view is that "the most important products to get right are the simplest, less glamorous ones: cleanser, sunscreen and moisturiser," and that these are the foundation everything else is optional on top of.[^guide] This routine is that foundation and nothing else.

- [[cerave-foaming-facial-cleanser|CeraVe Foaming Facial Cleanser]] is the cleanser, one Lab Muffin's basic-skincare guide names among its cleanser picks; it carries [[ceramides]] so it cleans without stripping the barrier.[^recs]
- [[cerave-moisturizing-cream|CeraVe Moisturizing Cream]] is a plain barrier moisturizer, chosen here as the site's top-graded plain moisturizer for the cornerstone she describes.
- [[eltamd-uv-clear-spf-46|EltaMD UV Clear SPF 46]] is the sunscreen, the cornerstone Wong rates above any active ingredient; it is the site's top-graded daily sunscreen. Its filter coverage is on [[sunscreen-uv-filters]].

The dashboard above rolls up how these products grade, the actives they carry as a whole, and the barrier-health goal the routine serves, all computed from each product's own page. Because the routine is intentionally minimal, the dashboard shows plainly that it does not contain a retinoid, vitamin C, or an exfoliant, which is the point rather than a gap.

## How it was sourced

This is the first routine on the site sourced from a social-media creator rather than assembled in-house. The design (three cornerstones, actives optional) is Wong's, drawn from her non-sponsored basic-skincare guidance; the specific products are the site's graded matches for the cornerstones she names, including the CeraVe cleanser she recommends. A single short-form video of hers that laid out a step-by-step routine was a sponsored CeraVe partnership, so it was not used as the source, and the routine rests on her general, non-conflicted guidance instead. Her credentials and why she is a credible source sit on [[michelle-wong]].
