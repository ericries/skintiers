CeraVe sells sixteen distinct sunscreen labels in the United States, and the shelf name does not reliably tell you which kind you are holding. Because sunscreens are regulated as over-the-counter drugs, every one of them has to declare its active ingredients on an FDA label, so the question is answerable exactly rather than by reading marketing. All sixteen are grouped below by what their labels declare.[^openfda]

> **Two labels differ by one word and by their entire filter system.** "Hydrating Mineral Sunscreen Broad Spectrum SPF 30 Face" declares titanium dioxide 5.5% and zinc oxide 10%. "Hydrating Sheer Sunscreen Broad Spectrum SPF 30 Face and Body" declares homosalate 6%, octisalate 5%, octocrylene 5% and zinc oxide 7%.[^openfda] Mineral and Sheer. One word apart on the shelf, and one of them is four-fifths organic filters.

## Mineral only: nine of sixteen

Titanium dioxide and zinc oxide, no organic filter declared.[^openfda]

- Hydrating Sunscreen SPF 30 Face: titanium dioxide 6%, zinc oxide 5%
- Hydrating Sunscreen SPF 30 Body: titanium dioxide 6%, zinc oxide 5%
- Hydrating Sunscreen SPF 50 Face: titanium dioxide 9%, zinc oxide 7%
- Hydrating Sunscreen SPF 50 Body: titanium dioxide 9%, zinc oxide 7%
- Sunscreen Broad Spectrum SPF 50: titanium dioxide 6%, zinc oxide 4.7%
- Invisible Mineral Sunscreen Face SPF 50: titanium dioxide 2.66%, zinc oxide 15.35%
- Hydrating Mineral Sunscreen SPF 30 Face Sheer Tint: titanium dioxide 5.5%, zinc oxide 10%
- Hydrating Mineral Sunscreen Light Sheer Tint SPF 30: titanium dioxide 5.5%, zinc oxide 10%
- Hydrating Mineral Sunscreen SPF 30 Deep Sheer Tint: titanium dioxide 5.5%, zinc oxide 10%

The three tinted SPF 30s declare identical actives. They differ in tint, not in filter.

## Hybrid: five of sixteen

Organic filters plus zinc oxide.[^openfda]

- Hydrating Sheer Sunscreen SPF 30 Face and Body: homosalate 6%, octisalate 5%, octocrylene 5%, zinc oxide 7%
- AM Facial Moisturizing SPF 30: homosalate 10%, meradimate 5%, octinoxate 5%, octocrylene 2%, zinc oxide 6.3%
- AM Facial Moisturizing SPF 50: homosalate 8%, octisalate 5%, octocrylene 5%, zinc oxide 7%
- Facial Moisturizing with Sunscreen SPF 30: homosalate 10%, octinoxate 5%, octisalate 5%, octocrylene 2%, zinc oxide 8%
- Skin Renewing SPF 30 Day: octinoxate 7.5%, zinc oxide 10.5%

**Only one of these five says so.** The Hydrating Sheer label carries the words "Hybrid Formula" in its own label name.[^openfda] The other four do not, and three of them are sold as moisturisers rather than as sunscreens, which is where a shopper is least likely to check a drug facts panel.

## Organic only: two of sixteen

No mineral filter at all, and nothing in either name indicates it.[^openfda]

- Skin Renewing Brightening with Sunscreen SPF 30: avobenzone 3%, homosalate 5%, octisalate 5%, octocrylene 7%
- Ultra Light Moisturizing SPF 30: avobenzone 3%, homosalate 10%, octisalate 5%, octocrylene 2.7%

These two are the only CeraVe sunscreen labels declaring avobenzone, and the only ones with no zinc oxide or titanium dioxide.

## What this does and does not tell you

It tells you what is in the bottle, exactly, from the label the manufacturer is legally required to file. It does not tell you which is better. Mineral and organic filters have different strengths and the arguments live at [[sunscreen-uv-filters]] and [[zinc-oxide]]; nothing on this page should be read as mineral being safer or more effective.

The counting rule is simple and worth stating because it is the whole method: a label goes in the mineral group if it declares titanium dioxide or zinc oxide and no organic filter, in the hybrid group if it declares both kinds, and in the organic group if it declares no mineral filter. Sixteen labels, nine, five and two.
