A small, bright red, slightly raised spot made of clustered blood vessels. They arrive with age, they are harmless, and there is no cream that removes them. **Bottom line:** the useful decisions here are whether it is actually a cherry angioma, and whether you care enough to have it removed by a procedure.

> **No over-the-counter drug is listed for this.** A search of the FDA's National Drug Code directory on 2026-11-16 for products whose brand name contains `angioma` returned no records, and the same search for `hemangioma` returned none.[^ndc] That is a statement about what is registered under those brand names, not proof that nobody sells a cream making the claim, but it does mean no such product reached the directory under a name that advertises the condition. Compare [[skin-tags]], where the identical query finds twenty-six.

## How to know you have this

Red to purple, usually 1 to 5 mm, flat or domed, and they do not resolve. They multiply with age rather than one growing larger. In a Finnish population study that examined 552 people aged 70 to 93 by total body skin examination, cherry angiomas were among the four benign tumour types catalogued, and benign skin tumours were present "in up to 78.7% in certain subsets of participants".[^finland]

The thing worth checking is that it is what you think. A red or pink lesion that bleeds without being knocked, grows, or changes shape is a reason to be examined rather than reassured by a web page. [[seborrhoeic-keratosis]] and [[skin-tags]] cover the other two common benign lesions people find on themselves.

## What they are associated with

The same study found associations rather than causes, in a cross-sectional design that cannot establish direction. Cherry angiomas were more common in females than males.[^finland] Among those with multiple lesions, the study reported "an association between lower glycated haemoglobin levels and the presence of multiple cherry angiomas", while females with multiple lesions "had higher levels of high-density cholesterol and lower triglyceride values than in those with fewer cherry angiomas" and males with multiple lesions had lower mean haemoglobin.[^finland] Each at p < 0.05.

None of that is a reason to act. The authors call these "several new possible etiological factors" behind lesions that "remain poorly characterized", which is the right weight to give them.[^finland]

## The medical point most people miss

Cherry angiomas are sometimes used as a biopsy target, and this is worth understanding precisely because it sounds alarming and is not.

Intravascular large B-cell lymphoma is a rare cancer that grows inside small blood vessels, which makes it hard to find. In a Japanese series, 161 patients already suspected of lymphoma underwent random skin biopsies; six were diagnosed with the condition, and those six tended to "include senile/cherry angioma(s) at the sites of random skin biopsies" (p = 0.040), along with B symptoms (p = 0.046) and thrombocytopenia (p = 0.009).[^ivlbcl] The patients in that series presented with "fever, night sweats, and unintentional weight loss".[^ivlbcl]

Read that in the right direction. A cherry angioma is a dense tangle of the exact vessels this lymphoma hides in, so it makes a good place to sample in someone **already** being investigated for it. The angioma is the window, not the disease. Having cherry angiomas, which most older people do, is not evidence of lymphoma and is not a reason to seek a biopsy.

## The Evidence

Two papers, both read from their PubMed abstracts: one cross-sectional population study for who gets them and what they track with, and one biopsy series for the diagnostic point above.[^finland][^ivlbcl] No treatment trial was sought, because no topical treatment exists to trial. Neither paper is about removing a cherry angioma, and this page makes no efficacy claim.

## Removal

There is no topical treatment. Removal is procedural, typically by laser, electrocautery or shave excision, and is usually cosmetic rather than medical. No tier list appears on this page because there are no graded products to rank; this is one of the conditions where the honest answer is that the shelf has nothing for you.
