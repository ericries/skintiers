Seborrheic dermatitis is "a common, chronic, or relapsing form of eczema/dermatitis that mainly affects the sebaceous gland-rich regions of the scalp, face, and trunk," and dandruff is simply its uninflamed scalp form.[^dermnet] It is driven largely by an overgrowth of the skin yeast Malassezia on oily skin, which is why topical antifungals are the mainstay of treatment.[^dermnet][^cochrane]

## How to know you have this

Adult seborrheic dermatitis shows up as salmon-pink, flaky, ill-defined patches with minimal itch in the oiliest areas: the scalp, the sides of the nose and nasolabial folds, the eyebrows and glabella, the ears and behind them, and the mid-chest.[^dermnet] It typically flares in winter and eases in summer with sun exposure, and it is chronic and relapsing.[^dermnet] It is common, affecting an estimated "3% to 12% of the population," and is one of the more frequent diagnoses in darker skin, where it can instead leave scaly hypopigmented (lighter) patches.[^dermnet]

## What is happening in the skin

The cause is not fully understood, but "proliferation of Malassezia yeast genus is believed to play a role": the yeast's enzymes cleave free fatty acids from the triglycerides in sebum, which appears to trigger the inflammation and flaking.[^dermnet] That is why the core of treatment is reducing the yeast itself rather than only lifting the scale off the surface.

## What actually works

Topical antifungals are first-line, and the best-evidenced is [[ketoconazole]] (2%, as a shampoo or cream); ciclopirox, zinc pyrithione, and selenium sulfide are used the same way.[^dermnet][^cochrane] Mild topical corticosteroids calm an acute flare over one to three weeks and clear it about as well as ketoconazole but with more side effects, so antifungals are preferred for the long term.[^cochrane][^dermnet] Topical calcineurin inhibitors ([[pimecrolimus]] and tacrolimus) are a steroid-sparing option on facial skin, and keratolytics like [[salicylic-acid]] help lift scale.[^dermnet] It is managed, not cured: treatment is used in courses and repeated when it relapses, and "long-term maintenance treatment is often necessary."[^dermnet]

## What's overhyped

Seborrheic dermatitis is not caused by poor hygiene, and scrubbing harder worsens the irritation rather than clearing it. Herbal and "natural" anti-dandruff remedies are "commonly used, but their efficacy is uncertain," and no product cures the condition; the realistic goal is control of a recurring problem, not eradication.[^dermnet]

## The Evidence

The strongest treatment evidence is the 2015 Cochrane systematic review of topical antifungals (51 randomized trials, 9,052 participants).[^cochrane] Ketoconazole 2% reduced the risk of failed clearance versus placebo (RR 0.69, 95% CI 0.59 to 0.81), matched topical steroids for remission while "occurrence of side effects was 44% lower in the ketoconazole group than in the steroid group," and performed similarly to ciclopirox.[^cochrane] Cochrane rated much of this "low-quality evidence," so the direction of benefit is clear but the size is less certain.
