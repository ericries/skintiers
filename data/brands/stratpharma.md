A Swiss medical device company whose range is silicone-based film-forming dressings, sold for scar management and post-procedure skin rather than as cosmetics.[^site][^listing] Its own site describes it as "an innovative medical device company dedicated to breakthrough post-procedure care, scar management, wound care, women's health, oncology supportive care, and dermatology solutions", which is the brand's own wording and not a claim verified here.[^site] No founder is named on the page read, so none is asserted. The homepage names Stratamark, StrataXRT and StrataCTX; the range is wider than this site covers.

Covered on this site: [[stratacel-film-forming-dressing]].

**The regulatory point belongs to that product page and is worth following.** Stratacel is FDA-listed as a class I device exempt from premarket notification, so no 510(k) file exists and no regulator reviewed its claims. That argument, and the one randomised trial behind it, are set out there rather than repeated here.
