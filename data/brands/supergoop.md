## Products reviewed here

- [[supergoop-unseen-sunscreen-spf-40]]: the brand's original Unseen Sunscreen formula, a broad-spectrum chemical sunscreen at SPF 40, since discontinued and replaced.
- [[supergoop-unseen-sunscreen-spf-50]]: the current Unseen Sunscreen formula, a broad-spectrum chemical sunscreen at SPF 50.
