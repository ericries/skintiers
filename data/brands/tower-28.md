Tower 28 Beauty is a United States clean-beauty makeup and skincare brand founded by Amy Liu, its founder and CEO.[^t28] Its best-known skincare item is a hypochlorous acid facial spray positioned for sensitive, eczema-, and acne-prone skin, which the brand says carries the National Eczema Association Seal of Acceptance.[^t28] That seal and the "clean" positioning are the brand's own claims; the evidence for any single product is on its own page.

- [[tower-28-sos-daily-rescue-facial-spray]] (SOS Daily Rescue Facial Spray), a treatment spray built on [[hypochlorous-acid]].
