Skinfix is a skincare brand sold direct-to-consumer at skinfix.com, whose product line includes physiologic-lipid barrier moisturizers such as [[skinfix-barrier-triple-lipid-peptide-cream|Triple Lipid-Peptide Cream]]. The brand's own site states it was founded by Amy Gordinier, "a beauty industry executive for over 20 years."[^1]
