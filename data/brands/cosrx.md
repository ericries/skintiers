COSRX is a South Korean skincare brand built around short, minimalist ingredient lists centered on one or two named actives; the South Korean beauty conglomerate Amorepacific has been its majority owner since a 2023 stake purchase that brought its holding to 93.2%.[^1][^2][^3]

## Ownership and history

COSRX's own corporate information page lists "Name of Company: COSRX inc.," "Date of business commencement: 2013," and "Founder: Jun Sang Hun," and states that the company "collected what our customers need and want regarding their skin concerns since 2002 and finally, 'Cosmetics + Rx = COSRX' was founded in 2013."[^1] Amorepacific's own announcement of its 2023 acquisition independently dates the company the same way, opening with "Founded in 2013, COSRX is a hypoallergenic skincare brand."[^2]

Amorepacific, whose other brands include Laneige, Sulwhasoo and Innisfree, first invested in COSRX in September 2021, when it "acquired 38.4% of COSRX shares" and was "granted a call option to purchase the remaining shares."[^2] On October 30, 2023, Amorepacific announced it was exercising that option, agreeing to "acquire 288,000 remaining shares held by COSRX's largest shareholder and related parties for 755.1 billion won," a deal that brought Amorepacific's stake to an expected 93.2% of COSRX and made COSRX a subsidiary.[^2] Trade outlet BeautyMatter independently reported the same transaction, putting the price at "$559.7 million" and confirming the resulting 93.2% stake and the prior 38.4%, 2021 investment.[^4] Amorepacific CEO Seunghwan Kim was quoted in the announcement: "We are thrilled that COSRX, which has shown rapid growth through differentiated products and marketing based on an exceptional understanding of digital channels, has become a part of our group."[^2]

## Positioning

COSRX's name is a contraction the company explains as "Cosmetics + Rx," pairing "cosmetics" with the prescription abbreviation "Rx."[^1] Industry trade coverage describes the brand's early positioning as "simple packaging, no-fuss formulas and affordable price points" built around "a list of simple active ingredients," aimed, in the brand's own words as quoted there, "to become the best hypoallergenic skincare brand for those with sensitive skin."[^5]

COSRX's own site lists its best-known products, including the Advanced Snail 96 Mucin Power Essence, formulated around [[snail-secretion-filtrate|snail mucin]] at a stated 96.3%, and the Low pH Good Morning Gel Cleanser, alongside the BHA Blackhead Power Liquid.[^1][^6]
