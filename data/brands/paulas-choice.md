Paula's Choice is a United States direct-to-consumer skin care brand founded by Paula Begoun, and it has been owned by the consumer goods company Unilever since 2021.[^1][^2][^3]

## Ownership and history

Paula's Choice was founded by Paula Begoun, whom TA Associates describes as "one of the original beauty influencers"; the same press release dates the company as "Founded more than 25 years ago."[^1] The brand's own site carries the tagline "Smart, Safe Skin Care Since 1995" and states that "Paula Begoun built Paula's Choice on research and proven formulas that actually work for skin."[^3]

The company was later owned by the growth private equity firm TA Associates. When the sale to Unilever was announced, Paula's Choice CEO Tara Poseley referred to "our experience with TA over the past five years," placing TA's investment around 2016.[^1] Paula Begoun, described in the release as Founder, said TA "helped to further develop our radically truthful skin care brand, backed by science not popular trends."[^1]

On June 14, 2021, TA Associates announced "the signing of a definitive agreement to sell Paula's Choice" to Unilever, a transaction it "expected to close in the third quarter of 2021"; TA said the terms "were not disclosed."[^1] Unilever's own filing does disclose them. Its 2021 Annual Report on Form 20-F records that on August 2, 2021 "the Group acquired 100 % of the shares of Paula's Choice Inc.," and that "The total consideration paid was EUR 1,832 million," comprising 1,818 million euros of cash on the completion date and 14 million euros of deferred consideration.[^2] The filing reports provisional fair values of 1,223 million euros of net assets acquired and 609 million euros of goodwill, and lists Paula's Choice among Unilever's indefinite-life intangible brands at a carrying value of 1,660 million euros (2020: nil).[^2] Unilever's board approved the deal in June 2021.[^2]

Within Unilever, Paula's Choice sits in the Prestige beauty portfolio; the 20-F describes it as "a Prestige Skin Care company based in the U.S." with "an established direct to consumer eCommerce business," and reports that Prestige beauty "reached EUR 1 billion turnover" in 2021 counting a full year of Paula's Choice.[^2] The 20-F's list of group companies records Paula's Choice operating entities in the United States and across Australia, China, Hong Kong, Korea, and the United Kingdom.[^2]

## Positioning and technology

- **Ingredient transparency and a science framing.** Unilever's board recorded the brand's appeal to consumers "seeking cruelty-free options and transparency on ingredients," and the 20-F characterizes it as the "digitally-led cruelty-free" Paula's Choice brand.[^2] The brand's own site states that Paula Begoun "built Paula's Choice on research and proven formulas that actually work for skin."[^3] Paula Begoun described it as a "radically truthful skin care brand, backed by science not popular trends."[^1]
- **Digital-first, direct-to-consumer model.** TA Associates called Paula's Choice "one of the largest digitally native beauty brands in the world," supported by "a global direct-to consumer" model.[^1] Unilever EVP and CEO of Prestige Vasiliki Petrou called the brand "a true pioneer in the digital space for beauty."[^1]

## Portfolio

Unilever classifies Paula's Choice within Prestige Skin Care and describes an established direct-to-consumer eCommerce business rather than a wholesale one.[^2] TA Associates described the brand's range as "a methodically curated product offering" sold through its global direct-to-consumer model.[^1]
