Naturium is a mass-market skincare brand describing itself as bringing "the science of consistent skincare to every one, every where, every day."[^1]

## Products reviewed here

- [[naturium-niacinamide-serum-12-zinc-2]]: a 12% niacinamide serum with zinc, at a concentration above the levels tested in the barrier and pigmentation trials linked on [[niacinamide]].
- [[naturium-retinol-complex-serum]]: a retinol serum that discloses neither a retinol percentage nor an encapsulation method, positioned against cheaper OTC retinols that do declare a concentration.
- [[naturium-vitamin-c-complex-serum]]: a vitamin C serum led by sodium ascorbyl phosphate ahead of ascorbic acid on the ingredient list, both undosed on the label.
