Torriden is a South Korean (K-beauty) skincare brand best known for its Dive-In line, built around several low-molecular-weight forms of hyaluronic acid, which the brand markets for lightweight hydration under a multi-type hyaluronic-complex branding. That positioning is Torriden's own marketing, not an independently verified claim.[^torriden]

Products profiled here so far:

- [[torriden-dive-in-low-molecular-hyaluronic-acid-serum]]: its flagship Dive-In Low Molecular Hyaluronic Acid Serum, a humectant serum whose hydration evidence is graded on its own page, with the active covered on [[hyaluronic-acid]]. It also appears in the [[best-glass-skin-products]] and [[best-moisturizing-ingredients-ranked]] lists.
