Avène is a French dermocosmetics brand owned by Pierre Fabre, which lists it among its own brands.[^1] Pierre Fabre describes the Avène laboratory as developing skin-care solutions across five areas: skin repair, photoprotection, acne, atopic-prone dry skin, and skin aging.[^1]

## Products reviewed here

- [[avene-cicalfate-restorative-protective-cream]]: Cicalfate+ Restorative Protective Cream, a barrier-repair moisturizer.
- [[avene-mineral-ultra-light-spf-50]]: Mineral Ultra-Light SPF 50+, a mineral sunscreen.
- [[avene-retrinal-0-1-intensive-cream]]: RetrinAL 0.1 Intensive Cream, a [[retinaldehyde]] retinoid cream.
