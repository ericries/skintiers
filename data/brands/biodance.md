A Korean skincare brand whose own storefront trades under the name 바이오던스 alongside the Latin spelling, and whose catalogue is built around collagen-positioned masks and ampoules.[^site] No founder is named on the pages read here, so none is asserted.

Covered on this site: [[biodance-bio-collagen-real-deep-mask]] and [[biodance-collagen-cleansing-oil]].

**The brand's central positioning is the thing its own product pages examine.** Both products are sold on collagen, and the mask page records that the declared collagen extract sits 25th of 34 ingredients at about 0.0055 percent, which is where the claim meets the ingredient list. That argument belongs to the product pages and is not repeated here; what this page is for is finding them.
