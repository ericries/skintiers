reVive Light Therapy is the skincare device line of LED Technologies, Inc., of Largo, Florida, the company named as applicant on the FDA clearances behind its devices.[^fda] The same company sells a separate dpl pain-relief line.[^site]

**On this site**

- [[revive-light-therapy-wrinkle-and-acne-led-device]], cleared under 510(k) K223482

**Two things worth knowing before you shop for it.**

The brand's own homepage is titled "Medical Grade LED Light Skin Therapy".[^site] That is the brand's wording. "Medical grade" is not an FDA category, and the clearance behind the device profiled above contains no clinical efficacy study.

The domain revivelighttherapy.com no longer serves the brand and returned an unrelated parked page when fetched on 2026-10-14. The live storefront is ledtechnologies.com.[^site] Its current reVive skincare range is built around a Lux Collection plus two spot treatments, and no product on it carries the name of the cleared device above, so match a product's stated wavelengths and indication rather than the brand name.[^site]

Category evidence is on [[home-led-light-therapy]]; the device ladder is on [[home-use-skincare-devices-by-evidence]].
