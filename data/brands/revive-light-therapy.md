reVive Light Therapy is a skincare device line of LED Technologies, Inc., the company named as applicant on the FDA clearances behind its devices; its four most recent filings, from 2021 and 2022, give a Largo, Florida address.[^fda][^openfda] One company owns both this line and [[dpl]]: all 15 LED Technologies clearances sit under the same applicant, and the device names in them span both brands.[^openfda] The brand's own navigation groups dpl as a pain-relief range, though the FDA record files cosmetic devices under dpl names too.[^site][^openfda]

**On this site**

- Lux Collection: [[revive-light-therapy-lux-clinical]], [[revive-light-therapy-lux-glo]], [[revive-light-therapy-lux-panel]], [[revive-light-therapy-lux-lip-care]], [[revive-light-therapy-lux-spot]]
- Others: [[revive-light-therapy-wrinkle-and-acne-led-device]] (K223482), [[revive-light-therapy-essentials]], [[revive-light-therapy-led-cleansing-system]]

**Two things worth knowing before you shop for it.**

The brand's own homepage is titled "Medical Grade LED Light Skin Therapy".[^site] That is the brand's wording. "Medical grade" is not an FDA category, and the clearance behind the device profiled above contains no clinical efficacy study.

The domain revivelighttherapy.com no longer serves the brand and returned an unrelated parked page when fetched on 2026-10-14. The brand sells from ledtechnologies.com, and a second live domain, revive-light-therapy.com, serves its product photography.[^site][^luxspot] Its current reVive skincare range is built around a Lux Collection plus two spot treatments, and no product on it carries the name of the cleared device above, so match a product's stated wavelengths and indication rather than the brand name.[^site] The retail names also move: the poof! acne product now serves a page titled Lux Spot, graded on [[revive-light-therapy-lux-spot]].[^luxspot]

Category evidence is on [[home-led-light-therapy]]; the device ladder is on [[home-use-skincare-devices-by-evidence]]; what this firm publishes against its rivals is on [[home-led-devices-by-what-the-file-publishes]].
