Pollogen is an Israeli aesthetic-device maker whose radiofrequency platforms go mostly to clinics, with a smaller consumer line alongside them. It describes its own technologies as "Clinically Proven Technologies. Immediate Results. Global Success."[^site] No founder or owner is named here because neither was verified on the page fetched.

**Its own catalogue splits the way its FDA file does.** The site divides products into PROFESSIONAL and HOME USE, and the firm's 18 clearances divide along the same line: eleven under product code GEI, "Electrosurgical, Cutting & Coagulation & Accessories", and five under PAY, "Over-The-Counter Radiofrequency Coagulation Device For Wrinkle Reduction", plus one muscle-conditioning stimulator and one radiofrequency massager. Every one of the 18 was published as a Summary, filed from Binyamina and later Tel Aviv.[^openfda]

The one device covered here, [[pollogen-stop-u-tripollar]], sits in that over-the-counter code while the efficacy study in its file was run with the prescription version, which is why it ranks where it does on [[home-rf-devices-by-whose-evidence-the-file-rests-on]].
