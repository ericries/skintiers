Bausch Health Companies Inc. is a global, diversified pharmaceutical company operating in gastroenterology, hepatology, neuroscience, dermatology, dentistry, aesthetics, and international pharmaceuticals, headquartered in Laval, Quebec, Canada, per its own corporate site.[^1]

## Products reviewed here

- [[renova-tretinoin-0-02-cream|Renova]] (tretinoin 0.02% emollient cream): a prescription topical retinoid FDA-approved for mitigating fine facial wrinkles.
- [[retin-a-micro-tretinoin-gel-microsphere|Retin-A Micro]] (tretinoin gel microsphere): a prescription topical retinoid FDA-approved for acne vulgaris, in four strengths.
