Caudalie is a French skincare brand built around antioxidant actives it derives from grapes and grapevines, notably resveratrol and grape polyphenols. The brand says it was founded by Mathilde and Bertrand Thomas and centres its formulas on grape-derived ingredients; that founding account and ingredient positioning are Caudalie's own, cited as the brand's claims rather than independently verified efficacy.[^caudalie]

Products profiled here so far:

- [[caudalie-resveratrol-lift-firming-serum|Resveratrol-Lift Instant Firming Serum]]: its resveratrol-and-peptide firming serum, whose evidence is graded on its own page.
- [[caudalie-vinoperfect-radiance-serum|Vinoperfect Radiance Serum]]: a grape-derived brightening serum marketed for dark spots, graded on its own page.
- [[caudalie-vinopure-acne-serum|Vinopure Acne Serum]]: a salicylic acid serum marketed for blemish-prone skin, graded on its own page.
