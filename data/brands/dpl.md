dpl is one of two device lines sold by LED Technologies, Inc. of Greenwood Village, Colorado, the other being [[revive-light-therapy]].[^openfda] No founder is named here: the only individuals in the company's FDA filings are a compliance manager and a specification contact, which does not establish who founded it.

No brand claims are quoted on this page. ledtechnologies.com returned a Cloudflare challenge rather than a page on both 2026-10-20 and 2026-10-21, so no brand copy, price or photograph could be read for any dpl product.

The FDA record is the usable source instead. LED Technologies holds 15 device clearances, every one filed as a 510(k) Summary rather than a Statement, so the testing description is public in each case.[^openfda] Eight carry the dpl name: DPL Therapy System, DPL Nuve, Nuve for Wrinkles, dpl II Panel, dpl SpectraLite, dpl IIa Panel, dpl SpotLite and dpl Faceware.[^openfda]

## Products reviewed here

- [[dpl-spotlite]]: a blue and red LED acne device whose only human study is a 16-person usability test.
- [[dpl-faceware]]: a five-wavelength face device cleared against three of the firm's own earlier products.
