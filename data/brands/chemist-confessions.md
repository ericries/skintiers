Chemist Confessions is a skincare-education blog, Instagram account, podcast, and product line founded by cosmetic chemists [[victoria-fu|Victoria Fu]] and [[gloria-lu|Gloria Lu]], former L'Oréal formulators who launched the Chemist Confessions Instagram account in August 2017.[^bi]

## Products reviewed here

- [[chemist-confessions-aquafix|Aquafix Hydrating Water Gel]]: a fragrance-free, oil-free hydrating gel built around a glycerin-led humectant complex.
