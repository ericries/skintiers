El Global Trade, Ltd. is not a name that appears on a box. It is the FDA applicant of record behind a group of home light and radiofrequency skin devices, filing from Netanya, Israel, with twelve 510(k) clearances between July 2014 and May 2026.[^openfda]

Those twelve carry at least eight consumer names: CurrentBody, sensiLight and Sensilight Pro, sensiLift and Sensilift Pro, sensiFirm, micro IPL, RF-Relief, IPL400 and DeepSkin.[^openfda] A shopper comparing two of them is comparing two filings from one company.

No founder is named here because none could be verified from a primary source.
