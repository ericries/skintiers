CurrentBody sells at-home beauty devices at currentbody.com and describes itself, in its own words, as "the world's leading beauty tech brand" developing "advanced, clinically proven beauty devices for at-home use".[^site] No founder is named here because none was verified.

**The name on the box is not the name on the FDA file.** The one CurrentBody device covered here, [[currentbody-skin-rf]], was cleared under K232424 with the applicant recorded as El Global Trade, Ltd. of Netanya, Israel.[^openfda] That firm holds 12 clearances in total, every one published as a Summary, under names including sensiLight, sensiLift, sensiFirm, micro IPL, RF-Relief, IPL400 and DeepSkin.[^openfda] Only one of the twelve carries the CurrentBody name.

**Read the brand's clinically-proven line against that device's own file**, which states that no clinical testing was performed on it.[^openfda] Both facts are on the product page.
