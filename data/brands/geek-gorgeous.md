Geek & Gorgeous is a Hungarian skincare brand selling budget-priced, actives-focused serums; its own site describes it as founded in 2017 by cosmetic formulator Judit Rácz together with Péter Budaházy of the Hungarian heritage brand Helia-D, and calls itself "the first and, since then, the most successful Hungarian pro-science skincare brand."[^1]
