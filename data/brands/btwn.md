BTWN is a small skincare line aimed at tweens and teenagers, founded by dermatologist [[brooke-jeffy]]. The brand's own Story page gives the origin in one sentence: "Dermatologist Dr. Brooke Jeffy noticed tweens and teens weren't properly caring for their skin."[^story]

The range is short. Its own shop lists a gentle cleanser, a moisturizer, a two-product duo kit and a BTWN Skincare Set priced at $69.00.[^home]

**Nothing here is graded yet.** No BTWN product has a page on this site, so this card links to none, and the brand's claims above are recorded as the brand's own rather than assessed. The reason it exists at all is the connection worth knowing: BTWN is the own-brand interest disclosed on [[brooke-jeffy]], which is what makes her product recommendations something to read differently from her educational content.
