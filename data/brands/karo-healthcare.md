Karo Healthcare is a consumer-health company with a Stockholm address that describes itself as "a healthcare company guided by a shared purpose of helping people stay in good health" and says it owns "a range of documented and trusted brands within key categories of everyday healthcare".[^site] No founder or owner is named here because neither appeared on the page fetched.

**It is the name behind a product already graded here.** Karo is the sponsor of record on both over-the-counter Lamisil approvals: NDA 020980 for the cream, originally approved 9 March 1999, and NDA 021124 for the solution and spray, approved 17 March 2000. As labeler "Karo Healthcare Inc." it lists five national drug codes, every one terbinafine hydrochloride 1%, with marketing start dates running from November 2024 to December 2025.[^openfda] The product covered here is [[lamisil-at-cream-terbinafine-1]] and the active is on [[terbinafine]].

Its own brand list names ACO, E45 and Remescar under skin health and Pevaryl under foot health, none of which has a page here yet.[^site]
