A US recovery-device company, formerly Theragun, whose TheraFace line is the part this site covers. It holds five FDA 510(k) clearances, every one published as a Summary rather than a Statement, so its filings can be read.[^openfda]

Covered on this site: [[theraface-led-light-ring]], listed on the brand's own feed at $99.99.[^feed]

**The brand renamed itself and its clearances did not follow, which is a discovery problem, not a trivia point.** Three clearances sit under applicant "Theragun, Inc." and two under "Therabody, Inc.", so an openFDA search on either name alone returns part of the picture and looks complete.[^openfda] The same split bit this site once already, on [[silkn]]. Under Theragun: TheraFace Microcurrent (K212238, NFO), RecoveryAir PRO (K211745, IRP) and TheraFace LED (K212155, OHS). Under Therabody: TheraFace Mask (K230293, OHS) and JetBoots PRO Plus (K241256, IRP).[^openfda]

Not yet covered here, from the same feed: the TheraFace Mask at $649.99, TheraFace Mask Glo at $379.99, the TheraFace PRO Microcurrent Ring at $149.99 and the TheraFace Depuffing Wand at $179.99.[^feed] No founder is asserted because the brand page read for one names none.
