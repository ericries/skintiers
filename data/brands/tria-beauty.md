Tria Beauty is a US maker of home laser devices. openFDA's 510(k) index returns exactly five clearances to it, and all five were filed with a 510(k) Summary rather than a Statement, which is the more informative of the two options: K090312 for the Tria Acne Treatment System (January 2010), K120737 for the Tria Laser Hair Removal System (September 2012), K130459 for the Tria FAN System (October 2013), K141868 for the Tria FANp (December 2014) and K160821 for the Tria Sapphire (June 2016).[^openfda]

Worth knowing before matching a box to a clearance: the retail names do not match the filed ones. The brand's own store sells a "Tria 4X Hair Removal Laser (2nd Gen)" at $599.99 and a "Tria FRX Skin Rejuvenating Fractional Laser (2nd Gen)" at $699.99, and neither name appears in the clearance list above.[^feed]

Covered here: [[tria-fanp-age-defying-laser]]. Modality evidence is on [[laser-hair-removal]].

No founder is named on this page because none was verified.
