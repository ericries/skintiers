Purito, marketed as Purito Seoul, is a South Korean skincare brand describing itself as "a contemporary Korean skincare brand that draws inspiration from Korean ingredients and culture."[^1]

## Products reviewed here

- [[purito-panthenol-moisturizer]]: a facial moisturizer built around a declared 10% panthenol, double the highest concentration directly tested in the barrier-repair trials on [[panthenol]].
- [[purito-oat-in-calming-gel-cream]]: a gel-format moisturizer marketed around "oat seed water" for sensitive, irritated skin, with no disclosed ingredient list or concentration.
- [[purito-cica-clearing-bb-cream]]: a tinted BB cream with mineral and organic UV filters plus centella asiatica and niacinamide, sold without a stated SPF or PA rating.
