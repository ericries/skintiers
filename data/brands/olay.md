Olay is a mass-market US skincare brand sold at retailers including Target, known for its Regenerist anti-aging line and the Retinol24 sub-line of retinoid moisturizers. It is one of the brands listed on Procter & Gamble's own "Our Brands" site, under Skin and Personal Care.[^1]

## Products reviewed here

- [[olay-regenerist-retinol24-night-serum]]: Olay's Retinol24 Night Moisturizer, pairing retinol with the weaker retinyl propionate ester and undisclosed active concentrations.
