L'Oreal is a French cosmetics and beauty company, founded in 1909 by chemist Eugene Schueller, per the brand's own corporate history.[^1]

## Products reviewed here

- [[loreal-paris-revitalift-0-3-pure-retinol-serum|L'Oreal Paris Revitalift 0.3% Pure Retinol Serum]]: a mass-market 0.3% retinol serum.

## Ingredients developed by L'Oreal

- [[ecamsule]] (Mexoryl SX): a UVA filter developed by L'Oreal, first approved by the FDA only inside named L'Oreal-brand sunscreens.
- [[mexoryl-400-mce]]: a newer L'Oreal UV filter extending coverage toward 400 nm, authorized for use in the EU.

## See Also

L'Oreal owns [[cerave]] (since 2017) and [[la-roche-posay]] (since 1989), and co-founded [[galderma]] in 1981; each is profiled separately on this site.
