Sciton, Inc is a US maker of professional aesthetic lasers, the kind installed in a clinic rather than bought by a consumer. openFDA's 510(k) index returns 23 clearances to it, every one filed with a 510(k) Summary rather than a Statement, spanning November 2000 to December 2025, under 17 distinct device names. Those names are almost all platforms: Joule, mJOULE, Profile, Image and Contour. Product code GEX accounts for 21 of the 23.[^openfda]

The distinction worth carrying into any clinic conversation is platform versus handpiece. Two handpiece names do appear in the index, inside platform names, as "PROFILE BBL SYSTEM" and "Joule diVa System". Several that the company markets heavily do not appear anywhere in those 17 names, including HALO, moxi, SkinTyte and ZoomScan.[^openfda] A treatment sold to you by handpiece name is therefore not necessarily a separately cleared device.
