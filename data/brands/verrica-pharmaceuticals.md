Verrica Pharmaceuticals Inc. is a prescription dermatology company in West Chester, Pennsylvania.[^spl] It has one FDA-approved product: [[ycanth-cantharidin-topical-solution]], cantharidin 0.7%, under NDA 212905, which is also the only entry openFDA lists under its labeler name.[^openfda]

**In its own words,** the company says it is "committed to the development and commercialization of novel treatments that provide significant benefit for people living with dermatological diseases, including skin cancer", and that on "July 21, 2023" YCANTH "became the first treatment approved by the FDA" for molluscum contagiosum in patients as young as two.[^company]

**Pipeline, as the company states it:** YCANTH is VP-102, which Verrica says it is also developing for common warts. VP-315, licensed from Lytix Biopharma AS, targets basal and squamous cell carcinoma. It says it is "not currently advancing" VP-102 for external genital warts or VP-103 for plantar warts.[^company]

**On this site:** [[ycanth-cantharidin-topical-solution]], and the hub for what it treats, [[molluscum-contagiosum]].
