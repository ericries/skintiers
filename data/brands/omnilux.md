A light-therapy device line whose name has outlasted the companies behind it. Omnilux holds eight FDA 510(k) clearances across three applicants: Photo Therapeutics Limited in the UK for the first five, Photo Therapeutics, Inc. of Carlsbad, California for the next two, and Globalmed Technologies for the most recent in 2021.[^openfda] The name on the box today is not the firm that filed the older devices.

**Unusual for this category, every one of the eight is a Summary rather than a Statement**, meaning the substance of each filing is published rather than merely available on request.[^openfda] That is the opposite of [[foreo]]'s record, and the disclosure test it enables is applied on [[home-led-devices-by-what-the-file-publishes]].

The eight also sit under four different product codes, from a laser surgical code to the infrared lamp code to the two over-the-counter light codes, a spread explained on [[infrared-heat-lamps-vs-led-skin-devices]].[^openfda]

Covered here: [[omnilux-clear-u]] and [[omnilux-new-u]].
