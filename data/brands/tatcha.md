Tatcha is a US prestige skincare brand founded by Vicky Tsai, who is named on the brand's site as its founder.[^1] The brand positions itself around Japanese-inspired botanical rituals and its proprietary "Hadasei-3" complex (Akita rice, Uji green tea, and Okinawa algae); those are the brand's own marketing claims, not independent evidence. Its products are typically premium-priced gel and cream moisturizers and serums.

Products profiled here so far:

- [[tatcha-the-water-cream]]: a lightweight oil-free gel-cream moisturizer.
- [[tatcha-the-dewy-skin-cream]]: the richer cream counterpart to the Water Cream, for drier skin.
- [[tatcha-indigo-overnight-repair]]: an overnight serum-in-cream marketed for sensitive, reactive skin.

Each product's real composition, price, and honest evidence grade are on its own page. See [[moisturizing]] for what a moisturizer can and cannot do.
