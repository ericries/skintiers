Medik8 is a British skincare brand best known for its Crystal Retinal line of encapsulated retinaldehyde serums, sold in a ladder of six strengths. Medik8's own company history states it was "founded in 2009 by UK scientist Elliot Isaacs."[^1]

## Products reviewed here

- [[medik8-crystal-retinal-3]]: a 0.03%-strength retinaldehyde serum from the Crystal Retinal ladder, built on [[retinaldehyde]].
- [[medik8-c-tetra-vitamin-c-serum]]: a vitamin C serum built on the lipid-soluble ester [[tetrahexyldecyl-ascorbate]] at a disclosed 7%.
