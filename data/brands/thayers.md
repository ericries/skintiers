A US skincare brand whose range is built on witch hazel, sold mainly as facial toners, including variants pairing witch hazel with 2% AHA and with salicylic acid.[^site] Its own storefront titles itself "Thayers Natural Remedies | Facial Toners & Witch Hazel Skin Care" and carries the line "since 1847", which is the brand's own claim rather than anything verified here.[^site] No founder is named on the page read, so none is asserted. The labeller of record on the FDA-filed label of its sulfur acne treatment is L'Oreal USA Products Inc.[^label]

Covered on this site: [[thayers-rapid-acne-treatment-sulfur-10]], which uses [[sulfur]], [[niacinamide]] and [[glycolic-acid]].

**The one finding worth following is on that product page, not here.** The sulfur treatment's own ingredient list contains no witch hazel, in a brand built on it. That argument belongs to the product page, which sources it; this page exists to find it.
