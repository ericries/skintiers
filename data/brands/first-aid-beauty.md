First Aid Beauty is a US skincare brand best known for fragrance-free, sensitive-skin-focused moisturizers and cleansers; its own site says it has been "Trusted Since 2009," and its email sign-up terms describe First Aid Beauty as one of several "trusted P&G brands," indicating it is now part of Procter & Gamble.[^1]

## Products reviewed here

- [[first-aid-beauty-ultra-repair-cream]]: a rich barrier moisturizer built on dimethicone, glycerin, shea butter, squalane, and ceramide NP, positioned for dry and eczema-prone skin.
