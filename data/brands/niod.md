NIOD (Non-Invasive Options in Dermal Science) is a higher-end skincare brand from the Canadian company DECIEM, the same parent as The Ordinary, positioned around complex, high-concentration multi-ingredient formulas that it markets as advanced or clinical-grade. That positioning is NIOD's own marketing, not an independently verified claim.[^niod]

Products profiled here so far:

- [[niod-copper-amino-isolate-serum-3-1-1]]: its Copper Amino Isolate Serum (CAIS3), a copper-peptide serum; graded on its own page, with the active covered on [[copper-peptides]].
- [[niod-multi-molecular-hyaluronic-complex]]: a multi-weight hyaluronic complex serum; graded on its own page, with the active on [[hyaluronic-acid]].
