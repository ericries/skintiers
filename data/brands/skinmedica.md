SkinMedica is an American physician-dispensed skincare brand, sold through dermatologists, plastic surgeons, and medical spas rather than mass retail, best known for its TNS growth-factor serums; it has operated as an Allergan Aesthetics brand since Allergan acquired it in 2012, and Allergan itself became an AbbVie company in 2020.[^skinmedica-story][^allergan-2012][^abbvie-2020]

## Origin

SkinMedica traces its founding to 1999 in Carlsbad, California. The company's own current site states, "Since 1999, we've been a leader in professional-grade skincare," and identifies itself as "SkinMedica, an Allergan Aesthetics Brand."[^skinmedica-story] A Carlsbad Chamber of Commerce account of the company's early history describes its start this way: "two local biotech veterans, David Hale and Cam Garner, learned that well-known San Diego dermatologist Dr. Richard E. Fitzpatrick had founded a small, promising cosmeceutical firm in Carlsbad and was seeking financing." It dates the company's incorporation to May 1999, describes Hale and Garner as the investors who financed it, and says the firm grew to roughly 205 employees across several Carlsbad locations, plus a Houston, Texas manufacturing site, within its first few years.[^carlsbad-chamber] SkinMedica's own site names Fitzpatrick, a dermatologist, as a co-founder and quotes him: "The most rewarding part of what we can do is watching the changes in a patient's self-esteem and happiness."[^skinmedica-story]

## Ownership and history

Allergan, Inc. announced on November 16, 2012 that it had agreed to acquire SkinMedica's topical aesthetics skin care business for $350 million up front, plus up to $25 million more contingent on the acquired products hitting a net-sales target; the SkinMedica Colorescience makeup line was excluded from the deal and spun out separately. Allergan's own announcement described SkinMedica as marketing "the revolutionary TNS Essential Serum and hallmark TNS Recovery Complex" along with the prescription products Vaniqa and NeoBenzMicro, and named Mary Fisher as SkinMedica's chief executive going into the integration.[^allergan-2012] A dermatology trade publication, Practical Dermatology, reported the acquisition had closed by the time of its December 21, 2012 story.[^practicaldermatology]

AbbVie completed its own acquisition of Allergan plc on May 8, 2020. Allergan shareholders received 0.8660 AbbVie shares plus $120.30 in cash for each Allergan share, a total consideration of $193.23 per Allergan share, and Allergan's aesthetics business, described in AbbVie's announcement as "a global aesthetics business, with leading brands including Botox and Juvederm," joined AbbVie alongside Allergan's neuroscience and eye care units.[^abbvie-2020] SkinMedica now operates as one of the brands under Allergan Aesthetics, which describes itself as "an AbbVie company" that develops, manufactures, and markets "a portfolio of leading aesthetics brands and products" spanning "facial injectables, body contouring, plastics, skin care, and more."[^allerganaesthetics-about]

## Positioning

SkinMedica's own site markets the brand as "Pioneer of regenerative skincare" and states that it pioneered and patented a process for extracting stabilized, concentrated growth factors for use in topical skincare, a claim made in the brand's own marketing rather than an independently established finding.[^skinmedica-story] Most SkinMedica products are marketed as cosmetics rather than FDA-approved drugs; Allergan Aesthetics' own site notes that some SkinMedica sunscreens and cleansers function as over-the-counter drug products under FDA rules, unlike the rest of the line.[^allerganaesthetics-about]

The brand's best-known line, sold as TNS Recovery Complex, TNS Essential Serum, and TNS Advanced+ Serum, is built around what SkinMedica calls TNS (Tissue Nutrient Solution). The current TNS Advanced+ Serum ingredient list, published on the manufacturer's own product page, names "Human Fibroblast Conditioned Media" as its second-listed ingredient, and the brand markets the formula around a "growth factor blend" and "peptide complex."[^tns-advanced] SkinMedica's other current lines include the Retinol Complex retinoids, sold in three strengths (see [[skinmedica-retinol-complex-1-0|SkinMedica Retinol Complex 1.0]], the top tier of that line), the HA5 hyaluronic-acid hydrators, the Lytera pigment-correcting serums, a Vitamin C+E Complex antioxidant serum pairing ascorbic acid with vitamin E, and the Total Defense + Repair and Essential Defense sunscreen lines.[^skinmedica-story][^vitc][^allproducts]
