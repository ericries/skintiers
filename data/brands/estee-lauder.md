Estee Lauder, which the company spells Estée Lauder, is a US luxury skincare and cosmetics brand and the namesake brand of The Estée Lauder Companies Inc. The company's own brand portfolio page lists it alongside Clinique, The Ordinary, NIOD, DECIEM, Dr.Jart+, La Mer, Origins and others.[^elc] No founder line is given here because no reachable primary source was consulted for one. Any positioning or efficacy language from the brand is its own claim, assessed on each product's page.

## Products reviewed here

- [[estee-lauder-advanced-night-repair-serum|Advanced Night Repair]]: the brand's flagship night serum, graded here as modest on preliminary evidence for hydration and minimal on anecdotal evidence for its anti-aging and firming claims.

Dermatologist [[aleksandra-brown]] compares this line against Lancome's in a video carded on that product page and on [[anti-aging]].

Sibling brands profiled here, per the same company listing: [[clinique]], [[the-ordinary]], [[niod]] and [[dr-jart]].[^elc]
