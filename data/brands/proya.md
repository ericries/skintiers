Proya Cosmetics Co. Ltd is a cosmetics company based in Hangzhou, China, and it runs an in-company research unit called the Research & Innovation Center.[^pubmed]

**Why it has a page here:** this site cites a 2025 paper on a blend called Encorelane, and every one of that paper's seven listed author affiliations is the company's own Research & Innovation Center.[^pubmed] The company owns the ingredient the paper tests. This card exists so a reader meeting that study can see who the company is; [[wang-2025-encorelane-sensitive-skin]] owns the findings, the conflict-of-interest declaration and the judgment about what the evidence is worth, and none of that is repeated here.

**On this site:** no Proya product has a page. A search of the indexed pages returns no Proya product, so the study above is currently the only place the company appears. No founder is named here because none was verified.
