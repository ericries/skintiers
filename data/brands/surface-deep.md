A small US brand selling glycolic acid body products pitched at odour rather than sweat. Its About page says the founder is "Dr. Alicia Zalka", whom it describes as having "over 25 years as a multi-practice dermatologist and an associate clinical professor of dermatology at Yale University School of Medicine"; that is the brand's own description and is not independently verified here.[^about]

**Covered here:** [[surface-deep-anti-odorant-wipes]], whose graded actives are [[glycolic-acid]] and [[salicylic-acid]]. The brand also lists a spray, a wash and a lotion, which have no pages here.

Two of its claims sit oddly against its own ingredient list, which is set out on the product page rather than re-argued here: the About page promises "You won't find any harsh chemicals, dyes, additives, or artificial fragrances in our products" on a formula whose second ingredient is glycolic acid and whose fifth is sodium hydroxide, and it says "Surface Deep helps lower the pH of your skin" while publishing no pH figure for the product.[^about]
