Hero Cosmetics is a US acne-focused skincare brand best known for its Mighty Patch line of [[hydrocolloid]] acne patches. It positions itself around "Empowering Acne Prone Skin," and its catalog centers on hydrocolloid pimple patches in several sizes and formats (nighttime Original, daytime Invisible+, nose, and larger Surface patches), alongside a few other acne-prone-skin products such as a sunscreen.[^hero]

Relevant pages here so far:

- [[hydrocolloid]]: the wound-dressing material behind Hero's Mighty Patch acne patches, and the ingredient whose modest, narrow evidence is graded on its own page. Individual Mighty Patch products are being added to the catalog.
