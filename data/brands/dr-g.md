Dr.G is a South Korean skincare brand founded by dermatologist Dr. Gun Young Ahn in 2003 and headquartered in Seoul; as of December 2024, L'Oréal Groupe has agreed to acquire it, via parent company Gowoonsesang Cosmetics, from Swiss retail group Migros.[^1]

## Products reviewed here

- [[dr-g-baby-mild-up-sun]]: a single-filter (zinc oxide) mineral sunscreen, SPF35/PA+++, rated to Korean regulatory standards.
- [[dr-g-green-mild-up-sun]]: a single-filter (zinc oxide) mineral sunscreen, SPF50+/PA++++, rated to Korean regulatory standards.
