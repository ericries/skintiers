Incyte is a biopharmaceutical company whose FDA portfolio is mostly oncology and haematology, with a single topical dermatology product in it. Its own home page titles itself "Biopharmaceutical Company | Solutions for Unmet Medical Needs" and splits its portfolio navigation into "Hematology and Oncology" and "Inflammation and Autoimmunity".[^site] No founder is named here because none appeared on the page fetched.

**Six approved applications, one of them a skin cream.** The agency's approvals database lists Incyte Corp as sponsor of Jakafi and Jakafi XR, both oral ruxolitinib, plus Pemazyre, Zynyz and Niktimvo, and of Opzelura, ruxolitinib cream at the equivalent of 1.5% base, approved under NDA 215309 on 21 September 2021 with four approved supplements since.[^openfda]

The product covered here is [[opzelura-ruxolitinib-cream]], and the trials behind it are on [[kircik-2025-ruxolitinib-vs-triamcinolone-atopic-dermatitis-pmid-41037525]] and [[zirwas-2026-ruxolitinib-cream-chronic-hand-eczema]]. There is no ruxolitinib ingredient page here yet.
