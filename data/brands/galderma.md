Galderma is a Swiss dermatology company operating across injectable aesthetics, dermatological skincare, and therapeutic dermatology, founded in 1981 as a joint venture between L'Oreal and Nestle and publicly listed on the SIX Swiss Exchange since 2024.[^1]

## Products reviewed here

- [[differin-adapalene-0-1-gel|Differin Gel 0.1%]] (adapalene 0.1%): the first topical retinoid the FDA cleared for over-the-counter acne treatment.
- [[differin-gel-0-3-adapalene|Differin Gel 0.3%]] (adapalene 0.3%): the prescription-strength adapalene gel for acne.
- [[epiduo-adapalene-benzoyl-peroxide-gel|Epiduo]] (adapalene 0.1% / benzoyl peroxide 2.5%): a combination acne gel.
- [[soolantra-ivermectin-1-cream|Soolantra]] (ivermectin 1% cream): a prescription rosacea treatment.
- [[mirvaso-brimonidine-gel|Mirvaso]] (brimonidine 0.33% gel): a prescription topical for the persistent facial redness of rosacea.
- [[metrogel-metronidazole-1-gel|MetroGel]] (metronidazole 1% gel): a prescription topical for the inflammatory lesions of rosacea.
- [[epiduo-forte-adapalene-benzoyl-peroxide-gel|Epiduo Forte]] (adapalene 0.3% / benzoyl peroxide 2.5%): the higher-strength combination acne gel.
- [[aklief-trifarotene-0-005-cream|Aklief]] (trifarotene 0.005% cream): a prescription fourth-generation topical retinoid for acne.
- [[twyneo-tretinoin-benzoyl-peroxide-cream|Twyneo]] (tretinoin 0.1% / benzoyl peroxide 3% cream): a prescription combination acne cream.
- [[epsolay-benzoyl-peroxide-5-cream|Epsolay]] (benzoyl peroxide 5% cream): a prescription encapsulated benzoyl peroxide for rosacea.
- [[oracea-doxycycline-40-mg|Oracea]] (doxycycline 40 mg modified-release): an oral anti-inflammatory-dose antibiotic for rosacea.
- [[tri-luma-hydroquinone-tretinoin-fluocinolone|Tri-Luma]] (hydroquinone 4% / tretinoin 0.05% / fluocinolone 0.01% cream): a prescription triple-combination cream for melasma.
