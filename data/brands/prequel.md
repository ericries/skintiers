Prequel is a direct-to-consumer skincare brand founded by dermatologist [[samantha-ellis|Dr. Samantha Ellis]], launched July 25, 2023 in collaboration with Los Angeles brand accelerator The Center.[^prnewswire][^yahoo]

## Founding

Prequel launched on July 25, 2023, with its first three products sold exclusively online at prequelskin.com.[^prnewswire] The launch press release identifies the founder as "Dr. Samantha Ellis, MD, FAAD," describing her as a "board-certified medical and cosmetic dermatologist based in the San Francisco Bay Area."[^prnewswire] Independent coverage of the same launch, syndicated from WWD, confirms her credentials in more detail: an MD from the University of Michigan, a dermatology residency at UC Davis, a continuing role there as a clinical instructor, and a private practice in Danville, California.[^yahoo] Prequel's own About page carries a first-person line from Ellis, quoted on her [[samantha-ellis|person page]]: "I'm Dr. Sam Ellis, a board-certified medical and cosmetic dermatologist and the founder of Prequel."[^about]

The brand launched through a partnership with The Center, a Los Angeles-based brand accelerator whose CEO, Ben Bennett, is quoted in the launch announcement saying "The Center builds and grows compelling and highly commercial brands in the beauty and wellness space."[^prnewswire] The independent WWD-syndicated report describes The Center as a brand incubator that also owns Naturium, Phlur, Saltair, and Make Beauty, and that had received a $15 million minority investment from Prelude Growth Partners in 2021; the same report quotes Bennett saying of Prequel's retail strategy, "I'm not a believer in a d-to-c brand only...we plan on working with or collaborating with a retail partner to build brand awareness."[^yahoo]

## Positioning

Prequel's own framing of its approach, in its founder's words, is "basic plus": the launch announcement quotes Ellis saying "I like to think of this brand as 'basic plus' - we've taken tried and true skincare ingredients and formats, but elevated them."[^prnewswire] Prequel's About page attributes the brand's origin to Ellis's clinical practice, quoting her: "When I see patients in my practice and they are coming to me with dermatologic concerns, whether it's medical or cosmetic, the first thing I want to understand is their story," and "My goal with Prequel was to create a line of products that can be used before you ever need to see a dermatologist."[^about] A third, independent account of the brand's history describes the same positioning as delivering "clinical-grade, barrier-repairing formulas at everyday prices."[^beautynewsdaily] These are the brand's own characterizations of its approach, not an independently established finding.

## Products reviewed here

Several Prequel products carry the National Rosacea Society's Seal of Acceptance, a designation independently confirmed on the Society's own brand listing rather than resting on Prequel's word alone; the Seal reflects an independent dermatologist panel's screen for ingredients tied to barrier damage, flushing, and stinging in rosacea-prone skin, detailed on [[prequel-gleanser-glycerin-cleanser]].[^nrs]

## Founder's dual role

Ellis is both a practicing, board-certified dermatologist and the founder of Prequel, which she operates as a commercial venture built on her own product line.[^about][^prnewswire] She also produces educational skincare content; her clinical publication record and credentials are catalogued on her own page, [[samantha-ellis]].
