A US mineral sunscreen brand, labelled by Fallien Cosmeceuticals, LTD, whose entire range is registered with FDA as OTC monograph drug products.[^openfda]

Covered on this site: [[cotz-sensitive-spf-40]].

**The reason this brand gets a page is a consistency that is easy to check and unusual to find.** openFDA returns 14 drug records under the CoTZ brand name, covering 13 distinct products from a lip balm and a baby cream to tinted sticks and foams. Across all of them the only active ingredients that appear are **zinc oxide and titanium dioxide**, at strengths from 38 to 200 mg/g for zinc and 30 to 80 mg/g for titanium.[^openfda] No organic UV filter appears in any record. That matters because a 2026 analysis of 150 top-selling sunscreens found nearly half of products labelled mineral also contained organic filters, a finding set out on [[zinc-oxide]] and not repeated here.

No founder is named, because cotz.com returned HTTP 436 when checked and no brand page could be read.
