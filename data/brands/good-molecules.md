Good Molecules is a direct-to-consumer skincare brand selling low-priced, single- or few-active
formulas online; its own site says it "started Good Molecules to change" products that "don't
work and cost too much," pairing "ingredients backed by science" with "low prices."[^1]

## Products reviewed here

- [[good-molecules-niacinamide-serum]]: a 10% niacinamide serum.
- [[good-molecules-discoloration-correcting-serum]]: a 4% niacinamide serum for facial
  hyperpigmentation and evening tone.
