REMEDY Science is a US skincare brand founded by dermatologist Dr. Muneeb Shah; its own store titles itself "Remedy Science by Dr. Muneeb Shah" and says its products are "Formulated by a leading dermatologist", with the positioning line "Facts are our only filter".[^brand] Treat those as the brand's own claims. Shah is also a vetted creator here, so any recommendation of these products by him is an own-brand one; see [[muneeb-shah]].

## Products reviewed here

- [[remedy-15-azelaic-acid-complex-serum]]: a solubilised azelaic serum whose 15% is a complex of 10% azelaic acid plus 5% potassium azeloyl diglycinate, not 15% azelaic acid. It is the most transparent azelaic serum profiled on this site, and the page sets out both what that transparency earns and what it does not.
