Silk'n is a home beauty-device line whose FDA filings are made by Home Skinovations, Ltd. of Yokneam, Israel.[^openfda] No founder is named here: the filings identify corporate contacts only, which does not establish who founded the company.

**Its record sits under two applicant names.** Searching the FDA database for Silk'n alone finds nothing. Fifteen filings are under "Home Skinovations , Ltd." and one, the Titan AllWays, under "Silk'N Beauty , Ltd."[^openfda]

**Disclosure record.** Of the 16 filings, the 15 that are 510(k) notifications are all published as Summaries rather than Statements, so the basis is readable in each case.[^openfda] The sixteenth is a De Novo request rather than a 510(k), which is the pathway used when no predicate exists; it covers a toothbrush, not skin.[^openfda]

**Products reviewed here**

- [[silkn-titan-allways]]: the radiofrequency and light device whose clearance is the one exception in its product code, since the firm ran its own 32-woman study.

No brand claims are quoted here: the storefront path tried returned HTTP 404.
