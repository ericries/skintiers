Lancome, which its parent company spells Lancôme, is a French luxury beauty brand. L'Oreal lists it in its own global brands portfolio under the Luxe Division, alongside Yves Saint Laurent, Kiehl's Since 1851, Biotherm and Helena Rubinstein.[^loreal] A founder is not named here because no reachable primary source confirmed one; the brand's own site blocked automated access. Any positioning or efficacy language from the brand is its own claim, and the evidence is assessed on each product's page.

## Products reviewed here

- [[lancome-advanced-genifique-serum|Advanced Genifique Radiance-Boosting Hydrating Serum]]: the brand's [[bifida-ferment-lysate|bifida ferment lysate]] serum at $220.00 for 3.4 oz (100 mL), graded here as modest on preliminary evidence for hydration and minimal on anecdotal evidence for its anti-aging and brightening claims.

Dermatologist [[aleksandra-brown]] compares this line against Estee Lauder's in a video carded on that product page and on [[anti-aging]].
