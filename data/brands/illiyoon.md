Illiyoon is a South Korean skincare brand, sold in the US through Target, built around ceramide-based moisturizers for dry and sensitive skin. Its official online store's site-disclosure footer names the operating company as Amorepacific Corporation,[^1] and Amorepacific's own group brand page separately lists Illiyoon among its brands, alongside COSRX, Laneige, Sulwhasoo, and Innisfree.[^2]

## Products reviewed here

- [[illiyoon-ceramide-ato-concentrate-cream]]: a ceramide-led face and body moisturizer built around a micro-encapsulated "Ceramide Skin Complex."
