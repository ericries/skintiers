Mederma is an over-the-counter scar and stretch-mark care brand, best known for topical products built around Cepalin, its onion (Allium cepa) bulb extract. It markets itself as "the #1 doctor-recommended brand," a brand claim rather than an independent finding.[^brand]

On this site, Mederma's [[mederma-stretch-marks-therapy|Stretch Marks Therapy]] cream is reviewed in full; it pairs the onion extract with [[centella-asiatica]] and [[hyaluronic-acid]]. As set out on [[stretch-marks]], the topical evidence for these ingredients is thin, so treat the brand's scar and stretch-mark claims as marketing rather than proof. Two more of its products are covered here: [[mederma-advanced-scar-gel|Advanced Scar Gel]] and [[mederma-quick-dry-oil|Quick Dry Oil]].
