Aveeno is a mass-market skincare brand best known for oat-based ("colloidal oatmeal") formulations for sensitive and dry skin, currently owned by Kenvue, whose own site lists it among its brands: "Kenvue, maker of Neutrogena®, Aveeno®, OGX®, and Zyrtec®."[^1]

## Products reviewed here

- [[aveeno-calm-restore-oat-gentle-cleanser]]: a fragrance-free, non-foaming cream cleanser from the Calm + Restore line built on oat and feverfew.

## Ingredients

- [[feverfew-extract]]: a parthenolide-depleted feverfew extract that appears in Aveeno's Ultra-Calming Daily Facial Moisturizer SPF 30.
