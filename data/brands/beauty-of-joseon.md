Beauty of Joseon is a South Korean skincare brand built around a "hanbang" (traditional Korean herbal medicine) positioning, sold internationally by the privately held company Goodai Global Inc.[^1][^2]

## Ownership and company

Beauty of Joseon is operated by Goodai Global Inc., which identifies itself on the brand's official store as the seller of the products. The store lists a head-office address at 541 Eonju-ro, Gangnam-gu, Seoul, and United States operations at 11240 Warland Dr, Cypress, California.[^1] The brand's US terms of service, effective 2023-06-14, place transactions with the company under "the laws of the United States and the state of California."[^2]

Goodai Global is privately held. A company search of DART, the electronic disclosure system run by South Korea's Financial Supervisory Service, returns no filings for the company, consistent with a business that is not a public reporting issuer and does not publish the audited financial statements a listed company would.[^3] Its revenue, ownership structure, and any acquisition history are therefore not documented in public regulatory filings.

## Positioning and technology

Beauty of Joseon markets itself with the tagline "Inspired By Hanbang. Connected by Community." and describes its range as "Authentic Hanbang Korean Skincare Products."[^1] Hanbang refers to traditional Korean herbal medicine. The brand ties that framing to a set of botanicals it features across the range, naming ginseng, rice bran, green tea, red bean, propolis, green plum, and centella asiatica.[^1]

Rice is the ingredient the brand foregrounds most. Its US sunscreen listing describes a formula "Enriched with rice extract," and the imported Relief Sun sunscreen lists rice extract at 30%.[^1] Whether these botanicals do more than a plainer formula is a product-level question, assessed on the product pages below; the hanbang framing is a marketing position, not a measured clinical outcome.

## Independent testing of the sunscreens

The brand markets its Relief Sun sunscreens on the reputation of Korean sun protection. An independent Consumer Reports lab comparison in July 2026 tested Beauty of Joseon's Korean Relief Sun: Rice + Probiotics formula against the brand's US reformulation and reported that "all three Korean formulas provided better protection than their U.S. counterparts." It scored the Korean Relief Sun at the top of its UVA and UVB scales with an average measured SPF of 36, while the brand's US "Day Dew Sunscreen SPF 50" measured an average SPF of 19.[^4] The two are different formulas: the imported SPF50+ version is built on modern organic UV filters, while the brand's US OTC sunscreens use the older US-monograph filters avobenzone, homosalate, octisalate, and octocrylene.[^1] The imported version's modern-filter set and its independently tested UVA and UVB protection are detailed on [[beauty-of-joseon-relief-sun-rice-probiotics-spf50]].

## Portfolio

The brand's official store groups its products into sunscreens, cleansers, exfoliators, toners and essences, serums, moisturizers, and masks, alongside a small lifestyle range.[^1] It sells both Korean-market formulas as imports and separate US-market reformulations, most visibly for its sunscreens, where the US "Daily Relief Sunscreen SPF 40" is a distinct product from the imported Relief Sun: Rice + Probiotics SPF50+.[^1]
