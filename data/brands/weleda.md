Weleda is a Swiss personal-care and pharmaceuticals company that traces its founding to 1921, when a laboratory in Switzerland connected to Rudolf Steiner's early anthroposophic medicine lectures was taken over and commercial production of homeopathic medicines and skin care products began in Schwäbisch Gmünd, Germany.[^1]
