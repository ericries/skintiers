Murad is a United States clinical skincare brand founded by dermatologist Dr. Howard Murad, which the brand markets as "the first modern doctor brand."[^m] Its ranges are organized around targeted skin concerns such as dark spots, acne, resurfacing, and hydration. The "doctor brand" framing and any efficacy positioning are the brand's own claims; the evidence for any single product is on its own page.
