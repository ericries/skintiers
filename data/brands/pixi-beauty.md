Pixi is a beauty brand selling skincare, makeup and fragrance from its own store, best known in skincare for a single glycolic toner. Its About Us page says Petra Strand, who "was a professional makeup artist and a busy mother of four", launched the brand "In 1999, from a tiny boutique in London's Soho"; that is the brand's own account of itself and is not independently verified here.[^about]

**Covered here:** [[pixi-glow-tonic]], the 5% glycolic acid leave-on toner. Its active is graded at [[glycolic-acid]], and [[witch-hazel]] sits further down the same list.

Pixi is one of the brands named on [[exfoliating-acid-products-that-publish-a-ph]] as stating an acid percentage while leaving the pH unstated.

The brand's own promises are listed as "Cruelty-Free Always", "Botanically Boosted", "Vegan Formulations", "Multi-Tasking for Real Life" and "Trust Through Consistency".[^about] None is a claim about efficacy.

Nothing else from the Pixi range has a page here yet.
