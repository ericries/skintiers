NuFACE sells at-home microcurrent devices for facial toning. Its FDA applicant of record is the Carol Cole Company, trading as NuFACE.[^openfda]

**Why it stands out on this site:** the company holds **14 FDA 510(k) clearances** spanning 2007 to 2024, and **every single one publishes a 510(k) Summary rather than a Statement**.[^openfda] A Summary sets out the testing the company submitted; a Statement only certifies that the firm will supply that information on request. So with NuFACE you can read the basis for each clearance. That is the opposite of [[foreo]], where 8 of 10 clearances publish nothing, and it matches the disclosure pattern on [[revive-light-therapy]]. Clearance is not proof of benefit either way, which [[fda-approved-vs-cleared-skincare]] explains.

**On this site:** [[nuface-trinity-microcurrent-device]], graded against the home-device evidence on [[home-led-light-therapy]] and ranked on [[home-use-skincare-devices-by-evidence]].
