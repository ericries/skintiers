LNHC, Inc. is the US labeler and approval holder for [[zelsuvmi-berdazimer-topical-gel]], berdazimer topical gel 10.3%, under NDA 217424. It is the only product openFDA lists under that labeler name.[^openfda] The prescribing information gives the company address as Durham, North Carolina, and an initial US approval of 2024.[^spl]

**Pelthos is the name it trades under.** The ZELSUVMI patient site carries the notice "Pelthos Therapeutics is a trademark and ZELSUVMI is a registered trademark of LNHC, Inc.", and tells patients they may report side effects "to Pelthos Inc." on the same phone number the prescribing information gives for LNHC.[^brand][^spl] Both names appear here because the company uses both in its own materials.

**Who makes it.** The tube artwork filed with the FDA names Orion Corporation, Orion Pharma, of Salo, Finland as manufacturer, for LNHC, Inc. of Durham.[^artwork]

**On this site:** [[zelsuvmi-berdazimer-topical-gel]], and the hub for what it treats, [[molluscum-contagiosum]], where it sits beside [[ycanth-cantharidin-topical-solution]].
