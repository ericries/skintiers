Youth To The People is a California vegan skincare brand founded in 2015 by cousins Joe Cloyes and Greg Gonzalez; L'Oréal signed an agreement to acquire it on December 8, 2021, and it now operates within L'Oréal Luxe.[^loreal-pr][^beautymatter]

## Origin

L'Oréal's own announcement of the acquisition states that "Youth to the People was founded in Los Angeles in 2015 by two cousins, Greg Gonzalez and Joe Cloyes," and that "the brand is inspired by their grandmother, Eva, who started a professional skincare line 40 years ago, utilizing plant extracts and innovative active ingredients."[^loreal-pr] Trade outlet BeautyMatter independently reported the same founding year and founders, describing Youth To The People as "the Los Angeles-based indie skincare brand" and naming Cloyes and Gonzalez as "cousins" who had "been immersed in the beauty industry since a young age."[^beautymatter] L'Oréal's current brand page, describing Youth To The People within its Luxe division, credits the same family history: "Inspired by their grandmother Eva, a pioneering aesthetician and pro-grade skincare expert, Joe Cloyes and Greg Gonzalez founded Youth to the People in 2015."[^loreal-brand]

Before the acquisition, the brand's growth was backed by outside investors. L'Oréal's announcement names "Sandbridge Capital, Strand Equity and Carisa Janes" as the investors that had supported the brand's growth.[^loreal-pr] BeautyMatter dates an earlier minority investment from Sandbridge Capital and Hourglass Cosmetics founder Carisa Janes to 2019, following a separate minority investment from Strand Equity in 2018.[^beautymatter]

## Ownership and history

L'Oréal's press release states the agreement was signed "Clichy, December 8th, 2021," to acquire "Youth to the People, an American skincare company based in California," with the deal "subject to the standard regulatory approvals and other customary conditions."[^loreal-pr] The financial terms were not disclosed; L'Oréal's release said only that the brand "is expected to record over USD 50 Million of sales in 2021."[^loreal-pr] BeautyMatter's independent report of the same deal confirms this, stating plainly that "the financial terms of the deal were not disclosed" and repeating the same $50 million 2021 sales projection.[^beautymatter]

Cyril Chapuy, President of L'Oréal Luxe, said in the announcement that Youth To The People's "skincare expertise based on healthy, vegan, high-efficacy formulas" made it "a very strategic addition to L'Oréal Luxe."[^loreal-pr] Joe Cloyes (Founder and CEO) and Greg Gonzalez (Founder and Chief Brand Officer) said jointly that they "founded Youth to the People to continue our family's legacy of making skincare and to inspire and represent our community."[^loreal-pr] Youth To The People now operates as one of the brands in L'Oréal's Luxe division.[^loreal-brand]

## Positioning

Youth To The People markets itself around vegan, plant-derived "superfood" ingredients paired with what it calls "pro-grade" formulation. L'Oréal's brand page describes the line as "Nutrient-Rich Skincare Powered By Biotech Superfoods + Science," brand marketing language rather than an independently established claim, and organizes current products into three named lines: Superfood System, Energy System, and Supershroom System.[^loreal-brand] The brand's current Brand President, Ayana Dabney, is quoted describing the brand as organized around "three pillars: Product, People, and Planet."[^loreal-brand]
