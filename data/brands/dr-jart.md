Dr. Jart+ is a South Korean skincare brand known for ceramide-based barrier-repair moisturizers, cushion compacts, and BB creams, and is listed as one of the Estée Lauder Companies' portfolio brands on ELC's own site.[^1]

## Products reviewed here

- [[dr-jart-ceramidin-cream]]: a ceramide-based moisturizer built around five named ceramides plus hyaluronic acid and panthenol.
