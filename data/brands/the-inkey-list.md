The INKEY List is a British direct-to-consumer skincare brand that sells single- or few-ingredient, budget-priced serums and moisturizers named after their headline active. The brand's own About page states it was "Founded by Colette Laxton and Mark Curry in 2018."[^about]

## Products reviewed here

- [[the-inkey-list-retinol-serum|The INKEY List Retinol Serum]]: a discontinued-in-the-US, budget entry-level retinoid serum built on retinyl acetate and a diluted Granactive Retinoid blend.
- [[the-inkey-list-starter-retinol-serum|The INKEY List Starter Retinol Serum]]: the replacement dual-retinoid serum, pairing hydroxypinacolone retinoate with 0.01% retinal.
