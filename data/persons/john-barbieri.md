John Barbieri, MD, is listed in the federal NPI registry with a primary specialty of Dermatology, an active Massachusetts license (286496), a second Dermatology taxonomy under an Ohio license, and a Boston practice location.[^npi] He is an author on the American Academy of Dermatology's 2024 "Guidelines of care for the management of acne vulgaris", where his affiliation is given as the Department of Dermatology, Brigham and Women's Hospital.[^aad] He posts skincare education on YouTube.

His transcript-verified videos here cluster on the two conditions he publishes guidelines work on: [[acne]], [[rosacea]], and the drug at the centre of severe acne treatment, [[isotretinoin]], which carries two of his videos including one on microdosing.

Every video is scanned for sponsorship before it is cited here, and sponsored ones are rejected.
