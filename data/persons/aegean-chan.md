Aegean Chan, MD, is listed in the federal NPI registry with a primary specialty of Dermatology, an active California license (A154683), and a Santa Barbara practice location.[^npi] She posts skincare education on TikTok.

**Disclosure that matters when reading her product views:** she created the postpartum skincare brand More Than Nine, whose own site says "Dr. Aegean created More Than Nine for moms like her and like you".[^brand] Her product recommendations are therefore treated as own-brand here and are not relayed as independent picks; her educational explainers are.

Her transcript-verified videos here are myth-correction rather than recommendation: chemical sunscreen on [[sunscreen-uv-filters]], retinoid claims on [[retinoids]], UV as the leading cause of skin ageing on [[sun-damage-photoaging]] and [[anti-aging]], and midlife acne on [[hormonal-acne]].
