The neck and chest (décolletage) age the same way the face does, through cumulative sun exposure, but they are thinner-skinned, more often left out of sun protection and skincare, and quicker to show crepiness, horizontal lines, and mottled red-brown pigment. The good news is that nothing new is needed: the actives that work on facial photoaging work here too, applied more gently.

## Two things that make this area different

- **It is thinner and more reactive.** Neck and chest skin has fewer oil glands and a more delicate barrier than the face, so strong retinoids and acids sting and flake more easily here. The fix is the same active at a lower strength or frequency, not a different ingredient.
- **Fixed sun damage here has a name, and it needs a laser.** Chronic sun exposure produces poikiloderma of Civatte, a red-brown mottled discoloration with fine visible vessels across the sides of the neck and upper chest, characteristically sparing the shaded skin under the chin. There is "no standard treatment guideline," and the treatments studied for it are in-office lasers, not topicals, which sit outside this site's scope.[^sarac] Daily sun protection is what prevents it and keeps it from worsening.

## The Evidence

The evidence for the individual actives lives on their own pages and on [[anti-aging]] and is not repeated here; this hub ranks them for the neck and chest and points to the products that suit the area.

**Sun protection first.** Extending a daily broad-spectrum sunscreen down onto the neck and chest is the single highest-value step, because those areas take heavy, under-protected sun exposure; filter choice is on [[sunscreen-uv-filters]].

**A retinoid, gently.** Retinoids are the best-evidenced anti-aging active, and they improve this skin specifically: in a randomized, no-treatment-controlled study, a vitamin A palmitate and antioxidant oil-based moisturizer "improved fine lines, coarse wrinkles, mottled pigmentation, uneven skin tone, roughness, firmness, and clarity of the skin on the face and neck" and improved "crepey skin texture, dryness, scaling and roughness" on the décolletage.[^rawlings] Because the area is thin, a gentler body-retinol product such as [[gold-bond-age-renew-retinol-overnight-body-lotion|Gold Bond Age Renew Retinol]] or [[cocokind-retinol-body-cream-0-05|Cocokind Retinol Body Cream]] is an easier start than a facial-strength prescription retinoid; the retinoid evidence is on [[retinoids]].

**Texture and tone add-ons.** For rough, crepey texture, a lactic-acid body lotion such as [[amlactin-daily-moisturizing-lotion-12-lactic-acid|AmLactin]] smooths and hydrates ([[lactic-acid]]); for uneven tone and antioxidant support, a vitamin C serum extended to the chest is a reasonable add-on ([[ascorbic-acid-vitamin-c]], with the pigment evidence on [[brightening]]).
