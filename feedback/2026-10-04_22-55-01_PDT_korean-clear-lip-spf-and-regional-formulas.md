## Recommendation and lessons

SOME BY MI Clear is the closest verified Korean match, not a clinically demonstrated best-in-class product. Explain the SPF 15 tradeoff and fragrance-containing formula. Do not override the user's clear preference with a tinted product, infer SPF from generic K-beauty branding, or silently substitute a Japanese/Thai brand.

Useful schema fields are market, exact shade, declared SPF and UVA designation, product type/finish, fragrance ingredients, and full formula provenance. Preserve user constraints across follow-up questions: Korean origin was added to clear/plain gloss, not a replacement for it.

Search absence is not product absence. Following an already-observed manufacturer navigation link found TIRTIR's relevant new product when keyword queries missed it. Record this as an assistant retrieval lesson rather than blaming local indexing for a web search limitation.
