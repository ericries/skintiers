## Lessons

Store finish as both a value and an evidence type: manufacturer description, independent wear assessment, user report, or inference. Keep fully matte, low-shine/satin, non-sticky, and clear color separate. The country of a retailer is not proof of brand origin. Facial sunscreen sticks must not be substituted for dedicated lip products solely because they are matte and have higher SPF.

No evidence establishes that blotting away shine preserves the labeled SPF. Do not suggest blotting or deliberately thin application as the solution to this requirement.

Retrieval was too broad for a short follow-up question. After checking the plausible Korean candidates, a concise statement of the remaining gap and a clearly labeled non-Korean alternative is more useful than repeatedly searching the same combination. Some uncertainty is a market/evidence limitation, not a missing local index. This is feedback on my own search discipline as well as the directory's product coverage.
